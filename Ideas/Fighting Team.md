## Strengths and Weaknesses

### Major Weaknesses

- Flying hits Beedrill hard.
- Psychic pressures Beedrill and Poliwrath.
- Ground hits Blaziken and Rotom hard.
- Dark hits Ralts, Rotom and Honedge hard.
- Rock hits Beedrill, Blaziken and Rayquaza hard.
- Fire hits Beedrill and Honedge hard.
- Ice hits Rayquaza hard.
- Dragon hits Rayquaza hard.
- Fighting hits Snorlax and Chansey hard.

### Defensive Answers

- Ralts (Fairy): answers Dark and Dragon.
- Snorlax: high HP and bulk.
- Chansey: special defensive wall with huge HP.
- Poliwrath: resists Fire, Water, Ice, Bug, Rock, Dark and Steel.
- Rotom: immune to Normal and Fighting.
- Honedge: resists Normal, Grass, Ice, Flying, Psychic, Bug, Rock, Dragon, Steel and Fairy. Immune to Poison and Fighting.
- Rayquaza: strong Dragon / Flying offense and coverage.

## Best Rideable

- Snorlax: large and sturdy. Slow.
- Blaziken: fast land travel.
- Poliwrath: water travel.
- Rotom: possesses vehicles.
- Rayquaza: aerial mount.
