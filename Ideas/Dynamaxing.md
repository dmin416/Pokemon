# Dynamaxing

## Power Spots

Power spots are places soaked in excess energy from wishing stars, fossilized meteor fragments that fell long ago. In Galar those spots make Dynamaxing possible. Elsewhere in canon the same effect does not appear.

## How They Work

- Dynamax only works inside a power spot's range: gym stadiums, Wild Area dens and Max Raid sites.
- A Dynamax Band does nothing outside that range.
- Main campaign gyms only open Dynamax for the final leader match once the stadium spot is live.
- Raid dens are strong enough that a wild Pokémon Dynamaxes on its own. Raid bosses arrive Dynamaxed or Gigantamaxed without a Band.
- Paldea, Kanto remakes and other later settings have no equivalent spots so Dynamax does not carry over there.

## Galar Origin

The densest mass of that energy sits under Hammerlocke and Rose Tower, tied to Eternatus. Wishing stars and the Darkest Day trace back to it. Rose tries to harvest the energy as Galar's permanent power source. That plan destabilizes Eternatus and forces the endgame crisis.

## D's Farm

A Deoxys meteor strikes D's farm and leaves a local power spot. Wishing-star style energy soaks the land the same way Galar's fragments do. Dynamax becomes possible on the farm within that crater range even though the region around it has no other spots. The farm dens and any arena D builds on the impact site count as power spots. Away from the farm Dynamax fails unless another meteor-fed spot exists.
