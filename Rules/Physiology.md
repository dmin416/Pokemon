## Sensory Deprivation

Removing vision, hearing or predictability forces the nervous system to allocate more attention to remaining touch input, since sensory processing shifts resources toward whatever channels are still active. Touch that would normally register as moderate can feel amplified with no competing visual or auditory data diluting attention. Unpredictability also raises baseline arousal through adrenaline and cortisol, since the body can't anticipate where or when the next touch will land, keeping the sympathetic nervous system engaged in a way that intensifies the pleasure signal that follows. ## Climax-Induced Fainting

A documented real phenomenon (orgasm-induced syncope), not purely fictional. Intense orgasm triggers a sharp autonomic swing: sympathetic surge during climax followed by an abrupt parasympathetic rebound as the vagus nerve fires strongly enough to drop heart rate and blood pressure quickly. Breath-holding or hyperventilation during arousal compounds this: breath-holding lowers oxygen and raises CO2, while hyperventilation lowers CO2 too far and constricts blood vessels including cerebral ones, reducing brain oxygen right as blood pressure is also swinging. ## General Mechanisms Referenced Throughout

- **Dopamine (mesolimbic pathway):** drives motivation, "wanting," and arousal buildup
