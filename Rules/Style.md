# Style

Prose and drafting style for `Stories/`. Follow `Formatting.md` for hard punctuation and markup rules.

## Inspiration sources

- Story 1 mainly uses `Inspiration/Rancher`.
- Story 2 mainly uses `Inspiration/Gym Leader`.

## Drafting workflow

- The writer owns the wording.
- Cursor only: capitalize, add periods, fix clear typos, break paragraphs, set chapter headings.
- Do not rewrite, rephrase, compress, expand for style, or "improve" sentences.
- Do not change word choice, sentence shape, or tenses.
- Do not invent plot, dialogue, or lore unless asked.
- When the writer sends a bare line number, pull that beat from the active reference chapter file for the current story (Story 1: `Inspiration/Rancher/Chapters/…`; Story 2: `Inspiration/Gym Leader/Chapters/…`) and put it into the story using the writer's wording habits as already on the page. Change reference names only (e.g. D → D). Do not polish the line into new prose.
- If a fix is needed for `Formatting.md` (four-word floor, no em dash), ask or make the smallest possible edit.

## Chapter files

- Heading form: `# N: Chapter N: Title`
- Separate chapters with `---`
- One sentence per paragraph when the writer sends one sentence.

## Hard limits from Formatting

- No em dashes. No Oxford commas. No commas before "but" or "and".
- No sentence shorter than four words (SFX and quoted speech may break this).
- No extremely long sentences only when the writer asks to split them.
- Speech in quotes and italics. Thoughts in italics. Bold onomatopoeia.
- Third person.

- When logging recipes, keep each rated dish as its own entry. Do not overwrite or merge an older grade into a newer one (e.g. Hot Soup Noodles C- and C stay separate).
