# Decolore Islands

Japanese: デコロラ諸島 Decolora Archipelago. Series-only chain between [Unova](Unova.md) and [Kanto](Kanto.md). Setting of *Adventures in Unova and Beyond*. Not in the location catalogue.

- **Real-world basis:** Unstated.
- **Land area:** Unlisted. Not in [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md).
- **Climate basis:** Island chain. Sparse people. Coastal towns and nature preserves.
- **Weather system:** Series weather. No stated regional cycle.
- **Official population:** None stated.
- **Name:** Decolora / Decolore. Italian uses Isole Cristalline.

## Overview

No professor. No first-partner gift. No regional villains. No League.

Ash, Iris and Cilan sail here after Unova, Nuvema Town toward Vermilion City. Debut: *Farewell, Unova! Setting Sail For New Adventures!*

Second series archipelago after the [Orange Islands](Orange-Islands.md). Unlike that chain, there is no Orange Crew equivalent. No one in the main cast catches a new Pokémon here. Team Rocket can appear. They are not a local syndicate.

Wilds mix [Kanto](Kanto.md), [Johto](Johto.md), [Hoenn](Hoenn.md), [Sinnoh](Sinnoh.md) and Unova species.

Japanese 2013 Wi-Fi Jirachi used Decolora as OT, a nod to *Searching for a Wish!*.

## Geography

Ship travel only. Towns sit on coasts. Much of the chain is preserve or empty.

**Inhabited:** Honey Island, Scalchop Island, Grand Spectrala Island, Torom Island, Wayfarer Island, Capacia Island, Harvest Island, Frond Island, Cave Island, Paladin Island.

**Deserted:** Grand Spectrala Islet, Deserted Island (*Mystery on a Deserted Island!*).

**Unnamed in-series:** two pirate islands (*The Pirates of Decolore!*), then islands from *Celebrating the Hero's Comet!*, *Go, Go Gogoat!* and *Team Rocket's Shocking Recruit!*.

Paladin Island hosts *Survival of the Striaton Gym!* Harvest Island hosts *The Journalist from Another Region!*

## Writer notes

Bridge water between Unova and Kanto in the series, not a game map. Do not invent a League or a professor. Do not treat it as a catch route for the traveling party. Pair with Orange Islands only as "Ash's earlier island chain." That one had a League. This one is a crossing.
