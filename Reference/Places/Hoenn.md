# Hoenn

Japanese: ホウエン地方. Third core region. South of [Sinnoh](Sinnoh.md). Southwest of [Kanto](Kanto.md) and [Johto](Johto.md). Setting of Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire.

110 catalogued places. Master list: [Locations.md](Locations.md).

- **Real-world basis:** Kyushu rotated 90° counterclockwise, plus Okinawa. Rotation was for GBA playability on a horizontal screen.
- **Land area:** 39,031 km²
- **Straight-line diameter:** ~198 km
- **Climate basis:** Subtropical Kyushu. Warmer, nearer the equator than the older regions.
- **Weather system:** First region with route weather: rain, storms, ash, sun. Groudon and Kyogre can force region-wide sun or rain. Emerald mixes both until Rayquaza stops them.
- **Notable weather:** Route 119/120 rain. Mt. Chimney ash on Fallarbor. Desert sandstorms. ORAS primal sun or rain. Emerald mixed drought and downpour over Sootopolis.
- **Official population:** 694 in ORAS (towns, cities and other areas). 598 in Ruby and Sapphire.
- **Largest named city:** Mauville 148 in ORAS. Lilycove 106, largest in RSE.
- **Name:** Abundant relations (豊縁). City names are two words joined, not colors or plants.
- **Adjective:** Hoennian

See [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md), [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md) and [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md).

## Overview

Professor Birch in Littleroot Town. First partners: Treecko, Torchic or Mudkip. Villains: Team Magma in Ruby/Omega Ruby, Team Aqua in Sapphire/Alpha Sapphire, both in Emerald.

Hoenn League at Ever Grande City. Sixteen settlements including the League. Most towns sit in nature. No Castelia- or Lumiose-scale megacity. Map shape reads as yin and yang: land vs sea, with Mt. Chimney and Sootopolis as the inner dots.

Most people live on the coast. Interior is forest and mountain. Fortree lives in tree houses. Ash from Mt. Chimney falls on the northwest. Sootopolis fills a volcanic crater (RSE) or impact crater (ORAS). Water routes cover about half the map. Diving reaches deep sea and offshore islands.

## History

Primal Kyogre filled the sea. Primal Groudon raised land. Rayquaza lived in the ozone. A clash for the world ended when Rayquaza came down and drove both into cavern sleep.

2,000 years before ORAS, meteors hit. The Draconid home becomes Meteor Falls. Natural energy wakes the pair again. Draconids wish on a meteor and call Rayquaza. The region then worships it as savior.

1,000 years before, AZ of Kalos plants a tree at the Cave of Origin. A primal-energy meteor opens the Sootopolis crater. The pair wake and fight. People wish on Rayquaza. It Mega Evolves for the first time with a special meteorite, strips their Primal forms and puts them to sleep. AZ watches and learns more of Mega Evolution. Sky Pillar is built. Draconids pass a prophecy that the meteor returns in 1,000 years.

Red and Blue Orbs are made to keep Kyogre and Groudon asleep and left on Mt. Pyre. 40 years before Ruby and Sapphire, Lunatone is found at a meteor site.

**Ruby/Sapphire/ORAS:** Brendan or May moves from Johto to Littleroot, saves Birch, keeps the first partner. Magma (land) or Aqua (sea) wakes Groudon or Kyogre in Seafloor Cavern. Sun or rain blankets Hoenn. The player calms the beast in the Cave of Origin (Primal forms in ORAS), then takes the League.

**Delta Episode (ORAS):** A meteor turns toward Hoenn. Zinnia steals Key Stones to call Rayquaza. At Sky Pillar the player feeds Rayquaza a meteorite so it can Mega Evolve, flies into space and breaks the rock. Deoxys comes from the wreck.

**Emerald:** Both teams wake both beasts. Mixed weather. They fight in Sootopolis. The player wakes Rayquaza at Sky Pillar. Then the League and Battle Frontier.
