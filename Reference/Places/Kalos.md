# Kalos

Japanese: カロス地方. Sixth core region. Setting of X, Y and Legends: Z-A. Shaped like a five-pointed star. [Paldea](Paldea.md) sits over a mountain range. [Galar](Galar.md) has a historically strained relationship with Kalos.

106 catalogued places. Master list: [Locations.md](Locations.md).

- **Real-world basis:** Northern metropolitan France. Chosen for X and Y's theme of beauty.
- **Land area:** 551,695 km²
- **Straight-line diameter:** ~743 km
- **Climate basis:** Temperate France. Northeast mountains run cold into the plains. Western ocean. Snowbelle stays frozen from Gym air.
- **Weather system:** No dynamic overworld cycle. Climate is local: snow in Mountain Kalos, milder coasts.
- **Notable weather:** Snowbelle ice. Frost Cavern fog and snow. Power Plant on a wasteland. Haunted marsh in the north of Mountain Kalos.
- **Official population:** 1,288 including towns, cities, routes and areas. Named settlements sum to 920.
- **Largest named city:** Lumiose City at 448.
- **Name:** Greek κάλλος, beauty. English town names from fragrance terms. Japanese from incense. European localizations from art terms.
- **Adjective:** Kalosian

See [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md), [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md) and [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md).

## Overview

Professor Sycamore in X and Y. First partners: Chespin, Fennekin or Froakie. Z-A uses Chikorita, Tepig and Totodile. Villains: Team Flare.

Kalos League at the League building. Anime championship in Lumiose. Sixteen cities and towns plus the League. Kiloude is a seventeenth site, train-only from Lumiose, outside the three map divisions.

22 routes, numbered from 1 like Unova. Every route also has an alternate name (Vaniville Pathway, Avance Trail and so on). Series travel from Kanto needs a plane.

Pentagon iconography: origin mark, Honor of Kalos, route signs, Pokédex tricolor (white central, blue coastal, red mountain). Lumiose plazas and gates form a pentagon around Prism Tower.

## Geography

Ocean west and northwest. Mountains east and south. Those borders plus rivers make the star. Three Pokédex divisions meet at Lumiose.

**Central Kalos:** South and center. Forested plains, rivers, little elevation. Walled towns, châteaus. Vaniville, Santalune, part of Lumiose, Parfum Palace.

**Coastal Kalos:** West strip between mountains and sea. Rocky southwest, lush beaches northwest. Menhir peninsula. Reflection Cave, Geosenge, Shalour and the Tower of Mastery.

**Mountain Kalos:** East. Cooler, snow on some routes. Haunted marsh to the north. Power Plant on wasteland. Poké Ball Factory in Laverre. League.

Kiloude sits in southern mountains, isolated, train from Lumiose only.

## History

AZ unites Kalos with era-ahead technology and keeps his late mother's Eternal Flower Floette.

3,000 years before X and Y a war with another region kills Floette in battle. AZ builds a machine with Infinity Energy, revives her, then turns it into the ultimate weapon and ends the war by blasting the field. AZ and Floette gain eternal life. She leaves him. He wanders, including [Galar](Galar.md) and [Hoenn](Hoenn.md). Mega Stones form when Evolution stones take Infinity Energy from the blast. AZ's younger brother, who wanted Kalos for himself, buries the weapon under what becomes Geosenge and tells only his line.

2,000 years before: a powerful man asks AZ to protect Kalos. AZ builds Ange in Prism Tower to share Floette's eternal life. He never reunites with her in time to use it.

800 years before: Xerneas releases (X) or Yveltal absorbs (Y) life energy, then hibernates in a forest (X) or mountains (Y). 300 years before: Parfum Palace.

Lysandre, descendant of AZ's brother, helps the suffering (including young Corbeau), then sours when people demand more. He founds Team Flare to fire the weapon and keep only Flare alive.

**X and Y:** Calem or Serena starts in Vaniville, gets a partner from Tierno and a Pokédex from Trevor. Flare steals Fossils, siphons the Power Plant, raids the Poké Ball Factory and hunts Abomasnow. After Olympia's Badge, Lysandre reveals the weapon plan. The player beats him in the Labs and Secret HQ, captures Xerneas (X) or Yveltal (Y). He fires anyway. The blast wrecks the weapon and buries HQ. The player becomes Champion over Diantha. At Sycamore's ceremony AZ battles, is freed of his grief, and Floette returns.

Z-A backfill: Lysandre meant to fire Ange and the weapon together. Without Eternal Flower Floette he fires only the weapon. The shock wakes Ange, Mega Power floods Lumiose, wild Pokémon move in. Zygarde saves Lysandre, who survives amnesiac, blinded in one eye, with AZ-like 3,000-year life.

Looker postgame: Lumiose cases, Emma and Mimi, Essentia (Emma in a suit under Xerosic). Looker takes Xerosic and leaves Emma the bureau.

**Between games:** AZ owns Hotel Z. Two years later Quasartico Inc. redevelops Lumiose with wild zones. Emma inherits the agency. Sycamore and Trevor leave. Mable plea-deals into lab director, confined to Lumiose. Six months before Z-A, Rogue Mega Evolution starts (Mega Pidgeot first). AZ cannot keep up. Urbain or Taunie steps in, founds Team MZ. Quasartico starts the Z-A Royale to find a Mega user who can stop Ange. Naveen and Lida join for studio space and lodging.

Darkrai on Prism Tower soaks Mega Power, nightmare power runs wild, Hyperspace Lumiose forms from the city's subconscious. Ansha finds a weakened Hoopa, feeds it a donut, and hunts Hyperspace Rayquaza as a gift for Diantha.

**Legends: Z-A:** Five years after X and Y. Paxton or Harmony arrives by train and joins Team MZ. Main plot is still thin in sources. Mega Dimension: distortions open to Hyperspace Lumiose.

## Writer notes

Largest regional Pokédex. Two status-healing local foods: Lumiose Galette and Shalour Sable. 3,000-year war may echo the War of the Spanish Succession and Versailles as Parfum Palace. Kalos-Galar strain echoes France-England.
