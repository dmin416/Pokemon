# Galar

Japanese: ガラル地方. Eighth core region. Setting of Sword and Shield plus the Isle of Armor and Crown Tundra expansions. Across a strait from [Kalos](Kalos.md). Far from [Hoenn](Hoenn.md), [Unova](Unova.md) and [Alola](Alola.md). Kanto travel needs aircraft.

92 catalogued places. Master list: [Locations.md](Locations.md).

- **Real-world basis (base):** England + Wales, map rotated 180°. Northern Galar matches southern England.
- **Land area (base):** 151,174 km²
- **Straight-line diameter (base):** ~389 km
- **Isle of Armor:** Isle of Man, 572 km², ~24 km
- **Crown Tundra:** Scotland, 78,772 km², ~281 km
- **Full region (both DLC):** Great Britain + Isle of Man, 230,518 km², ~480 km
- **Climate basis:** Temperate/industrial Britain
- **Weather system:** Wild Area has a rotating daily weather cycle (fog, sandstorm, snow, rain, thunderstorm, intense sun) tied to Max Raid spawns
- **Notable weather:** Motostoke industrial haze, Hulbury coastal rain, Circhester permanent alpine snow, Route 6 constant rain. Journeys often shows a gray cloudy sky.
- **Isle of Armor weather:** Static, temperate, no major weather events
- **Crown Tundra weather:** Static permanent snow, Freezington and Giant's Bed under constant snowfall
- **Official population:** 715 named settlements. 727 including other areas.
- **Largest named city:** Wyndon at 157. Motostoke 120. Hammerlocke 102. Circhester 97.

See [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md), [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md) and [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md).

## Overview

Professor Magnolia, then Sonia. First partners: Grookey, Scorbunny or Sobble. Villains: Team Yell, Macro Cosmos, then Sordward and Shielbert. Horizons also uses the Explorers.

Galar League. No Elite Four. Champion Cup at Wyndon Stadium. No Victory Road. No Pokémon academy. Adjective: Galarian.

Battles are spectator sport. Town names pull from sports terms. Team Yell plays like football hooligans. Motostoke and Galarian Weezing lean on the Industrial Revolution. Curry and Isle of Armor martial arts mark non-British threads in a multicultural UK analog. Galarian Meowth, Perrserker, Yamask and Runerigus nod to Norse and Anglo-Saxon work.

No English settlement uses City or Town in its name. Ten numbered routes, second fewest after Paldea's zero. Routes do not span the region. Trains link the clusters.

Name notes: gala / gallant / γίγας (giant) / stellar, plus Galahad and the Grail. Irish and Scottish Gaelic *galar* means disease.

## Geography

Long north-south mainland. Isle of Armor off the east, nearest Spikemuth. Crown Tundra at the southern end, cut off by mountains, reached by train.

South of those mountains: Slumbering Weald and Postwick, then a long plains valley through Motostoke and Hammerlocke. Arid rock at the northern plains: Route 6 cliffs by Stow-on-Side, Route 8 ruins south of Circhester. Northwest: Glimwood Tangle around Ballonlea. East of that: Circhester's tundra and the snow range that walls Wyndon from the rest.

Isle of Armor: beaches, forest, hills, caves, valleys. On the train net and Flying Taxis.

## History

20,000 years before Sword and Shield a meteor carrying Eternatus hits Galar. 3,000 years before, the Darkest Day: Eternatus wakes, a black storm covers the region, two humans and two legendary heroes stop it and become kings. Body fragments become Wishing Pieces and Wishing Stars, then Dynamax Bands. Stow-on-Side holds statues of the kings and the two Pokémon. The wolves fade from memory.

In the Crown Tundra, people heal a wounded Calyrex. It turns frozen land into harvest country and is worshipped as king of bountiful harvests. It binds Glastrier or Spectrier as its steed. Faith fades, Calyrex weakens, the steed leaves.

Galar and Kalos have a historically strained relationship.

70 years before the games, Opal takes the Ballonlea Gym from her mother. 50 years before, Mustard beats Opal and becomes Champion. He holds the title 18 years, the longest run, then loses it when his partner dies. He later buys an island, names it the Isle of Armor and founds the Master Dojo.

**Sword and Shield:** Leon gives Victor or Gloria and Hop their first partners. They meet a mystery Pokémon in the Slumbering Weald, get Pokédexes from Sonia, Dynamax Bands from Magnolia and Leon's endorsement for the Gym Challenge. Team Yell backs Marnie. Macro Cosmos, led by Rose, backs Bede. During the Champion Cup Rose wakes Eternatus to power Galar for 1,000 years. The player and Hop take the Rusted Sword and Rusted Shield from the Weald, beat Rose at the Hammerlocke Energy Plant, fight Eternatus with Zacian and Zamazenta, catch Eternatus, then beat Leon for the Championship.

Postgame: Sordward and Shielbert steal the rusted relics, dump Dynamax particles into stadiums and try to turn one hero against the region. The player, Hop and Piers stop the rampages. Each version's hero is caught after being calmed.

**Isle of Armor:** The player joins Mustard's dojo, rivals Klara (Sword) or Avery (Shield), earns Kubfu, evolves it at the Tower of Darkness or Tower of Waters, then Gigantamaxes Urshifu and beats Mustard at full strength.

**Crown Tundra:** Peony sends the player in Peonia's place. Calyrex is restored with the Reins of Unity and Glastrier or Spectrier. The player also finds the legendary giants including Regieleki and Regidrago, hunts Galarian legendary birds across the Tundra, Armor and Wild Area, then Dynamax Adventures in the Max Lair (Necrozma, Ultra Beasts). Leon later hosts the Galarian Star Tournament in Wyndon.

## Writer notes

No Mythical debuted with the base games. Four fossils, the most of any region, but they do not evolve and are not the most Fossil Pokémon (Kanto still holds five). Galarian fossils are not Rock-type, have no gender and cannot breed. First region without a dedicated Ash series that fully walks the Gym circuit. Journeys visits without that full run.
