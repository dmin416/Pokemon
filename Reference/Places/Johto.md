# Johto

Japanese: ジョウト地方. West of [Kanto](Kanto.md). The two regions share one landmass south of [Sinnoh](Sinnoh.md) and the Sinjoh Ruins. Second core region. Playable in Generations II and IV.

67 catalogued places. Master list: [Locations.md](Locations.md).

- **Real-world basis:** Kansai. Violet from Nara. Ecruteak from Kyoto. Goldenrod from Osaka.
- **Land area:** 33,097 km²+
- **Straight-line diameter:** ~182 km+
- **Climate basis:** Temperate Japan, wetter than Kanto
- **Weather system:** No overworld weather in Gen II. Gen IV adds conditions such as constant rain on Route 33.
- **Notable weather:** Whirl Islands storms, Mt. Silver blizzards
- **Official population:** 428 named towns and cities. 452 in HeartGold/SoulSilver including Safari Zone Gate, Frontier Access and other areas.
- **Largest named city:** Goldenrod City at 126
- **Name:** Castle capital or lattice-plan capital (城都 / 条都). Most English city names are plants.
- **Adjective:** Johtonian

See [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md), [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md) and [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md).

## Overview

Professor Elm in New Bark Town. First partners: Chikorita, Cyndaquil or Totodile. Team Rocket is the villain group, revived from Kanto. Horizons also uses the Explorers.

Johto has no separate League building. Eight Johto Badges still send a Trainer to the Indigo Plateau Elite Four shared with Kanto. Anime championship: Silver Conference at Silver Town under Mt. Silver.

Kansai analog: temples, shrines, wooden towers. Traditional next to Kanto's modern Kantō. Goldenrod is the ultra-modern exception, the industrial and economic hub in the center of the west coast.

## Geography

Temperate and wetter than Kanto. More rural. Water to the west and south. Cianwood is likely a separate landmass. West Johto is low forest and holds most of the people. Goldenrod sits on the coast at the region's heart. East Johto is almost all mountains, including Mt. Silver on the Kanto border. Blackthorn is a Dragon Tamer city cut into rock. The series places the [Orange Islands](Orange-Islands.md) south of this landmass.

Routes continue Kanto's numbers: 29 through 48. Routes 47 and 48 open west in Generation IV only. Few sea routes. Route 28 is Kanto-owned but sits on the border. Routes 26 and 27 are Kanto sites that Johto can Fly to in Gen IV.

Safari Zone Gate and Frontier Access function as tourist towns, not counted as the ten core settlements.

## History

1,500 years before Gold and Silver an Alph civilization lives with Unown. Outsiders arrive. Unown refuse anyone else. The builders leave Johto after setting puzzles so Unown can be summoned later. Far north they meet Hisui people and raise a temple to Arceus that mixes Alph and the Temple of Sinnoh: the Sinjoh Ruins. Legendary dance rites travel back to Ecruteak.

700 years before: Ecruteak raises two nine-tier towers. Brass Tower in the west (awakening). Bell Tower in the east (rest). Lugia and Ho-Oh roost there.

500 years before: Cianwood Pharmacy, oldest in the world.

400 years before: Azalea drought. Slowpoke brings rain by yawning and earns the town's honor.

150 years before: Lightning hits Brass Tower. Fire burns three days until rain. Three nameless Pokémon die. Ho-Oh revives them as the beasts of lightning, fire and rain. People fear them. The three flee into Johto grassland. Lugia and Ho-Oh leave as humans and Pokémon drift apart.

**Gold/Silver/Crystal/HeartGold/SoulSilver:** Ethan, Kris or Lyra runs an errand for Elm. A red-haired boy steals one of Elm's three Pokémon. Team Rocket, using a three-year-old plan, enrages Gyarados including a red shiny at the Lake of Rage, then seizes Goldenrod Radio Tower to call Giovanni. Celebi keeps Giovanni away. Archer vows Rocket will not reform. The player takes eight Johto Badges, beats the Indigo Elite Four and Champion, then can meet the beasts, Unown, Lugia and Ho-Oh.

## Writer notes

Johto recycles Kanto's League, PC storage developer and villain team. No burial grounds. No Fairy-type game set here yet. In the series Ash takes no new human companion through Johto and uses Pikachu in every Gym battle. Pokémon caught in Johto show as "faraway place" when traded into Sinnoh games.
