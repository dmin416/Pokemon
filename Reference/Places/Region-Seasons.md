# Region Seasons (Origin Climate)

Seasonal feel from each region's real-world basis. Game weather systems stay in [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md). Numeric highs/lows by zone stay in [Region-Temperatures.md](Region-Temperatures.md). Land scale: [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md).

Use this for what a place is like at a time of year. Keep Pokémon overrides (Ice Gym freeze, legendary storms, Area Zero, Wild Area roulette, permanent route rain) as exceptions on top.

Northern-hemisphere calendar unless noted. Months are approximate.

## Kanto (Kantō, Japan)

Humid subtropical. Four seasons. Hot sticky summers. Cool clear winters. Rainy season (tsuyu) early summer. Typhoons late summer to autumn.

| Season | Feel |
|---|---|
| Spring (Mar–May) | Mild. Cherry blossom. Occasional rain. Comfortable travel. |
| Summer (Jun–Aug) | Tsuyu then heat and humidity. Thunderstorms. Typhoon risk late. |
| Autumn (Sep–Nov) | Warm then crisp. Still storm risk early. Clear skies later. |
| Winter (Dec–Feb) | Cool to cold. Dry. Little snow on the plain. Mountains colder. |

**Pokémon leeway:** Cinnabar heat/ash. Seafoam cave ice. No game overworld seasons.

## Johto (Kansai / Kinki, Japan)

Same family as Kanto. Often a bit wetter. Inland basins can be hotter in summer and colder in winter than the coast.

| Season | Feel |
|---|---|
| Spring | Mild. Wet spells. Blossom. |
| Summer | Humid heat. Heavy rain periods. Typhoons. |
| Autumn | Clearing. Good harvest weather. Early storms possible. |
| Winter | Cool. Dryish on the Pacific side. West coast and mountains get more winter weather. Mt. Silver is harsh. |

**Pokémon leeway:** Route 33 rain (Gen IV). Whirl Islands storms. Mt. Silver blizzards.

## Hoenn (Kyushu + Okinawa)

Subtropical to nearly tropical on islands. Long hot wet summers. Mild winters. Strong typhoon exposure. Okinawa stays warm year-round.

| Season | Feel |
|---|---|
| Spring | Warm fast. Rain increases. |
| Summer | Hot. Very wet. Typhoons. Lush growth. |
| Autumn | Still warm. Storms taper. |
| Winter | Mild on coasts. Cooler inland and at height. Rarely harsh cold. |

**Pokémon leeway:** Route 119/120 rain. Desert sandstorms. Mt. Chimney ash on Fallarbor. Groudon sun / Kyogre rain / Emerald mix.

## Sinnoh (Hokkaido + Kurils)

Cool summers. Long cold winters. Snow is normal, not rare. Short blossom spring. Battle Zone (Sakhalin-like) can run milder and wetter.

| Season | Feel |
|---|---|
| Spring (Apr–May) | Late thaw. Mud. Brief blossom. |
| Summer (Jun–Aug) | Mild to warm. Comfortable. Not Kantō-hot. |
| Autumn (Sep–Oct) | Short. Clear then sharp cold. |
| Winter (Nov–Mar) | Long. Heavy snow north and mountains. South gets light snow more often now in lore. |

**Pokémon leeway:** Snowpoint permanent snow look. Coronet / Route 217 blizzards. Route 210 fog. Route 228 sandstorm. Stark ash.

## Hisui (same land, earlier)

Same Hokkaido pattern, harsher and emptier. People dress for cold. Sub-regions keep their moods year-round on top of seasons.

| Area mood | Baseline |
|---|---|
| Fieldlands | Clearest. Seasonal snow in winter. |
| Mirelands | Damp. Fog. Mud worse in thaw. |
| Coastlands | Rain and wind common. |
| Highlands | Cold with height. Snow on upper slopes much of the year. |
| Icelands | Wind and snow dominant. |

**Pokémon leeway:** Dynamic map weather. Distortions. Daybreak mass-outbreak storms.

## Unova (New York metro)

Four sharp seasons. Humid summer. Cold winter. Spring and autumn change fast. Game compresses each season to ~30 days, three cycles per year.

| Season | Feel |
|---|---|
| Spring | Mild to warm. Rain common. |
| Summer | Hot. Humid. Clear stretches. City heat in Castelia / Nimbasa. |
| Autumn | Cool. Clear. Sandstorm flavor on Route 4 / Desert Resort in-game. |
| Winter | Cold. Snow and blizzards inland north (Icirrus, Twist Mountain, Route 8). East fog stays as a local trait. |

**Pokémon leeway:** Keep the game season calendar for events and spawns. Permanent eastern fog.

## Kalos (mainland France)

Temperate. West milder (Atlantic). East and northeast colder with real winter. South warmer. Mountains hold snow.

| Season | Feel |
|---|---|
| Spring | Mild. Rain. Green coasts and fields. |
| Summer | Warm to hot inland. Milder on the west coast. Storms possible. |
| Autumn | Cool. Wet spells. Harvest. |
| Winter | Cool on the Atlantic edge. Cold and snow in the northeast mountains. |

**Pokémon leeway:** Snowbelle held in Ice Gym freeze. Frost Cavern snow/fog. Haunted marsh. Power Plant wasteland.

## Alola (Hawaii)

Tropical. No cold winter. Wet season vs dry season matters more than four temperate seasons. Windward sides wetter. Leeward drier. High peaks cold.

| Period | Feel |
|---|---|
| Wet (roughly Nov–Apr) | More showers. Lush. Trade-wind clouds. |
| Dry (roughly May–Oct) | Warmer. Sunnier leeward coasts. Still local showers. |
| Year-round coasts | Warm. Beach weather. |
| High peaks | Cool to cold. Lanakila can snow or hail. |

**Pokémon leeway:** Haina Desert arid pocket. Lush Jungle always rainforest. Exeggutor Island showers then rainbows or meteors. Game has no season cycle.

## Galar (England + Wales; Armor = Isle of Man; Crown = Scotland)

Maritime temperate. Mild winters. Cool summers. Frequent cloud and rain. West wetter. North colder.

| Season | Feel |
|---|---|
| Spring | Mild. Showers. Long daylight building. |
| Summer | Mild to warm. Rarely hot. Changeable. |
| Autumn | Wet. Wind. Early dark. |
| Winter | Cool. Grey. Lowland snow uncommon but possible. Hills and Scotland snow proper. |

**Local notes:** Motostoke haze. Hulbury coastal rain. Circhester alpine snow pocket. Route 6 constant rain. Isle of Armor mild static. Crown Tundra Scottish highland cold and snow.

**Pokémon leeway:** Wild Area daily weather roulette is gameplay, not climate.

## Paldea (Iberian Peninsula)

Much of the peninsula is hot-summer Mediterranean or continental. Atlantic west is wetter. South can be arid. North mountains cold. Interior heats hard in summer.

| Season | Feel |
|---|---|
| Spring | Mild. Green. Good travel. South blooms early. |
| Summer | Hot inland and south. Dry in Asado-like zones. Coasts a bit kinder. Storms rare except north. |
| Autumn | Warm then cooling. Harvest. Rain returns in many areas. |
| Winter | Mild on southern coasts. Cold and snow on Glaseado / north. Interior can be chilly and clear. |

**Province lean:** South wetlands and olive heat. West desert/cliff dryness. East grass and forest. North lakes and snow. Area Zero: no weather.

**Farm detail:** D Ranch near Porto Marinada uses [Paldea-Farm-Climate.md](Paldea-Farm-Climate.md) (El Bierzo / La Rioja).

## Orre (Phoenix, Arizona)

Hot desert. Brutal summers. Mild winters. Late-summer monsoon storms. Big day-night temperature swing.

| Season | Feel |
|---|---|
| Spring | Warm. Dry. Pleasant before the heat. |
| Summer | Extreme heat. Monsoon storms late summer. Dust. |
| Autumn | Heat eases. Dry again. |
| Winter | Mild days. Cool nights. Rare light rain. |

**Local notes:** Phenac oasis irrigation. Agate cooler green highland. Citadark volcanic. Outskirt Stand exposed desert.

## Orange Islands (tropical chain, south of Kanto/Johto)

No exact real map. Treat as warm subtropical/tropical island weather year-round. Tourist climate. Pinkan and empty isles still warm.

| Period | Feel |
|---|---|
| Year-round | Warm to hot. Sea breezes. Showers. |
| Storm season | Heavier rain and rough seas (use Pacific typhoon timing as leeway). |

**Pokémon leeway:** Shamouti can break world weather when the birds are disturbed.

## Decolore Islands (between Unova and Kanto)

No stated climate. Writing default: temperate maritime islands. Milder than Unova winters. Wetter than inland Kanto. Coastal towns and preserves.

| Season | Feel |
|---|---|
| Spring / Autumn | Mild. Showers. |
| Summer | Warm. Humid. |
| Winter | Cool. Rarely severe. Sea wind. |
