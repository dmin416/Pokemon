# Orange Islands

Japanese: オレンジ諸島. Also Orange Archipelago, a name used only a handful of times. Tropical chain south of [Kanto](Kanto.md) and [Johto](Johto.md). Series-only. Setting of *Adventures in the Orange Islands* and *The Power of One*. Not in the location catalogue.

- **Real-world basis:** Unstated. Island names are citrus: oranges and related fruit.
- **Land area:** Unlisted. Not in [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md).
- **Climate basis:** Tropical. Tourists come for the weather. Officer Jennys and Nurse Joys wear lighter uniforms. Some have tans.
- **Weather system:** Series weather. Shamouti legend can wreck global climate. See [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md).
- **Official population:** None stated.
- **Name:** Citrus. Maps in English and Japanese books place the chain south of Kanto and Johto.

## Overview

Professor Ivy on Valencia Island. No first-partner gift. Villains: Team Rocket.

Orange League. Four Orange Crew challenges, then a Full Battle against Supreme Gym Leader Drake on Pummelo Island. Challenges are not locked to a Gym building. Ash wins here. Journeys never visits. *Ride, Lapras, Ride!* mentions it.

Likely written as filler while Generation II was still in development. Alternate-colored Pokémon live here, notably on Pinkan. Many islands are empty.

Ancient texts call the center of the chain the source of all the world's waters and possibly the origin of life.

## Geography

Scattered islands. Travel by ship. Mandarin Island South holds the towns Yambera and Trovitopolis.

**Inhabited:** Valencia (Ivy's lab), Tangelo (Pokémon Park), Mikan (Gym), Mandarin North, Sunburst, Kinnow (Pokémon Showboat), Navel (Gym), Moro (ghost ship), Golden, Murcott, Mandarin South, Trovita (Gym), Shamouti, Ascorbia, Butwal, Kumquat (Gym), Pummelo (Stadium, Palace of Victory), Tarroco, Hamlin.

**Deserted:** Pinkan, Fukuhara Island No. 4, Seven Grapefruit Islands, Fairchild, Fire, Lightning, Ice, Cleopatra, Rind.

**Unnamed in-series:** islands from *The Joy of Pokémon*, *A Way Off Day Off*, *Pikachu's Rescue Adventure* and *The Stun Spore Detour*.

## Orange League

Four badges to enter Pummelo. The Orange Crew sets tasks, not a standard eight-Gym circuit.

## Shamouti

*The Power of One*. Fire Island, Lightning Island and Ice Island sit with Shamouti. Lawrence III steals the birds' orbs. Weather fails worldwide until Ash restores the shrine as the Chosen One. Lugia is bound to that balance.

## Manga

*The Electric Tale of Pikachu* covers Lapras, Snorlax, Rudy and Drake. Ash travels mostly alone. Tracey is absent. The GS Ball is absent. The League is the reason he sails.

*Pokémon Newspaper Strip* once calls it "Orange Island" in a baggage gag.

## Writer notes

Series Kanto south, not a game region. Do not mix with the [Sevii Islands](Kanto.md#sevii-islands). Tracey Sketchit is the local watcher. Pinkan colors are poison-berry coats, not Alolan forms.

Closest later echo is [Alola](Alola.md): tropical chain, four island trials to enter a League, non-building challenges, Ash as champion. Alola then became a core game region. This chain did not.

Ash's later island crossing is the [Decolore Islands](Decolore-Islands.md), between Unova and Kanto. No League there.
