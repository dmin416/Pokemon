# Orre

Japanese: オーレ地方 Ōre. Generation III side series. Setting of Colosseum and XD: Gale of Darkness. Other games list Pokémon from here as a [distant land](Other.md).

18 catalogued places. Master list: [Locations.md](Locations.md).

- **Real-world basis:** Phoenix, Arizona. Place names from minerals and ore.
- **Land area:** Unlisted. Not in [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md).
- **Climate basis:** Arid Arizona. Huge desert wasteland plus a small green mountain pocket.
- **Weather system:** Static per location. No overworld weather cycle.
- **Notable weather:** Desert heat. Phenac as an oasis. Agate stays green. Mt. Battle is a dormant volcano. Citadark Isle is ringed by magma.
- **Official population:** 113 in Colosseum (Phenac, Pyrite, Agate and The Under). Gateon Port is 47 in XD and sits outside that sum. XD's full total is unpublished.
- **Largest named city:** Phenac 34 in Colosseum. Gateon Port 47 in XD.
- **Name:** Ore. Spanish Aura, French Rhode and Italian Auros lean on gold and related metals.

See [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md) and [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md).

## Overview

Professor Krane. No first-partner gift. Michael starts with Eevee. Villains: Team Snagem and Cipher. Strategy Memo instead of a Pokédex.

No League. No numbered routes. Battles are mostly Doubles. Facilities run consecutive fights: colosseums, stadiums, Mt. Battle, Realgam Tower. Michael wanting a journey when he comes of age implies some League-like path may exist offscreen.

Colosseum has no wild Pokémon. XD adds Poké Spots. Trainers rely on imports, mainly from [Johto](Johto.md) and [Hoenn](Hoenn.md). Snag Machines steal Pokémon. Cipher closes hearts to make Shadow Pokémon. Purification reverses that.

Science and architecture run ahead of the climate. Currency is $ even in Japanese text, unlike yen in the Japan-based core regions of that era. Phenac holds the only trade-capable Pokémon Center.

## Geography

Desert dominates. A small mountainous green belt holds Agate. Mt. Battle sits in the center. Few towns. Phenac is the showpiece city in the harshest sand, fed by water. Outskirt Stand is a lonely desert gas station.

Southwest: Gateon Port. Ships such as the S.S. Libra link Orre to other regions. The Colosseum map reads landlocked. XD retcons a sea or ocean on the west. Citadark Isle is a volcanic Cipher HQ offshore. No known connecting routes. Wilds appear only at a handful of Poké Spots in XD (rock, oasis, cave).

## History

Before Colosseum, Snagem and Cipher ally. Cipher supplies Snag Machines. Snagem steals Pokémon. Cipher turns them Shadow. The plan is world rule through closed hearts.

**Colosseum:** Wes steals a Snag Machine from Snagem HQ. In Phenac he finds Rui, taken by Snagem. They snag Shadows and track Cipher. At Realgam Tower, Gonzap admits Cipher gave Snagem the machines and took the stolen Pokémon back. Wes beats Nascour, then Phenac's mayor Es Cade, who is Evice, Cipher's true boss. Evice flees. Ho-Oh's Sacred Fire stops him. Wes hunts leftover Cipher and remaining Shadows.

**Five years later:** Cipher builds Shadow Lugia, code XD001, billed as immune to purification. Helicopters and XD001 lift the S.S. Libra, then drop it in the desert. Admin Gorigan strips the cargo to Cipher Key Lair. One Pokémon is left behind.

**XD: Gale of Darkness:** Cipher hits Pokémon HQ Lab in the northwest and kidnaps Krane. Michael sees it, follows them to an abandoned lab and frees him. Krane builds a Snag Machine. Michael purifies the new Shadows, fights admins and even wins Snagem's help. Snagem wants Cipher gone so they can be Orre's top criminals again. On Citadark Isle, Grand Master Greevil and sons Ardos and Eldes send out XD001. Michael snags it and the rest of Greevil's Shadows. Greevil moves to blow the island. Ardos pushes it. Eldes asks him to take the loss. Greevil leaves with Eldes. Ardos leaves angry. Cipher is presumed broken. Michael keeps snagging strays and purifies XD001.

## Battle facilities

No Gyms. No Elite Four.

Mt. Battle `mt-battle`: 100-area gauntlet. Cipher holds it in early Colosseum, then it opens to all.

Realgam Tower `realgam-tower`: Colosseum on top. Cipher base in Colo, members-only entertainment in XD.

Also (no catalogue tags): Phenac Stadium, Pyrite Colosseum, Under Colosseum, Realgam Colosseum, Deep Colosseum, Orre Colosseum.

## Writer notes

Side series, not core. Imports and Shadows, not a local wild dex. Snagging is theft with a device, then purification. Ho-Oh appears in Orre at the Colo climax. Distant-land met data is how core games hide this map. Double Battle is the default grammar of a fight here.
