# Plants Without Berry Analogues

Derived from [Growth-and-Season.md](Growth-and-Season.md) Iberian plant lists vs [Berry-Plant-Replacements.md](Berry-Plant-Replacements.md).

These crops and landscape plants have **no clear Pokémon berry stand-in**. Keep them as real plants in ranch / Paldea writing, or invent story names. Soft-only berries (listed at the bottom) are not clean 1:1 swaps.

---

## D Ranch shortlist (keep as real plants)

Highest-value gaps for Porto Marinada / West Province farm writing:

- Olive
- Potato
- Onion
- Carrot
- Cabbage / lettuce greens
- Wheat or barley
- Almond (and/or walnut)
- Avocado (if warm-coast lean)
- Rosemary, thyme, lavender (scrub and kitchen herbs)
