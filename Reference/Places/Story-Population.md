# Story Population Scale

House numbers for writing. Official Town Map / settlement figures stay in [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md). Land area stays in [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md).

Official counts are a roster of named people on the playable map. They are not a census. This file gives story populations that match how places are described (ports, stadiums, skylines, academies) and still leave room for wild Pokémon on each landmass.

## Rules

- Keep official figures for **rank** (Mesagoza > Levincia > Cabo Poco).
- Use this file for **how many people actually live there**.
- Hamlets stay small. Capitals, ports, media cities and stadium towns expand.
- Region totals include unnamed villages, farms, suburbs and route folk not on the Town Map.
- **Hisui** stays near the official headcount. Sparse is the point.
- **Orre** stays a thin desert frontier.
- Orange Islands and Decolore Islands are tourist chains, not full regions.

Known-world humans in the modern League era (ex-Hisui): roughly **70–75M**. Enough for regional celebrities and stadiums. Not Earth-scale. Cross-region TV carries global fame.

## What to keep thin vs expand

**Keep thin:** Hisui. Orre desert towns. Start hamlets (Pallet, New Bark, Littleroot, Twinleaf, Nuvema, Postwick, Cabo Poco, Vaniville). Tiny shrine or ruin places (Celestic, Po Town, Tapu Village, Freezington, Pacifidlog).

**Expand hard:** Capitals and skyline cities (Saffron, Celadon, Goldenrod, Castelia, Nimbasa, Lumiose, Wyndon, Mesagoza, Jubilife, Mauville, Lilycove, Levincia, Hau'oli). Stadium and League spectacle towns. Major ports. Media and tourism hubs.

**Expand medium:** Ordinary Gym towns and mid ports.

## Quick multipliers (if you need a shortcut)

| Official band | Default story band |
|---|---|
| Under ~15 | 800–3,000 |
| ~15–30 | 5,000–15,000 |
| ~30–50 | 15,000–40,000 |
| ~50–80 | 40,000–100,000 |
| ~80–120 | 100,000–250,000 |
| ~120–200 | 250,000–600,000 |
| 200+ or clear megacity | 500,000–3,000,000+ |

Override the band when the place has a stadium, academy, department store, major port or national media. Override downward for Hisui, ruin towns and true hamlets.
