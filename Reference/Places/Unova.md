# Unova

Japanese: イッシュ地方 Issyu. Fifth core region. Far from [Kanto](Kanto.md), [Johto](Johto.md), [Hoenn](Hoenn.md) and [Sinnoh](Sinnoh.md). Setting of Black, White, Black 2 and White 2. [Blueberry Academy](Paldea.md) in The Indigo Disk sits off Unova's coast but is filed under Paldea in the source tables.

122 catalogued places. Master list: [Locations.md](Locations.md).

- **Real-world basis:** New York metropolitan area, with landmarks from the whole state. Castelia from Lower Manhattan. Skyarrow Bridge from Brooklyn Bridge. Nacrene from Williamsburg lofts. Battle Subway from the NYC subway. Nimbasa nods to Broadway.
- **Land area:** ~3,000 km²
- **Straight-line diameter:** ~55 km
- **Climate basis:** Temperate NYC
- **Weather system:** Region-wide seasons. Spring, summer, autumn and winter each last about 30 days and cycle three times a year. Weather, events and spawns shift with the season.
- **Notable weather:** Spring rain. Summer clear skies. Autumn sandstorm on Route 4 and Desert Resort. Winter snow and blizzards at Icirrus, Twist Mountain and Route 8. Permanent fog on eastern routes.
- **Official population:** 949 including all towns, cities and areas. Largest tabulated region at launch.
- **Largest named city:** Nimbasa City at 239. Castelia 130 (137 in B2W2).
- **Name:** Issyu from 一種, one variety: diverse up close, one kind of life from far off. Unova from a universal melting pot and a sound like United States. Only core region whose English name is not a romanization of the Japanese, and the only one whose name changes in every translation.
- **Adjective:** Unovan

See [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md), [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md) and [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md).

## Overview

Professor Juniper. Professor Burnet also appears. First partners: Snivy, Tepig or Oshawott. Villains: Team Plasma. The series also uses Team Rocket.

Unova League at the League building. Anime championship: Vertress City. Blueberry Academy runs an unofficial BB League.

Town English names (except Black City and White Forest) come from clouds. Japanese names come from traditional patterns. Black versions look more modern. White versions look more rural. That split hits Opelucid, Mistralton, Reversal Mountain and B2W2 Route 4.

21 settlements, most of any core region. Gyms sit only in cities, 11 Gym buildings across the two games. Tied with Galar for 14 Gym Leaders.

Series travel from Kanto needs a plane in, a ship back through the [Decolore Islands](Decolore-Islands.md).

## Geography

Two rivers split Unova into a western landmass, a central peninsula and an eastern peninsula. Rivers are not Surfable except under Village Bridge. Five bridges join the three strips: Skyarrow, Driftveil Drawbridge, Tubeline, Marvelous, Village Bridge. B2W2 adds the Marine Tube between Undella and Humilau.

**Center:** Castelia and Nimbasa, two of the world's largest cities, then Opelucid north. Every bridge touches the center.

**West:** Medium towns, more varied land, three Gyms.

**East:** Mountains split southeast (two Gyms, League path) from northeast (tourism, foreign languages, non-Unova species outside swarms). League sits farther still.

Northeastern Unova is the only place to find foreign species outside swarms. Black City (Black) vs White Forest (White). Dive spots exist. Desert sits immediately north of Castelia.

## History

Thousands of years ago two hero brothers unite the land with one dragon and become kings. They split over truth (elder) and ideals (younger). The dragon splits into Reshiram and Zekrom. Neither can win. Dragonspiral Tower, oldest building, and the DNA Splicers appear around then. Relic Castle (~2,500 years) and Abyssal Ruins (~3,000 years) rise as ancient cities. Relic items are made. Darmanitan sleep in Relic Castle.

Kyurem: Lacunosa says an icy meteor at Giant Chasm brought a man-eater, hence the town wall. Drayden says Kyurem is leftover dragon without truth or ideals, able to fuse through the Splicers.

The brothers' sons fight again. Fire and lightning wreck Unova. Reshiram and Zekrom become Light and Dark Stones.

Swords of Justice (Cobalion, Terrakion, Virizion) save Pokémon from a civil-war forest fire, later raise Keldeo, who leaves them. Landorus punishes Tornadus and Thundurus for tearing the land.

200 years ago: Liberty Garden named. Village Bridge built after a flood. 100 years: Nacrene warehouses. Klink appear in Chargestone Cave. 50 years: Elgyem from the desert. DNA Splicers pass to Drayden's line. Meloetta loses its melody and red shoes.

**Black/White:** Hilbert or Hilda, Cheren and Bianca start from Nuvema. Plasma and N. N takes Zekrom (Black) or Reshiram (White) at Dragonspiral, beats Alder, raises N's Castle around the League using overworked gifted Pokémon. The player revives the other dragon from Nacrene Museum's stone, beats N, then Ghetsis, whose real plan was to keep Plasma's Pokémon while stripping everyone else's. N leaves. The player clears east Unova, the remaining Sages and Alder.

**Between games:** Castelia-Nimbasa desert road finished (B2) or abandoned for ruins (W2). Join Avenue from the Nimbasa gate. PWT replaces Cold Storage. Entralink moat dries. Lentimas cut into a mountain. Undella rebuilt, Reversal Mountain opened, Marine Tube built. Victory Road collapses. Route 10 closes. Routes 22-23 open. Challenger's Cave sealed. Coastline shifts. Ghetsis reforms Plasma.

**Black 2/White 2:** Nate or Rosa starts in Aspertia with Bianca's gift, travels with Hugh. After Drayden's Badge, Plasma ices Opelucid and steals the Splicers. Giant Chasm: Ghetsis fuses Kyurem with N's dragon. The player splits them, beats Ghetsis, then Iris at the new League. Postgame: capture the Tao dragons and Kyurem, recover the Splicers.

**Indigo Disk:** Cyrano founds underwater Blueberry Academy as sister school to Paldea's Naranja/Uva. Kitakami trip sours Kieran. He becomes BB Champion. Briar installs the Terarium Core so the school can Terastallize. Florian or Juliana takes the BB League from Kieran. Later they reconcile in Area Zero's underdepths.

## Writer notes

First core region based outside Japan. No Pikachu or Magikarp lines in the regional Pokédex. Pokédex starts at Victini #000, then Snivy #001. No origin mark. UK pre-release name: Alleos. Caitlin is a Gen IV character first battled here, so Unova adds no new Psychic specialist.
