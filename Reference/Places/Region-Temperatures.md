# Region Temperatures

Typical air temperatures by climate zone and season. Basis is each region's real-world origin. Pokémon extremes are marked. Pair with [Region-Seasons.md](Region-Seasons.md) and [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md).

Values are daytime highs / nighttime lows unless noted. Round numbers for writing, not lab precision.

**Reading the tables:** Sp = spring, Su = summer, Au = autumn, Wi = winter. Format: `high / low` in °C (°F).

Cave, deep forest shade and night wind can sit 3–8°C colder than the open high. Pavement cities run a few degrees hotter in summer.

## Quick extremes cheat sheet

| Kind of place | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|
| Ice Gym / permanent snow town | At or below freezing most of the year |
| Alpine summit | ~6–10°C colder per rough 1000 m vs local plain |
| Active volcano vent | 40–100°C+ at the rock; back up for breathable air scenes |
| Ash belt | Plain season + cooler nights, dirty sky |
| Hot desert | Summer days 40°C+; nights crash hard |
| Deep forest / jungle | −2 to −5°C vs open sun; sticky |
| Big city summer | +1 to +3°C vs countryside |
| Sea cave ice | ~0°C stable |
| Primal / legendary weather | Follow the plot, not the table |
