# Paldea

Japanese: パルデア地方. Ninth core region. Setting of Scarlet and Violet. Large peninsula around the Great Crater of Paldea. [Kalos](Kalos.md) sits over the mountains. [Kitakami](#kitakami) and [Blueberry Academy](#blueberry-academy) are filed here in the source tables. Blueberry itself sits off [Unova](Unova.md)'s coast.

84 catalogued places. Master list: [Locations.md](Locations.md).

- **Real-world basis:** Iberian Peninsula: Spain, Portugal and Andorra. Culture, food, music and speech lean Spanish. West shows Portuguese architecture and language. Treasure hunts nod to the Age of Discovery. Area Zero's internal name points at Atlantis. Paradox Pokémon read as out-of-place artifacts.
- **Land area:** 598,202 km²
- **Straight-line diameter:** ~773 km
- **Climate basis:** Iberian. South wetlands and olive country. West desert and cliffs. East grassland, forest and badlands. North lakes, glaciers and Glaseado. Farm detail (D Ranch / West Province): [Paldea-Farm-Climate.md](Paldea-Farm-Climate.md).
- **Weather system:** Static regional gradient. No daily cycle.
- **Notable weather:** Asado Desert arid. Glaseado snow. South wetlands and mudflats. Area Zero has no weather.
- **Official population:** At least 1,523 across the twelve settlements. Excludes Trainers outside towns, Team Star bases and most walking NPCs (Los Platos and Mesagoza are the exceptions).
- **Largest named city:** Mesagoza at 756. Largest named city in these tables.
- **Name:** Paradise and Arcadia. Pangaea. Greek paideía (education) for the Academy. España, aldea (village), paradox, paella.
- **Adjective:** Paldean

See [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md), [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md) and [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md).

## Overview

Professor Sada (Scarlet) or Turo (Violet). First partners: Sprigatito, Fuecoco or Quaxly. Villains: Team Star, then the Paradise Protection Protocol. Horizons uses the Explorers.

Paldea League. Eight Gyms in any order. Champion Assessment at League HQ. Champion Rank, not a single Champion title. Geeta is Top Champion. No Victory Road. No battle facility, first time since Kanto. A Badge exists for every type once Gyms, Titans and Team Star are counted. Type experts cover every type.

Naranja Academy (Scarlet) or Uva Academy (Violet) in Mesagoza. The Treasure Hunt sends students across the map to find a personal treasure. Food is the other spine: picnics, sandwiches, restaurants, bars and street stalls. Settlement and landmark names are meals, tools and cooking terms.

Twelve cities and towns. No slogans. No numbered routes. Four provinces with three areas each, except South with six. The map reads as a turtle, specifically Terapagos: southwest rock as the head, the crater as the hex on the shell.

## Geography

Four Paldean seas. Beaches, sandbars and islets. Artazon sits mostly in the East Province with a slice in the South.

**South Province:** Player home, three other towns and Mesagoza. River basin splits toward the seas. Grass, ponds, beaches and a cave. Southwest olive orchard and a high pond with a leaning watchtower. Southeast rock, wetlands and a hidden beach. South sea nearly empty.

**West Province:** Hills, cliffs, rivers, Cascarrafa, Medali and Porto Marinada. Asado Desert under a pillar-held rock roof. West sea has outcrops and islets. Portuguese lean.

**East Province:** Plains, mining rock and Tagtree Thicket. Levincia skyline. East sea nearly empty aside from a sandbar and two islands.

**North Province:** Montenevera on snow. Glaseado is the tallest peak, with a hand-shaped cliff. Dalizapa Passage cuts east-west through caves. West: Casseroya Lake and Socarrat Trail. East: bamboo, waterfalls, a pond with three watchtowers. North sea: rock pillars, sandbars, islets.

**Great Crater of Paldea:** Center. Cloud cap. Mountain ring. Area Zero inside. Area Zero Underdepths below.

## History

Two million years back, seismic shifts form the Underdepths and drive Terapagos nearly extinct. One survivor remains. Unknown underground symbols and a steel plate sit at the bottom. One million years back, the crater forms.

Two thousand years back, the Paldean Empire rules. The emperor sends expeditions into the crater for rumored treasure. A thousand years back, failed trips drain the empire. Forts and watchtowers watch neighboring lands in wartime. An eastern merchant sells the king four treasures: a vessel, a broken sword, wooden tablets and beads. They are Pokémon. Greed wakes them. They nearly wreck the kingdom. Strong Trainers seal them in four shrines.

805 years back, the Academy opens in Mesagoza. 800 years back, the empire unifies with its neighbors as modern Paldea.

200 years back, the Area Zero Expedition claims the crater floor. Heath publishes the Scarlet Book or Violet Book and is called a liar. The team plants Herba Mystica at five sites. Local Pokémon eat it and become Titans. 140 years back, Terastallized Pokémon are first seen in Area Zero. 87 years back, a research facility is built there. Ten years back, Sada or Turo ties crystal energy to Terastal and shares the Tera Orb with the League and the Academy.

Eighteen months back, Nemona becomes the youngest Champion Rank. Team Star confronts bullies. The clash scandals the school. Cassiopeia takes the blame. Director Harrington and the staff resign. Cassiopeia leaves for home. The rest of Team Star wait and skip class. Clavell becomes director. Jacq teaches biology. Tyme leaves the Montenevera Rock Gym for math. Ryme takes the Ghost Gym.

One year back, the Professor and an AI copy finish a time machine to stock Area Zero with past (Scarlet) or future (Violet) Pokémon. Two Koraidon or Miraidon fight. The Professor dies. The AI sees Paradox Pokémon as a threat and hunts Academy help, including Arven, to shut the machine down.

**Scarlet and Violet:** Florian or Juliana moves to Cabo Poco. Clavell offers a first partner. Neighbor Nemona, already Champion Rank, takes another. On Poco Path a weak Koraidon or Miraidon saves them from Houndour. Arven names it and hands over the Ball. Nemona introduces Terastal and a Tera Orb. In Mesagoza the player saves Penny from Team Star. The Professor asks them to keep the ride legendary.

Three days later Clavell starts the Treasure Hunt. Three plots run in any order.

**Victory Road:** Eight Gyms, then League HQ. Interview, Elite Four, Geeta. Nemona's full-power rematch ends the plot.

**Path of Legends:** Five Titans, five Herba Mystica. Sandwiches restore the ride legendary's field moves. Arven wants the herbs for Mabosstiff. After the fifth Titan, Mabosstiff heals. The Professor calls them to the crater. Arven's full battle tests them.

**Starfall Street:** Cassiopeia hacks the Rotom Phone. Operation Starfall. Clavell joins as student Clive. Five squad bases fall. Team Star were bullying victims. Their big boss rallied them. Bullies fled and dropped out. A deputy wiped the records, so Star looked like the bullies. Staff resigned. Once all five squads fall, Cassiopeia is Penny. Clavell tests the player first. After Penny loses, the bosses may keep the bases as training grounds.

**The Way Home:** After all three, Florian or Juliana, Arven, Nemona and Penny enter Area Zero. Four research stations unlock the lab. Paradox Pokémon from the time machine. The Professor is the AI. The original died to the aggressive legendary. The AI wants the machine off but is coded to guard it. After that battle the Paradise Protection Protocol seizes the robot and locks Poké Balls without the Professor's ID. The ride legendary fights again and wins. The AI is wired into the machine, so it leaves through time. The machine dies. The group walks back to Mesagoza.

**The Teal Mask:** Annual trip with Blueberry Academy to Kitakami.

**The Indigo Disk:** After the BB League, Briar, Carmine and Kieran enter Area Zero with League leave. Geeta and Rika give the Indigo Disk. It drops the Zero Lab elevator into the Underdepths. Stellar Tera Type lives there. Terapagos wakes. Kieran Master Balls it, loses a battle, Terastallizes it and loses control. The Ball breaks. The player and Kieran beat it. The player may catch it. Briar writes a book. At Kitakami's Crystal Pool, Terapagos and mist bring the original Professor across time. They trade Briar's book for their Scarlet or Violet Book and vanish.

**Mochi Mayhem:** Arven calls from Kitakami. Nemona and Penny tag along. A letter from Kieran in Mossui Town. The four visit.

## Writer notes

Open map. Gyms, Titans and Star bases are three parallel circuits, then Area Zero. Companion plots (Nemona, Arven, Penny) matter as much as the League. Tera is local crystal energy, not a weather gimmick. Area Zero has no overworld weather. Place names are food. Watchtowers and forts are empire leftovers. Kalos is the mountain neighbor, not a sea crossing.
