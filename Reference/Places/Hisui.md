# Hisui

Japanese: ヒスイ地方. [Sinnoh](Sinnoh.md) during discovery and settlement, before Generation IV. Setting of Legends: Arceus. At most 300 years before Diamond and Pearl. Later renamed Sinnoh. Kamado even muses that the land could take the clans' deity name.

89 catalogued places. Master list: [Locations.md](Locations.md).

- **Real-world basis:** Hokkaido as Ezo, before Meiji annexation. Ainu lands. Jubilife Village from early Sapporo and the canal plain. Galaxy Hall from the Former Hokkaidō Government Office. Temple of Sinnoh and Celestica Ruins read Greek/Roman.
- **Land area:** 93,900 km²
- **Straight-line diameter:** ~306 km
- **Climate basis:** Ancient Hokkaido. Colder north. People dress in bundled clothes.
- **Weather system:** Dynamic per map. Changes on its own. Affects behavior and spawns. Space-time distortions add lightning and odd weather. Daybreak mass outbreaks: rain in four areas, blizzard in the Icelands.
- **Notable weather:** Fieldlands and Highlands mostly clear. Mirelands damp and foggy. Coastlands frequent rain. Icelands wind and snow. Distortions anywhere once the rift sits over Coronet.
- **Official population:** 111 people across Galaxy Team, Diamond Clan and Pearl Clan. Smallest tabulated region. Settlers from Hoenn, Alola, Galar and elsewhere.
- **Name:** Jade (翡翠) and hidden-profound (秘邃), parallel to Sinnoh's 神奥.
- **Adjective:** Hisuian

Same scale as [Sinnoh](Sinnoh.md). See [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md), [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md) and [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md).

## Overview

Professor Laventon. First partners: Rowlet, Cyndaquil or Oshawott. Villains: Miss Fortune sisters, then Volo and Giratina. No Gyms. No League. Survey Corps builds the first Hisui Pokédex. Crafting, Ride Pokémon and Noble balms replace modern Trainer infrastructure.

One village, two clan settlements, Cogita's Ancient Retreat, plus base camps that some talk of turning into towns. Pokémon outnumber people and many are hostile. Alphas and frenzied Nobles define the era.

Four area names track the Chinese Four Symbols by color: Obsidian Fieldlands (Black Tortoise), Crimson Mirelands (Vermilion Bird), Cobalt Coastlands (Azure Dragon), Alabaster Icelands (White Tiger). Coronet Highlands is the center. Only two sit on the matching cardinal: Mirelands south/southeast, Coastlands east. After a request, the Forces of Nature roam those four on set weather.

## Geography

Mt. Coronet still splits east and west. Shellos/Gastrodon and Burmy cloaks follow that split. Five sub-regions sit on top of that divide. Terrain is harsher than modern Sinnoh. Little construction.

**Obsidian Fieldlands (southwest):** Least hostile. Plains, flowers, forest, beaches. Becomes Sandgem and Route 201. Two rivers to Sandgem Flats and Ramanas Island. Heartwood in the southeast hills. Lake Verity in the southwest. Ride Wyrdeer.

**Crimson Mirelands (southeast):** Swamp, red mud, peat, fog, hills. Becomes Pastoria, the Great Marsh and Solaceon. Solaceon Ruins. Diamond Settlement and Lake Valor on the north edge.

**Cobalt Coastlands (east):** Rocky crescent beaches. Becomes Sunyshore and Valor Lakefront. Ride Basculegion. Sand's Reach is a hand-shaped spit. Cargo wrecks. Veilstone Cape. Spring Path, fourth lake, Turnback Cave. Firespit Island volcano. Heatran's origin.

**Coronet Highlands (center):** Coronet range. Magnetic fields (Nosepass). Ride Sneasler. Fabled Spring Clefairy. Wayward Cave. Celestica Ruins. Snowy summit holds the Temple of Sinnoh. Three camps.

**Alabaster Icelands (north):** Snow, ice that Sneasler cannot climb. Becomes Routes 216-217 and Snowpoint. Barren with trees, a river, hot springs. Pearl Settlement under Snowpoint Temple cliffs. Lake Acuity above.

## History

Myths match [Sinnoh](Sinnoh.md): Arceus, lake guardians, Heatran, Regigigas, the plate hero, Nobles and Rides, Temple of Sinnoh. Ancient Sinnoh people (from Sinjoh) vanish. Celestica (People of Kaminagi) appear in Old Verse. Diamond Clan (time) and Pearl Clan (space) cross the sea, feud and take warden posts. Neither clan is Celestica.

Two years before the game, Galaxy Expedition Team founds Jubilife Village. They distrust Pokémon and bring new Poké Balls. Volo, ancient-Sinnoh descendant, partners with Giratina to drag Arceus out. A rift over Coronet maddens Dialga and Palkia, frenzies Nobles, spawns Alphas and distortions. Lord Arcanine dies at sea saving a pup. Palina raises the pup. Ingo arrives from the future with almost no memory.

**Legends: Arceus:** Arceus sends Rei or Akari from the future. Laventon finds them on Prelude Beach. Cyllene takes them into Survey Corps. Lightning from the rift has struck four Nobles. The player quells all five, teaches Jubilife companionship and eases clan tensions. When the sky turns red, Kamado exiles them. Volo leads them to Cogita. Lake guardians yield a Red Chain. Adaman and Irida help. At the Temple they earn Dialga or Palkia, then the Origin Forme counterpart wrecks the chain and the temple. An Origin Ball of chain shards and Origin Ore catches it.

Volo then hunts remaining Plates, summons Giratina at the ruined temple (spears in the ground, later Spear Pillar) and admits the rift. After defeat he still seeks Arceus. The Pokédex is finished. Azure Flute, Hall of Origin, Arceus gifts a piece of itself. Postgame: massive mass outbreaks in storms. Galaxy Team later disbands. Cyllene's name survives. Ingo leaves: "It is time for me to return." The land becomes Sinnoh.

## Ecology

Many Hisui species are gone or rare by DP: Wyrdeer extinct (Violet says Stantler used to evolve), Overqwil migrated to colder seas, White-Striped Basculin and Basculegion only in Kitakami's Timeless Woods, Bloodmoon Ursaluna a single old beast that drifted to Kitakami. Hisuian forms and evolutions track cold and season (Hisuian Decidueye). Outbreaks swarm species.

Unique plants: Brown Apricorns, Sand Radishes (Icelands), Sootfoot Roots, Pop Pods (Coastlands), Medicinal Leeks everywhere. Crafting: balls, potions, Noble balms (Crunchy Salt for Electrode on peaks). Petilil leaves and Croagunk toxin for medicine.

## Writer notes

Ride Pokémon already exist here. Laventon compares them to Alola's. No Gym circuit. Companion and survey replace League ambition. Distortions can drop people (Ingo) as well as items and Pokémon. Same land as [Sinnoh](Sinnoh.md), earlier name and far fewer people.
