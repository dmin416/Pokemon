# Alola

Japanese: アローラ地方. Seventh core region. Four natural islands plus Aether Paradise. Setting of Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon. Humans and Pokémon live close. Culture differs from the older regions. Popular tourist resort.

101 catalogued places. Master list: [Locations.md](Locations.md).

- **Real-world basis:** Hawaii. Melemele from Oʻahu. Akala from Maui. Ula'ula from Hawaiʻi (Big Island). Poni from Kauaʻi. Each island is named for a Hawaiian color that matches its real counterpart, nectar, flowers and tapu.
- **Land area:** 16,635 km²
- **Straight-line diameter:** ~129 km
- **Climate basis:** Tropical Hawaii. Mount Lanakila snows from altitude. Ula'ula holds sun, sandstorm and rain besides that snow. Exeggutor Island often rains, then rainbows by day or meteors by night.
- **Weather system:** Static per island. No season cycle.
- **Notable weather:** Lanakila hail. Haina Desert on Ula'ula. Lush Jungle rainforest on Akala.
- **Official population:** 1,005 in Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon including towns, cities and other areas.
- **Largest named city:** Hau'oli City at 97 (USUM).
- **Name:** Life overflowing (Hawaiian ola, aloha greeting). Used as hello and goodbye in-region. Palindrome.
- **Adjective:** Alolan

See [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md), [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md) and [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md).

## Overview

Professor Kukui. Also Burnet and Samson Oak. First partners: Rowlet, Litten or Popplio, offered by Kahuna Hala (SM) or by Kukui for Hala (USUM). Villains: Team Skull, Aether Foundation, Necrozma, Team Rainbow Rocket (USUM). The series also uses Team Rocket.

No Gyms or Badges. Island challenge: captains, Totem Pokémon, then kahuna grand trials. League is new on Mount Lanakila. No prior Champion. SM finale vs Kukui. USUM finale vs Hau. Anime championship: Manalo Stadium.

Player house sits on Route 1, not in a town. Unique among core regions. 17 numbered routes plus many named paths. Travel between islands by boat, Charizard Glide or Mantine Surf. Lapras, Sharpedo and field moves cannot cross open ocean.

Sun/Moon and Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon are parallel universes. Moon versions run 12 hours off the Sun versions.

## Geography

**Melemele (northwest, yellow, Tapu Koko):** Mildest island. Hau'oli, Alola's largest city. Routes slope to Ruins of Conflict. Ten Carat Hill is an old underwater volcano with an open hollow.

**Akala (northeast, pink, Tapu Lele):** Most settlements. Only active volcano (Wela). Heahea resorts, Hano Grand Resort, Brooklet Hill terraces, Lush Jungle.

**Ula'ula (southeast, red, Tapu Bulu):** Largest island. Few towns. Malie, ruined Tapu Village, Skull-held Po Town. Central mountains, three peaks, Haina Desert. Southwest lagoon. Circular Lake of the Moone and Lake of the Sunne. Lanakila and the League.

**Poni (southwest, purple, Tapu Fini):** Sparse. Seafolk Village on ships. Ancient Poni Path ruins. Vast Poni Canyon takes over half the island. Exeggutor Island off Seafolk.

**Aether Paradise:** Artificial VLFS west of center. Modern HQ and Lusamine's mansion.

**Poké Pelago:** Uninhabited isles for Trainer Pokémon. Mohn oversees them. Poké Beans grow here.

## History

Ultra Megalopolis wounds Necrozma. Prism shards fall as Sparkling Stones, Z-Crystals, Z-Rings (SM) or Z-Power Rings (USUM). Solgaleo (S/US) or Lunala (M/UM) comes through an Ultra Wormhole, beats the tapu, gifts Tapunium Z, then leaves a Cosmog for them. Cycle repeats.

Necrozma later steals Alola's light, fuses with Solgaleo (US) or Lunala (UM). Royalty, the four tapu and a youth with a Sparkling Stone banish it. Island challenge tradition starts. Necrozma books are burned.

Ride Pokémon are centuries old. Battle Royal copies tapu fights. Thrifty Megamart on Tapu Bulu's ground is wrecked. A kahuna-centered group angers all four tapu. Survivors become Team Skull. Magearna is built 500 years ago for a king's daughter.

Professor Mohn finds Ultra Wormholes, Cosmog and Nihilego, then vanishes. He later works Pelago with no memory. Lusamine hardens. Aether's beast-killer project makes Type: Full. Three units reject RKS and rage. Faba masks them, freezes them, renames them Type: Null. Gladion steals one two years before the games. Lillie steals Cosmog three months before, lands on a beach, lives with Burnet and Kukui. After Kalos's ultimate weapon, Zygarde Cores and Cells reach Alola, soak Z-Power and seek aid (Dexio, Sina, the player).

**Sun/Moon:** Elio or Selene meets Kukui, Hala, Hau, Lillie and Nebby. Tapu Koko gifts a Sparkling Stone. Seven trials, four grand trials. Skull misfits plus Aether. Nihilego at Paradise. After Guzma, they raid Paradise, Lusamine opens wormholes and leaves with Guzma. Nebby becomes Solgaleo (S) or Lunala (M). Ultra Space rescue. Player is first Champion vs Kukui. Lillie sails to Kanto with sick Lusamine.

Looker Episode: Ultra Beasts loose after the wormholes. Catch them with Anabel. Necrozma at Ten Carat Hill's farthest hollow is not classed as a UB.

**Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon:** Eight trials (Mina added). Ultra Recon Squad. Lusamine opens one wormhole to stop Necrozma, is thrown back beaten. Necrozma takes Nebby as Dusk Mane (US) or Dawn Wings (UM), steals Alola's light, summons UBs. Player rides the other legendary to Ultra Megalopolis, beats Ultra Necrozma, restores light. Champion vs Hau. Then Blacephalon (US) or Stakataka (UM).

Episode RR: Rainbow Rocket seizes Festival Plaza and Paradise (Team Rocket's Castle). Archie, Maxie, Cyrus, Lysandre and Ghetsis from worlds where they won. Giovanni falls and leaves, hinting at more. Gladion returns a month later. Family meets Mohn.

## Alola League

Elite Four SM: Hala (Fighting), Olivia (Rock), Acerola (Ghost), Kahili (Flying). Then Kukui.

Elite Four USUM: Molayne (Steel) replaces Hala. Then Hau.

Only core region whose Elite Four roster changes inside one generation. No native Ice-type specialist. Sina (Kalos) fills that role when she appears.

## Writer notes

No HMs, no cut plants, no roaming legendaries, no Pokédex diploma from an NPC. Least visited core region in Journeys (three episodes). Ride Pokémon already exist in Hisui's era per Laventon. Alolan forms of Kanto species (Vulpix Ice, tall Exeggutor). Invasive Alolan Rattata and Gumshoos. Ultra Wormholes dump Z-Power and Ultra Beasts. Oricorio nectar matches island color. Evolution spots: Vast Poni Canyon, Blush Mountain, Lush Jungle, Mount Lanakila. Series [Orange Islands](Orange-Islands.md) already used a tropical chain, four island trials and a League Ash won.
