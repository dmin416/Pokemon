# Pokémon Region Population

Official town and city population figures. Named places are in [Locations.md](Locations.md). Scale is in [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md). Weather is in [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md). Story-scale populations for writing (stadiums, capitals, land density) are in [Story-Population.md](Story-Population.md).

Real official numbers exist here, unlike the land area and weather files. Generations I through IV give each town and city a stated population in the Town Map, PokéGear map or PokéNav flavor text. Unova through Alola keep a version of that stat. Galar and Paldea still publish per-settlement headcounts. These UI figures are a roster of named people, not a census.

Kanto's Town Map towns sum to 358 before the Cinnabar eruption and 331 after. Broader in-game headcounts are 391 in Red/Blue/Yellow/FireRed/LeafGreen and 364 in Gold/Silver/Crystal/HeartGold/SoulSilver. Celadon is the largest named Kanto city at 68. No Kanto city reaches 100. Mauville jumps to 148 briefly in ORAS and Goldenrod hits 126.

Galar named settlements sum to 715. The full in-game headcount is 727. Wyndon is 157, Motostoke 120, Hammerlocke 102 and Circhester 97.

Unova's full headcount is 949. Nimbasa City is 239. Castelia is 130 in Black/White and 137 in Black 2/White 2.

Kalos's full headcount is 1,288. Named settlements sum to 920. Lumiose City is 448.

Alola's USUM headcount is 1,005. Hau'oli City is 97, the largest named city in the archipelago.

Paldea's twelve settlements sum to at least 1,523. That figure skips Trainers outside towns, Team Star bases and most walking NPCs. Mesagoza is 756, the largest named city in these tables. Levincia 174 and Cascarrafa 156 follow.

Hisui is the other extreme. 111 people split among Galaxy Team, Diamond Clan and Pearl Clan, the smallest tabulated population.

[Orre](Orre.md) is close. Colosseum towns and other areas sum to 113: Phenac 34, Pyrite 32, Agate 19 and The Under 28. Gateon Port is 47 in XD and is not in that 113. XD never published a full headcount.

For writing with stadiums, capitals and land-area density, use [Story-Population.md](Story-Population.md). That file treats these official counts as rank only.
