# Iberian Land Values (Ranch Scale)

Real-world land prices for Paldea / D Ranch writing. Sources: Spain Ministry of Agriculture 2024 averages; Eurostat 2024 Portugal arable. No single number covers the peninsula.

Farm weather: [Paldea-Farm-Climate.md](Paldea-Farm-Climate.md). Region scale: [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md).

**1 hectare (ha)** = 10,000 m² ≈ 2.47 acres.

---

## Useful writing anchors

| Use case | Rough price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Spain national agricultural average (2024) | ~€10,250 / ha | Up ~3% from 2023; rising since 2020 |
| Dry interior cereal / mixed farm (Castilla y León, Aragón) | ~€5,100 to €5,200 / ha | Closest regional band to Bierzo (León) climate anchor |
| Andalucía agricultural average | ~€17,000 / ha | Higher southern demand |
| Cataluña agricultural average | ~€13,000 / ha | |
| National irrigated average (Spain) | ~€30,000 / ha | Water rights and irrigation drive the jump |
| Specialty irrigated / greenhouse vegetables | €170,000+ / ha | Extreme high end |
| Balearic Islands agricultural | ~€21,400 / ha | Island premium |
| Canarias banana land | ~€148,000 / ha | Outlier; not a mainland ranch baseline |
| Portugal arable average (Eurostat 2024) | ~€76,600 / ha | Skewed by irrigated and permanent crops; not a flat rural baseline |

Urban and coastal plots sit far above these. Prime Madrid, Barcelona, or Lisbon land is priced per m² (thousands of euros), orders of magnitude above rural per-hectare rates.

## Ranch math (quick)

Using dry-interior ~€5,200 / ha or Spain average ~€10,250 / ha:

| Parcel | At €5,200 / ha | At €10,250 / ha |
|---|---|---|
| 1 ha | €5,200 | €10,250 |
| 5 ha | €26,000 | €51,250 |
| 10 ha | €52,000 | €102,500 |
| 20 ha | €104,000 | €205,000 |
| 50 ha | €260,000 | €512,500 |

Irrigated land: multiply dry figures by roughly 3× at the national irrigated average (~€30,000 / ha), more for greenhouse specialty.

League-coin conversion is story-side; keep these as real-world order-of-magnitude only.

## What to ignore for ranch scenes

- National “total agricultural land worth hundreds of billions” aggregates: useful for macro scale, not plot prices.
- Canarias / Balears / greenhouse peaks as if they were typical Porto Marinada hinterland.
- Portugal’s EU-high arable average as if every Portuguese hectare cost that: the mix is what inflates it.
