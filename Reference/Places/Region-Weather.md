# Pokémon Region Weather

Climate and in-game weather. Named places are in [Locations.md](Locations.md). Scale is in [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md). Population is in [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md). Origin-based seasons are in [Region-Seasons.md](Region-Seasons.md). Zone temperatures (°C/°F by season) are in [Region-Temperatures.md](Region-Temperatures.md).

| Region | Real-world climate basis | In-game weather system | Notable weather locations/events |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Kanto](Kanto.md) | Temperate Japan | No overworld weather system, static per location | Cinnabar volcanic, Seafoam Islands icy caves |
| [Johto](Johto.md) | Temperate Japan, wetter than Kanto | No overworld weather in Gen II. Gen IV adds conditions such as constant rain on Route 33 | Whirl Islands storms, Mt. Silver blizzards |
| [Hoenn](Hoenn.md) | Subtropical Kyushu, nearer the equator | Route weather (rain, storms, ash, sun). Groudon/Kyogre can force region-wide sun or rain. Emerald mixes both until Rayquaza | Route 119/120 rain, Mt. Chimney ash on Fallarbor, desert sandstorms, ORAS primal sun or rain, Emerald mixed weather over Sootopolis |
| [Sinnoh](Sinnoh.md) | Cold Hokkaido | Latitude-based gradient, no daily cycle | Snowpoint permanent snow, Mt. Coronet and Route 217 blizzards, Route 210 fog, Route 228 sandstorm, light snow reaching Twinleaf and Route 201 |
| [Unova](Unova.md) | Temperate NYC | Full four-season calendar cycling real months, each season shifting weather patterns | Spring rain, summer clear skies, autumn sandstorm on Route 4/Desert Resort, winter snow and blizzards at Icirrus City/Twist Mountain/Route 8, permanent fog on eastern routes |
| [Kalos](Kalos.md) | Temperate France | No dynamic overworld cycle. Climate is local | Snowbelle frozen by the Ice Gym, Frost Cavern snow and fog, northeast mountains cold, haunted marsh in Mountain Kalos |
| [Alola](Alola.md) | Tropical Hawaii | Static per island, no season cycle | Mount Lanakila snow and hail, Haina Desert on Ula'ula, Ula'ula sun/sandstorm/rain, Exeggutor Island showers then rainbows or meteors, Lush Jungle rainforest |
| [Galar](Galar.md) | Temperate/industrial Britain | Wild Area has a rotating daily weather cycle (fog, sandstorm, snow, rain, thunderstorm, intense sun) tied to Max Raid spawns | Motostoke industrial haze, Hulbury coastal rain, Circhester permanent alpine snow, Route 6 constant rain |
| Isle of Armor | Isle of Man | Static, temperate | No major weather events |
| Crown Tundra | Scottish Highlands | Static, permanent snow | Freezington and Giant's Bed under constant snowfall |
| [Paldea](Paldea.md) | Iberian Peninsula | Static regional gradient, no dynamic cycle | South wetlands and olive country, Asado Desert arid, Glaseado snow and glaciers, Casseroya waterfalls, Area Zero has no weather |
| [Hisui](Hisui.md) | Ancient Hokkaido | Dynamic per-map weather. Distortions add lightning. Daybreak mass outbreaks in rain or Icelands blizzard | Fieldlands/Highlands often clear, Mirelands fog and damp, Coastlands rain, Icelands wind and snow |
| [Orre](Orre.md) | Phoenix, Arizona | Static per location. No overworld cycle | Desert wasteland, Phenac oasis, Agate green mountains, Mt. Battle dormant volcano, Citadark Isle magma |

Two anime-only weather events sit outside this table. *The Power of One* has Lawrence III stealing the elemental orbs of Zapdos, Articuno and Moltres, which throws global weather into chaos until Ash restores balance at the Shamouti shrine in the [Orange Islands](Orange-Islands.md). The Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald Groudon-Kyogre clash in the games has an anime-adjacent equivalent where the primal battle threatens Hoenn's climate directly before Rayquaza intervenes.
