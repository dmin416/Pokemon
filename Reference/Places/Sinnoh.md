# Sinnoh

Japanese: シンオウ地方. Formerly [Hisui](Hisui.md). Fourth core region. North of [Kanto](Kanto.md), [Johto](Johto.md) and [Hoenn](Hoenn.md). Setting of Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl. Legends: Arceus covers the Hisui era.

128 catalogued places. Master list: [Locations.md](Locations.md).

- **Real-world basis:** Hokkaido without the Oshima Peninsula. Battle Zone from southern Sakhalin (Karafuto). League island from Kunashir in the Kurils. Jubilife from Sapporo. Canalave from Otaru. Veilstone from Abashiri. Floaroma from Furano/Biei flower fields. Pastoria marsh from Kushiro-shitsugen.
- **Land area:** 93,900 km²
- **Straight-line diameter:** ~306 km
- **Climate basis:** Cold Hokkaido. Cool summers, icy winters. South has started showing light snow (Twinleaf, Route 201).
- **Weather system:** Latitude gradient. Snow in the north. Fog on Route 210. Sandstorm on Route 228. No daily cycle.
- **Notable weather:** Snowpoint under permanent snow. Mt. Coronet and Route 217 blizzards. Stark Mountain ash. Battle Zone warmer, with jungle in the south.
- **Official population:** 704 in Diamond/Pearl (towns, cities and other areas). 755 in Platinum.
- **Largest named city:** Hearthome 91 in DP. Jubilife 124 in Platinum.
- **Name:** Mysterious and profound (神奥). Also 深奥 in old verse. "Almighty Sinnoh" is Arceus in clan speech.
- **Adjective:** Sinnohan

Same scale as [Hisui](Hisui.md). See [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md), [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md) and [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md).

## Overview

Professor Rowan in Sandgem. First partners: Turtwig, Chimchar or Piplup. Villains: Team Galactic. The series also uses J and Team Rocket. League building on its own island. Anime championship: Lily of the Valley Island.

Theme: the ultimate. Time and space. Myth. Cities lean Western, a nod to Hokkaido's Meiji colonization. Spear Pillar reads Greek/Roman. Shellos and Gastrodon split pink west / blue east around Mt. Coronet.

Fourteen cities and towns, three Battle Zone "areas," plus the League: eighteen settlements. Thirty routes numbered 201-230. Few sea routes. Some paths turn (210, 212). Kanto travel in the series needs a ship or a truck.

## Geography

Mt. Coronet splits the mainland like Kanto/Johto share one landmass.

**West:** Rockier coasts, more fertile land, more rural towns. Valley Windworks in a canyon. Gyms in Oreburgh, Eterna, Canalave. Jubilife is the tech hub.

**East:** Contests in Hearthome, Veilstone department store, Pastoria's Great Marsh, Sunyshore's Vista Lighthouse. Four Gyms.

**North:** Sparse. Snowpoint only. Ice Gym. Snowpoint Temple.

**Battle Zone:** Large island northeast. Locals sometimes call "Sinnoh" a separate place, like the Sevii Islands vs Kanto. Fight, Survival and Resort Areas. Stark Mountain.

**League island:** Reached by Route 223. Victory Road has three mouths: mainland, Elite Four, and a foggy exit to Route 224 (a miniature Sinnoh). Seabreak Path then Flower Paradise for Shaymin.

**Offshore west:** Newmoon Island, Fullmoon Island, Iron Island.

Lakes Verity (west), Valor (east) and Acuity (north) form a triangle around Coronet. Sendoff Spring is a hidden fourth lake with Turnback Cave.

## Energy

Oreburgh Mine: coal for Jubilife and beyond, automated, vents for steam and air. Valley Windworks: eight turbines off Route 205, power for Floaroma and Eterna. Sunyshore: solar walkways, cheap and clean, often broken, blackouts common.

## History

Full Hisui map and plot: [Hisui](Hisui.md). Short spine for Sinnoh writing:

Arceus makes Coronet, then Hisui, then the world. Lake guardians take Verity (Uxie), Valor (Azelf) and Acuity (Mesprit). Heatran is born at Firespit. Regigigas sleeps in Snowpoint Temple. An ancient hero gathers 18 Plates, meets almighty Sinnoh, and his ten partners become Nobles and Rides. The Temple of Sinnoh is raised on Coronet. Ancient Sinnoh people (from Sinjoh) vanish. Diamond and Pearl Clans cross the sea, feud over whether almighty Sinnoh made time or space, and serve as wardens. Galaxy Team founds Jubilife Village with new Poké Balls. Volo and Giratina open a rift, frenzy the Nobles, spawn Alphas and distortions. At most 300 years before DP, Legends: Arceus happens. Hisui is later called Sinnoh. Galaxy Team dissolves. Cyllene's name survives through descendants. Floaroma was barren until thanks for a blessing made the hill bloom.

**Diamond/Pearl/BDSP:** Lucas or Dawn and Barry find Rowan's case at Lake Verity, take first partners, then eight Gyms. Galactic builds a Red Chain from the lake trio. Cyrus calls Dialga (D/BD) or Palkia (P/SP) at Spear Pillar. The player stops him. Cynthia falls at the League. Postgame: Battle Zone, Stark Mountain, Magma Stone.

**Platinum:** Same Gyms, Fantina third. Looker and Cynthia help. Cyrus calls both Dialga and Palkia. The lake trio and Giratina drag him into the Distortion World. The player and Cynthia follow and stop him. Postgame: Battle Frontier on the Battle Zone. Charon joins Mars and Jupiter at Stark Mountain.

## Writer notes

Only core region that once had another name. First snow routes. First side-facing cave mouths. Most magnetic-field sites (three). Connecting the three lakes draws a triangle with Coronet in the middle, matching the Spear Pillar formation. Fiore is "far away" in the Manaphy event. Series Sinnoh shows the most Shinies of any region (seven).
