# Kanto

Japanese: カントー地方. East of [Johto](Johto.md). The two regions share one landmass south of [Sinnoh](Sinnoh.md). First main region. Playable in Generations I, II, III, IV and VII, plus a spinoff setting in IX.

96 catalogued places. Master list: [Locations.md](Locations.md).

- **Real-world basis:** Kantō, Japan
- **Land area:** 32,424 km²
- **Straight-line diameter:** ~180 km
- **Climate basis:** Temperate Japan
- **Weather system:** No overworld weather system, static per location
- **Notable weather:** Cinnabar volcanic, Seafoam Islands icy caves
- **Official population:** 358 Town Map before the Cinnabar eruption, 331 after. 391 in RBY/FRLG. 364 in GSC/HGSS.
- **Population basis:** Pallet 10, Viridian 34, Pewter 30, Cerulean 33, Vermilion 34, Lavender 30, Celadon 68, Fuchsia 36, Saffron 47, Cinnabar 36 / 9
- **Largest named city:** Celadon City at 68. No Kanto city reaches 100.

See [Region-Sizes.md](Region-Sizes.md), [Region-Weather.md](Region-Weather.md) and [Region-Population.md](Region-Population.md).

## Overview

Professor Oak is the resident professor. Trainers usually pick Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle in Pallet Town. Yellow gives Pikachu after Blue takes the intended Eevee. Let's Go lets the player start with Pikachu or Eevee. The journey ends at the Indigo Plateau.

Team Rocket is the regional villain group. Pokémon Horizons also uses the Explorers. Professor Cerise appears in Pokémon the Series at a Vermilion lab.

The Indigo League is shared with Johto. League site: Indigo Plateau. Adjective: Kantonian.

Cities are named after colors. Pallet Town is the exception: a palette of colors. Kanto is the only core region whose fictional name matches its real-world namesake.

Kanto reads as modern and technological next to Johto's traditional Kansai tone. Celadon and Saffron stand in for Tokyo. Vermilion stands in for Yokohama. Early art shows grassland. From Generation III the map shows denser trees. Kanto is the only main region with no native Pokémon myths.

English Generation I never uses the name Kanto. Japanese Red/Green uses it once, on the Town Map in Blue's house. FireRed, LeafGreen and Let's Go use the name freely. Gold and Silver introduced it to English.

## Geography

Temperate. Mostly forests and plains. Major cities sit near the center with access to sea, mountains and woods. A large bay opens southwest to the sea. Mountains concentrate in the north and west. Indigo Plateau and Victory Road divide Kanto from Johto.

Routes number 1 through 25 in Generations I, III and VII. Routes 26, 27 and 28 appear in Generations II and IV to reach Johto. Route 28 is a Kanto route that also sits on the Johto border. Routes 26 and 27 are Kanto locations that Johto games can Fly to in Generation IV. No Kanto route has a weather condition like Hoenn rain or Sinnoh hail.

South of the shared Kanto-Johto landmass the series places the [Orange Islands](Orange-Islands.md). They are not a game map.

Oak mentions a Kanto stone tablet like Sinnoh's Shaymin tablet. It is not seen in any game.

## History

Researchers in Kanto created Porygon. Mr. Fuji found Mew, named it, then used its DNA at the Pokémon Mansion to make Mewtwo. Mewtwo destroyed the mansion and hid in Cerulean Cave. Professor Rowan later visits Oak to study Pokédex systems.

Red/Blue/Yellow/FireRed/LeafGreen: Oak sends Red or Leaf out with a first partner. They take eight Badges, break Team Rocket, beat Giovanni at Viridian Gym and then beat Blue for the Indigo Championship.

Let's Go: Chase or Elaine follows Trace, stops Team Rocket with Blue and Trace, then beats Trace for the Champion title. They catch Mewtwo, meet Green and fight Archer of the Rocket remnants.

Gold/Silver/Crystal/HeartGold/SoulSilver: After Johto's Badges the player clears the Indigo Elite Four and Lance, then collects Kanto's Badges. Oak opens Mt. Silver, where Red waits.

## Generation changes

Mainland layout stays close from Gen I to the FireRed remake. Sevii Islands are the big Gen III add.

Three years after Gen I:

- Safari Zone closed. Johto gets a Safari in Gen IV. Kanto's site becomes Pal Park.
- Cerulean Cave collapsed in Gen II. Open again in Gen IV.
- Cinnabar destroyed by the volcano. Gym moves to Seafoam. Population 9.
- Power Plant running.
- Pokémon Tower converted to a radio tower.
- Blue runs Viridian Gym. Janine runs Fuchsia Gym.
- Viridian Forest shrinks into Route 2 in Gen II only.
- Mt. Moon shrinks.
- Route 23's south stretch is removed. The stone maze is gone.

Red/Green, Blue and Yellow each tweaked tiles and details. International Red/Blue follows Japanese Blue. Later Kanto maps reuse the original Red/Green layout.
