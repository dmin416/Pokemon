# Pokémon Games: Detailed In-Universe Timeline

Ancient background is given in "years before/after the present day" of the game listed. Present-day plot summarizes the game's main story: the antagonist organization, its goal, and the resolution.

## Kanto
**Ancient background:** none specifically dated.
**Red, Blue, Yellow:** Team Rocket, led by Giovanni, runs illegal Pokémon trafficking and experimentation from a hideout beneath the Celadon City Game Corner and a mansion on Cinnabar Island, where Mewtwo was created by splicing the DNA of Mew. The player dismantles Team Rocket's takeover of Silph Co. in Saffron City and the Celadon hideout, then defeats the Elite Four and the Champion (the player's rival) to become Champion.
**FireRed, LeafGreen:** the same core story, with an added Sevii Islands post-game arc in which a Team Rocket admin disguised as a scientist attempts to revive the organization, and the player encounters the Mythical Pokémon Deoxys, tied to a meteorite on Birth Island.

## Johto
**Ancient background:** none specifically dated beyond the legend of Ho-Oh and the three Legendary beasts, Raikou, Entei, and Suicune, said to have been reborn from a fire at the Brass Tower.
**Gold, Silver, Crystal:** three years after Kanto's story, remnants of Team Rocket attempt to rebuild without Giovanni, seizing the Radio Tower in Goldenrod City to broadcast a signal that agitates Pokémon. The player dismantles the plot, then travels to Kanto for a post-game arc, finding Giovanni vanished and ultimately challenging Red, the previous game's protagonist, atop Mt. Silver. Crystal adds a sidequest pursuing the Legendary beast Suicune alongside the researcher Eusine.
**HeartGold, SoulSilver:** the same story, remade with the added Pokéwalker accessory and an expanded post-game.

## Hoenn
**Ancient background:** in the primal age, Groudon and Kyogre formed from natural energy and fought until Rayquaza intervened. 2,000 years before the present day, meteor showers created Meteor Falls and the two awakened again; the Draconid people summoned Rayquaza a second time to stop them. 1,000 years before the present day, another meteor struck and created Sootopolis City's crater; Rayquaza Mega Evolved for the first time to end a second clash, and Sky Pillar was built in its honor, with a prophecy that the meteor would return in another thousand years. Lunatone was discovered at a meteor strike site 40 years before the present day.
**Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald:** Team Aqua (Ruby, Emerald) or Team Magma (Sapphire, Emerald), led by Archie or Maxie respectively, seek to expand the sea or the land by awakening Kyogre or Groudon. In Emerald, both teams are active, and Rayquaza must intervene at the Sky Pillar to halt the primal clash between the two.
**Omega Ruby, Alpha Sapphire:** the same base plot, with an added Delta Episode in which the Draconid Lorekeeper Zinnia enlists the player to stop the returning meteor by having Rayquaza Mega Evolve, fulfilling the ancient prophecy.

## Sinnoh and Hisui
**Ancient background:** the Hisui era sits at most 300 years before the present-day Sinnoh story, based on an in-game clue about how long Spiritomb had been sealed.
**Diamond, Pearl, Platinum:** Team Galactic, led by Cyrus, seeks to use Spear Pillar's power to summon Dialga (Diamond), Palkia (Pearl), or both (Platinum) in order to erase the current universe and remake it without emotion. The player and Champion Cynthia stop Cyrus, who is pulled into a rift. Platinum adds the Distortion World arc, featuring Giratina.
**Brilliant Diamond, Shining Pearl:** the same story, faithfully remade, with an added Grand Underground area.
**Legends: Arceus:** set in the Hisui era. The Ginkgo Guild merchant Volo, secretly a descendant of the ancient Celestica people, manipulates the player into helping him collect the Plates of Arceus, all while secretly causing Dialga and Palkia's rampage and summoning a maddened Giratina, hoping to draw out and control Arceus itself. The player defeats Volo and Giratina, then Arceus, restoring peace to Hisui.

## Unova
**Ancient background:** over 2,500 years before the present day, twin brothers used a single dragon to found Unova. Their falling-out over truth versus ideals split the dragon into Reshiram and Zekrom. Their sons later reignited the conflict, and the dragon's further division produced Kyurem.
**Black, White:** Team Plasma, publicly led by the boy-king N (secretly controlled by Ghetsis), campaigns for Pokémon liberation while Ghetsis privately plans to monopolize Pokémon use for himself. N bonds with Reshiram or Zekrom to battle the player at N's Castle, then departs.
**Black 2, White 2:** two years later, remnants of Team Plasma under Ghetsis attempt to resurface using Kyurem, which a returning N fuses with the version's dragon. The player and returning characters stop them.

## Kalos
**Ancient background:** 3,000 years before the present day, war broke out in Kalos. AZ's Floette died in the fighting; he built a machine to revive it, then turned that machine into the ultimate weapon and ended the war by draining Pokémon life force on Route 10.
**X, Y:** Team Flare, led by Lysandre, plans to use the ultimate weapon, recharged with the power of Xerneas or Yveltal, to wipe out all life except his own followers, believing the world's resources too scarce to share. The player, aided by Professor Sycamore and AZ, stops him at Geosenge Town.
**Legends: Z-A:** set five years after X and Y, entirely within Lumiose City. Quasartico Inc. leads an urban redevelopment plan, disrupted by "Rogue Mega Evolution," a phenomenon of Pokémon Mega Evolving without a Trainer. The player joins Team MZ (Urbain/Taunie, Naveen, and Lida) under AZ at Hotel Z, rising through the Z-A Royale tournament while investigating a mysterious man named L and the Pokémon Zygarde. The source is revealed to be Ange, a device AZ built roughly 2,000 years before this story inside Prism Tower, destabilized by Team Flare's earlier misuse of the ultimate weapon and transforming into the monstrous Hyperrogue Ange Floette, which the player and Team MZ must stop.

## Alola
**Ancient background:** none specifically dated.
**Sun, Moon:** the Aether Foundation, publicly a Pokémon conservation charity run by Lusamine, is revealed to be harboring and exploiting Ultra Beasts from other worlds. Lusamine's obsession leads her to open a permanent Ultra Wormhole; the player, Lillie, and Team Skull's Guzma must stop the resulting chaos and rescue her.
**Ultra Sun, Ultra Moon:** the same base plot, with the endgame replaced by Necrozma's rampage after it steals Solgaleo or Lunala's light, forming Dusk Mane or Dawn Wings Necrozma, then Ultra Necrozma.
**Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!:** a retelling of the Kanto story built around Let's Go-style capture mechanics, including Team Rocket and Giovanni, with the added Mythical Pokémon Meltan and its evolution Melmetal.

## Galar
**Ancient background:** a meteorite carrying Eternatus struck Galar 20,000 years before the present day. Eternatus woke and caused the Darkest Day 3,000 years before the present day; Zacian, Zamazenta, and two ancient Galarian kings defeated it. Mustard defeated Opal and became Champion 50 years before the present day, holding the title for 18 years before founding the Isle of Armor's Master Dojo.
**Sword, Shield:** Chairman Rose of Macro Cosmos, fearing a predicted Dynamax-energy shortage, plans to force a second Darkest Day by awakening Eternatus. The player, Hop, and Champion Leon stop him, then face and catch Eternatus itself alongside Zacian or Zamazenta.
**The Isle of Armor:** the player trains under former Champion Mustard at his Master Dojo, raising the Legendary Kubfu into one of two Urshifu forms depending on which of two trials is completed.
**The Crown Tundra:** the player leads Dynamax Adventure expeditions to catch the Legendary birds' Galarian forms and the Regi trio, culminating in unlocking the ancient king Calyrex, who can fuse with either Glastrier or Spectrier.

## Paldea
**Ancient background:** the Great Crater's Underdepths formed 2,000,000 years before the present day by seismic shifts. A Terapagos survived near-extinction there by crystallizing itself. The Paldean Empire began roughly 2,000 years before the present day; rumors of treasure in Area Zero triggered the Great Age of Exploration, and the empire declined roughly 1,000 years later. The Academy was founded 805 years before the present day. Heath's Area Zero Expedition reached the bottom 200 years before the present day and encountered Terapagos. The Terastal phenomenon was discovered 60 years before the present day. Professor Sada/Turo began Project Tera 10 years before the present day and invented Tera Orbs.
**Scarlet, Violet:** the Academy's "Treasure Hunt" independent-study assignment intertwines three threads. Team Star, a group of bullied students turned vigilantes led by Penny, uses a school-skipping AI called Cassiopeia. Arven searches for a cure for his ailing partner Mabosstiff, tied to Area Zero's Herba Mystica and the rampaging Titan Pokémon. Professor Sada/Turo's AI copy has secretly been raising the player's rival Nemona's world's counterpart while pursuing a scheme to bring a paradox creature home through time-travel research. The three threads converge in Area Zero, where the player faces ancient or future paradox forms of the box Legendary and stops the professor's plan.
**The Teal Mask:** on a school trip to Kitakami, the player and classmates Carmine and Kieran uncover the true story behind Ogerpon and the "Loyal Three," a trio of Pokémon worshipped as heroes who once helped drive out an ogre. Kieran's fascination with the "ogre" and the reveal that Ogerpon was in fact wronged culminate in a mask-powered battle.
**The Indigo Disk:** at Blueberry Academy, an indoor recreation of past regions' biomes, the player and the now more adversarial Kitakami siblings uncover Terapagos, the true, near-extinct origin of the Terastal phenomenon, hidden in the Area Zero Underdepths since the mass seismic event that nearly wiped it out.
