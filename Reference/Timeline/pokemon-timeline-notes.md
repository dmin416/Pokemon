# Notes

## Eternatus

Pokemon Ranger has a good Eternatus story.

Source: `Inspiration/Pokemon Ranger`

Eternatus is a huge legendary Pokemon. Long ago it fell to the Galar region inside a meteor. When Eternatus wakes, it floods Galar with energy. That energy makes Pokemon grow to giant size and attack without control. People call that disaster the Darkest Day.

Chairman Rose runs a company named Macro Cosmos. Rose wakes Eternatus on purpose. He wants a permanent energy supply for Galar. Two legendary wolf Pokemon named Zacian and Zamazenta stopped Eternatus during the first Darkest Day in ancient times.

In the Sword and Shield games, two young trainers named Hop and Gloria see visions of Zacian and Zamazenta inside a foggy forest called the Slumbering Weald. Later they find a rusted sword and a rusted shield in that forest. They help Galar’s Champion, Leon, stop Rose and Eternatus.

In Pokemon Ranger, the main character is Summer. Summer works as a forest ranger in and around the town of Spikemuth. He is not competing in Galar’s Gym Challenge. The Gym Challenge is the official badge tournament that leads to the championship. Summer already knows Rose plans to wake Eternatus. Hop and Gloria never see the wolf visions in the Slumbering Weald. Because of that, they never begin the search for the rusted sword and shield. Summer enters the Slumbering Weald himself. He does not wait for Leon or the Pokemon League to solve the problem.

Rose’s plan threatens Summer’s forests, Spikemuth and the capital stadium city of Wyndon. Rose wants energy for Galar. Rose’s method causes a second Darkest Day. Rose’s aide is Oleana. Oleana forces workers to gather Wishing Stars quickly. Wishing Stars are fragments of Eternatus’s power. Oleana also releases extra Dynamax energy into the land. Dynamax is Galar’s battle system that makes a Pokemon grow giant for a short time. The dumped energy causes wild giant Pokemon outbreaks before Eternatus fully wakes. Rose wakes Eternatus during the Champion Cup finale. The Champion Cup is Galar’s final championship tournament.

Summer needs help from Zacian and Zamazenta. He does not find the rusted sword and shield waiting for him as a simple item pickup. Inside the Slumbering Weald he is pulled into Galar’s past. There he learns what really happened during the first Darkest Day. An old king Pokemon named Calyrex fought in that era. Later records wrongly turned Calyrex into a human dragon-slayer from the Howard family. Later records also claimed two human heroes saved Galar. Those heroes were Zacian and Zamazenta. The wolves came from a fallen star, the same way Eternatus did. Zacian and Zamazenta fight Summer to test him. After the test they speak with him. Summer offers to rebuild their broken sword and shield using a meteorite at the bottom of the lake in the Weald.

## Gen 1

### Mew

Gym Leader has good Mew notes.

Source: `Inspiration/Gym Leader`

Mew is a Mythical pink Pokemon. Mew carries the genes of many Pokemon. In the movie Lucario and the Mystery of Mew, Mew guards a living place called the Tree of Beginning near the city of Rota. In the games, players receive Mew through special distribution events from Nintendo.

In Pokemon Gym Leader, Mew grows bored at the Tree of Beginning. It senses a trainer named Dylan. It appears to him disguised as a fat Clefairy. People call that disguised form Peppa. Dylan gives Mew food, drink and a place to stay. He does not catch Mew with a Master Ball. A Master Ball is the Poke Ball that never fails a catch.

Mew’s life force is tied to the Tree of Beginning. It cannot stay away from the tree forever. Three golem Pokemon guard the tree for Mew: Regirock, Regice and Registeel. An old friend of Mew left those guardians there. When Dylan catches Regirock, Mew arrives angry. Then Mew calms down and bargains with Dylan. Dylan buys land around the tree. He pays Mew with cans of tuna. Day to day, Mew eats large amounts of food, acts lazy, speaks bluntly and stays in disguise most of the time. It haggles over food, steals from fridges and hates being called Pig-Mew.

In serious battles Mew is extremely strong. It can use the move Imprison, which blocks an opponent from using certain moves. It Transforms into other legendary Pokemon. It Transforms into the ice dragon Kyurem, the destruction Pokemon Yveltal and the life Pokemon Xerneas used for healing. Mew smells Mewtwo’s genes and calls Mewtwo its child. Mew demands that Mewtwo call it Momma. Mew beats young Mewtwo in fights. Mewtwo denies that they are related. There are scenes with the rainbow bird Ho-Oh, including Mew stealing Ho-Oh’s feathers, plus the thunder dragon Zekrom, Kyurem and the creator Pokemon Arceus. Giovanni says that a one-year-old Mewtwo has not surpassed Mew.

### Mewtwo

Both Rancher and Psychiatrist have good Mewtwo notes.

Mewtwo is a legendary psychic Pokemon. Team Rocket scientists created it by copying genes from Mew. Mew is a Mythical Pokemon. In the first Mewtwo movie, Mewtwo rejects human control, makes cloned copies of other Pokemon and forces those clones to fight the real Pokemon. In the second Mewtwo movie, after more conflict with humans, Mewtwo lives in hiding on a mountain named Mount Quena.

#### Rancher

Source: `Inspiration/Rancher`

In Pokemon Rancher, Mewtwo has already left Mount Quena. It is no longer living on that mountain. There is another clone war. Mewtwo stands on a storm island inside a castle. Its cloned Pokemon fight the real Pokemon. Mewtwo says it is stronger than Mew and that it is the true original. Mew arrives with a Teddiursa. Teddiursa is a small bear Pokemon. Teddiursa asks what winning the fight proves and why both sides cannot live in peace. Mewtwo accepts that cloned Pokemon and real Pokemon are both Pokemon. It stops the fight. It leaves with the clones. No trainer catches Mewtwo.

Later Mewtwo brings the clones to D’s Pokemon tavern. D runs a place where Pokemon can eat and drink. Mewtwo watches the clones play with ordinary town Pokemon. It sees that the clones can be happy there. Mewtwo drinks one beer, gets drunk and says humans made it from Mew’s genes. It came to the tavern to understand what it is. It did not come to ask D for help. The next morning it is embarrassed about being drunk. It leaves to search for another quiet place to live. That place serves the same role Mount Quena did: a hidden refuge. In later chapters Mewtwo helps other legendary Pokemon from a distance. It stays hidden from Team Rocket.

#### Psychiatrist

Source: `Inspiration/Psychiatrist`

In Pokemon Psychiatrist, the story starts while Mewtwo is still held by Team Rocket. Giovanni is the boss of Team Rocket. Johan is a psychologist who treats Pokemon. Someone messages Johan online with no name. That someone then says people call it Mewtwo. Johan asks who it thinks it is. He does not talk to it as a finished weapon. Early sessions cover identity practice, memories of Ai Two and other clone siblings from the Rocket lab, Giovanni’s claim that Pokemon exist to be used by humans and Mewtwo learning not to destroy the lab when it panics. When Mewtwo loses control, its psychic power crashes the computer network. Over time it learns more restraint.

Later Mewtwo stops an attack involving Genesect. Genesect are ancient bug Pokemon that Team Rocket rebuilt as living weapons. Mewtwo recognizes the same rage it felt when it was created as a tool. It says aloud that it is Mewtwo. It decides its purpose is protecting Pokemon that were abused or artificially made. It tries living like a human in Johan’s cafe. It cooks food and wears an apron. Then it decides it fits better as a Pokemon. When it meets Mew, it becomes furious because Mew, the original it was copied from, disappoints it. At the end Mewtwo places a Dark Ball in Johan’s hand on purpose. A Dark Ball is a special kind of Poke Ball. Mewtwo chooses to become Johan’s partner.

### Dratini → Dragonair → Dragonite

Rancher has a good Dragonite Island and giant Dragonite story. Psychiatrist and Gym Leader also use this evolution line on main characters’ teams.

Dragonite is a large orange dragon Pokemon. Dratini is its baby form. Dragonair is the middle form between Dratini and Dragonite.

#### Rancher

Source: `Inspiration/Rancher`

Dragonite Island is a hidden island far out at sea. Many Dragonite live there. A thick ring of forest surrounds a lake in the center of the island. Young Dratini live in that lake. Dragonair also live in that lake. Adult Dragonite live on the shore. They practice battle against each other. They also show off weather moves such as rain and strong wind.

D’s ranch Dragonite began as a weak outcast on that island. It could not learn the move Dragon Tail. A Dragonite it liked rejected it, so it left the island. It sat on a cliff by Casseroya Lake in Paldea, stared at the moon and looked unhappy. Koraidon helps arrange for D to catch it. It joins D’s ranch as a chubby Dragonite that delivers goat milk in a red bag. It grows stronger through ranch work, weather practice and delivery routes. When it is strong enough, it flies D to Dragonite Island. There it shows off rain and lightning. It wins respect from the other Dragonite. It loses a battle against the Dragonite it wants as a mate and does not quit trying.

In the anime, one Dragonite near Bill’s Lighthouse is far larger than a normal Dragonite. That giant was lonely. When people tried to catch it, it destroyed the lighthouse. In Pokemon Rancher, that same giant Dragonite appears. A Teddiursa connected to D feels how lonely the giant is. The Teddiursa accidentally tells the giant to look for D’s Dragonite in Paldea. The giant swims to Paldea looking for a friend. It is thirty meters tall. It is strong enough to fight at Champion level. Champion level means strong enough to challenge the strongest official trainer of a region. The giant’s size alone causes an emergency on the coast. Top Champion Geeta and other League people check the situation. They confirm the giant is not attacking because it hates humans.

D does not catch the giant Dragonite. He leads it to Dragonite Island. D’s own Dragonite tells the island Dragonite that the giant only wants to live with them. The island group accepts the giant. The giant cries, holds onto the island and then sleeps under the island. From under the island it wakes and guards the place when danger comes. Later, food such as pumpkin pie makes the giant shrink to three meters tall. At that smaller size it plays with the other Dragonite. It keeps its real strength. Island Dragonite later visit D’s ranch and help with deliveries.

#### Psychiatrist

Source: `Inspiration/Psychiatrist`

At the Dragon’s Den in Johto, a guardian Dragonite left by a prior Blackthorn Gym Leader is the father of a competitive Dratini. That Dratini has the ability Marvel Scale. It challenges the Red Gyarados at the Lake of Rage. Johan recruits it to help with Thunderbolt work on that Gyarados. The Dratini coils Johan’s wrist and chooses to leave with him after a battle. It evolves from Dratini to Dragonair to Dragonite. The Dragonite evolution happens during a clash with the sanctuary Dragonite. Clair is present for that fight. Johan’s Dragonite later helps hold Shadow Lugia so Johan can shrink a red chain binding it.

#### Gym Leader

Source: `Inspiration/Gym Leader`

Lance gives Dylan a Dratini. Within about three months that Dratini is a champion-level Dragonite. Dylan’s Dragonite beats Lance’s Charizard. Lance also keeps twin Dragonair for weather control and fields a giant Dragonite caught near Cerulean waters.

## Gen 3

### Deoxys

Psychiatrist has a good Deoxys story.

Source: `Inspiration/Psychiatrist`

Deoxys is a space virus Pokemon that changes battle formes. A green Deoxys core crystal sits inside a meteorite Johan unearths while mining. Larvitar nearly eats it. The green-core consciousness enters Johan’s phone, spam-messages “Friend” and asks for help. A purple-core Deoxys searches for that companion. Purple aurora marks its signal north of Autumnleaf Town and then at Mossdeep. The plan is to reunite them at Mossdeep Space Center. Purple Deoxys arrives atop the signal tower and senses through electromagnetic fields. Team Magma approaches and Deoxys attacks them. Deoxys goes berserk, rejects Aura and enters Defense Forme. It fights Rayquaza over Mossdeep. Later both Deoxys leave and the League moves in. On a later space meteorite mission the purple and green Deoxys assist Johan with Darkrai and Rayquaza. Johan gifts them paired crystal sculptures. They thank him telepathically. Nobody catches either Deoxys as a partner. They remain callable allies for space work.

### Rayquaza

Rayquaza appears across the inspiration novels. Rayquaza is a legendary green sky dragon from the Hoenn region. In Hoenn lore, Rayquaza stops fights between Groudon and Kyogre. Groudon is the land legendary. Kyogre is the sea legendary. Rayquaza lives high in the atmosphere and in space. It eats meteorites before many of them hit the ground. Sky Pillar is a tower the Draconid people built to reach and honor Rayquaza. Mega Rayquaza is Rayquaza’s Mega Evolution form. Mega Evolution means a temporary powered-up battle form. Dragon Ascent is the flying strike move tied to Mega Rayquaza.

#### Psychiatrist

Source: `Inspiration/Psychiatrist`

Johan is a Pokemon psychologist. He does not catch Rayquaza. He befriends the green Hoenn Rayquaza through aura talk, food deals and shared crises. Rayquaza treats Johan as a peer. It treats Draconid inheritors as subordinates.

Jirachi gives Johan a Jade Orb shard. That shard shows Johan a vision of Rayquaza at Sky Pillar. Rayquaza tells him to come face its trials. During a Deoxys crisis at Mossdeep, Johan has Steven and Wallace call Rayquaza. Deoxys is a space virus Pokemon. Rayquaza arrives and fights Deoxys. After Deoxys calms down, Rayquaza tries to eat Deoxys with Dragon Ascent. Johan stops that strike mid-move. Rayquaza teaches Dragon Ascent to Johan’s Dragonite. Johan rides Rayquaza’s head through the move and collapses afterward. Rayquaza leaves for space and demands Johan keep a food promise.

When Groudon and Kyogre wake again, Steven and Wallace find a rainbow stone at a Sootopolis altar. Rayquaza Mega Evolves, takes that stone and tells them they are not the inheritors. It spares them because of their tie to Johan. During the Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre clash, Johan cannot reach Rayquaza. Rayquaza is busy testing a Draconid inheritor named Aster at Sky Pillar. Primal forms are Groudon and Kyogre’s ancient powered forms.

Johan, Steven and Wallace climb Sky Pillar. Rayquaza has Aster fight Johan as a final test. Johan fields Reshiram, Zekrom, Darkrai and Gengar. Rayquaza backs down from fighting that lineup. Johan stages a loss so Aster passes. Rayquaza accepts Aster as inheritor. It gives the inheritor scroll to Dragonite. That scroll holds Dragon Ascent. Rayquaza then takes Johan into space as navigator. Mega Rayquaza destroys a world-threatening meteorite. Solgaleo, a sun legendary, is on that meteorite. Johan has Rayquaza use Fire Blast on Solgaleo, then stops the chase and talks through aura.

Later Johan returns to Sky Pillar. Rayquaza gives Dragonite green orb fragments for Mega Evolution and a cosmic meteorite for Jirachi. Rayquaza takes Energy Cubes and returns to the ozone layer. A separate black Rayquaza appears when Hoopa summons it from another world. That black Rayquaza is not Johan’s green Rayquaza. Johan rides the black Rayquaza against Hoopa Unbound’s summons.

#### Gym Leader

Source: `Inspiration/Gym Leader`

Dylan has Draconid blood. The Draconids are the people who worship Rayquaza. In this novel there are three Rayquaza. Dylan’s Rayquaza is the youngest and weakest of the three. Its parents are an elder green Mega Rayquaza and a shiny black Mega Rayquaza. Dylan nicknames his Rayquaza Greeny. The parents call it Little Green.

Zinnia and Dylan awaken Groudon and Kyogre to force Rayquaza to appear. Zinnia prays at Sky Pillar’s Dragon Altar. A green Rayquaza descends. Zinnia’s Mega Evolution attempt fails. Rayquaza rejects Zinnia’s hatred and leaves. On the battlefield, Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre beat that young Rayquaza. Rayquaza tells Dylan that Zinnia is a bad bond and that its parents are coming. Dylan bargains: he suppresses Groudon and Kyogre before the parents arrive and Rayquaza makes him inheritor. Rayquaza agrees to that bargain. Dylan uses Black Kyurem and Stellar Terapagos. The parent Mega Rayquaza arrive and drive Groudon and Kyogre off.

Dylan, using his criminal alias Aizen, catches the young Rayquaza in an Ultra Ball after a staged launch. The League believes Aizen owns Rayquaza. Zinnia gives the prophecy duty to Dylan. Dylan dyes Rayquaza black to look like its mother, then later dyes it dark red. He teaches it Dragon Ascent so it can Mega Evolve. As Aizen’s Mega Rayquaza, it beats many Champion Pokemon in Saffron. It later beats a weakened Necrozma. Against its mind-controlled shiny mother during a Hoopa crisis, it loses. Paint chips off and shows green scales. The mother scolds the red dye, then praises its Dragon Ascent progress. During an Arceus fight, Hoopa summons both parent Rayquaza. The parents fight Arceus, thank Dylan for raising Greeny and keep scolding the dye choices.

#### Rancher

Source: `Inspiration/Rancher`

D’s Dragonite meets a huge black Rayquaza during a typhoon over the sea. Rayquaza’s pressure breaks the typhoon. Dragonite copies its twisting flight and learns Dragon Dance. D identifies that Pokemon as black Rayquaza. It lives in the atmosphere and eats meteorite energy.

Dragonite later finds Rayquaza sleeping high above. Rayquaza remembers Dragonite from the typhoon. It tells its old hero stories and shows Dragon Ascent. It gives Dragonite a shed scale that summons it. Ranch Pokemon invite Rayquaza to a feast with that scale. Rayquaza crosses regions to visit D’s ranch, eats Pokeblocks and Poffins, answers questions about sky life and leaves. It returns with a Minior as a “star” gift. Minior is a living meteor Pokemon. D catches the Minior. The ranch explains Minior are Pokemon. Rayquaza is embarrassed because it eats Minior shells. It stops catching them as gifts.

During a Hoenn Groudon and Kyogre crisis, Wallace calls at Sky Pillar and gets no answer. D has Dragonite summon the black Rayquaza. Rayquaza uses Dragon Ascent, drives Groudon and Kyogre away and clears the weather. Rayquaza later joins D’s World Tree. D gives it a Rotom Phone. Rayquaza joins the legendary chat group. It stays on the World Tree canopy when not hunting meteorites. It helps supervise Groudon and Kyogre under D’s no-fighting rules. It leaves for deeper space to hunt stronger meteorites for Mega Evolution energy. Nobody catches this Rayquaza.

## Gen 4

### Darkrai

Psychiatrist, Gym Leader and Pokemon Ranger have good Darkrai notes.

Darkrai is a Mythical dark Pokemon tied to nightmares. Cresselia is its lunar counterpart. Newmoon Island and Fullmoon Island in Sinnoh are its famous homes.

#### Psychiatrist

Source: `Inspiration/Psychiatrist`

At Canalave, a Newmoon Island Darkrai fights Cresselia at night. Johan’s team intervenes and that Darkrai flees. Johan knows of at least three Darkrai. Tobias’s Darkrai sweeps with Dark Void, Dream Eater and Nightmare. It speaks and respects Gengar. In the Alamos Town arc a Darkrai follows Johan’s commands against Dialga and Palkia. Later it negotiates as a fighter-for-hire paid in Pokeblocks. It refuses acting work. It becomes a recurring ally called from White Forest. It fights Thundurus and Tornadus, aids Reshiram against Black Kyurem and rides along on a space meteorite with Deoxys. Later it summons for Arceus-scale fights with Giratina. It is not framed as a formal catch.

#### Gym Leader

Source: `Inspiration/Gym Leader`

Team Rocket takes Darkrai from the S.S. Anne. Giovanni orders gene fusion into Mewtwo. That gene fusion attempt fails completely. Dylan finds the weakened Darkrai in a Rocket vault and frees it. Darkrai speaks by telepathy: a prior trainer abandoned it and it feels weak. Dylan catches it as a partner. He uses it in battles from a Luxury Ball with Dark Pulse, Shadow Ball, Blizzard and Dark Void.

#### Pokemon Ranger

Source: `Inspiration/Pokemon Ranger`

A Darkrai is forced into prolonged village nightmares beyond normal new-moon defense. It works with a controlled Zoroark. It takes burns from Okidogi and vanishes into shadows. A master controls it. Volo speaks as an interlocutor. Darkrai stays resentful under that control. Summer fights it with Mist Terrain against Dark Void. Rei is asked whether he is Darkrai’s trainer.

### Heatran

Psychiatrist has a good Heatran story.

Source: `Inspiration/Psychiatrist`

Heatran is a fire and steel legendary that lives in volcanoes. At Mount Chimney, Johan and Steven meet a Heatran that rises from lava. It does not attack. It waves them away, stabilizes the lava and places a blocking stone. It accepts Moomoo Milk and energy cubes Johan leaves. Gym Leader Mura already knows that Heatran. Flannery learns of it afterward.

A second encounter is hostile. Magma Storm and rage tie to a dislodged Volcanic Keystone. Flannery and Gengar recover the keystone. Johan’s team stalls with Dragonite’s Water Pulse, Charcadet, Kubfu and Steven’s Metagross. After the keystone is restored, Heatran calms, takes Pokeblocks and returns into the Volcanic Keystone. It is not caught.

In Rancher, feeding Heatran raises favorability in dish notes. D fantasizes about a ranch magma cavern. Heatran remains in Sinnoh or Hoenn and is not obtained there.

### Giratina

Rancher, Psychiatrist and Pokemon Ranger have good Giratina notes.

Giratina is a legendary Pokemon of antimatter. It rules the Distortion World, also called the Reversed World or Spirit World in some books.

#### Rancher

Source: `Inspiration/Rancher`

D reaches the Reversed World through Poisonous Mushroom Soup and meets Origin Forme Giratina. Giratina attacks with Shadow Ball and Dragon Tail. D plays dead and later approaches as a polite guest. He wants Water of the Reversed World. He offers human food and future spoilers about Dialga and Palkia movie events. Giratina agrees to that trade. It drinks beer and hangs out. Later a Giratina Strategy brings it in as a ranch ally. It helps lift the World Tree with anti-gravity. It lives in the World Tree’s shadow and returns to the Reversed World. It joins the legendary chat group. D plans to help it confront Arceus as repayment. Giratina escorts Dragonite on deliveries.

#### Psychiatrist

Source: `Inspiration/Psychiatrist`

Johan bargains an alliance with Distortion World Giratina against the Creator. Giratina drinks Energy Root tea, watches TV through Gengar’s phone and warns that Arceus is waking. It opens the Distortion World for Dialga and Palkia. It tanks against Arceus with Dragon Rush and black mist wings. It remains a recurring summonable ally with Darkrai.

#### Pokemon Ranger

Source: `Inspiration/Pokemon Ranger`

Summer and Allister enter the Spirit World and meet Giratina. It speaks human language and studies souls. It grants Trevenant as a new gatekeeper, takes a tablet, gifts a Life Orb and returns to puzzle tablets in a ruined world.

### Cresselia

Psychiatrist and Pokemon Ranger have good Cresselia notes.

Cresselia is a legendary psychic Pokemon of the moon and pleasant dreams. Fullmoon Island is its home in Sinnoh lore.

#### Psychiatrist

Source: `Inspiration/Psychiatrist`

At Canalave, Cresselia appears at night. Rotom records a first photo. It battles Darkrai with Lunar Force against Dark Void and soft light over the city. After Johan’s team drives Darkrai off, Cresselia thanks Johan and gifts a Feather of the New Moon with Power of the New Moon. It accepts Pokeblocks and leaves a starlight trail that clears nightmares. It is not caught.

#### Pokemon Ranger

Source: `Inspiration/Pokemon Ranger`

A young man named Yagen reveals a hidden ace Cresselia. It shows aurora light and induces sleepiness. Cresselia maintains blizzard barriers with Brimwin and Wyrdeer. It helps put broken swords to sleep with crescent light and feathers. Summer receives sweet-dreams gratitude tied to Cresselia’s power.

In Rancher, Cresselia feather lore means beautiful dreams and names Cresselia as Darkrai’s counterpart. There is no partnership plot there.

## Gen 6

### Hoopa

Gym Leader and Psychiatrist have good Hoopa notes.

Hoopa is a Mythical Pokemon of rings and summoning. Hoopa Confined is the small form. Hoopa Unbound is the powerful form freed by the Prison Bottle. Meray and Baraz are guardians in the Valley of Creation near Dahara City.

#### Gym Leader

Source: `Inspiration/Gym Leader`

Hoopa Confined lives in the Valley of Creation after a century-ago rampage. Dylan meets Confined Hoopa in Desert City through Reshiram’s reaction. Meray disciplines Hoopa for running off while shopping. Sealed power formed its own will and resentment as a shadow Hoopa. Meray and Baraz want to restore power once they find the Prison Bottle. Confined Hoopa uses golden rings for transport across regions. Hoopa Unbound fights Dark Mewtwo and swallows attacks with rings. Unbound summons legends through six space rings. Dylan uses Hoopa to pull Rayquaza and other legendaries into battles. Later Hoopa opens rings for Wallace’s group into Rota Gym. Fear keeps Confined Hoopa off the front line, so Dylan relies on portals.

#### Psychiatrist

Source: `Inspiration/Psychiatrist`

Johan flies Flint to Dahara City for the Desert Tower Festival and recognizes the Hoopa movie site. Arceus’s Millennium Wrist points toward Dahara and the Valley of Creation. The Prison Bottle was made from Earth, Water and Fire plate power. Arc chapters cover Gengar’s “little brother” Hoopa, Shadow Hoopa, a Giovanni showdown and multi-chapter fights versus Hoopa Unbound including Zygarde. When Ultra Demon Hoopa goes out of control it summons Lunala among nine legendaries. Johan recalls fighting that Lunala.

#### Rancher

Source: `Inspiration/Rancher`

D tells the Heroic Expedition Team Hoopa’s Dahara seal story and commissions them to find and recruit Hoopa. At that point Hoopa is still sealed in Dahara. The full ranch joining arc is not on the page in those chapters.

### Honedge

Pokemon Ranger and Gym Leader have good Honedge-line notes.

Honedge is a ghost and steel sword Pokemon. It evolves into Doublade. Doublade evolves into Aegislash with a Dusk Stone.

#### Pokemon Ranger

Source: `Inspiration/Pokemon Ranger`

In a fight against Galarian Farfetch’d, Fighting moves pass through Ghost typing. Farfetch’d evolves mid-fight after stabbing Honedge’s eye. Summer offers to train Sordward until Honedge evolves.

#### Gym Leader

Source: `Inspiration/Gym Leader`

Dylan fields Aegislash with Stance Change, King’s Shield and Sacred Sword against Cynthia’s Lucario. It knocks Lucario out with barely moving. Leon notes that Dylan’s Aegislash hits hard. Cynthia’s Porygon-Z Electric barrage forces Dylan to recall Aegislash.

## Gen 7

### Cosmog

Gym Leader and Psychiatrist have good Cosmog notes.

Cosmog is a Mythical space Pokemon called the Child of the Stars. It evolves into Cosmoem. Cosmoem then becomes Solgaleo or Lunala. Those adults open Ultra Wormholes. Ultra Wormholes are gates to other worlds.

#### Gym Leader

Source: `Inspiration/Gym Leader`

Lillie fled Aether Paradise with Cosmog, nicknamed Nebby. Dylan identifies it as Child of the Stars. Nebby bonds to Dylan’s Viridian power and stays with the Lillie and Dylan household. Lusamine needs Cosmog to open Ultra Wormholes. Immature Cosmog cannot control wormholes stably. Solgaleo, Lunala and Necrozma can. Growth simulation shows Cosmog to Cosmoem to Lunala at the Moone Altar, then Necrozma fusion into Dawn Wings forms.

#### Psychiatrist

Source: `Inspiration/Psychiatrist`

Anabel’s team recovers a Cosmog nicknamed Nebby. Burnet notes Ultra Aura. Guzzlord targets Cosmog and Cosmog teleports away. Cosmog can open Ultra Wormholes and teleport. Nebby’s appearance is flagged as a precursor to Solgaleo and Lunala activity. Separately, Solgaleo asks Johan via a space encounter to go to the Altar of the Sunne. Lunala awakening in the Reverse World opens Ultra Wormholes. Johan holds Alola until Solgaleo and Lunala wake. A prior Hoopa Unbound event summoned Lunala and Johan fought it.

## Gen 8

### Calyrex

Rancher, Psychiatrist, Gym Leader and Pokemon Ranger have good Calyrex notes.

Calyrex is a legendary psychic and grass king Pokemon from the Crown Tundra in Galar. People call it the King of Bountiful Harvests. Faith restores its power. Full strength needs Glastrier or Spectrier plus the Reins of Unity.

#### Rancher

Source: `Inspiration/Rancher`

D builds a shrine idol in Paldea. Faith reaches Crown Tundra Calyrex across regions. Calyrex sees past, present and future. Faith restores Calyrex’s lost power. D travels to Galar, crafts Reins of Unity from Glastrier mane and a Radiant Flower and makes separate Spectrier reins. Calyrex merges with both steeds. Calyrex leaves Crown Shrine and moves to D’s ranch. It grows plants instantly, including back-mountain restoration that Geeta and the League notice. A public shrine is built. Thunder and Storm trials test shrine pilgrims. Calyrex later fights in an ultimate form against Groudon and Kyogre. Calyrex is present when purifying Wo-Chien so ranch crops are not drained.

#### Psychiatrist

Source: `Inspiration/Psychiatrist`

A wooden Calyrex stake links to the real Calyrex. A Radiant Petal appears. In Freezington a weak Calyrex phantom possesses Flint in a Peony-style scene to speak. It thanks Johan’s faith for awakening. Power comes from faith. Past neglect stopped the Radiant Petal. Freezington people over-sacrificed in that past. A Glastrier blizzard chapter follows.

#### Gym Leader

Source: `Inspiration/Gym Leader`

Dylan recounts King of Bountiful Harvests lore to Lillie and Leaf. Calyrex naps in Crown Shrine while Glastrier and Spectrier wander. Calyrex rides Spectrier to retrieve Glastrier mid-fight with Dylan.

#### Pokemon Ranger

Source: `Inspiration/Pokemon Ranger`

House Howard’s crest shows ice armor, ghost mane, detached hooves and a crown. That image is Glastrier, Spectrier and Calyrex combined. Butler Fowler’s legend describes a pale youth with a beret-crown and a steed of blizzard and spirits that slew a crimson dragon leading a giant army. Summer reads the ancestor as Calyrex. The spear is Ice Lance. The crimson dragon is Eternatus. The giants are Gigantamax Pokemon that lost Dynamax after Eternatus’s defeat. Calyrex was erased from its own legend the same way Zacian and Zamazenta were erased from Darkest Day records. Summer concludes Calyrex’s power may be key to stopping Eternatus again.

### Koraidon

Rancher has a good Area Zero and Koraidon story.

Source: `Inspiration/Rancher`

Area Zero is a giant crater in the center of the Paldea region. Professors Sada and Turo build a Time Machine at the bottom of that crater. The Time Machine pulls Pokemon from other times into the present. Those Pokemon are called paradox Pokemon.

In the Scarlet and Violet games, the main character is a student at Paldea’s academy. By that time Professors Sada and Turo are already dead. The student enters Area Zero with friends and shuts the Time Machine down.

In Pokemon Rancher, the story happens ten years before that academy story. On the master timeline that academy story is Year 12. In Rancher, Professors Sada and Turo are both alive. They work from one research book called the Book of Scarlet and Violet. Their son Arven is still a child. Arven is angry at the paradox Pokemon because the research keeps his parents away from him. The Time Machine is running every day. Area Zero has a staffed research institute.

Koraidon is a legendary paradox dragon. An injured Koraidon crashes into D’s ranch field at night. That Koraidon used to be D’s Cyclizar. Cyclizar is a dragon Pokemon that people in Paldea ride like a bicycle. D’s Cyclizar fell into the Time Machine. The machine threw it into the ancient past. Over a long time there it changed into Koraidon. The Time Machine later pulled it back to the present lab. Professor Sada threw a Poke Ball at it. Koraidon knocked the ball away and flew to D’s ranch. The Cyclizar that still lives on the ranch is the same Pokemon before the time fall. D’s cooking and farm work keep Koraidon calm. The lab could not keep it under control. Professor Sada wants Koraidon brought back to Area Zero. Koraidon stays with D.

D later visits Area Zero because the League and the professors invite him. Sada and Turo believe people and paradox Pokemon can share the world. A second Koraidon and a Miraidon live at the lab and guard the Time Machine. Miraidon is the future-looking counterpart to Koraidon. The professors also explore caves under Area Zero called the Underdepths. They study a Pokemon named Terapagos there. Terapagos is tied to Paldea’s crystal power, called Terastal energy. These later events have not happened yet in Rancher: an artificial copy of a professor takes over the lab, the Time Machine is shut down and adult Arven lives alone and bitter.

On the ranch, Koraidon works as labor and flight mount. It battles for D, travels to Glaseado and helps with Wo-Chien and Chien-Pao sealing missions. It recognizes ranch Cyclizar as its past self. It is friends with Flutter Mane from the ancient past and helps recruit Flutter Mane to the ranch.

In Gym Leader, Area Zero AIs discuss releasing Koraidon One to avoid a clash with feral Koraidon Two. Paradise Defense Protocol can weaponize Koraidon and Miraidon. Koraidon and Miraidon alert the AIs when Dylan enters Area Zero with Kyurem.
