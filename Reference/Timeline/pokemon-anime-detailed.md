# Pokémon Anime: Detailed In-Universe Timeline

Companions, recurring antagonists, key Legendary Pokémon encounters, and tournament results for each stage of Ash Ketchum's journey, in the order he experienced them.

## Kanto
Ash Ketchum, age ten, leaves Pallet Town with a disobedient Pikachu, traveling with Misty and Brock. Team Rocket's Jessie, James, and Meowth begin their recurring pursuit of Pikachu here. Ash competes in the Indigo Plateau Conference and loses in the Top 16 to his friend Richie, whose own Pikachu-partnered team mirrors Ash's.

## Orange Islands
Ash, Misty, and Brock continue on, joined by Pokémon Watcher Tracey Sketchit. Rather than Gym Badges, the group collects four island badges from the Orange Crew. Ash defeats Orange Islands Champion Drake to win the Orange League, an unofficial title not tied to the mainline games.

## Johto
Ash returns briefly to Kanto, then travels Johto with Misty and Brock. Team Rocket continues its pursuit. The Legendary bird Ho-Oh, first glimpsed at the very start of Ash's journey in Kanto, recurs as a guiding presence. Ash competes in the Silver Conference and loses in the quarterfinals (Top 8) to Harrison, a trainer from Hoenn.

## Hoenn
Ash returns home, then travels Hoenn with new companions May and Max, alongside Brock. Team Rocket persists as a recurring nuisance; Team Aqua and Team Magma appear in a handful of standalone episodes without adapting the games' full plot. Ash competes in the Ever Grande Conference and loses in the quarterfinals (Top 8) to Tyson, who goes on to win the tournament.

## Kanto Battle Frontier
Ash, May, Max, and Brock remain together as Ash challenges the Battle Frontier, a set of seven facilities each overseen by a Frontier Brain, including Brandon of the Battle Pyramid. Ash defeats all seven Brains to earn the Frontier Symbols.

## Sinnoh
Ash travels Sinnoh with new companion Dawn, who competes in Pokémon Contests and wins the Sinnoh Grand Festival, and Brock. Team Galactic recurs across the arc through members Saturn, Mars, Jupiter, and leader Cyrus; Legendary encounters include Dialga, Palkia, Giratina, Darkrai, Shaymin, and, in a tie-in special, Arceus. Ash competes in the Lily of the Valley Conference and reaches the semifinals (Top 4) before losing to Tobias, the only trainer shown to defeat Ash using just two Pokémon, his Darkrai and Latios.

## Unova
Ash travels Unova with new companions Iris and Cilan, the latter a Pokémon Connoisseur. Team Plasma recurs through N and Ghetsis. Ash competes in the Vertress Conference and loses in the quarterfinals (Top 8) to Cameron, whose Riolu evolves into Lucario mid-battle; Virgil goes on to win the tournament. Further adventures follow in the Decolora Archipelago, where Ash reunites with his Charizard and Team Plasma, including Colress, resurfaces.

## Kalos
Ash travels Kalos with new companions Clemont, Bonnie, and Serena. Team Flare, led by Lysandre, recurs across the arc, tied to a young Zygarde Core the group names Squishy. Ash bonds with his Greninja, achieving the unique Ash-Greninja form through the Bond Phenomenon. Ash reaches the final of the Lumiose Conference before losing to Alain, becoming runner-up; the closing ceremony is disrupted by Team Flare's mind-controlled Zygarde attack on Lumiose City, which Clemont ultimately halts.

## Alola
Ash enrolls as a student at Professor Kukui's Pokémon School rather than undertaking a traditional badge journey. The Aether Foundation's Lusamine and her Ultra Beast obsession, alongside Team Skull's Guzma, recur across the arc. Ash encounters and helps stop Necrozma's rampage. Ash wins the inaugural Manalo Conference, defeating Gladion in the final, becoming the first Champion of Alola, his first outright League Championship. He then wins an exhibition match against Kukui, who is also the masked wrestler the Masked Royal.

## World Journey
Ash travels with new companion Goh, a researcher-in-training obsessed with catching Mew, visiting every region at once and competing in the World Coronation Series ranking system. Recurring threads include a rebuilt Team Rocket trio, a Sinnoh-set Team Galactic tie-in, and a Galar-set arc adapting the Darkest Day and Eternatus. Ash advances to the Masters Eight tournament and defeats reigning World Champion Leon in the final, becoming World Champion himself. Ash and Pikachu then depart together on a new, unshown journey, closing out Ash's personal story.

## Post-Ash Era
A new story begins with protagonists Liko and Roy, unconnected to Ash, enrolling at Naranja/Uva Academy in Paldea and later traveling beyond it. Recurring elements include Liko's pendant, tied to a legend of "six heroes," and the criminal Explorers organization.
