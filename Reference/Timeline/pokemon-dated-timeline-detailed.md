# Pokémon Dated Master Timeline (Detailed): Ash's Kanto Journey as Year 1

Year 1 is set at Ash Ketchum's departure from Pallet Town. Every other event is dated relative to that point, using the same method as before: ancient "X years before the present" references are converted using the year assigned to that region's own "present day," following the order Ash visited each region in the anime. Where the games state an official numeric offset between regions, that can conflict with the anime's travel order; both are noted. Games retelling an earlier region (Let's Go, Brilliant Diamond/Shining Pearl) and DLC (Isle of Armor, Crown Tundra, The Teal Mask, The Indigo Disk) are placed at the same year as their base game. The four-film alternate-continuity trilogy is not dated against this spine at all, since it does not share this timeline.

## Full Timeline

- **Year -1,999,988**: The Great Crater's Underdepths form beneath Paldea. A Terapagos survives near-extinction by crystallizing itself.
- **Year -19,990**: A meteorite carrying Eternatus falls in Galar.
- **Year -2,992**: War breaks out in Kalos. AZ's Floette dies in the fighting; he later builds a machine to revive it and turns it into the ultimate weapon to end the war.
- **Year -2,990**: Eternatus wakes and causes the Darkest Day in Galar. Zacian, Zamazenta, and two ancient Galarian kings defeat it.
- **Year -2,493 or earlier**: Twin brothers found Unova using a single dragon. Their falling-out splits the dragon into Reshiram and Zekrom. Their sons later reignite the conflict, and the dragon's further division produces Kyurem.
- **Year -1,996**: Meteor showers create Meteor Falls in Hoenn. Groudon and Kyogre awaken and fight again. The Draconids summon Rayquaza to stop them.
- **Year -1,988**: The Paldean Empire begins. Rumors of treasure in Area Zero start the Great Age of Exploration.
- **Year -996**: A second meteor strikes Hoenn, creating the crater that becomes Sootopolis City. Rayquaza Mega Evolves for the first time to end a second Groudon/Kyogre battle. Sky Pillar is built, and the Draconids record a prophecy that the meteor will return in 1,000 years.
- **Year -988**: The Paldean Empire declines.
- **Year -793**: The Paldea-region Academy is founded.
- **Year -294 (upper bound)**: The Hisui era begins in what will become Sinnoh. Legends: Arceus takes place somewhere between here and Year 6; Volo manipulates the player into helping him summon Giratina and collect the Plates of Arceus, and is defeated alongside Giratina and then Arceus itself.
- **Year -188**: Heath's Area Zero Expedition reaches the bottom of the crater and encounters Terapagos.
- **Year -48**: The Terastal phenomenon is discovered in Area Zero.
- **Year -40**: Mustard defeats Opal and becomes Galar's Champion. He later founds the Isle of Armor's Master Dojo.
- **Year -36**: Lunatone is discovered at a meteor strike site in Hoenn.
- **Year 1**: Ash Ketchum, age ten, leaves Pallet Town with Pikachu, watched by Team Rocket's Jessie, James, and Meowth. He earns eight Kanto badges, defeats Team Rocket's takeovers of Silph Co. and the Celadon hideout (games only), and places in the Top 16 at the Indigo Plateau Conference, losing to Richie. Movie: *Mewtwo Strikes Back*. Separate game-only retelling at this same point: Let's Go, Pikachu!/Let's Go, Eevee!, and the Sevii Islands arc of FireRed/LeafGreen.
- **Year 2**: Ash collects four Orange Islands badges and defeats Drake to win the Orange League. Movie: *The Power of One*. (Unrelated storyline: Sada/Turo begin Project Tera and invent Tera Orbs, ten years before Year 12.)
- **Year 3**: Ash earns eight Johto badges and places in the Top 8 at the Silver Conference, losing to Harrison. Movies: *Spell of the Unown*, *Celebi: Voice of the Forest*, *Latios and Latias*. The games state Johto's story happens three years after Kanto's, which would place it in Year 4 instead.
- **Year 4**: Ash earns eight Hoenn badges and places in the Top 8 at the Ever Grande Conference, losing to Tyson, who goes on to win. Movies: *Jirachi: Wish Maker*, *Destiny Deoxys*, *Lucario and the Mystery of Mew*. The games state Hoenn's story happens at the same time as Kanto's, which would place it in Year 1 instead.
- **Year 5**: Ash defeats all seven Frontier Brains at the Kanto Battle Frontier. Movie: *Pokémon Ranger and the Temple of the Sea*.
- **Year 6**: Ash earns eight Sinnoh badges and places in the Top 4 at the Lily of the Valley Conference, losing to Tobias. Movies: *The Rise of Darkrai*, *Giratina and the Sky Warrior*, *Arceus and the Jewel of Life*, *Zoroark: Master of Illusions*.
- **Year 7**: Ash earns eight Unova badges and places in the Top 8 at the Vertress Conference, losing to Cameron; Virgil wins the tournament. Further adventures follow in the Decolora Archipelago. Movies: *Black: Victini and Reshiram*/*White: Victini and Zekrom*, *Kyurem vs. the Sword of Justice*, *Genesect and the Legend Awakened*. (Unrelated storyline: Black 2/White 2's events take place around Year 9 instead, two years after Year 7.)
- **Year 8**: Ash travels Kalos, clashes with Team Flare, bonds with a Greninja through the Bond Phenomenon, and reaches the final of the Lumiose Conference, losing to Alain and becoming runner-up. Movies: *Diancie and the Cocoon of Destruction*, *Hoopa and the Clash of Ages*, *Volcanion and the Mechanical Marvel*.
- **Year 9**: Ash enrolls at the Pokémon School in Alola, encounters Ultra Beasts and Necrozma, and wins the Manalo Conference, defeating Gladion in the final to become the first Champion of Alola, his first outright League Championship. (Unrelated storyline: the Black 2/White 2 story takes place in Unova around this same year.)
- **Year 10**: Ash travels every region at once with Goh, competing in the World Coronation Series. He reaches the Masters Eight and defeats Leon to become World Champion. Galar's Sword/Shield story, including Chairman Rose's plot to force a second Darkest Day, is treated as happening around this point, along with its Isle of Armor and Crown Tundra expansions.
- **Year 11**: Ash and Pikachu depart together on a new journey, closing out Ash's personal story.
- **Year 12**: A new story begins with Liko and Roy at the Paldea-region Academy, unconnected to Ash. Scarlet/Violet's story, including Team Star, Arven, and Professor Sada/Turo's scheme, is treated as happening around this point, along with its Teal Mask and Indigo Disk expansions.
- **Year 13**: AZ opens Hotel Z in Lumiose City, five years after the Kalos story. Legends: Z-A is set here: Quasartico Inc.'s redevelopment of Lumiose City is disrupted by Rogue Mega Evolution, traced to Ange, a device AZ built roughly 2,000 years earlier, destabilized by Team Flare's prior misuse of the ultimate weapon.

## Not Part of This Spine
- *Mewtwo Strikes Back: Evolution* is a full remake of the Year 1 film and does not add a new dated event.
- The *I Choose You!* trilogy (*I Choose You!*, *The Power of Us*, *Secrets of the Jungle*) and its companion special *The Distant Blue Sky* form a separate, self-contained continuity and are not dated against this timeline at all.

## Notes on This Timeline
- Kanto and Hoenn's core game stories are officially stated to occur at the same point in time. This timeline separates them into Year 1 and Year 4 to match the order Ash actually visited them in the anime. Treat Year 4 as equivalent to Year 1 if the official game statement is preferred instead.
- Johto's core game story is officially stated to occur three years after Kanto's, which would place it in Year 4 rather than the Year 3 used here. Year 3 was used to match the anime's travel order.
- Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar, and Paldea have no officially stated numeric offset from Kanto. Their placement here, one year per region in anime travel order, is an estimate for the sake of a single continuous timeline, not an official figure.
- Years marked with a specific whole number come directly from stated in-game "X years before the present" references. Years marked "upper bound" or "or earlier" come from looser or partial in-game clues and could sit closer to the present than shown.
- Movie placements follow which region's traveling companions appear in each film, per Bulbapedia-documented conventions, not an official release-based chronology.
