# Pokémon Movies: Detailed Plot Summaries

Listed in release order. The films sharing a region's traveling companions occur during that region's anime story; see the anime timeline file for placement. Four films at the end sit in a separate, self-contained continuity and are not part of Ash's main personal history.

1. **Mewtwo Strikes Back:** Team Rocket scientists clone Mew to create Mewtwo in a lab on Cinnabar Island. Mewtwo turns on its creators, then lures trainers, including Ash, to New Island to pit human-raised Pokémon against its own clones. Ash is turned to stone shielding Pikachu during a clash between the clones and their originals; the combined tears of every Pokémon present revive him. Moved by this display of unity, Mewtwo erases the witnesses' memories of the event and departs to search for its own purpose.

2. **The Power of One (Pokémon the Movie 2000):** The collector Lawrence III steals the elemental orbs of the Legendary birds Zapdos, Articuno, and Moltres to draw out Lugia, throwing the world's weather into chaos. At the Shamouti Islands' shrine, Ash fulfills a local prophecy of a "Chosen One" who restores balance between the birds and Lugia.

3. **Spell of the Unown (Pokémon 3):** Grieving scientist Spencer Hale unknowingly uses Unown symbols to create a pocket dimension and an Entei-formed father figure for his daughter Molly, who has been drawn into the dimension along with Ash's mother Delia. Ash and his friends enter the Unown's crystal tower to rescue them and calm Entei.

4. **Celebi: Voice of the Forest (Pokémon 4Ever):** The time-traveling Mythical Pokémon Celebi and a boy from forty years in the past, later revealed to be a young Professor Oak, are hunted by the Iron-Masked Marauder, who wants to steal Celebi's power to force-evolve his own Pokémon. Ash and friends help Celebi heal and return safely through time.

5. **Latios and Latias (Pokémon Heroes):** In the canal city of Alto Mare, the DNA thieves Annie and Oakley pursue the Soul Dew and the twin Eon Pokémon Latios and Latias to power an ancient defense mechanism guarding the city. Latios sacrifices itself to save Alto Mare from the resulting flood.

6. **Jirachi: Wish Maker:** Once every thousand years, the Wish Pokémon Jirachi wakes for seven days during the passage of the Millennium Comet. The magician Butler, a disgraced former Team Magma scientist, exploits Jirachi's power to try reviving Groudon through cloning, unintentionally creating a rampaging "Meta Groudon." Ash's friend Max, whom Jirachi had bonded with, helps stop Butler and return Jirachi safely to its slumber.

7. **Destiny Deoxys:** A Deoxys crash-lands in LaRousse City and, mistaking the artificial Pokémon Rayquaza-detection defense system for a threat, clashes with the city's guardian Rayquaza. Ash and friends, alongside a boy who befriends the Deoxys, work to end the destructive standoff between the two.

8. **Lucario and the Mystery of Mew:** A Lucario, sealed in its Aura-reading staff for a thousand years after a past misunderstanding involving its trainer Sir Aaron, is freed and joins Ash's group at the ancient Tree of Beginning, guardian home of Mew, where a treasure hunter's excavation threatens the tree's life force.

9. **Pokémon Ranger and the Temple of the Sea:** A Pokémon Ranger and Ash's group protect the Sea Temple and the Mythical Pokémon Manaphy's egg from the pirate Phantom, who seeks the temple's treasure, the Sea Crown.

10. **The Rise of Darkrai:** In the town of Alamos, built between two dimensions, a rift opens allowing the reality-warping Palkia and Dialga to fight, threatening to destroy the town's foundational tree. The Mythical Darkrai, long feared as a menace for the nightmares its presence causes, is revealed to be protecting the town, and works with Ash to separate the two combatants.

11. **Giratina and the Sky Warrior:** The scientist Zero seeks to force Giratina out of the Reverse World (the Distortion World) to power a machine capable of altering reality, using a captured Shaymin as a component. Ash and friends help Shaymin and the Legendary Pokémon Giratina return the situation to balance.

12. **Arceus and the Jewel of Life:** Centuries ago, a human stole the Jewel of Life from Arceus, believing it had been betrayed; enraged, Arceus vowed to return and destroy humanity. Ash and friends travel to the past using Dialga, Palkia, and Celebi's combined power to return the recovered Jewel of Life to Arceus and avert its wrath in the present.

13. **Zoroark: Master of Illusions:** In Crown City, the illusion Pokémon Zoroark disguises itself to protect its injured partner Zorua, clashing with a television producer who wants to capture three Legendary beasts, Raikou, Entei, and Suicune, for a broadcast stunt.

14. **Black: Victini and Reshiram / White: Victini and Zekrom:** In the kingdom of Eindoak, the Mythical Pokémon Victini and its human friend Damon try to stop the descendant of an ancient king from draining the town dry to restore it, a conflict that draws in Reshiram or Zekrom depending on the film's version.

15. **Kyurem vs. the Sword of Justice:** Keldeo, training to join the Swords of Justice (Cobalion, Terrakion, and Virizion), recklessly challenges the powerful Kyurem before it is ready and is badly hurt. Ash and friends help Keldeo rescue its mentors from Kyurem's icy rampage, in which the dragon draws on Reshiram's or Zekrom's power to transform into Black or White Kyurem.

16. **Genesect and the Legend Awakened:** A group of five Genesect, artificially enhanced Pokémon displaced 300 million years from their original era, invade a modern city looking for a home, threatening its power supply. Mewtwo, sympathetic to their plight as a fellow human-made Pokémon, tries to reason with their leader, the Red Genesect, before the standoff turns violent.

17. **Diancie and the Cocoon of Destruction:** In the underground Diamond Domain, the Mythical Pokémon Diancie, its ruler, cannot yet create a new Heart Diamond to sustain the land. Thieves seeking Diancie's diamond-creating power accidentally awaken the Legendary Pokémon Yveltal from its cocoon; Ash and friends help Diancie find its true power, obtained by seeking out Xerneas, and stop Yveltal's rampage.

18. **Hoopa and the Clash of Ages:** The Mythical Pokémon Hoopa, capable of summoning anything through its magic rings, has bottled up a darker, more powerful side of itself for years after a past rampage. When that side threatens to break free again, Ash helps Hoopa confront it before the rivalry erupts into open conflict.

19. **Volcanion and the Mechanical Marvel:** The Mythical Pokémon Volcanion, who distrusts humans, is magically bound to Ash after crash-landing near him. Forced to cooperate, the two travel to a city of gears and cogs to rescue the artificial Pokémon Magearna, created 500 years earlier, from a corrupt minister who has stolen it.

20. **Mewtwo Strikes Back: Evolution:** A fully computer-animated remake of the first film, retelling the same core story of Mewtwo's creation, rebellion, and confrontation with Ash on New Island with updated animation and some restructured scenes.

## The I Choose You! Trilogy (Separate Continuity)
This set of films retells Ash's story from the beginning in an alternate version of events, sharing no direct continuity with the mainline anime or with each other's finer details beyond the core cast.

- **I Choose You!:** A retelling of Ash's first meeting with Pikachu and early Kanto journey, reworked around a quest involving the Legendary Ho-Oh and a mysterious, powerful Pokémon called Marshadow.
- **The Power of Us:** Ash and a group of trainers in the city of Fula must work together, overcoming personal grudges, to stop a Legendary bird's rampage from destroying the town during a festival.
- **Secrets of the Jungle:** Koko, a human boy raised since infancy by a lone Zarude in the Forest of Okoya, meets Ash and Pikachu and begins to question whether he is truly a Pokémon, as the jungle and its Zarude troop come under threat.

A related television special, **The Distant Blue Sky**, also belongs to this same separate continuity rather than the mainline anime.
