# Pokémon Master Timeline

Single chronology for this project. **Year 1 = Ash Ketchum leaves Pallet Town.**

Writer notes: `pokemon-timeline-notes.md`.

Plot detail (not dating):
- `pokemon-anime-detailed.md`
- `pokemon-games-detailed.md`
- `pokemon-movies-detailed.md`
- `pokemon-dated-timeline-detailed.md`

This file answers “when does this happen?” Sources are merged into one spine. Placement follows Ash’s regional travel order for the present day. Ancient “X years before present” figures are converted against that region’s present year on this spine. Remakes and expansion stories share their base year’s slot.

**Dating choices locked on this spine:**
- Kanto and Hoenn are not simultaneous: Year 1 and Year 4.
- Johto is Year 3 (not the games’ +3 → Year 4).
- Black 2 / White 2 sit near Year 9 (two years after Unova Year 7).
- Z-A is Year 13 (five years after Kalos Year 8).
- Sinnoh through Paldea use one year per region where no official offset exists.
- **Story 1 / Rancher League calendar** (authority: `Stories/Story 1/Story 1 References/Dates.md`): **League 298 = Year 2** on this spine. Convert with **Master Year = League year − 296**. Story 1 beat file: `Stories/Story 1/Story 1 References/Timeline.md`. Protagonist **D** (source D); ranch near Porto Marinada.

**Not on this spine:** *I Choose You!* trilogy, *The Distant Blue Sky*, and *Mewtwo Strikes Back: Evolution* (remake of Year 1’s New Island events; no new date).

## Ancient History

- **Year −1,999,988 (Paldea):** Great Crater Underdepths form. A Terapagos survives near-extinction by crystallizing itself.
- **Year −19,990 (Galar):** Meteorite carrying Eternatus falls in Galar.
- **Year −2,992 (Kalos):** Kalos war. AZ’s Floette dies; he builds a revival machine, then turns it into the ultimate weapon to end the war.
- **Year −2,990 (Galar):** Darkest Day. Zacian, Zamazenta, and two ancient Galarian kings defeat Eternatus.
- **Year −2,493 or earlier (Unova):** Twin brothers found Unova with a single dragon; their falling-out splits it into Reshiram and Zekrom. Later conflict produces Kyurem.
- **Year −1,996 (Hoenn):** Meteor showers create Meteor Falls. Groudon and Kyogre awaken; Draconids summon Rayquaza.
- **Year −1,988 (Paldea):** Paldean Empire begins. Area Zero treasure rumors start the Great Age of Exploration.
- **Year −996 (Hoenn):** Second meteor creates Sootopolis crater. Rayquaza Mega Evolves for the first time; Sky Pillar built; prophecy of the meteor’s return in 1,000 years.
- **Year −988 (Paldea):** Paldean Empire declines.
- **Year −793 (Paldea):** Paldea-region Academy founded.
- **Year −294 (upper bound) → Year 6 (Hisui / Sinnoh):** Hisui era window. Volo manipulates a traveler to summon Giratina and collect Arceus’s Plates; Volo, Giratina, then Arceus are defeated. Ends no later than Sinnoh’s present (Year 6).
- **Year −188 (Paldea):** Heath’s Area Zero Expedition reaches the crater floor and encounters Terapagos. (Game-derived “~200 years before” Year 12.)
- **Year −194 / League 102-07-08 (Paldea, Story 1):** Heath’s journal while exploring the Paldea pit (`Dates.md` Ch232). Same expedition on the League bridge (League 298 = Year 2 → League 102 = Year −194).
- **Year −48 (Paldea):** Terastal phenomenon discovered in Area Zero.
- **Year −40 (Galar):** Mustard defeats Opal, becomes Champion; later founds the Isle of Armor Master Dojo.
- **Year −36 (Hoenn):** Lunatone discovered at a meteor strike site.

## Present Day

### Year 1 — Kanto

Travel order for Ash’s path follows the Indigo League journey. Game-route Rocket and Gym beats are folded into the same year where geography matches. Anime Gym order puts Saffron (Marsh) before Celadon (Rainbow); the core games put Celadon and the Rocket Hideout before Silph Co. and Saffron Gym. Both are kept below in the order Ash’s year actually runs, with the game Celadon → Silph → Saffron block noted where it sits on the map.

#### Mewtwo’s origin (Cinnabar / Team Rocket)
- Dr. Fuji, funded by Giovanni, clones Mew from a fossilized eyelash after a Guyana expedition. Mewtwo wakes in the Cinnabar lab, destroys the facility and the scientists, and is later armored and used by Team Rocket.
- Over the following weeks Mewtwo does Rocket dirty work and beats challengers at Viridian Gym, including Gary Oak. When Giovanni says it exists only to obey humans, Mewtwo blasts free of its armor, wrecks the HQ, and leaves to prepare New Island.

#### Opening — Pallet → Viridian Forest
- Ash (10) overslepts Oak’s starter handout; only Pikachu remains. Pikachu is disobedient; Spearow attack; rain; Pikachu trusts Ash; Ho-Oh is glimpsed in the clearing sky.
- Viridian Pokémon Center: first major Team Rocket strike (Jessie, James, Meowth). The trio begins the recurring chase for Pikachu.
- Ash’s first catches: Caterpie → Metapod → Butterfree; Pidgeotto. Samurai challenge in the forest.

#### Pewter → Cerulean
- Pewter Gym: Ash loses to Brock, then wins on rematch (Onix vs Pikachu with water/sprinklers). Boulder Badge. Brock leaves the Gym to travel as a breeder; joins Ash.
- Mt. Moon: Clefairy, Moon Stone, Team Rocket fossil/thievery beat (matches early Rocket presence on the game route).
- Cerulean Gym: Cascade Badge from Misty (sisters run the Gym; Misty battles). Misty joins the party for the road.
- Bill’s lighthouse / Seaside cottage threads; early rival brushes with Gary.

#### Vermilion and the S.S. Anne
- Hidden Village: Ash receives Bulbasaur.
- Charmander abandoned by Damian; Ash takes it in.
- Squirtle Squad; Ash’s Squirtle joins.
- Vermilion Gym: loss to Lt. Surge’s Raichu, then rematch; Pikachu grounds Thunderbolt with its tail. Thunder Badge.
- S.S. Anne cruise: Magikarp salesman, then Team Rocket’s Battle Aboard / shipwreck; the ship sinks; the group washes ashore. (Game parallel: S.S. Anne Cut HM, then Vermilion Gym.)
- Ash catches a Krabby (sent to Oak). Temporary Butterfree trade for Raticate, reversed. Butterfree released to mate during migration.
- Misty’s Horsea (later given away before Cinnabar stretch).

#### Lavender → Saffron (Marsh, anime order)
- Lavender Tower of Terror: ghosts; Ash befriends a Haunter.
- Saffron Gym first try fails. Haunter returns; Sabrina’s psychic hold breaks through comedy, not a straight win. Marsh Badge. Haunter stays with Sabrina.
- Game map alternate for this city block (same year): Celadon Rocket Hideout and Silph Co. must be cleared before Saffron Gym opens; Marsh Badge then comes after Silph (see Celadon / Saffron game block below).

#### Celadon, Fuchsia, and mid-journey team
- Celadon Gym: Team Rocket bomb/fire; Ash saves Erika’s Gloom; Rainbow Badge awarded. Brock’s Vulpix (from Suzy / later returned in continuity) enters the party around this stretch.
- Ash’s Primeape (from a fighting dojo beat) is left with Anthony for P1 Grand Prix training.
- Ash catches Muk (left at Oak’s). Jessie’s Ekans → Arbok; James’s Koffing → Weezing.
- Fuchsia Gym: ninja showdown with Koga; Misty’s Psyduck interrupts Rocket interference; Soul Badge. Misty obtains Psyduck (stuck on her head beat).
- Safari Zone: Ash catches 30 Tauros (sent to Oak). Failed attempt to release Pikachu.
- Charmander → Charmeleon (Exeggutor march). Jigglypuff begins following the group. Fossil Pokémon museum/extinct five glimpse. Charmeleon → Charizard (disobedient thereafter for a long stretch).
- Togepi egg; hatching tournament; Togepi imprints on Misty. Bulbasaur refuses evolution. Jessie’s Lickitung. James’s Weepinbell → Victreebel.
- Game Celadon / Saffron block (same year, map order): Game Corner poster → Rocket Hideout under Celadon; Giovanni defeated; Silph Scope recovered → Pokémon Tower freed → Silph Co. takeover in Saffron broken; Giovanni faced again; Lapras gift; Master Ball from the President → Saffron Gym / Sabrina for Marsh Badge if not already taken on the anime path.

#### Cinnabar → Viridian (eighth badge)
- Cinnabar Gym: Blaine; Ash wins Volcano Badge on rematch with Charizard. Pokémon Mansion records document Mewtwo’s creation. (Game: Mansion Key, then Gym.)
- First Sevii access can open after Volcano Badge (Tri-Pass / One–Three Island ferry), before League, for early island travel.
- Viridian Gym: Giovanni leaves; Jessie, James, and Meowth run it briefly; Ash’s side wins the Earth Badge after the Rocket farce collapses. Delia obtains Mr. Mime (Mimey).
- Movie prelude overlap: Mewtwo still serving Giovanni, crushing Gym challengers (including Gary), then rebelling and destroying the armored lab/HQ (see origin above). Chronology places New Island after all eight badges and the Po-ké Corral reunion stretch.

#### New Island (Mewtwo Strikes Back)
- After eight badges / around Showdown at the Po-ké Corral: Dragonite messenger invites top trainers to New Island.
- Storm closes the ferry; Ash’s group reaches the fortress anyway. Mewtwo (posing through a Nurse Joy clone / hologram lure) stages clone-vs-original battles.
- Charizard, and others’ Venusaur/Blastoise, fall to clones and are sealed in Mewtwo Balls; cloning machine runs. Ash wrecks the machine saving Pikachu.
- Mew arrives. Clone army vs originals; special moves nullified; melee. Ash shields Pikachu from Mewtwo/Mew clash, turns to stone; Pokémon tears revive him.
- Mewtwo accepts that birth does not decide destiny, leaves with the clones for a place of peace, and erases witnesses’ memories. The group wakes at a Pokémon Center with no memory; Ash glimpses Mew in clearing clouds. Team Rocket stranded on a greened New Island for a “vacation.”
- *Mewtwo Strikes Back: Evolution* is the same New Island day retold; no second date.

- Ash reaches the Top 16 and loses to Richie (Sparky the Pikachu; mirrored team feel). Friendship and rivalry both land hard; Gary underperforms relative to expectation this tournament.
- Friends to the End wrap; Ash returns toward Pallet. Pidgeotto → Pidgeot; Ash leaves Pidgeot to guard a Pidgey flock from Fearow (Pallet Party Panic), then looks toward the Orange Islands.

#### Champion path (same year, Indigo Plateau)

- Champion: the rival (Blue / Gary’s game counterpart) is defeated; Hall of Fame. Giovanni’s mainland Rocket leadership is broken for this era.
- Cerulean Cave / Mewtwo can be faced in the wild post-Hall of Fame (separate from New Island memory-wipe; wild Mewtwo here is the roaming powerhouse of the caves).

#### Sevii Islands, Deoxys, Meltan (late Year 1)

- Icefall Cave: Lorelei vs Rockets; Warehouse passwords. Dotted Hole on Six Island: Sapphire stolen by Scientist Gideon. Five Island Rocket Warehouse: Admins defeated; they stand down on learning Giovanni already fell; Gideon returns the Sapphire. Sevii Rocket evolution research collapses (seed for later Johto remnant plots).
- Birth Island: Deoxys tied to the meteorite / triangle puzzle (Attack or Defense forme by version). Navel Rock: Lugia and Ho-Oh accessible with the matching ticket.
- Let’s Go–era Kanto retelling occupies this same year: partner Pikachu or Eevee path, same Rocket/Gym/Champion spine, GO Park transfers in Fuchsia; Mythical Meltan appears in the Kanto record and can become Melmetal.

### Year 10 - Galar and the world championship (+ Story 1 League 306)

Two big stories happen in the same year.

In the region of Galar, a new trainer takes the official Gym Challenge: travel the country, defeat eight Gym Leaders in stadium matches, and try to become Champion of Galar by beating Leon, who has never lost that title. Along the way the company Macro Cosmos and its chairman, Rose, cause a disaster by waking a legendary Pokémon named Eternatus. That disaster is a second "Darkest Day," named after a catastrophe from three thousand years earlier when Eternatus first ran wild and the legendary wolves Zacian and Zamazenta stopped it.

In the same year, Ash Ketchum leaves regional leagues behind and enters a worldwide ranking tournament called the World Coronation Series. He works from Professor Cerise's laboratory in Vermilion City with a new friend, Goh, who wants to meet the Mythical Pokémon Mew and to catch as many kinds of Pokémon as he can. Ash's ranking climb and Galar's crisis meet in the same places and the same months. They are one timeline, not two separate calendars.

#### A new trainer starts in Galar
A child in the town of Postwick sets out with a first partner Pokémon (Grookey, Scorbunny, or Sobble) and a rival named Hop, who is Leon's younger brother. In the foggy Slumbering Weald a legendary wolf Pokémon briefly protects them. At the Wedgehurst lab, Professor Magnolia and her granddaughter Sonia give the trainers Dynamax Bands. Dynamax is Galar's special battle spectacle: for a short time a Pokémon grows enormous in a stadium powered by Galar's energy grid.

The trainers go to Motostoke for the opening of the Gym Challenge. Rose and Leon appear in public. Once endorsed, the challenger may travel the Wild Area and the Gym towns.

#### The eight Gyms
The challenger earns eight badges by defeating:
1. Milo in Turffield (Grass-type).
2. Nessa in Hulbury (Water-type). Here Rose, his aide Oleana, and a boy named Bede (an orphan Rose sponsors) appear in the story.
3. Kabu in Motostoke (Fire-type).
4. Bea in Stow-on-Side if this is the Sword version of events, or Allister if this is the Shield version (Fighting-type or Ghost-type). Old murals in town show two heroes and a dark disaster. Sonia studies them as clues to the first Darkest Day.
5. Opal in Ballonlea (Fairy-type), reached through the glowing forest Glimwood Tangle. Bede later fails Opal's standards. Rose drops him. Opal takes Bede on as the next Fairy Gym Leader.
6. Gordie in Circhester (Rock-type) or Melony (Ice-type), again depending on version.
7. Piers in Spikemuth (Dark-type). Spikemuth is not on the Dynamax power grid. Marnie, Piers's sister, and her fans in Team Yell clear interference so the match can happen.
8. Raihan in Hammerlocke (Dragon-type), the last badge.

By then Sonia's research says the old "two human heroes" story is wrong: the heroes were two Pokémon, and a third monstrous Pokémon caused the darkness.

#### Rose wakes Eternatus
In the capital, Wyndon, the Champion Cup begins. The new challenger beats Hop in the semifinals. Inside Rose's tower, Oleana and Macro Cosmos staff try to block the way.

Before the title match against Leon can finish as planned, Rose announces on broadcast that he will deliberately cause a second Darkest Day. He claims Galar will run out of energy a thousand years in the future, and that only a huge store of power now can prevent it. He has been feeding Wishing Stars to Eternatus under Hammerlocke's energy plant to force it awake.

Leon goes to stop him. The challenger and Hop return to the Slumbering Weald and take a rusted sword and rusted shield. At the plant they defeat Rose. On the roof Leon fails to catch Eternatus. Eternatus enters a more extreme form. Zacian and Zamazenta answer the rusted arms. Together with the trainers they defeat Eternatus in a raid battle and it is caught. Pokémon that had Dynamaxed out of control across Galar return to normal.

Three days later the championship match is held. The challenger defeats Leon and becomes the new Champion of Galar. Leon's long unbeaten run as regional Champion ends.

Afterward, two aristocrats named Sordward and Shielbert steal relics tied to the wolf heroes, force more Dynamax incidents at Gym stadiums, and cause trouble until the true partner wolf can be caught and Sonia's corrected history is secured for a museum.

#### The Isle of Armor
Off Galar's coast, the Isle of Armor holds the Master Dojo run by Mustard (a former Champion who once taught Leon) and his wife Honey. A rival student named Klara or Avery competes for the dojo's "secret armor." After three trials (recovering uniforms from Slowpoke, gathering Max Mushrooms for a soup that unlocks Gigantamax, and a Dynamax court battle), the winner receives Kubfu, a rare Fighting-type. Training and friendship lead to one of two towers. Clearing the Tower of Darkness evolves Kubfu into Single Strike Style Urshifu. Clearing the Tower of Waters evolves it into Rapid Strike Style Urshifu. Mustard tests the student at the top of the chosen tower.

#### The Crown Tundra
In the cold Crown Tundra, Peony leads an expedition with his daughter. Trainers enter Max Lair for Dynamax Adventures: temporary teams against powerful legendary Pokémon, including Galarian forms of Articuno, Zapdos, and Moltres, and the Regi constructs.

In the village of Freezington a statue of the "King of Bountiful Harvests" is repaired. That king is the legendary Pokémon Calyrex. It has grown weak without its horse companion. Planting special carrot seeds at one of two sites draws either the Ice-type horse Glastrier or the Ghost-type horse Spectrier. With a reconstructed Reins of Unity, Calyrex bonds with the horse again at the Crown Shrine, recovers its full power, battles the trainer, and allows itself to be caught.

#### Ash, Goh, and the World Coronation Series
Ash meets Goh during an encounter with Lugia. Professor Cerise hires both to help research Pokémon while based at his laboratory. Chrysa and Ren work there. Cerise's daughter Chloe begins training with an Eevee. Jessie, James, and Meowth still chase Ash's Pikachu and sometimes act with more planning than before.

The World Coronation Series ranks trainers worldwide. Classes rise from Normal to Great to Ultra to Master. Ash climbs by winning ranked battles in many regions. Important wins include matches against Iris, Volkner, Bea, and finally Raihan, which places Ash among the eight highest-ranked trainers.

Those eight are Leon (still treated as the world's top trainer and Galar's star), Cynthia of Sinnoh, Steven of Hoenn, Lance of Kanto and Johto, Diantha of Kalos, Alain of Kalos, Iris of Unova, and Ash (already Champion of Alola from Year 9). They meet in a tournament. Ash reaches the final against Leon in Wyndon.

#### The same Darkest Day, seen with Ash and Goh
When Rose wakes Eternatus, Ash and Goh are part of the fight in Galar. Leon again fails to catch it. With Zacian and Zamazenta's help, Ash and Goh finish the battle. Goh's Poké Ball catches Eternatus. Professor Magnolia locks it away for safety. Leon later becomes the person trusted to watch over it for Galar. This is the same crisis as the Gym Challenge climax, not a second apocalypse in a later year.

In the same travel year, Ash and Goh also brush a return of Team Galactic and Cyrus in Sinnoh. It is a shorter crisis than Year 6, not a full repeat of that story.

#### Project Mew
A separate research effort led by Professor Amaranth recruits trainers to help find Mew, hoping to learn about the origins of Pokémon. Senior members called Chasers, including Danika and Quillon, give trial missions to applicants called Challengers. Goh joins. Gary Oak and a trainer named Horace compete seriously too. The three best Challengers earn Chaser status. An expedition reaches a remote island in the short season when the mountain can be searched. Goh meets Mew. Mew is not turned into an ordinary caught pet of the series; the meeting is the research peak of Goh's year.

#### Ash becomes World Champion
In the Wyndon final, Ash and Leon battle with full teams. They agree to allow Mega Evolution, Z-Moves, and Dynamax or Gigantamax. Mid-battle their strongest attacks disturb Galar's energy particles so badly that a storm forms. Eternatus arrives above the stadium, fires its Dynamax Cannon into the sky, calms the particles, and restores both trainers' Dynamax Bands, then leaves. The match continues. Pikachu eventually defeats Leon's Charizard. Ash becomes World Champion.

Galar now has a regional Champion from the Gym Challenge path. Ash holds the separate world title won in the Coronation Series. Training on the Isle of Armor and exploration in the Crown Tundra still belong to this year. Ash and Pikachu's decision to leave on a new private journey begins as Year 11 opens.

- **Story 1 (League 306 = 9 years after League 298 / Year 2):** Sada and Turo death framing (`Dates.md` Ch675). Paradox Pokémon attack news **306-09-17**. Area Zero station scene intentionally vague. Kitsu pregnant again in November.

### Year 12 - Paldea (+ Story 1 League 308)

Ash's story is over. Year 12 follows new people in the region of Paldea and does not continue Ash's travelogue.

Paldea's big public school is Naranja Academy or Uva Academy (the same institution under different school colors). Students are sent out on a long independent study. In practice that study has three jobs that can be done in any order, then a fourth crisis that only opens when all three are done.

About ten years before this year (Year 2 / **League 298** on the Story 1 bridge), Professors Sada and Turo began Project Tera and invented Tera Orbs, the tools that let a Pokémon Terastallize (change its crystal type in battle). By Year 12 the living professors are already dead (**League 306** / Year 10 on that bridge). What remains in the Great Crater of Paldea is their time machine and an artificial copy of one professor's mind.

**Story 1 (League 308):** Year stamp; mid-March Unova → Paldea (Ch683). Ranch plot is still running while the academy / Area Zero game year opens.

A second story in the same year follows Liko and Roy, children who join an airship research crew called the Rising Volt Tacklers and are hunted by a criminal group called the Explorers. That story also centers on Paldea, on Terastallization, and on a legendary Pokémon named Terapagos. It is not about Ash.

Where Scarlet and Violet disagree only on whether Area Zero's strange Pokémon come from the ancient past or the far future, this spine allows one combined world (both professors in the record) or parallel versions. The plot steps are the same either way.

#### A student leaves home
A child leaves the seaside house at Cabo Poco with a parent, a neighbor rival named Nemona, and a first partner (Sprigatito, Fuecoco, or Quaxly). On the path they find a weakened legendary ride Pokémon: Koraidon or Miraidon. It is nursed at a lighthouse. Later it can be caught and used to travel.

They reach the school city Mesagoza, meet Director Clavell, and begin school life. Nemona pushes the student to challenge Gyms. A boy named Arven asks for help: his partner Mabosstiff is badly ill, and he believes giant "Titan" Pokémon guard herbal medicines that can heal it. A masked contact calling itself Cassiopeia asks the student to shut down Team Star, a gang of students who built bases across Paldea after being bullied.

Clavell's independent study therefore means:
1. Defeat the eight Gym Leaders, then the Elite Four and Top Champion, to finish the League path.
2. Help Arven defeat the five Titans and gather the herbs.
3. Defeat the five Team Star bosses.
When those three are finished, the crater called Area Zero can be entered.

4. Kofu in Cascarrafa (Water-type).
5. Larry in Medali (Normal-type).
6. Ryme in Montenevera (Ghost-type).
7. Tulip in Alfornada (Psychic-type).
8. Grusha on Glaseado Mountain (Ice-type).

Nemona battles the student several times as both grow stronger. With eight badges the student challenges the Pokémon League. The Elite Four are Rika (Ground), Poppy (Steel), Larry again in a Flying-type exhibition, and Hassel (Dragon). The Top Champion is Geeta. Afterward Nemona demands a full champion-level rematch in Mesagoza and Terastallizes in that fight. The student stands as her equal.

#### Arven and the Titans
The Titans are ordinary species grown huge and aggressive around special herbs:
1. Klawf on a southern cliff.
2. Bombirdier in the west.
3. Orthworm in the east.
4. Great Tusk or Iron Treads in the Asado Desert (the first clear meeting with a "paradox" Pokémon: a creature pulled from another time).
5. Dondozo and Tatsugiri at Casseroya Lake.

Each herb helps Mabosstiff recover and teaches the ride legendary a new way to travel (sprint, swim, leap, glide, climb). The path ends at the lighthouse laboratory with a hard battle against Arven. Mabosstiff is healed. Arven has to face the empty place his parent left behind.

#### Team Star
Team Star's bosses are Giacomo (Dark), Mela (Fire), Atticus (Poison), Ortega (Fairy), and Eri (Fighting). A man calling himself Clive helps from the sidelines; he is Director Clavell in disguise. The final confrontation at the academy reveals that Cassiopeia is a shy student named Penny who built Team Star's systems after she and others were bullied. The bases fall. Clavell begins reforming how the school treats those students.

#### Area Zero
With all three paths done, a message that seems to come from Arven's parent calls the group to Area Zero, the forbidden Great Crater. The student, Arven, Nemona, and Penny descend on Koraidon or Miraidon.

Four research stations lead downward. The crater is full of paradox Pokémon. At the bottom laboratory the "professor" admits to being an artificial intelligence copy. The real Sada or Turo died years earlier while protecting the tame ride legendary from a second, violent one of the same kind.

The artificial professor wants the time machine stopped because endless paradox Pokémon would ruin Paldea's ecology, but its programming also forces it to defend the machine. The student defeats its paradox team. Then an emergency program seizes the artificial body, locks every Poké Ball that does not carry the professor's mark, and sends the violent ride legendary to kill the intruders. The tame Koraidon or Miraidon is still able to fight. It Terastallizes and wins.

The artificial professor uses the time machine one last time to leave the present and shut the machine down from another era. The machine dies. The tame legendary stays with the student. The four friends climb home.

#### The school trip to Kitakami
The academy sends students to the land of Kitakami, including siblings Carmine and Kieran. Local festival stories say three Pokémon called the Loyal Three once drove away a dangerous ogre. The "ogre" is Ogerpon. The truth, told by Carmine's grandfather, is the opposite: Ogerpon and a human friend were hated for looking strange, and the Loyal Three (with a Pokémon named Pecharunt behind them) were the attackers. Masks and battles restore Ogerpon's name. Ogerpon chooses the visiting student as its partner. Kieran wanted that bond himself and leaves angry.

#### Blueberry Academy and the deeper crater
The student transfers for a time to Blueberry Academy, a school under a dome on the sea with indoor copies of many climates. Its strongest students form a league. Kieran has become champion there and fights with open resentment. The visitor tops that league. Kieran collapses emotionally.

Researcher Briar, who studies Area Zero and descends from the old explorer Heath, wins permission to go deeper into the crater than before. League officials support the trip. A key called the Indigo Disk reopens an elevator under the old laboratory into the Area Zero Underdepths.

Notes left by Sada and Turo identify Terapagos as the true "hidden treasure" of the crater and the living source of Terastal energy. Terapagos wakes. Kieran catches it in a Master Ball and forces a battle, then Terastallizes it toward a stronger form when Briar says its power is too low. Terapagos goes berserk and breaks free. The student and Carmine fight it; Kieran joins them to undo the harm; the student catches Terapagos. Briar apologizes for pushing too hard. Kieran begins to change.

#### Pecharunt's leftover scheme
Back in Kitakami, Pecharunt uses binding mochi to control people and Pokémon, including the Loyal Three again. The student and friends break the control and end Pecharunt's plan.

#### Liko, Roy, and the airship crew
Separately, a Paldean girl named Liko studies at Indigo Academy in Kanto and receives a Sprigatito. A criminal group, the Explorers, tries to steal a pendant her grandmother Diana gave her. A research crew on the airship Brave Olivine, the Rising Volt Tacklers, led by Friede with his partner Captain Pikachu, rescues her. Crew members include Murdock, Mollie, Orla, and Dot.

On an island, a boy named Roy finds the ship's missing flag, bonds with a Fuecoco, and joins. His grandfather's ancient Poké Ball is tied to a black Rayquaza.

In Paldea, Liko visits her parents, repairs her trust with Sprigatito, and chooses to keep traveling instead of staying home. The pendant hatches into Terapagos. Diana tells them about an ancient traveler, Lucius, his six partner Pokémon called the Six Heroes, and a paradise called Laqua. She gives Liko Lucius's belt. The crew searches for the remaining Heroes. Dot catches a Quaxly and later a Tinkatink; her mother Franka allows her to stay aboard.

Explorer agents, especially Amethio, keep attacking. Their leader Gibeon also owns a differently colored Zygarde. When the Explorers try to capture the black Rayquaza, the airship is badly damaged. Amethio uses Terastallization in the fight. Liko's Sprigatito evolves into Floragato. Rayquaza still is not taken. Gibeon orders the pendant and Terapagos watched.

While the ship is repaired, Liko, Roy, and Dot enroll at Naranja Academy. Clavell and Top Champion Geeta oversee training with Tera Orbs. They battle Gym Leaders to learn Terastallization properly.

Later the crew gathers the rest of the Six Heroes and learns how Lucius, a companion named Rystal, Gibeon, and a substance called Laquium connect to Laqua. At Laqua, Spinel of the Explorers betrays the others. The Six Heroes are forced to fight one another. Terapagos reaches a stellar form and cleanses the Laquium. Gibeon and Lucius give the future to the next generation and pass on. Spinel seizes control of the company Exceed, blames the Rising Volt Tacklers in public, and keeps the Six Heroes. Friede and his Charizard fall from the ship and go missing. Liko loses Lucius's belt and notebook while escaping on the black Rayquaza.

After Laqua, one full year passes before that cast's next chapter. That skipped year opens Year 13 on this spine (alongside the separate Lumiose story). Year 12 itself ends with Area Zero's time machine stopped, Terapagos known and caught in the school plot, Kitakami's lies corrected, and the airship crew's disaster at Laqua.
