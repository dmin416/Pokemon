# Noodles/Soup staples

## Soups

### Beef pho

- **Ingredients:** Rice noodles, beef broth
- **Technique:** Slow simmer

### Chicken noodle soup

- **Ingredients:** Chicken, noodles, broth
- **Technique:** Simmer

### French onion soup

- **Ingredients:** Caramelized onion, beef broth, cheese
- **Technique:** Caramelize, simmer, broil

### Lentil soup

- **Ingredients:** Lentils, vegetables, broth
- **Technique:** Simmer

### Minestrone

- **Ingredients:** Vegetables, beans, pasta
- **Technique:** Simmer

### Miso ramen

- **Ingredients:** Wheat noodles, miso broth, pork
- **Technique:** Simmer

### Tortilla soup

- **Ingredients:** Tomato broth, fried tortilla strips, chicken
- **Technique:** Simmer, fry

### Wonton soup

- **Ingredients:** Wheat wrapper dumplings, broth
- **Technique:** Boil, simmer
