# Middle East

## Meat

### Kebab

- **Ingredients:** Ground or cubed meat, spices
- **Technique:** Skewer, grill

### Mansaf

- **Ingredients:** Lamb, jameed yogurt sauce, rice
- **Technique:** Braise

### Msakhan

- **Ingredients:** Chicken, sumac, onion, flatbread
- **Technique:** Roast

### Shawarma

- **Ingredients:** Marinated meat, garlic sauce, flatbread
- **Technique:** Vertical roast

### Shish tawook

- **Ingredients:** Chicken, yogurt-garlic marinade
- **Technique:** Marinate, grill

## Vegetables

### Baba ghanoush

- **Ingredients:** Eggplant, tahini, garlic
- **Technique:** Char, mash

### Dolma

- **Ingredients:** Grape leaves, rice, herbs
- **Technique:** Stuff, simmer

### Fattoush

- **Ingredients:** Mixed greens, toasted pita, sumac
- **Technique:** Toss

### Hummus with pita

- **Ingredients:** Chickpeas, tahini, garlic, lemon
- **Technique:** Puree

### Muhammara

- **Ingredients:** Walnuts, red pepper, pomegranate molasses
- **Technique:** Puree

### Tabbouleh

- **Ingredients:** Parsley, bulgur, tomato, lemon
- **Technique:** Chop, toss

## Rice

### Freekeh pilaf

- **Ingredients:** Cracked wheat, broth, spices
- **Technique:** Simmer

### Kabsa

- **Ingredients:** Rice, spiced meat, tomato
- **Technique:** Simmer

### Koshari

- **Ingredients:** Rice, lentils, pasta, tomato sauce, fried onion
- **Technique:** Layer, simmer

## Bread, Wraps & Sandwiches

### Falafel

- **Ingredients:** Chickpeas/fava beans, herbs, spices
- **Technique:** Form, deep-fry

### Manakish

- **Ingredients:** Flatbread dough, za'atar, olive oil
- **Technique:** Bake

### Sfiha

- **Ingredients:** Flatbread, spiced ground meat topping
- **Technique:** Bake

## Dumplings & Filled

### Kibbeh

- **Ingredients:** Bulgur, ground meat, spices
- **Technique:** Form, fry or bake

### Ouzi

- **Ingredients:** Rice, lamb, nuts, pastry wrap
- **Technique:** Bake

## Curries & Stews

### Shakshuka

- **Ingredients:** Eggs, tomato, pepper, spices
- **Technique:** Simmer, poach

## Desserts

### Baklava

- **Ingredients:** Phyllo, nuts, honey syrup
- **Technique:** Layer, bake, soak
