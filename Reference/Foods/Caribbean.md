# Caribbean

## Meat

### Curry goat

- **Ingredients:** Goat, curry spices
- **Technique:** Slow braise

### Jerk chicken

- **Ingredients:** Chicken, scotch bonnet, allspice marinade
- **Technique:** Marinate, grill/smoke

## Vegetables

### Rice and peas

- **Ingredients:** Rice, kidney beans, coconut milk
- **Technique:** Simmer

## Seafood

### Ackee and saltfish

- **Ingredients:** Ackee fruit, salted cod
- **Technique:** Sauté

## Bread, Wraps & Sandwiches

### Trinidadian roti

- **Ingredients:** Wheat flatbread, curry filling
- **Technique:** Griddle, simmer

## Snacks & Street Foods

### Mofongo

- **Ingredients:** Fried plantain, garlic
- **Technique:** Fry, mash
