# Southeast Asia

## Soups

### Bakso

- **Ingredients:** Beef meatballs, broth, noodles
- **Technique:** Simmer

### Laksa

- **Ingredients:** Rice noodles, coconut-curry broth, shrimp
- **Technique:** Simmer

### Pho

- **Ingredients:** Rice noodles, beef/chicken broth, herbs
- **Technique:** Slow simmer

### Sinigang

- **Ingredients:** Meat, tamarind broth, vegetables
- **Technique:** Simmer

### Tom yum soup

- **Ingredients:** Shrimp, lemongrass, lime, chili
- **Technique:** Simmer

## Meat

### Adobo

- **Ingredients:** Chicken/pork, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic
- **Technique:** Braise

### Ayam penyet

- **Ingredients:** Fried chicken, sambal
- **Technique:** Deep-fry, smash

### Bicol express

- **Ingredients:** Pork, coconut milk, chili
- **Technique:** Simmer

### Bun cha

- **Ingredients:** Grilled pork, rice noodles, herbs, fish sauce dip
- **Technique:** Grill

### Kare-kare

- **Ingredients:** Oxtail, peanut sauce, vegetables
- **Technique:** Braise

### Lechon

- **Ingredients:** Whole pig
- **Technique:** Spit-roast

### Rendang

- **Ingredients:** Beef, coconut milk, spice paste
- **Technique:** Slow braise

### Satay

- **Ingredients:** Skewered meat, peanut sauce
- **Technique:** Marinate, grill

### Sate padang

- **Ingredients:** Beef skewers, spiced yellow sauce
- **Technique:** Grill, simmer

## Vegetables

### Gado gado

- **Ingredients:** Mixed vegetables, peanut sauce
- **Technique:** Blanch, toss

### Som tam

- **Ingredients:** Green papaya, chili, lime, fish sauce
- **Technique:** Pound, toss

## Seafood

### Amok

- **Ingredients:** Fish, coconut milk, kroeung paste
- **Technique:** Steam

## Rice

### Khao pad

- **Ingredients:** Rice, egg, vegetables
- **Technique:** Stir-fry

### Nasi goreng

- **Ingredients:** Rice, kecap manis, egg, chili
- **Technique:** Stir-fry

### Nasi lemak

- **Ingredients:** Coconut rice, sambal, anchovies, egg
- **Technique:** Steam, fry

## Noodles & Pasta

### Char kway teow

- **Ingredients:** Flat rice noodles, shrimp, egg, dark soy
- **Technique:** Stir-fry

### Khao soi

- **Ingredients:** Egg noodles, coconut curry broth
- **Technique:** Simmer

### Mee goreng

- **Ingredients:** Egg noodles, vegetables, chili sauce
- **Technique:** Stir-fry

### Pad thai

- **Ingredients:** Rice noodles, egg, tamarind, peanuts
- **Technique:** Stir-fry

## Bread, Wraps & Sandwiches

### Banh mi

- **Ingredients:** Baguette, pork, pickled vegetables, pate
- **Technique:** Grill, assemble

### Roti canai

- **Ingredients:** Layered flatbread
- **Technique:** Stretch, pan-fry

## Dumplings & Filled

### Lumpia

- **Ingredients:** Wheat/rice wrapper, vegetable-meat filling
- **Technique:** Roll, deep-fry

### Spring rolls

- **Ingredients:** Rice paper/wheat wrapper, vegetables, meat
- **Technique:** Roll, fry or fresh

## Curries & Stews

### Green curry

- **Ingredients:** Coconut milk, green chili paste, meat
- **Technique:** Simmer

### Larb

- **Ingredients:** Minced meat, herbs, lime, toasted rice powder
- **Technique:** Toss, sear

### Massaman curry

- **Ingredients:** Coconut milk, potato, meat, massaman paste
- **Technique:** Simmer
