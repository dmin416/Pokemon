# Forge

Stardew Valley (1.5+). Floor 10 of the Volcano Dungeon. Upgrades melee weapons, enchants weapons and tools, merges weapon looks, combines rings. After Combat Mastery, craft a **Mini-Forge** that does the same anywhere you place it.

Cinder Shards are the forge currency. Costs rise with repeat forges.

## Weapon forging (gems)

Melee only: dagger, hammer or sword. Up to **three** gem forges. Cost: 1 gem + **10 / 15 / 20** Cinder Shards by forge count (1st / 2nd / 3rd).

Levels stack **per gem type**. Example: Ruby then Emerald then Amethyst = +10% damage, +2 speed, +1 weight.

**Diamond:** fills all empty forge slots with random gem upgrades. Costs only **10** Cinder Shards (still consumes the Diamond).

| Gem | Stat | Per level |
|---|---|---|
| Amethyst | Knockback (weight) | +1 |
| Aquamarine | Crit chance | +4.6% flat |
| Emerald | Weapon speed | +2 / +3 / +2 on levels 1 / 2 / 3 |
| Jade | Crit power | +10% |
| Ruby | Damage (min and max) | +10% |
| Topaz | Defense | +1 |
| Diamond | Random fill of remaining slots | special |

## Enchantments (Prismatic Shard)

Any melee weapon or tool except Scythes (all variants) and the Trash Can. Cost: **1 Prismatic Shard + 20 Cinder Shards**.

One combat/tool enchantment at a time. Re-roll by paying again. Tools remember the last **two** enchantments and will not pick those again. Tool upgrades keep the enchantment.

Enchant outcomes depend on how many times you have enchanted **any** tool total. Reload-and-reorder can change results.

### Innate (Dragon Tooth)

Melee only. Not Galaxy, not Infinity, not weapons level 15+, not slingshot. Cost: **1 Dragon Tooth + 10 Cinder Shards**.

Two sets. At most one from each. When forging:

**Set A (optional, not guaranteed)**

| Name | Effect |
|---|---|
| +1–2 Defense | Only if weapon level ≤ 10 |
| −1–5 Weight | Less knockback (about −10% to −50%) |
| Slime Gatherer | Extra slime from Slimes and Big Slimes |

**Set B (one guaranteed)**

| Name | Effect |
|---|---|
| Slime Slayer | +33% + 1 damage to Slimes (not Big Slimes) |
| +25 / 50 / 75 Crit Power | +75 ≈ ×2.5 crit damage multiplier piece |
| +1–3 Crit Chance | ×1.02 / 1.04 / 1.06 crit chance |
| +1–5 Attack | +3 to +15 damage via Attack stat |
| +1–X Speed | −10% swing time per level. Cap depends on weapon (daggers/swords max natural+enchant speed 4; clubs up to 11) |

Stat stacking with gem forges can be fiddly. Check the individual stat pages when exact math matters.

## Infinity weapons

Forge **Galaxy Soul ×3** into a Galaxy Sword / Dagger / Hammer. Each soul costs **20** Cinder Shards (**60** total + 3 souls).

Partial souls show on the weapon but do nothing until three. Gems and enchantments are kept. Appearance becomes the Infinity look (overrides a prior cosmetic merge).

| Result | Base |
|---|---|
| Infinity Blade | Galaxy Sword |
| Infinity Dagger | Galaxy Dagger |
| Infinity Gavel | Galaxy Hammer |

## Weapon appearance

Merge two same-type melee weapons (not slingshots). Left weapon keeps stats and identity. Right weapon's look is applied and the right item is consumed. Cost: **10** Cinder Shards.

Galaxy weapons that changed looks lose that look when upgraded to Infinity.

## Combined rings

Two **different** rings → one ring with both effects. Cost: **20** Cinder Shards.

Cannot combine two of the same ring. Cannot combine a combined ring further. Different rings that share an effect (e.g. Glow + Glowstone) still stack as if worn separately.

## Unforge

Free. Left slot + red ✕.

- Weapon: strips gem forges and appearance reset. Enchantments stay.
- Combined ring: splits back into the two originals.
- Refunds only **some** of the Cinder Shards spent.

## Fishing (Forge floor only)

Only Volcano floor where you can fish. One of two Lava Eel spots. No crab pots.

5% chance of a one-time-per-player present with the painting **Physics 101**.

## Secrets

- Perfection: red monkey → secret hat.
- Plaque above the Forge: enciphered message.
- Throw an Ancient Doll into the lava → secret item.

## Rancher / story use

Volcano end-forge: scarce Cinder Shards, Prismatic / Dragon Tooth / Galaxy Soul as legendary reagents. Mini-Forge = portable mastery after Combat Mastery. Gem forges = visible stat craft. Enchant rerolls = expensive and semi-random. Infinity line = endgame galaxy metal. Ring fuse = one jewelry slot carrying two powers.
