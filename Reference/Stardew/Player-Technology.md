# Player Technology and Abilities

Stardew Valley systems a player (or Rancher-analog) can use. Pair with [Forge.md](Forge.md) for Volcano / Mini-Forge detail.

## Tools and upgrades

Hoe, axe, pickaxe, watering can, scythe, fishing rod. Blacksmith path: copper → steel → gold → iridium. Higher tiers cover more tiles and hit harder.

Weapons: swords, daggers, clubs, slingshots. Upgrade at the Blacksmith. Enchant and gem-forge later at the Ginger Island Forge (or Mini-Forge after Combat Mastery).

## Farming

Till, plant, water, harvest. Fertilizer and quality soil additives.

Sprinklers: basic, quality, iridium. Automate watering.

Processing: kegs (wine and more), preserves jars (jam), Seed Maker (crop → seeds).

Off-season grow space: Greenhouse, then Ginger Island farmland.

## Ranching

Animals: chickens, cows, goats, sheep, pigs, rabbits, ducks, ostriches.

Collect: milk, wool, eggs. Process: mayonnaise machines, cheese presses, looms.

Auto-Grabbers pull products from coops and barns without daily hand collection.

## Mining and combat

Mines and Skull Cavern: random floors downward.

Fight with melee, dodge, bombs, traps, Monster Musk.

Furnaces and charcoal kilns smelt and fuel. Geodes crack into gems and minerals. Crystalarium copies gems over time.

## Fishing

Rod + bait + tackle. Crab pots for passive catches. Fish ponds breed and yield.

Legendary and seasonal fish on calendars and locations.

## Foraging

Wild plants, mushrooms, fruit. Tappers on trees (syrup, resin). Bee houses (honey). Chop, seed and farm trees.

## Skills and professions

Five skills: Farming, Mining, Foraging, Fishing, Combat. Profession picks at levels 5 and 10 in each tree.

## Crafting and construction

Large craft list from forage and ore.

Robin builds: coops, barns, silos, sheds, Junimo Huts, stables and more.

Sewing machine and dye pots for clothes.

## Social systems

Friendship, romance, marriage, children.

Museum donations. Community Center bundles or JojaMart route.

Special Orders. Qi's Walnut Room challenges (Ginger Island / late game).

## Travel and convenience

Horse (later coach for more speed). Warp totems. Return Scepter. Mine elevator and minecarts. Bus to the Desert. Boat to Ginger Island.

Rings: night vision, luck, combat and other passives (combinable at the Forge).

## Ginger Island

Volcano Dungeon Forge. Parrot Express. Island farm, forage and local resources. See [Forge.md](Forge.md).

## Charged tools (area)

Hold to charge hoe, watering can and pan:

1. Single tile  
2. Row of three  
3. 3×3  
4. Larger tiers with better tools  

Iridium + **Reaching** enchantment: full charge = **5×5** in one use.

## Flavored ability: Channeled Craft

He pours his own energy into whatever tool sits in his hand. The tool does not change shape. It works like an extension of him.

| Focus | Effect |
|---|---|
| Hoe | Pulse turns wide soil. Up to full 5×5 at peak focus. |
| Watering can | Water carries a trace of his vitality and speeds growth. Same 5×5 reach when fully charged. |
| Scythe | Wide energy arc clears crops in one sweep at full strength. |
| Axe | Wood splits clean and fast as the blade takes his push. |
| Pickaxe | Stone cracks further than the swing alone. Sometimes extra ore. |
| Fishing rod | Focus down the line settles the fish before it fights the hook. |
| Weapon | Swing hits harder than his frame should allow. |
| Husbandry | Calm settles nearby animals. Better yield without force. |
| Foraging | Sense of ripe or valuable growth before he sees it. |
| Forge work | Held long enough, a tool or weapon keeps a lasting trace of what he poured in. |
| Travel | Energy in legs or mount = speed bursts. Charged token can fold distance to a marked place. |

**Full output:** every discipline at once. Clear a whole field in one motion, triple catch on one cast, or call a banked boon that shifts with the day. Needs the full width of that training. Always costs him afterward.
