# Celesteela: Narrative Speed

Recalculated for the smaller world with Earth-standard gravity. World size and gravity are in [Scale.md](Scale.md).

World parameters used:

- Radius: 2,677 km (2,677,000 m)
- Gravity: 9.8 m/s² (unchanged, Earth-standard)

## Claim 1: Atmospheric high-speed flight (unchanged)

This range does not depend on planet size, so it stays as previously estimated.

Estimated range: **900–1,200 km/h**

## Claim 2: Rocket-thruster launch into space (recalculated)

Orbital velocity (v = √(g × r)):

√(9.8 × 2,677,000) = √26,234,600 ≈ 5,122 m/s ≈ **18,439 km/h**

Escape velocity (v = √(2 × g × r)):

5,122 × √2 ≈ 7,244 m/s ≈ **26,078 km/h**

| Basis | Original (Earth) | Recalculated (smaller world) |
|---|---|---|
| Atmospheric high-speed flight | 900–1,200 km/h | 900–1,200 km/h (unchanged) |
| Orbital velocity | ~28,000 km/h | ~18,439 km/h |
| Escape velocity | ~40,270 km/h | ~26,078 km/h |

The smaller radius cuts both orbital and escape velocity by roughly a third compared to Earth-scale figures, since both scale with the square root of radius under fixed gravity. Celesteela's rocket-thruster space launch becomes noticeably easier to achieve on this world despite gravity staying at Earth-standard.

## Claim 3: Time under constant 10 g

Assume constant acceleration of 10 g (98 m/s²) from rest, no drag. Time is t = v / a. Distance covered while accelerating is d = v² / (2a).

Sound barrier uses Mach 1 at sea level (~343 m/s, ~1,235 km/h).

| Target | Speed | Time from rest | Distance while accelerating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sound barrier (Mach 1) | ~1,235 km/h | **~3.5 s** | ~0.60 km |
| Atmospheric high-speed (low) | 900 km/h | ~2.6 s | ~0.32 km |
| Atmospheric high-speed (high) | 1,200 km/h | ~3.4 s | ~0.57 km |
| Orbital velocity | ~18,439 km/h | **~52 s** | ~134 km |
| Escape velocity | ~26,078 km/h | **~74 s** | ~268 km |

At 10 g, Celesteela breaks the sound barrier in about **3.5 seconds**. Reaching orbital speed takes under a minute (~52 s). Escape speed takes about **1 minute 14 seconds**.
