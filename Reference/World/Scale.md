# Scale

Planet size from a Mach 0.99 circumnavigation. Regional land areas are in [Region-Sizes.md](../Places/Region-Sizes.md).

Using Mach 0.99 at cruising altitude as the circumnavigation speed, 16 hours covers about 16,820 km (10,450 miles). If that's the full trip around the planet, that distance is the planet's circumference.

## Size

| Measure | Metric | Imperial |
|---|---|---|
| Circumference | ~16,820 km | 10,450 mi |
| Radius (C / 2π) | ~2,677 km | 1,663 mi |
| Diameter | ~5,354 km | 3,327 mi |
| Surface gravity | 1 g | same as Earth |

## Gravity

Surface gravity is the same as Earth's (9.8 m/s²). People and Pokémon weigh what they would on Earth.

To hold 1 g at about 42% of Earth's radius, mass is about 18% of Earth's and mean density is about 2.4 times Earth's.

## Orbit

Orbital velocity is √(g × r). Escape velocity is √(2 × g × r). With g fixed at 9.8 m/s², both scale with the square root of radius.

| Basis | This world | Earth |
|---|---|---|
| Orbital velocity | ~18,439 km/h (5,122 m/s) | ~28,000 km/h |
| Escape velocity | ~26,078 km/h (7,244 m/s) | ~40,270 km/h |

The smaller radius cuts both figures by roughly a third compared to Earth.

Celesteela narrative speeds are in [Celesteela.md](Celesteela.md).

## Comparison

Earth's circumference is about 40,075 km (24,901 mi), roughly 2.4 times bigger. This planet sits at about 42% of Earth's size. It is smaller than Mars (diameter ~6,779 km) and closer to Mercury (diameter ~4,879 km), landing somewhere between the two.

| Body | Diameter |
|---|---|
| Earth | ~12,742 km |
| Mars | ~6,779 km |
