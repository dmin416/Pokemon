# Bug

## Physical
### Attack Order
The user calls out its underlings to pummel the target. This move has a heightened chance of landing a critical hit.
Physical | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
High critical hit ratio.

### Bug Bite
The user attacks by biting the target. If the target is holding a Berry, the user eats it and gains its effect.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20
Receives the effect from the opponent's held berry.

### Fell Stinger
When the user knocks out a target with this move, the user's Attack stat is boosted drastically.
Physical | Power 50 | Accuracy 100 | PP 25
Drastically raises user's Attack if target is KO'd.

### First Impression
Although this move has great power, it only works the first turn each time the user enters battle.
Physical | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Although this move has great power, it only works the first turn the user is in battle.

### Fury Cutter
The user attacks by slashing the target with scythes, claws, or the like. This attack becomes more powerful if it hits in succession.
Physical | Power 40 | Accuracy 95 | PP 20
Power increases each turn.

### Leech Life
The user drains the target's blood. The user's HP is restored by up to half the damage taken by the target.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
User recovers half the HP inflicted on opponent.

### Lunge
The user makes a lunge at the target, attacking with full force. This also lowers the target's Attack stat.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15 | Chance 100%

### Megahorn
Using its tough and impressive horn, the user rams into the target with no letup.
Physical | Power 120 | Accuracy 85 | PP 10

### Pin Missile
The user attacks by shooting sharp spikes at the target. This move hits two to five times in a row.
Physical | Power 25 | Accuracy 95 | PP 20
Hits 2-5 times in one turn.

### Pounce
The user attacks by pouncing on the target. This also lowers the target's Speed stat.
Physical | Power 50 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20 | Chance 100%
Lowers opponent's Speed.

### Skitter Smack
The user skitters behind the target to attack. This also lowers the target's Sp. Atk stat.
Physical | Power 70 | Accuracy 90 | PP 10
Lowers opponent's Sp. Attack.

### Steamroller
The user crushes its target by rolling over the target with its rounded body. This may also make the target flinch.
Physical | Power 65 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20 | Chance 30%
May cause flinching.

### Twineedle
The user damages the target twice in succession by jabbing it with two spikes. This may also poison the target.
Physical | Power 25 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20 | Chance 20%
Hits twice in one turn. May poison opponent.

### U-turn
After making its attack, the user rushes back to switch places with a party Pokémon in waiting.
Physical | Power 70 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20
User switches out immediately after attacking.

### X-Scissor
The user slashes at the target by crossing its scythes, claws, or the like as if they were a pair of scissors.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15

## Special
### Bug Buzz
The user vibrates to generate a damaging sound wave. This may also lower the target's Sp. Def stat.
Special | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10 | Chance 10%
May lower opponent's Special Defense.

### Infestation
The target is infested and attacked for four to five turns. The target can't flee during this time.
Special | Power 20 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20 | Chance 100%
Traps opponent, damaging them for 4-5 turns.

### Pollen Puff
The user attacks the enemy with a pollen puff that explodes. If the target is an ally, it gives the ally a pollen puff that restores its HP instead.
Special | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Deals damage to opponent or restores HP of teammate.

### Signal Beam
The user attacks with a sinister beam of light. This may also confuse the target.
Special | Power 75 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15 | Chance 10%
May confuse opponent.

### Silver Wind
The target is attacked with powdery scales blown by the wind. This may also boost all the user's stats.
Special | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5 | Chance 10%
May raise all stats of user at once.

### Struggle Bug
The user attacks by struggling against opposing Pokémon. This also lowers their Sp. Atk stats.
Special | Power 50 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20 | Chance 100%
Lowers opponent's Special Attack.

## Status
### Defend Order
The user calls out its underlings to shield its body, boosting its Defense and Sp. Def stats.
Status | PP 10
Raises user's Defense and Special Defense.

### Heal Order
The user calls out its underlings to heal it. The user regains up to half of its max HP.
Status | PP 10
User recovers half its max HP.

### Powder
The user covers the target in a combustible powder. If the target uses a Fire-type move, the powder explodes and damages the target.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 20
Damages Pokémon using Fire type moves.

### Quiver Dance
The user lightly performs a beautiful, mystic dance. This boosts the user's Sp. Atk, Sp. Def, and Speed stats.
Status | PP 20
Raises user's Special Attack, Special Defense and Speed.

### Rage Powder
The user scatters a cloud of irritating powder to draw attention to itself. Opposing Pokémon aim only at the user.
Status | PP 20
Forces attacks to hit user, not team-mates.

### Silk Trap
The user spins a silken trap, protecting itself from damage while lowering the Speed stat of any attacker that makes direct contact.
Status | PP 10
Protects the user and lowers opponent's Speed on contact.

### Spider Web
The user ensnares the target with a thin, gooey silk so it can't flee from battle.
Status | PP 10
Opponent cannot escape/switch.

### Sticky Web
The user weaves a sticky net around the opposing team, which lowers their Speed stats upon switching into battle.
Status | PP 20
Lowers opponent's Speed when switching into battle.

### String Shot
The user blows silk from its mouth that binds opposing Pokémon and harshly lowers their Speed stats.
Status | Accuracy 95 | PP 40
Sharply lowers opponent's Speed.

### Tail Glow
The user stares at flashing lights to focus its mind, drastically boosting its Sp. Atk stat.
Status | PP 20
Drastically raises user's Special Attack.
