# Dragon

## Physical
### Breaking Swipe
The user swings its tough tail wildly and attacks opposing Pokémon. This also lowers their Attack stats.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15 | Chance 100%
Hits multiple opponents and lowers their attack.

### Dragon Claw
The user slashes the target with huge, sharp claws.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15

### Dragon Darts
The user attacks twice using Dreepy. If there are two opposing Pokémon, this move hits each of them once.
Physical | Power 50 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
User attacks twice.

### Dragon Hammer
The user uses its body like a hammer to attack the target and inflict damage.
Physical | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15

### Dragon Rush
The user tackles the target while exhibiting overwhelming menace. This may also make the target flinch.
Physical | Power 100 | Accuracy 75 | PP 10 | Chance 20%
May cause flinching.

### Dragon Tail
The target is knocked away, and a different Pokémon is dragged out.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 90 | PP 10
In battles, the opponent switches. In the wild, the Pokémon runs.

### Dual Chop
The user attacks its target by hitting it with brutal strikes. The target is hit twice in a row.
Physical | Power 40 | Accuracy 90 | PP 15
Hits twice in one turn.

### Glaive Rush
The user throws its entire body into a reckless charge. After this move is used, attacks on the user cannot miss and will inflict double damage until the user's next turn.
Physical | Power 120 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Attacks from opposing Pokémon during the next turn cannot miss and will inflict double damage.

### Order Up
The user attacks with elegant poise. If the user has a Tatsugiri in its mouth, this move boosts one of the user's stats based on the Tatsugiri's form.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Boosts Attack/Defense/Speed depending on ally Tatsugiri.

### Outrage
The user attacks by going on a rampage, then becomes fixated on using this move.
Physical | Power 120 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
User attacks for 2-3 turns but then becomes confused.

### Scale Shot
The user attacks by shooting scales two to five times in a row. This move boosts the user's Speed stat but lowers its Defense stat.
Physical | Power 25 | Accuracy 90 | PP 20
Hits 2-5 times in one turn. Boosts user's Speed but lowers its Defense.

## Special
### Clanging Scales
The user rubs the scales on its entire body and makes a huge noise to attack opposing Pokémon. The user's Defense stat is lowered after the attack.
Special | Power 110 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5 | Chance 100%
Lowers user's Defense.

### Clangorous Soulblaze
Kommo-o uses its Z-Power to attack the target with full force. This move boosts all the user's stats.
Special | Power 185 | PP 1
Kommo-o exclusive Z-Move.

### Core Enforcer
If the Pokémon the user has inflicted damage on have already used their moves, this move eliminates the effect of the target's Ability.
Special | Power 100 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Suppresses the target's ability if the target has already moved.

### Draco Meteor
Comets are summoned down from the sky onto the target. The recoil from this move harshly lowers the user's Sp. Atk stat.
Special | Power 130 | Accuracy 90 | PP 5 | Chance 100%
Sharply lowers user's Special Attack.

### Dragon Breath
The user exhales a mighty gust that inflicts damage. This may also leave the target with paralysis.
Special | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20 | Chance 30%
May paralyze opponent.

### Dragon Energy
Converting its life-force into power, the user attacks opposing Pokémon. The lower the user's HP, the lower the move's power.
Special | Power 150 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
The higher the user's HP, the higher the power.

### Dragon Pulse
The target is attacked with a shock wave generated from the user's mouth.
Special | Power 85 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10

### Dragon Rage
This attack hits the target with a shock wave of pure rage. This attack always inflicts 40 HP damage.
Special | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Always inflicts 40 HP.

### Dynamax Cannon
The user condenses energy within its body and unleashes that energy from its core to inflict damage.
Special | Power 100 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Damage doubles if opponent is Dynamaxed.

### Eternabeam
This is Eternatus's most powerful attack in its original form. The user can't move on the next turn.
Special | Power 160 | Accuracy 90 | PP 5
User can't move on the next turn.

### Fickle Beam
The user shoots a beam of light to inflict damage. Sometimes all the user's heads shoot beams in unison, doubling the move's power.
Special | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
May deal double damage.

### Roar of Time
The user blasts the target with power that distorts even time. The user can't move on the next turn.
Special | Power 150 | Accuracy 90 | PP 5
User must recharge next turn.

### Spacial Rend
The user tears the target along with the space around it. This move has a heightened chance of landing a critical hit.
Special | Power 100 | Accuracy 95 | PP 5
High critical hit ratio.

### Twister
The user whips up a vicious tornado to tear at the target.
Special | Power 40 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20 | Chance 20%
May cause flinching. Hits Pokémon using Fly/Bounce with double power.

## Status
### Clangorous Soul
The user boosts all its stats by using some of its own HP.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 5 | Chance 100%
Raises all user's stats but loses HP.

### Dragon Cheer
The user raises its allies' morale with a draconic cry so that their future attacks have a heightened chance of landing critical hits. This rouses Dragon types more.
Status | Accuracy ∞ | PP 15

### Dragon Dance
The user vigorously performs a mystic, powerful dance that raises its Attack and Speed stats.
Status | PP 20
Raises user's Attack and Speed.

## Other
### Devastating Drake
The user materializes its aura using its Z-Power and attacks the target with full force. The power varies, depending on the original move.
PP 1
Dragon type Z-Move.

### G-Max Depletion
A Dragon-type attack that Gigantamax Duraludon use. Reduces the PP of the last move used.
Accuracy ∞ | PP 5
Duraludon-exclusive G-Max Move. Reduces opponent's PP.

### Max Wyrmwind
This is a Dragon-type attack Dynamax Pokémon use. This lowers the Attack stats of opponents.
Chance 100%
Dragon type Dynamax move. Lowers the target's Attack.
