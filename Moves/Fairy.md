# Fairy

## Physical
### Let's Snuggle Forever
After obtaining Z-Power, the user, Mimikyu, punches the target with full force.
Physical | Power 190 | PP 1
Mimikyu-exclusive Z-Move.

### Magical Torque
Physical | Power 100 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
May confuse the opponent.

### Play Rough
The user attacks by playing rough with the target. This may also lower the target's Attack stat.
Physical | Power 90 | Accuracy 90 | PP 10 | Chance 10%
May lower opponent's Attack.

### Spirit Break
The user attacks the target with so much force that it could break the target's spirit. This also lowers the target's Sp. Atk stat.
Physical | Power 75 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15 | Chance 100%
Lowers opponent's Special Attack.

## Special
### Alluring Voice
The user attacks the target using its angelic voice. This also confuses the target if its stats have been boosted during the turn.
Special | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10

### Dazzling Gleam
The user emits a powerful flash of light to inflict damage on the target.
Special | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Hits all adjacent opponents.

### Disarming Voice
Letting out a charming cry, the user does emotional damage to opposing Pokémon. This attack never misses.
Special | Power 40 | Accuracy ∞ | PP 15
Ignores Accuracy and Evasiveness.

### Draining Kiss
The user steals the target's HP with a kiss. The user's HP is restored by over half the damage taken by the target.
Special | Power 50 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
User recovers most the HP inflicted on opponent.

### Fairy Wind
The user stirs up a fairy wind and strikes the target with it.
Special | Power 40 | Accuracy 100 | PP 30

### Fleur Cannon
The user unleashes a strong beam. The recoil from this move harshly lowers the user's Sp. Atk stat.
Special | Power 130 | Accuracy 90 | PP 5 | Chance 100%
Sharply lowers user's Special Attack.

### Guardian of Alola
The user, the Land Spirit Pokémon, obtains Alola's energy using its Z-Power and attacks the target with full force. This reduces the target's HP greatly.
Special | PP 1
Tapu-exclusive Z-move. Cuts opponent's HP by 75%.

### Light of Ruin
Drawing power from the Eternal Flower, the user fires a powerful beam of light. This also damages the user quite a lot.
Special | Power 140 | Accuracy 90 | PP 5
User receives recoil damage.

### Misty Explosion
The user attacks everything around it and faints upon using this move. This move's power is boosted on Misty Terrain.
Special | Power 100 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Power increases on Misty Terrain.

### Moonblast
Borrowing the power of the moon, the user attacks the target. This may also lower the target's Sp. Atk stat.
Special | Power 95 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15 | Chance 30%
May lower opponent's Special Attack.

### Nature's Madness
The user hits the target with the force of nature. It halves the target’s HP.
Special | Accuracy 90 | PP 10
Halves the foe's HP.

### Sparkly Swirl
The user attacks the target by wrapping it with a whirlwind of an alluring scent. This also heals all status conditions of the user's party.
Special | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Cures all status problems in the party Pokémon.

### Springtide Storm
The user attacks by wrapping opposing Pokémon in fierce winds brimming with love and hate. This may also lower their Attack stats.
Special | Power 100 | Accuracy 80 | PP 5
Boosts user's stats in Incarnate Forme, or lowers opponent's stats in Therian Forme.

### Strange Steam
The user attacks the target by emitting steam. This may also confuse the target.
Special | Power 90 | Accuracy 95 | PP 10 | Chance 20%
May confuse opponent.

## Status
### Aromatic Mist
The user boosts the Sp. Def stat of an ally Pokémon by using a mysterious aroma.
Status | PP 20
Raises Special Defense of an ally.

### Baby-Doll Eyes
The user stares at the target with its baby-doll eyes, which lowers the target's Attack stat. This move always goes first.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 30
Always goes first. Lowers the target's attack.

### Charm
The user gazes at the target rather charmingly, making it less wary. This harshly lowers the target's Attack stat.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 20
Sharply lowers opponent's Attack.

### Crafty Shield
The user protects itself and its allies from status moves with a mysterious power. This does not stop moves that do damage.
Status | PP 10
Protects the Pokémon from status moves.

### Decorate
The user sharply boosts the target's Attack and Sp. Atk stats by decorating the target.
Status | PP 15 | Chance 100%
Sharply raises target's Attack and Special Attack.

### Fairy Lock
By locking down the battlefield, the user keeps all Pokémon from fleeing during the next turn.
Status | PP 10
Prevents fleeing in the next turn.

### Floral Healing
The user restores the target's HP by up to half of its max HP. It restores more HP when the terrain is grass.
Status | PP 10

### Flower Shield
The user raises the Defense stats of all Grass-type Pokémon in battle with a mysterious power.
Status | PP 10 | Chance 100%
Sharply raises Defense of all Grass-type Pokémon on the field.

### Geomancy
The user absorbs energy and sharply raises its Sp. Atk, Sp. Def, and Speed stats on the next turn.
Status | PP 10
Charges on first turn, sharply raises user's Sp. Attack, Sp. Defense and Speed on the second.

### Misty Terrain
This protects Pokémon on the ground from status conditions and halves damage from Dragon-type moves for five turns.
Status | PP 10
Protects the field from status conditions for 5 turns.

### Moonlight
The user restores its own HP. The amount of HP regained varies with the weather.
Status | PP 5
User recovers HP. Amount varies with the weather.

### Sweet Kiss
The user kisses the target with a sweet, angelic cuteness that causes confusion.
Status | Accuracy 75 | PP 10
Confuses opponent.
