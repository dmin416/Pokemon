# Ground

## Physical
### Bone Club
The user clubs the target with a bone. This may also make the target flinch.
Physical | Power 65 | Accuracy 85 | PP 20 | Chance 10%
May cause flinching.

### Bone Rush
The user attacks by striking the target with a hard bone. This move hits two to five times in a row.
Physical | Power 25 | Accuracy 90 | PP 10
Hits 2-5 times in one turn.

### Bonemerang
The user throws the bone it holds. The bone loops around to hit the target twice—coming and going.
Physical | Power 50 | Accuracy 90 | PP 10
Hits twice in one turn.

### Bulldoze
The user tramples its target into the ground, dealing damage. This also lowers the target's action speed.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20 | Chance 100%
Lowers opponent's Speed.

### Dig
The user burrows into the ground on the first turn, then attacks on the next turn.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Digs underground on first turn, attacks on second. Can also escape from caves.

### Drill Run
The user crashes into the target while rotating its body like a drill. This move has a heightened chance of landing a critical hit.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 95 | PP 10
High critical hit ratio.

### Earthquake
The user sets off an earthquake that strikes every Pokémon around it.
Physical | Power 100 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Power is doubled if opponent is underground from using Dig.

### Fissure
The user opens up a fissure in the ground and drops the target in. The target faints instantly if this attack hits.
Physical | Accuracy 30 | PP 5
One-Hit-KO, if it hits.

### Headlong Rush
The user smashes into the target in a full-body tackle. This also lowers the user's Defense and Sp. Def stats.
Physical | Power 120 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Lowers user's Defense.

### High Horsepower
The user fiercely attacks the target using its entire body.
Physical | Power 95 | Accuracy 95 | PP 10

### Land's Wrath
The user gathers the energy of the land and focuses that power on opposing Pokémon to damage them.
Physical | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10

### Magnitude
The user attacks everything around it with a ground-shaking quake. Its power varies.
Physical | Accuracy 100 | PP 30
Hits with random power.

### Precipice Blades
The user attacks opposing Pokémon by manifesting the power of the land in fearsome blades of stone.
Physical | Power 120 | Accuracy 85 | PP 10
Hits all adjacent opponents.

### Sand Tomb
The user traps the target inside a harshly raging sandstorm that inflicts damage for four to five turns.
Physical | Power 35 | Accuracy 85 | PP 15 | Chance 100%
Traps opponent, damaging them for 4-5 turns.

### Stomping Tantrum
Driven by frustration, the user attacks the target. If the user's previous move has failed, the power of this move doubles.
Physical | Power 75 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10

### Thousand Arrows
This move also hits opposing Pokémon that are in the air. Those Pokémon are knocked down to the ground.
Physical | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Makes Flying-type Pokémon vulnerable to Ground moves.

### Thousand Waves
The user attacks with a wave that crawls along the ground. Those it hits can't flee from battle.
Physical | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Opponent cannot flee or switch.

## Special
### Earth Power
The user makes the ground under the target erupt with power. This may also lower the target's Sp. Def stat.
Special | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10 | Chance 10%
May lower opponent's Special Defense.

### Mud Bomb
The user launches a hard-packed mud ball to attack. This may also lower the target's accuracy.
Special | Power 65 | Accuracy 85 | PP 10 | Chance 30%
May lower opponent's Accuracy.

### Mud Shot
The user attacks by hurling a blob of mud at the target. This also lowers the target's Speed stat.
Special | Power 55 | Accuracy 95 | PP 15 | Chance 100%
Lowers opponent's Speed.

### Mud-Slap
The user hurls mud in the target's face to inflict damage and lower its accuracy.
Special | Power 20 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10 | Chance 100%
Lowers opponent's Accuracy.

### Sandsear Storm
The user attacks by wrapping opposing Pokémon in fierce winds and searingly hot sand. This may also leave them with a burn.
Special | Power 100 | Accuracy 80 | PP 10
May burn target.

### Scorching Sands
The user throws scorching sand at the target to attack. This may also leave the target with a burn.
Special | Power 70 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
May burn the target.

## Status
### Mud Sport
The user soaks the battlefield with mud. This weakens Electric-type moves for five turns.
Status | PP 15
Weakens the power of Electric-type moves.

### Rototiller
The user tills the soil on the ground. This boosts the Attack and Sp. Atk stats of Grass-type Pokémon.
Status | PP 10 | Chance 100%
Raises Attack and Special Attack of Grass-types.

### Sand Attack
Sand is hurled in the target's face, lowering the target's accuracy.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Lowers opponent's Accuracy.

### Shore Up
The user regains up to half of its max HP. It restores more HP in a sandstorm.
Status | PP 5

### Spikes
The user lays a trap of spikes at the feet of the opposing team. The spikes will damage opposing Pokémon that switch into battle.
Status | PP 20
Damages opponents switching into battle.
