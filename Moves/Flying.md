# Flying

## Physical
### Acrobatics
The user nimbly strikes the target. This attack does massive damage if the user isn't holding an item.
Physical | Power 55 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Stronger when the user does not have a held item.

### Aerial Ace
The user confounds the target with speed, then slashes. This attack never misses.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy ∞ | PP 20
Ignores Accuracy and Evasiveness.

### Beak Blast
The user first heats up its beak, and then it attacks the target. Making direct contact with the Pokémon while it's heating up its beak results in a burn.
Physical | Power 100 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15

### Bounce
The user bounces up high on the first turn, then drops onto the target on the next turn. This may also leave the target with paralysis.
Physical | Power 85 | Accuracy 85 | PP 5 | Chance 30%
Springs up on first turn, attacks on second. May paralyze opponent.

### Brave Bird
The user tucks in its wings and charges at a low altitude. This also damages the user quite a lot.
Physical | Power 120 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
User receives recoil damage.

### Dragon Ascent
The user attacks the target by dropping out of the sky at high speed. This also lowers the user's Defense and Sp. Def stats.
Physical | Power 120 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5 | Chance 100%
Lowers user's Defense and Special Defense.

### Drill Peck
A corkscrewing attack that strikes the target with a sharp beak acting as a drill.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20

### Dual Wingbeat
The user slams the target with its wings. The target is hit twice in a row.
Physical | Power 40 | Accuracy 90 | PP 10

### Floaty Fall
The user floats in the air, and then dives at a steep angle to attack the target. This may also make the target flinch.
Physical | Power 90 | Accuracy 95 | PP 15 | Chance 30%
May cause flinching.

### Fly
The user flies up into the sky on the first turn, then attacks on the next turn.
Physical | Power 90 | Accuracy 95 | PP 15
Flies up on first turn, attacks on second turn.

### Peck
The target is jabbed with a sharply pointed beak or horn.
Physical | Power 35 | Accuracy 100 | PP 35

### Pluck
The user attacks by pecking the target. If the target is holding a Berry, the user eats it and gains its effect.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20
If the opponent is holding a berry, its effect is stolen by user.

### Sky Attack
The user attacks the turn after using this move. This move has a heightened chance of landing a critical hit and may also make the target flinch.
Physical | Power 140 | Accuracy 90 | PP 5 | Chance 30%
Charges on first turn, attacks on second. May cause flinching. High critical hit ratio.

### Sky Drop
The user takes the target into the sky, then drops it during the next turn. The target cannot attack while in the sky.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Takes opponent into the air on first turn, drops them on second turn.

### Wing Attack
The target is struck with large, imposing wings spread wide to inflict damage.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 35

## Special
### Aeroblast
A vortex of air is shot at the target to inflict damage. This move has a heightened chance of landing a critical hit.
Special | Power 100 | Accuracy 95 | PP 5
High critical hit ratio.

### Air Cutter
The user launches razor-like wind to slash opposing Pokémon. This move has a heightened chance of landing a critical hit.
Special | Power 60 | Accuracy 95 | PP 25
High critical hit ratio.

### Air Slash
The user attacks with a blade of air that slices even the sky.
Special | Power 75 | Accuracy 95 | PP 15 | Chance 30%
May cause flinching.

### Bleakwind Storm
The user attacks with savagely cold winds that cause both body and spirit to tremble. This may also lower the Speed stats of opposing Pokémon.
Special | Power 100 | Accuracy 80 | PP 10
May lower opponent's Speed.

### Chatter
The user attacks the target with sound waves of deafening chatter. This confuses the target.
Special | Power 65 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20 | Chance 100%
Confuses opponent.

### Gust
A gust of wind is whipped up by wings and launched at the target to inflict damage.
Special | Power 40 | Accuracy 100 | PP 35
Hits Pokémon using Fly/Bounce/Sky Drop with double power.

### Hurricane
The user attacks by wrapping its opponent in a fierce wind. This may also confuse the target.
Special | Power 110 | Accuracy 70 | PP 10 | Chance 30%
May confuse opponent.

### Oblivion Wing
The user absorbs its target's HP. The user's HP is restored by over half of the damage taken by the target.
Special | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
User recovers most of the HP inflicted on opponent.

## Status
### Defog
A strong wind blows away the target's barriers such as Reflect or Light Screen. This also lowers the target's evasiveness.
Status | PP 15
Lowers opponent's Evasiveness and clears fog.

### Feather Dance
The user covers the target's body with a mass of down that harshly lowers its Attack stat.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Sharply lowers opponent's Attack.

### Mirror Move
The user counters the target by mimicking the target's last move.
Status | PP 20
User performs the opponent's last move.

### Roost
The user lands and rests its body. This move restores the user's HP by up to half its max HP.
Status | PP 5
User recovers half of its max HP and loses the Flying type temporarily.

### Tailwind
The user whips up a turbulent whirlwind that boosts the Speed stats of itself and its allies for four turns.
Status | PP 15
Doubles Speed for 4 turns.

## Other
### G-Max Wind Rage
A Flying-type attack that Gigantamax Corviknight use. This move removes the effects of moves like Reflect and Light Screen.
Accuracy ∞ | PP 10
Corviknight-exclusive G-Max Move. Removes battlefield hazards.

### Max Airstream
This is a Flying-type attack Dynamax Pokémon use. This boosts the Speed stats of allies.
Chance 100%
Flying type Dynamax move. Raises the team's Speed.

### Supersonic Skystrike
The user soars up with its Z-Power and plummets toward the target at full speed. The power varies, depending on the original move.
PP 1
Flying type Z-Move.
