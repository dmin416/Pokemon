# Steel

## Physical
### Anchor Shot
The user entangles the target with its anchor chain while attacking. The target becomes unable to flee.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20

### Behemoth Bash
The user becomes a gigantic shield and slams into the target. This move deals twice the damage if the target is Dynamaxed.
Physical | Power 100 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Damage doubles if target is Dynamaxed.

### Behemoth Blade
The user becomes a gigantic sword and cuts the target. This move deals twice the damage if the target is Dynamaxed.
Physical | Power 100 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Damage doubles if target is Dynamaxed.

### Bullet Punch
The user strikes the target with tough punches as fast as bullets. This also raises the user's action speed.
Physical | Power 40 | Accuracy 100 | PP 30
User attacks first.

### Double Iron Bash
The user rotates, centering the hex nut in its chest, and then strikes with its arms twice in a row. This may also make the target flinch.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5 | Chance 30%
Hits twice in one turn; may cause flinching.

### Gear Grind
The user attacks by throwing steel gears at its target twice.
Physical | Power 50 | Accuracy 85 | PP 15
Hits twice in one turn.

### Gigaton Hammer
The user swings its whole body around to attack with its huge hammer. This move can't be used twice in a row.
Physical | Power 160 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Cannot be used twice in a row.

### Gyro Ball
The user tackles the target with a high-speed spin. The slower the user is than the target, the greater the move's power.
Physical | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
The slower the user, the stronger the attack.

### Hard Press
The target is crushed with an arm, a claw, or the like to inflict damage. The more HP the target has left, the greater the move's power.
Physical | Accuracy 100 | PP 10

### Heavy Slam
The user slams into the target with its heavy body. The more the user outweighs the target, the greater the move's power.
Physical | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
The heavier the user, the stronger the attack.

### Iron Head
The user slams the target with its steel-hard head.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15 | Chance 30%
May cause flinching.

### Iron Tail
The target is slammed with a steel-hard tail. This may also lower the target's Defense stat.
Physical | Power 100 | Accuracy 75 | PP 15 | Chance 30%
May lower opponent's Defense.

### Magnet Bomb
The user launches steel bombs that stick to the target. This attack never misses.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy ∞ | PP 20
Ignores Accuracy and Evasiveness.

### Metal Burst
The user retaliates with much greater force against the opponent that last inflicted damage on it.
Physical | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Deals damage equal to 1.5x opponent's attack.

### Metal Claw
The target is raked with steel claws. This may also boost the user's Attack stat.
Physical | Power 50 | Accuracy 95 | PP 35 | Chance 10%
May raise user's Attack.

### Meteor Mash
The target is hit with a hard punch fired like a meteor. This may also boost the user's Attack stat.
Physical | Power 90 | Accuracy 90 | PP 10 | Chance 20%
May raise user's Attack.

### Searing Sunraze Smash
Solgaleo uses its Z-Power to attack the target with full force. This move can ignore the effects of the Ability.
Physical | Power 200 | PP 1
Solgaleo-exclusive Z-Move.

### Smart Strike
The user stabs the target with a sharp horn. This attack never misses.
Physical | Power 70 | PP 10

### Spin Out
The user spins furiously by straining its legs, inflicting damage on the target. This also harshly lowers the user's Speed stat.
Physical | Power 100 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Harshly lowers user's Speed.

### Steel Roller
The user attacks while destroying the terrain. This move fails if the ground hasn't turned into a terrain.
Physical | Power 130 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Fails if no Terrain in effect.

### Steel Wing
The target is hit with wings of steel. This may also boost the user's Defense stat.
Physical | Power 70 | Accuracy 90 | PP 25 | Chance 10%
May raise user's Defense.

### Sunsteel Strike
The user slams into the target with the force of a meteor. This move can be used on the target regardless of its Ability.
Physical | Power 100 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Ignores the target's ability.

## Special
### Doom Desire
Two turns after this move is used, a concentrated bundle of light blasts the target.
Special | Power 140 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Damage occurs 2 turns later.

### Flash Cannon
The user gathers all its light energy and releases it at once. This may also lower the target's Sp. Def stat.
Special | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10 | Chance 10%
May lower opponent's Special Defense.

### Make It Rain
The user attacks by throwing out a mass of coins. This also lowers the user's Sp. Atk stat. Money is earned after the battle.
Special | Power 120 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Lowers user's Special Attack. Money is earned after the battle.

### Mirror Shot
The user lets loose a flash of energy at the target from its polished body. This may also lower the target's accuracy.
Special | Power 65 | Accuracy 85 | PP 10 | Chance 30%
May lower opponent's Accuracy.

### Steel Beam
The user fires a beam of steel that it collected from its entire body. This also damages the user.
Special | Power 140 | Accuracy 95 | PP 5
User loses 50% of its HP.

### Tachyon Cutter
The user attacks by launching particle blades at the target twice in a row. This attack never misses.
Special | Power 50 | Accuracy ∞ | PP 10
Guaranteed to hit twice in a row.

## Status

Status | PP 15
Reduces weight and sharply raises Speed.

### Gear Up

Status | PP 20

### Iron Defense
The user hardens its body's surface like iron, sharply boosting its Defense stat.
Status | PP 15
Sharply raises user's Defense.

### King's Shield
The user takes a defensive stance while it protects itself from damage. It also lowers the Attack stat of any attacker that makes direct contact.
Status | PP 10
Protects the user and lowers opponent's Attack on contact.

### Metal Sound
A horrible sound like scraping metal harshly lowers the target's Sp. Def stat.
Status | Accuracy 85 | PP 40
Sharply lowers opponent's Special Defense.

### Shelter
The user makes its skin as hard as an iron shield, sharply boosting its Defense stat.
Status | PP 10
Raises user's Defense.

### Shift Gear
The user rotates its gears, boosting its Attack stat and sharply boosting its Speed stat.
Status | PP 10
Raises user's Attack and sharply raises Speed.
