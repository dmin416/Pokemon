# Dark

## Physical
### Assurance
This move's power is doubled if the target has already taken some damage in the same turn.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Power doubles if opponent already took damage in the same turn.

### Beat Up
The user gets all party Pokémon to attack the target. The more party Pokémon, the greater the number of attacks.
Physical | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Each Pokémon in user's party attacks.

### Bite
The user bites the target with viciously sharp fangs to inflict damage.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 25 | Chance 30%
May cause flinching.

### Brutal Swing
The user swings its body around violently to inflict damage on everything in its vicinity.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20

### Ceaseless Edge
The user slashes its shell blade at the target. Shell splinters left behind by this attack remain scattered under the target as spikes.
Physical | Power 65 | Accuracy 90 | PP 15
Sets up Spikes.

### Comeuppance
The user retaliates with much greater force against the opponent that last inflicted damage on it.
Physical | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Deals more damage to the opponent that last inflicted damage on it.

### Crunch
The user crunches up the target with sharp fangs. This may also lower the target's Defense stat.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15 | Chance 20%
May lower opponent's Defense.

### Darkest Lariat
The user swings both arms and hits the target. The target's stat changes don't affect the damage inflicted by this move.
Physical | Power 85 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Ignores opponent's stat changes.

### False Surrender
The user pretends to bow its head, but then it stabs the target with its disheveled hair. This attack never misses.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy ∞ | PP 10
Ignores Accuracy and Evasiveness.

### Feint Attack
The user approaches the target disarmingly, then throws a sucker punch. This attack never misses.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy ∞ | PP 20
Ignores Accuracy and Evasiveness.

### Fling
The user flings its held item at the target to attack. This move's power and effects depend on the item.
Physical | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Power depends on held item.

### Foul Play
The user turns the target's strength against it. The higher the target's Attack stat, the greater the damage this move inflicts.
Physical | Power 95 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Uses the opponent's Attack stat.

### Hyperspace Fury
Using its many arms, the user unleashes a barrage of attacks that ignore the effects of moves like Protect and Detect. This also lowers the user's Defense stat.
Physical | Power 100 | Accuracy ∞ | PP 5 | Chance 100%
Lowers user's Defense. Can strike through Protect/Detect.

### Jaw Lock
This move prevents the user and the target from switching out until either of them faints. The effect goes away if either of the Pokémon leaves the field.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Prevents user and opponent from switching out.

### Knock Off
The user slaps down the target's held item, making it unusable for that battle. This move does more damage if the target has a held item.
Physical | Power 65 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20
Removes opponent's held item for the rest of the battle.

### Kowtow Cleave
The user slashes at the target after kowtowing to make the target let down its guard. This attack never misses.
Physical | Power 85 | Accuracy ∞ | PP 10
Always hits.

### Lash Out
The user lashes out to vent its frustration toward the target. This move's power is doubled if the user's stats were lowered during this turn.
Physical | Power 75 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Double power if stats were lowered during the turn.

### Malicious Moonsault
The user, Incineroar, strengthens its body using its Z-Power and crashes into the target with full force.
Physical | Power 180 | PP 1
Incineroar-exclusive Z-Move.

### Night Slash
The user slashes the target the instant an opportunity arises. This move has a heightened chance of landing a critical hit.
Physical | Power 70 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
High critical hit ratio.

### Payback
The user stores power, then attacks. This move's power is doubled if the user moves after the target.
Physical | Power 50 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Power doubles if the user was attacked first.

### Power Trip
The user boasts its strength and attacks the target. The more the user's stats are raised, the greater the move's power.
Physical | Power 20 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10

### Punishment
The more the target has powered up with stat changes, the greater the move's power.
Physical | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Power increases when opponent's stats have been raised.

### Pursuit
The user gets revenge on the target if the target switches out during the same turn. If so, this move hits with double power.
Physical | Power 40 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20
Double power if the opponent is switching out.

### Sucker Punch
This move enables the user to attack first. This move fails if the target is not readying an attack.
Physical | Power 70 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
User attacks first, but only works if opponent is readying an attack.

### Thief
The user attacks and steals the target's held item simultaneously. The user can't steal anything if it already holds an item.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 25
Also steals opponent's held item.

### Throat Chop
The user attacks the target's throat, and the resultant suffering prevents the target from using sound-based moves for two turns.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15 | Chance 100%
Prevents use of sound moves for two turns.

### Wicked Blow
The user, having mastered the Dark style, strikes the target with a fierce blow. This move always lands a critical hit.
Physical | Power 75 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5
Always results in a critical hit and ignores stat changes.

### Wicked Torque
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
May put opponent to sleep.

## Special
### Baddy Bad
The user acts badly and attacks the target. A wondrous wall of light is put up to reduce damage from physical attacks for five turns.
Special | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Reduces damage from Physical attacks.

### Dark Pulse
The user releases a horrible aura imbued with ill intent. This may also make the target flinch.
Special | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15 | Chance 20%
May cause flinching.

### Fiery Wrath
The user transforms its wrath into a fire-like aura to attack. This may also make opposing Pokémon flinch.
Special | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10 | Chance 20%
May cause flinching.

### Night Daze
The user looses a pitch-black shock wave at the target to inflict damage. This may also lower the target's accuracy.
Special | Power 85 | Accuracy 95 | PP 10 | Chance 40%
May lower opponent's Accuracy.

### Ruination
The user summons a ruinous disaster. This cuts the target's HP in half.
Special | Accuracy 90 | PP 10
Halves the opponent's HP.

### Snarl
The user yells as if it's ranting about something, which lowers the Sp. Atk stats of opposing Pokémon.
Special | Power 55 | Accuracy 95 | PP 15 | Chance 100%
Lowers opponent's Special Attack.

## Status
### Dark Void
Opposing Pokémon are dragged into a world of total darkness that puts them to sleep.
Status | Accuracy 50 | PP 10
Puts all adjacent opponents to sleep.

### Embargo
This move prevents the target from using its held item for five turns. Its Trainer is also prevented from using items on it.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Opponent cannot use items.

### Fake Tears
The user feigns crying to fluster the target. This harshly lowers the target's Sp. Def stat.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 20
Sharply lowers opponent's Special Defense.

### Flatter
Flattery is used to confuse the target. However, this also boosts the target's Sp. Atk stat.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Confuses opponent, but raises its Special Attack.

### Hone Claws
The user sharpens its claws to boost its Attack stat and accuracy.
Status | PP 15
Raises user's Attack and Accuracy.

### Memento
The user faints. In return, the target's Attack and Sp. Atk stats are harshly lowered.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
User faints, sharply lowers opponent's Attack and Special Attack.

### Nasty Plot
The user stimulates its brain by thinking bad thoughts. This sharply boosts the user's Sp. Atk stat.
Status | PP 20
Sharply raises user's Special Attack.

### Obstruct
This move enables the user to protect itself from all attacks. Its chance of failing rises if it is used in succession. Direct contact harshly lowers the attacker's Defense stat.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Protects the user and sharply lowers Defence on contact.

### Parting Shot
With a parting threat, the user lowers the target's Attack and Sp. Atk stats. Then it switches places with a party Pokémon in waiting.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 20 | Chance 100%
Lowers opponent's Attack and Special Attack then switches out.

### Quash
The user suppresses the target and makes its move go last.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Makes the target act last this turn.

### Snatch
The user steals the effects of any attempt to use a healing or stat-changing move.
Status | PP 10
Steals the effects of the opponent's next move.

### Switcheroo
The user trades held items with the target faster than the eye can follow.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Swaps held items with the opponent.

### Taunt
The target is taunted into a rage that allows it to use only attack moves for three turns.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 20
Opponent can only use moves that attack.

### Topsy-Turvy
All stat changes affecting the target turn topsy-turvy and become the opposite of what they were.
Status | PP 20
Reverses stat changes of opponent.

### Torment
The user torments and enrages the target, making it incapable of using the same move twice in a row.
Status | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Opponent cannot use the same move in a row.

PP 1
Dark type Z-Move.

Accuracy ∞ | PP 5
Grimmsnarl-exclusive G-Max Move. Makes opponents drowsy.

Chance 100%
