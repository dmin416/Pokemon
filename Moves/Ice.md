# Ice

## Physical
### Avalanche
This move's power is doubled if the target has inflicted damage on the user in the same turn.
Physical | Power 60 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10
Power doubles if user took damage first.

### Freeze Shock
The turn after using this move, the user hits the target with electrically charged ice to inflict damage. This may also leave the target with paralysis.
Physical | Power 140 | Accuracy 90 | PP 5 | Chance 30%
Charges on first turn, attacks on second. May paralyze opponent.

### Glacial Lance
The user attacks by hurling a blizzard-cloaked icicle lance at opposing Pokémon.
Physical | Power 120 | Accuracy 100 | PP 5

### Ice Ball
The user attacks the target for five turns. The move's power increases each time it hits.
Physical | Power 30 | Accuracy 90 | PP 20
Doubles in power each turn for 5 turns.

### Ice Fang
The user bites the target with cold-infused fangs to deal damage. This may also leave the target with frostbite.
Physical | Power 65 | Accuracy 95 | PP 15 | Chance 10%
May cause flinching and/or freeze opponent.

### Ice Hammer
The user swings its strong, heavy fist at the target to inflict damage. This also lowers the user's Speed stat.
Physical | Power 100 | Accuracy 90 | PP 10 | Chance 100%
Lowers user's Speed.

### Ice Punch
The target is attacked with an icy punch. This may also leave the target frozen.
Physical | Power 75 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15 | Chance 10%
May freeze opponent.

### Ice Shard
The user flash-freezes chunks of ice and hurls them at the target. This also raises the user's action speed.
Physical | Power 40 | Accuracy 100 | PP 30
User attacks first.

### Ice Spinner
The user covers its feet in thin ice and twirls around, slamming into the target. This move's spinning motion also destroys the terrain.
Physical | Power 80 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Removes effects of Terrain.

### Icicle Crash
The user attacks by crashing large icicles onto the target. This may also make the target flinch.
Physical | Power 85 | Accuracy 90 | PP 10 | Chance 30%
May cause flinching.

### Icicle Spear
The user attacks by launching sharp icicles at the target. This move hits two to five times in a row.
Physical | Power 25 | Accuracy 100 | PP 30
Hits 2-5 times in one turn.

### Mountain Gale
The user hurls giant chunks of ice at the target to inflict damage. This may also make the target flinch.
Physical | Power 100 | Accuracy 85 | PP 10
Lowers user's Speed.

### Triple Axel
A consecutive three-kick attack that becomes more powerful with each successful hit.
Physical | Power 20 | Accuracy 90 | PP 10
Attacks thrice with more power each time.

## Special

Special | Power 65 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20 | Chance 10%
May lower opponent's Attack.

### Blizzard

Special | Power 110 | Accuracy 70 | PP 5 | Chance 10%
May freeze opponent.

### Freeze-Dry
The user rapidly cools the target. This may also leave the target frozen. This move is super effective on Water types.
Special | Power 70 | Accuracy 100 | PP 20 | Chance 10%
May freeze opponent. Super-effective against Water types.

### Freezy Frost

Special | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 15
Resets all stat changes.

### Frost Breath

Special | Power 60 | Accuracy 90 | PP 10 | Chance 100%
Always results in a critical hit.

### Glaciate

Special | Power 65 | Accuracy 95 | PP 10 | Chance 100%
Lowers opponent's Speed.

### Ice Beam

Special | Power 90 | Accuracy 100 | PP 10 | Chance 10%
May freeze opponent.

### Ice Burn

### Icy Wind

### Powder Snow

Special | Power 40 | Accuracy 100 | PP 25 | Chance 10%

### Sheer Cold

Special | Accuracy 30 | PP 5

## Status
### Aurora Veil
This move reduces damage from physical and special moves for five turns. This can be used only when it is snowing.
Status | PP 20
Halves damage from Physical and Special attacks for five turns.

### Chilly Reception
The user tells a chillingly bad joke before switching places with a party Pokémon in waiting. This summons a snowstorm lasting five turns.
Status | PP 10
Switches out and summons a snowstorm lasting 5 turns.

### Hail
The user summons a hailstorm lasting five turns. It damages all Pokémon except Ice types.
Status | PP 10
Non-Ice types are damaged for 5 turns.

### Haze
The user creates a haze that eliminates every stat change among all the Pokémon engaged in battle.
Status | PP 30
Resets all stat changes.

### Mist
The user cloaks itself and its allies in a white mist that prevents any of their stats from being lowered for five turns.
Status | PP 30
User's stats cannot be changed for a period of time.

### Snowscape
The user summons a snowstorm lasting five turns. This boosts the Defense stats of Ice types.
Status | PP 10
Raises Defense of Ice types for 5 turns.
