Johan had paid a huge price to get Groudon, Kyogre, and Rayquaza to temporarily accept Palkia entering Hoenn to "work."

So the spatial tunnel had just begun construction and would take considerable time to complete.

Hoenn Region, Kanaz City

Clear skies, not a cloud in sight.

"Mewtwo, back already?"

Just stepping out of Hoopa's golden ring, he saw Mewtwo wearing a cool hoodie with the hood up, carrying a shopping basket back.

Hearing this, Mewtwo glanced at him and nodded slightly.

[Yes.]

Hm?

Mewtwo's reaction made Johan pause slightly.

This was...

Cynthia also sensed Mewtwo's state, released Johan's hand, and said softly:

"Go take a look."

"Mm."

Johan followed Mewtwo into the kitchen.

Mewtwo, who had removed its hood and was processing vegetables, wasn't surprised to see Johan enter and showed little reaction.

Johan didn't immediately ask questions, just rolled up his sleeves, put on an apron, and helped Mewtwo wash vegetables.

Splash splash splash—

Only the sound of water remained in the quiet kitchen.

After a while, Mewtwo spoke first.

[Johan.]

"Mm?"

Mewtwo looked at Johan across from it, its originally cold expression unconsciously softening considerably.

[I want... to go out and see the world.]

Hearing this, Johan paused again.

Go out and see the world?

That's right.

When Mewtwo originally came to him, it was also to experience what it felt like to be human. With so much time having passed, it made sense.

It just inexplicably made Johan feel like maybe he had been "exploiting" it too harshly.

Thinking back over a year ago, Mewtwo couldn't even determine its own name or existence and was still seeking psychological counseling...

On second thought—

The current Mewtwo had grown a lot and learned many things. Whether steaming, frying, sautéing, or stir-frying, it was proficient in everything.

"That's good, go out and see the world." Johan nodded with a smile.

His agreement wasn't beyond Mewtwo's expectations.

[Before leaving...]

Under Johan's bewildered gaze, Mewtwo took out a "Dark Ball."

"This is..."

[This is what I chose from your pile of Poké Balls.]

While speaking, Mewtwo pressed the Dark Ball into Johan's hand.

Then, under Johan's increasingly astonished gaze, it gently touched the switch on the Dark Ball.

Hum—

The Dark Ball opened with red light flashing, and Mewtwo disappeared from the kitchen.

Click—

Without any extra commotion, the Dark Ball locked directly.

I... captured Mewtwo?

Johan looked at the Dark Ball in his hand, completely dazed.

"Ka-kaah!!"

At the doorway, Gengar suddenly shouted loudly, startling Johan as he looked toward the sound.

He saw...

Tyranitar, Dragonite, Urshifu, Serperior, Ceruledge, Gengar, Indeedee, Ninetales, Tinkaton, Rotom—all these Pokémon had their heads stacked from top to bottom, filling the doorframe completely.

"Tar!" "Draaa!" "Garruu!" "Sni!" "Goka!" "Ka-kaah!" "Ai!" "Oowoo!" "Cha!" "Roto!"...

On the other side of the doorframe, Cynthia stood with arms crossed, also smiling.

Captured a "new" Pokémon!

Click—

The Dark Ball opened, and Mewtwo appeared with a stern face, pulled up its hood, and continued washing vegetables with its back to everyone.

"Ka-kaah?"

Gengar suddenly appeared in front of Mewtwo, squinting at the space under the hood.

"Ka-kaah! (σ゜∀゜)σ"

It's blushing!

[You're the one blushing!]

Boom!

The little ones all burst into laughter.

Seeing Mewtwo about to explode, Johan quickly said:

"Alright, alright, today I'll show you all my skills—consider it celebrating that tomorrow is another beautiful day!"

With that, Indeedee, Alcremie, and Rotom immediately came in to help.

Gengar also wanted to enter but was stopped by Johan pointing at the sign on the door reading "Cynthia and Gengar Not Allowed."

"Ka-kah..."

Gengar drooped its ears, sneaking a glance at Johan who wasn't fooled by its pitiful act, then wiggled its little butt and ran off to play in the backyard.

Seeing this, Johan couldn't help but smile wryly.

Thinking back to when he first captured Gengar, it was still a Haunter that liked to secretly suck gas. At that time, Johan's wallet was tight, yet he kept paying gas bills, until later learning that Gastly had been sneaking gas every night...

In the blink of an eye—

That Gastly who called itself "this old man" had now grown to nearly rival legendary Pokémon...

"Time," Johan murmured softly, "truly is a mysterious thing."

Night fell.

Old Jing's family sat around the dining table in the garden, with stars twinkling above and moonlight flowing down like black velvet.

Below, warm orange light flickered with endless laughter.

"Ahem."

Johan cleared his throat and raised his berry juice: "I'll say a few words."

Cynthia, Mewtwo, Gengar, and all the little ones looked toward him.

Feeling everyone's gaze, Johan didn't know what inspiring or touching words to say.

After all, this wasn't some life-and-death separation. Tomorrow's sun would rise as usual, and everyone was destined to be together for a long, long, long time...

So...

"Cheers!"

He took Cynthia's hand and smiled: "To a beautiful tomorrow!"

"Ka-kaah!" Gengar leaped up.

Click—

Rotom timely recorded this day among the many celebratory days—unremarkable, without much commotion, and quite "ordinary."

But in each day that followed, everyone's smiles would be just as brilliant as today.

Cheers to a beautiful tomorrow!
