# 151: Chapter 152: Flowers Bloom, Combee Visits

In front of the wooden house, Summer set up a bench and watched as Rillaboom and Ogerpon engaged in a practice battle.

Rillaboom had learned various techniques for controlling roots from Trevenant and was now experimenting with new drumming styles, using the agile and nimble Ogerpon as a real combat target.

Enemies like Ogerpon were the most difficult for Rillaboom to deal with.

As for Ogerpon, it treated the battle as a game, simply using it to burn off its overflowing energy.

Countless thin vines sprouted from the ground around Ogerpon, simultaneously launching an attack, attempting to restrict its movements.

"Boni-Oh?!"

Ogerpon was startled and frantically looked around. Thinking it couldn't dodge, it pulled out a burning thorny stick and swung it in a wide arc, burning away all the vines trying to approach it.

"Gao!"

It let out a proud cry, seemingly pleased with its quick judgment.

It was Rillaboom who had asked Ogerpon to use the Hearthflame Mask. It didn't want the training to be too easy and wanted to increase the difficulty this way.

"Trying to completely restrict the opponent's movements with numbers isn't a bad strategy," Summer analyzed, "but spreading them out weakens their strength, making it easier for them to be destroyed. That defeats the original purpose. Also, some of the vines' actions are conflicting, and having too many vines might affect your control, making your attacks easier to predict. Try reducing the number, Rillaboom."

"Boom…" Rillaboom nodded silently, absorbing Summer's advice.

"Ding-ling~"

At this moment, a melodious sound echoed from afar. Lilligant and Hisuian Lilligant approached, one with graceful steps and the other with energetic strides.

Ogerpon lost interest in the battle immediately and joyfully ran toward the two sisters.

"Lilligant, Hisuian Lilligant, it's rare to see you leave the Gracidea Prairie on your own. Is something the matter?" Summer stood up and asked.

Lilligant smiled mysteriously and pulled out a small pink flower from behind her. Summer took a closer look and then suddenly realized, "Ah, so the Gracidea Flowers are blooming."

As plants, the Gracidea Flowers naturally bloom, but due to their small size and short blooming period, they're hardly ever considered ornamental flowers.

"Yes, it's earlier than expected. Could it be because of the plate? By the way, how are the other flowers doing?"

Lilligant shook her head, indicating that they hadn't bloomed yet. However, she smiled again and gestured that many buds had already formed.

"Heh, I'm starting to look forward to it," Summer smiled. "Rillaboom, Ogerpon, you've been training for a while now. Let's take this opportunity to rest and go enjoy the flowers."

"Boni-Oh!"

Ogerpon raised both hands in agreement, and Rillaboom also happily agreed. Then, Summer thought for a moment and said, "Since that's the case, let's invite everyone to join us."

"So, let's split up and meet later at Gracidea Prairie for a flower-viewing party."

The lush green grass was dotted with small pink Gracidea Flowers. Although the flowers were small, they were numerous, creating a magnificent sight like a sky full of stars.

Summer's Pokémon and their friends gathered there, admiring the blooming flowers together.

Lilligant and Hisuian Lilligant danced gracefully in the meadow, adding joy to the event.

Rillaboom also brought out its wooden drum. In such a lively setting, there's no better time to play a cheerful tune!

Nuzleaf saw this and pulled out a wooden flute to accompany its leader, while Simisage sat on a branch with a sullen expression, feeling defeated. After all this time, it still hadn't won their loyalty.

However, as it listened to the lively drumbeats, a new thought occurred to it. This isn't so bad. Nuzleaf rewards it with berries due to its strategies, and this forest has never discriminated against it.

Perhaps following Summer was the wisest decision it ever made.

Ogerpon sat on Summer's lap, waving its stick in rhythm with the music. Milotic also decided to sing a song. Its voice was clear and melodious—no wonder it's known as the Songstress of the Lake.

Yamper obediently stayed in place, glancing at the Ursaluna clapping beside it. It also wanted to join in on the fun.

Noticing this, Milotic nodded in agreement. Yamper excitedly wagged its tail and raised its arms to hype up the atmosphere. It had been a while since they had such an event—what a great feeling.

Hatterene's eyes gleamed with joy, realizing that even in such an emotionally charged environment, it didn't succumb to rage. All the hard work was finally paying off!

It glanced at Corviknight. Thanks to you, loud bird. But now, is it just me, or do you seem a bit uneasy?

Corviknight puffed out its chest, trying to appear composed. Fezandipiti beside it gazed admiringly at the Lilligant sisters. No wonder they're the masters—they effortlessly captivated the whole crowd. I still have much to learn!

In the spirit world, Trevenant sensed the joyful rhythm through its doppelgänger here. Meanwhile, it captured another Ghost-type Pokémon with one claw. As the newly appointed leader, many still sought to challenge its authority.

The ghostly Pokémon of those troublemakers had been subdued by Trevenant. After witnessing Giratina's blessings, they dared not resist, obediently staying behind to work for Trevenant.

During the battles, they shed tears of regret—it was greed that led them astray.

"Buzz~"

Suddenly, a sound interrupted the concert. Corviknight's eyes widened as it let out a cry. Caw! A bug!

The Pokémon all turned to look at the Combee flying in the air. The little Combee had never seen such a scene before and showed fear on all three of its faces.

Sorry, Your Majesty. I just followed the scent, hoping to collect some nectar. But it seems today will be my end.

"Gao!"

Ursaluna blocked the attacking Corviknight and advised it to calm down. Corviknight, do you know what the appearance of a Combee means?

"Caw?" Corviknight looked confused. What does it mean?

Ursaring smiled nostalgically and replied, "It means honey!"

On the other side, Summer waved, signaling everyone not to scare the little one. At this rate, the poor Combee might die of fright.

"Combee can help pollinate flowers, so they're beneficial for both the flowers and the fruit trees."

Summer picked up Ogerpon, then walked towards the Combee with a smile and said, "Go ahead, don't be shy—gather the nectar."

Combee looked incredulous. Why is this human so kind?

"But, I do have a favor to ask you for your queen."

"Buzz?"

"Consider settling in this forest. We have excellent fruit trees and flowers."

In reality, Summer's goal was also honey, but he had other considerations. Besides aiding in natural pollination, he was curious about the taste of honey produced in Sunset Forest.

With superior seeds, improved varieties, and the energy nourishment from the plate, these conditions might just create the finest honey ever!

In the cabin, Summer was researching the habits of Combee online. No matter how he looked at it, the conditions in the Sunset Forest seemed irresistible to them.

"I'm heading to the Snowy Forest. Rillaboom, you haven't been there before, so come with me."

Ogerpon pointed at herself. What about me?

"Of course, you're coming too, Ogerpon. But first, you both need scarves—Rillaboom, you too." Summer held up a large and small scarf, handing them to his Pokémon.

Rillaboom watched as Summer carefully wrapped a scarf around Ogerpon's neck and then tried to wrap one around himself. You know, this does feel warmer right away.

In the Snowy Forest, a white-scarved elderly man rested his hands on a cane, eyes closed, relishing the familiar cold wind with a serene expression.

"Grandpa Pryce?"

The old man slowly opened his eyes, turning to face Summer, who was dressed in winter gear. He nodded and said, "Ah, it's you, Summer. Yes, proper warmth is essential."

"Well, it is cold after all. But Grandpa Pryce, you seem unusually calm?" Summer asked.

"My usual training involves sitting on ice, so this cold doesn't bother me."

Sitting on ice—how much willpower would that take? No, that's not the point, Summer thought, shaking his head. "A forest suddenly sprouting a snowy area—doesn't that surprise you?"

Grandpa Pryce chuckled. "At my age, little surprises me anymore. This world is far more magical than we can imagine."

"Hmm, I find you rather pure, actually. And it must take great resolve to maintain such rigorous training even now," Summer said sincerely.

Grandpa Pryce withdrew one hand, placing it in his pocket, and said softly, "I can see why Gideon values you so highly. Long ago, I was quite immature, changed by something unforgettable."

"Unforgettable?"

"Yes, in my youth, I thought myself powerful and sought to conquer the legendary being, Articuno." Grandpa Pryce smiled wryly. "It was a mighty force of ice, and my arrogance put my partner in danger. I tried to save it, but even I collapsed."

"Maybe it was luck that saved us both. As I lay in bed recovering, I reflected deeply, abandoning my arrogance and rashness, becoming as rational as the cold, as resilient as ice."

"And then I truly grew. Since then, I've believed that both humans and Pokémon grow through hardship, like the bright spring after a harsh winter."

Grandpa Pryce retrieved a Poké Ball, looking at Summer. "So, Summer, how about a battle to experience the cold? And like Gideon, I'll have a prize for the winner."

"I'd love that! Rillaboom, it's time to show the results of your training," Summer replied enthusiastically.

"Oh, Rillaboom, is it?" Grandpa Pryce threw the Poké Ball, his expression turning icy. "Let's see how well you endure the winter!"

"Weavile!"

A fierce Weavile appeared before Grandpa Pryce, sharpening its icy claws with a predatory look.

"I see, Weavile. We should take this seriously, Rillaboom!"

Weavile, known for its danger, cunning, and agility, was a tough opponent for Rillaboom. On top of that, it had a type advantage.

"Agility," Grandpa Pryce commanded first.

Weavile, accustomed to cold environments, moved effortlessly through the snow, zigzagging and relaxing its body. It was confident it could dodge any attack.

"Rillaboom, Drum Beating, Trap!"

Rillaboom pounded on its drum, and three tough vines surged from the snowy ground, forming a triangular cage around Weavile.

"Ogerpon!" cheered Ogerpon, waving its club. Show them how scary this move is!

Grandpa Pryce's eyes gleamed with surprise. "Charge forward, cut through them without hesitation."

Weavile darted forward like a shadow. As the vines struck, it leaped and slashed one apart. Another vine appeared behind it as it attacked, but it effortlessly cut that one too.

Yet, the final vine struck from an angle Weavile couldn't dodge. It smirked, confident in its speed after using Agility.

Weavile rolled nimbly, landing gracefully. Now, it only needed a moment to close in and strike. In other words—

"Checkmate."

Huh? Weavile froze, shocked. Didn't I dodge all the attacks? Wait, what?

A thick vine suddenly shot up beneath Weavile's feet. Darn it, those attacks were just bait!

Weavile quickly reacted, slashing at the vine, but it was sturdier than expected, trapping its claw.

"Hammer Arm!"

Rillaboom's full-force punch landed on Weavile, who, as a Dark and Ice-type Pokémon, couldn't withstand it. Its frail defenses were its greatest weakness.

"Well done, Summer," Grandpa Pryce smiled as he recalled Weavile. "Your understanding of Pokémon, mastery of moves, and creativity are impressive."

"It seems I didn't show you the cold after all. Instead, I got a lesson from you. You're like a breath of spring after winter."

"You flatter me. This technique is still in its early stages, and it just happens to counter Pokémon like Weavile," Summer replied modestly.

"You've got plenty of time to refine it, so accept the praise," Grandpa Pryce said, waving his hand. He then took out a luxury ball. "As promised, here's a prize for the victor. I believe you'll encounter a similar Pokémon in the future."

The temperature around them dropped instantly as a magnificent bird appeared. Its wings were made of icy blue feathers, and the ice crown on its head symbolized its reign over ice. Its tail fluttered elegantly like a frozen banner, creating ice crystals with every move.

"Articuno! Grandpa Pryce, did you catch the Articuno from Twin Islands?"

"No, this one is from a similar group, but it's not the Ice God itself. It's a different, though still powerful, Articuno," Grandpa Pryce explained.

A beautiful, crystalline feather drifted from Articuno's body into Grandpa Pryce's hand. It was accustomed to gifting its feathers to those it acknowledged.

"This feather will help you master and enhance Ice-type moves and aid in raising Ice-type Pokémon. I'm sure you'll make good use of it."

Grandpa Pryce solemnly handed the feather to Summer. Even through his gloves, he could feel its coldness. Just then, his phone vibrated, and Grandpa Pryce motioned for him to go ahead.

Summer nodded, checking his phone. It was filled with messages, but they all conveyed the same thing: Marnie was challenging the Ballonlea Gym tomorrow.
