# 51: Chapter 51: Dragonite's Decision

Dragonite had never been this happy before.

When it was young, no one taught it, and it couldn't grasp those powerful moves by itself, so every time it could only stand aside and watch the other Dragonites on the island summoning the wind and rain with longing eyes.

On the island, it was known as the dumb Dragonite.

Although it wasn't ridiculed by others, Dragonite still felt sad because of it.

Especially since the Dragonite it liked was also stronger than it.

So, every night, it would quietly leave Dragonite Island and go to a secluded place to daydream.

Now, it had successfully learned Dragon Tail, and the number of moves it mastered had increased from four to five.

This was truly something that made the dragon happy.

Thus, it wagged its tail, strutting proudly around the pasture.

First stop, it came to Cyclizar lying under the eaves enjoying the shade.

Dragonite used Dragon Tail and happily exclaimed to Cyclizar, "Awoo!"

"Gao?"

Cyclizar looked at Dragonite in confusion, with a dumbfounded and adorable expression.

Then, its eyes lit up, as if it had thought of something, and it stood up, performing Dragon Tail towards the air.

It learned this move too!

Dragonite: "Awoo..."

Seeing Cyclizar's bewildered gaze, Dragonite turned around and headed to the Skiddo pen.

Like a child who scored high on a test, it couldn't wait to share its "grades" with its friends.

The three Skiddos watched Dragonite's actions innocently.

At first, they were also a bit confused.

But soon, the three Skiddos suddenly understood.

The new moves, they had also comprehended a lot of them!

The Skiddos began to demonstrate as well.

Grassy Terrain, Synthesis, Solar Beam, Vine Whip, Razor Leaf...

Green light enveloped the entire pen, the robust Solar Beam swept across the sky, green vines danced gracefully, and verdant leaves fluttered like snowflakes.

What was clearly an ordinary pen turned into an international fashion stage at this moment.

The Solar Beam became a laser spotlight, the Vine Whip became ropes, and Razor Leaf turned into snowflakes created by a snow machine.

The three Skiddos transformed into top supermodels, showcasing themselves in the dazzling light beams.

Amidst the bleating, Dragonite felt a bit dazed.

Thus, D, who was busy in the field, soon saw Dragonite running back, even forgetting to take off the red pouch on its waist.

"Awoo!"

D was a bit surprised, "What happened this time?"

"Awoo..."

Dragonite pointed with its claw at the Skiddos and then at itself, feeling sad.

D was at a loss and guessed, "Did the Skiddos bully you?"

Dragonite shook its head, then used Dragon Tail, and let out a determined cry, "Awoo!"

D understood, "You learned Dragon Tail and now want to learn other moves?"

"Awoo!"

Dragonite nodded repeatedly.

"I see!"

D began to ponder what other moves would be suitable for Dragonite to learn.

And at that moment, he suddenly thought of the Berry Platter from before.

Previously, he was worried that Dragonite's trust levels weren't high enough, and consuming the Berry Platter might cause it to forget moves, losing trust in him, so D didn't let it use it.

Now, seeing Dragonite's current state, it seemed ready to accept it.

With a brief thought, D gave the choice to Dragonite:

"I have a very special way to help you learn new moves."

Dragonite's eyes lit up with a gleam, looking over expectantly, "Awoo?"

D continued, "But that method is beyond my control and isn't guaranteed. It might make you forget current moves, or it might help you learn new ones."

"Awoo..." Dragonite hesitated upon hearing it might forget moves.

D didn't push, patiently waiting by the side.

Dragonite's mind flashed with life on the island and the dazzling moves just showcased by the Skiddos.

After a while, it finally made up its mind.

"Awoo!"

"Willing to try?" D asked.

Dragonite nodded.

"Alright then."

D exhaled, having just finished the farm work, and led Dragonite back to the cabin.

He first washed his hands covered in soil and then took out the remaining few berries from the cupboard.

The berries were enough to make two servings.

D cut the berries into pieces, placed them on a plate, and handed them to Dragonite.

Dragonite took it with its claws, looking at the berry pieces with curiosity and doubt.

"Awoo?"

Could these ordinary berries really help it comprehend new moves?

"Wanna try?" D suggested.

"Awoo!"

Dragonite nodded, tipped its head back, and poured the berries into its mouth, chewing a little before swallowing them all.

After a moment, D eagerly asked, "Feel any changes within?"

Dragonite carefully sensed, then its eyes brightened, excitedly exclaiming, "Awoo!"

"Figured something out? What is it?" D asked eagerly.

The room was too small for Dragonite to demonstrate, so they both went outside.

Dragonite flapped its wings and left the ground, its energy shifted, and its eyes turned determined.

In the next moment, its body accelerated like a bolt of lightning, moving so fast that it was hard for any opponent to react.

"Extreme Speed," D's mind identified the move.

Soon, Dragonite flew back.

It appeared excited, eager to try once more.

Seeing this, D returned indoors and used the remaining berries to prepare another Berry Platter.

But upon completing it, he had a sudden idea and took out Black Truffle from the cupboard, sprinkling some powder on top.

As D looked down, he noticed that the description of the Berry Platter had changed.

[Berry Platter (B+): A delicacy made from various berries with a fusion of different berry flavors, enhanced with Black Truffle powder to enrich its taste, favored by some Pokemon.

Cooking Effect: Skill Refinement, upon consumption, random change in one of the Pokemon's moves, with increased chance of comprehending powerful rare moves.

Evaluation: A basic dish, easy to make, a great choice for home or travel.]

"Increased chance of comprehending rare moves?" D raised his eyebrows, honestly expecting this effect.

He wasn't sure if it expanded the skill pool or increased the chance of learning moves.

With a brief thought, he handed the enhanced Berry Platter to Dragonite.

Dragonite gulped it down.

Moments later, it became teary-eyed.

"Awoo..."

D: "..."

"What did you forget?" he asked.

Dragonite wagged its tail, trying to use Dragon Tail but failed.

Well, that's unfortunate, just learned and already forgotten?

With a brief thought, D comforted, "Don't worry, you remember how you learned Dragon Tail, right?"

"Awoo..." Dragonite nodded sadly.

D: "So, the experience is still there. As long as you practice again, it shouldn't take long to recall the technique for using Dragon Tail."

After experiencing the power of the Berry Platter, Dragonite felt a bit timid inside.

It didn't dare continue and pitifully went outside to resume practicing Dragon Tail.

D followed it out, standing under the eaves watching the diligent Dragonite practice, and couldn't help but sigh.

After a while, he murmured, "Out of berries at home, seems like I'll need to replenish them from town."

"That's amazing!" Yousuke exclaimed in admiration.

D chuckled silently, thinking that it's okay if the crops get harvested a bit later; he took this time to show Yousuke around his pasture.

"Are they the three you captured last time?" Yousuke asked.

D nodded. Just as he was about to say something more, one of the Skiddos stood up and lazily shook its head.

The next second, a rich green light emanated from its body.

This light seeped into the ground, creating a large area of Grassy Terrain.

D: "..."

Was it an illusion? This Grassy Terrain seemed to cover a larger range than the ones he had seen before.

The Skiddo had become so proficient with the Grassy Terrain move that it was almost turning from Grassy Terrain into Grassy Field.

These days, the three Skiddos ate many Grass Knot balls every day.

Consuming this magical food over time kept their bodies filled with Grass Type Energy, so much that they felt uncomfortable if it wasn't released daily.

Compared to the destructive Solar Beam, Grassy Terrain has no such concerns.

So, to vent their excess Grass Type Energy, the three Skiddos consistently used Grassy Terrain every day.

At first, D was a bit worried.

To address this, he specifically went to the town's bookstore to buy a related book and learned a professional term from it.

— Energy Leak.

This refers to when some Pokémon have more energy than they can control, causing it to overflow and negatively impact the nearby environment.

For example, consider a Pokémon like Magneton.

If they have too much Electric Type Energy, it can cause electromagnetic disturbances throughout the town, affecting the communication signals and power systems.

However, for now, it seems the situation with the Skiddo is quite different from the case with Magneton because it can manage its Energy well.

"That's incredible!" Yousuke was dumbfounded.

D thought to himself that Yousuke hasn't seen anything yet; these three Skiddos sometimes use Grassy Terrain as a dance floor!

But he only thought about it and didn't say it out loud.

Yousuke murmured, "When I saw them battle with Koraidon before, I couldn't tell they were this incredible! Did you give them specific special training?"

"No, maybe it's their own hard work?" D continued, "Come on, let me show you how Nacli are living now."

The door to the Salt House was wide open, and inside, the Naclis were playfully chasing each other around the stones on the ground.

"Hey, little guys! Long time no see!"

"Salt salt!"

"Do you remember me?" Yousuke pointed to himself and asked.

The Naclis replied affirmatively.

D stood by, smiling as he watched this scene.

Yousuke, observing the Naclis' condition, couldn't help but say, "It looks like you took great care of them! That's wonderful."

"Yes, indeed!"

D also felt a bit sentimental.

Just then, Dragonite's call came from outside.

Upon hearing this, Yousuke appeared puzzled.

D explained, "It should be Dragonite coming back."

With that, he said goodbye to the Naclis and turned to leave the Salt House.

Sure enough, they saw Dragonite on the lawn outside, carrying a red waist pack.

It slowly flew over to D, happily taking out the payment for delivering milk from its pack and handing it over, "Awoo~"

"Deliver... milk?" Yousuke's eyes widened, his thoughts momentarily stalled.

"Any issues?" D asked, puzzled.

Nothing they do should be surprising.

For instance, Professor Laventon and the earliest Poke Balls.

New life would soon thrive in the pasture again.

D led Yousuke back to the wooden house to serve him a glass of juice.

His attention was instantly caught, "Is that... Metapod?"

Due to the impact of [Furious Parrot], coastal areas are expected to have heavy rainfall over the next three days, occasionally accompanied by strong winds. We ask all citizens to secure their doors and windows and prepare in advance."

Typhoon?

Koraidon also stood up, shook its head, and regained its spirit.

For ordinary Pokémon, weather with such oppressive nature is innately terrifying.

Mayor Thomas, who takes good care of him, Zack, who is straightforward and generous, the warm and patient ranch elder, and Keri, who is diligent and hardworking...

The rain outside grew heavier, gradually forming a curtain of rain. Unlike the gloomy and somber exterior, the inside of the wooden house was warm and harmonious.

"Wahoo!"

The audience below erupted into cheers, and in this lively atmosphere, this year's Spring Competition officially came to a close.

Carrying the champion trophy and a photograph with the three Gogoat taken with the help of Mayor Thomas, D rode back to the ranch on Koraidon, content and satisfied.

He placed the trophy and the photo on his desk and gazed at the picture.

In the photo, he stood on the grass with the three Gogoat, looking at the camera, capturing the moment.

It was quite a memorable scene, and D thought to himself that in the future, he should buy a special trophy rack to place the trophies and photos.

As for the half-year's worth of Pokemon food, it was better than nothing!

Mayor Thomas said someone would deliver it tomorrow.

What a joyous day!

Now, it's time to sort out the Teddiursa issue.

Looking at the Teddiursa with the crescent moon mark on its forehead in front of him, D took an empty Poke Ball from the drawer and asked:

"Teddiursa, would you like to stay at the ranch and live here?"

"Yumma?" Teddiursa seemed surprised by D's invitation, letting out a startled growl: "Yumma..."

"What's the matter, you don't want to?"

Teddiursa quickly shook its head: "Yumma Yumma!"

After thinking it over carefully, it made a decision.

The Teddiursa jumped high with force and touched the Poke Ball with its paw, then its body transformed into a red light and was sucked inside.

The Poke Ball gently rocked a few times, then stopped moving.

This indicated a successful capture.

Watching this, D beamed with joy.

Great! The ranch has a new friend!

He opened the Poke Ball, and Teddiursa was released.

D smiled at it, saying, "From now on, make yourself at home at the ranch! I promise there'll be plenty of honey!"

"Yumma~"

Feeling someone care for it, Teddiursa was touched; this time, it didn't pretend to be cute but genuinely acted playfully towards D.

D reached out and scooped up the small bear, rubbing its head vigorously, saying, "That's more like it!"

He played with Teddiursa for a while before turning to prepare lunch.

But Teddiursa seemed to suddenly recall something; after a few exchanges with D, it ran off.

D was puzzled but figured Teddiursa was familiar with the area, so he didn't worry.

So when he finished preparing lunch, he saw Teddiursa struggling to drag a cloth bag back to the ranch.

It handed the cloth bag to D, saying happily, "Yumma Yumma!"

Oh? D was a bit surprised.

He opened the bag, filled with curiosity, and found it contained a pile of trinkets.

Colorful glass marbles, pebbles, small bottles, unknown plant seeds, Big Root, shallow shells, Hard Stone, bones...

Looking at these things, D was slightly astonished and soon realized this was likely the "property" Teddiursa had accumulated over the years.

It brought everything over since it got a new home at the ranch.

Thinking of this, D smiled and said, "Don't worry, I'll help you keep these things safe."

"Yumma~"

At night, after a tiring day, D went to bed early with Teddiursa and Pawmi.

The moon was beautiful that night, and Dragonite, for once, didn't go back to sleep. After a day of practicing moves, it sat alone on the ranch's grass, gazing at the moon.

Meanwhile, a restless Cyclizar also came out, and upon seeing Dragonite moon-gazing on the grass, it walked over and sat down beside it.

Ever since the last Flying Disc game, Cyclizar had been pondering something.

It wanted to become stronger, as strong as the mighty Koraidon, to be of more help to D.

But it couldn't think of a good way. Just then, seeing Dragonite, Cyclizar remembered how it had been diligently practicing ever since coming to the ranch and couldn't help but ask:

"Gao?" (Dragonite, why do you work so hard?)

"Awoo?"

Dragonite blinked. Hearing Cyclizar's question, it started telling its story.

About its birth on a small island, its favorite Dragonite, feeling sad due to weakness, sitting alone on a cliff every night, gazing at the moon.

Cyclizar listened quietly, showing a somewhat understanding expression.

Then it asked, "Gao?" (You learned so many strong moves. Can I learn with you?)

Hearing this, Dragonite was puzzled: "Awoo?" (Koraidon is stronger than me, why not learn from it instead?)

"Gao..." Cyclizar didn't know what to say. It lowered its head, saying, "Gao, Gao..." (It's scary, sometimes it looks at me with frightening eyes, I'm afraid of it...)

Dragonite was a bit confused: "Gao?"

Is there such a thing? It never felt that way itself.
