# Pokemon: Jordinio Version — Granular Timeline

No chapter titles exist anywhere for this story (both FF.net and Webnovel label every chapter just "Chapter N"), so this is built entirely from actual chapter text pulled from fanfiction.net/s/12796968 — chapters 1, 4, 8, 12, 15, 22, 23, 39, 40, 41, 45, 49, 52, 57, 61, 62 — plus the Webnovel catalog for structural markers (Season breaks, the R-18 flag). Gaps between these numbers are real chapters not individually reread in this pass.

**Ch. 1 — Route 1.** Jord Odhar, an adult mind reborn twelve years old, wakes on Route 1 near Pallet Town. The narration is immediately crude and self-aware about the self-insert premise ("short as shit and somehow twelve again... at the very least I can get that Charizard I always dreamed of"). His very first trainer action, before reaching Viridian City, is punching a wild Pidgey to stun it long enough to catch it — that Pidgey is the bird that becomes his signature Pidgeot for the rest of the series.

**Ch. 4 — Team Rocket, day one.** Within his first real day as a trainer, Jord has already tangled with and beaten Team Rocket badly enough that Nurse Joy and Misty rush a recovered-but-shaken Ash and Pikachu into a Pokémon Center recovery room while Officer Jenny processes the arrest. This is the chapter that establishes his relationship to canon: he's not following Ash's story, he's running parallel to it and cleaning up collateral damage.

**Ch. 8 — Viridian Forest and Pewter City.** Framed in-text as "the first big hurdle of most Pokémon stories," Jord clears the forest riding a partly-evolved Pidgeotto for the last stretch specifically to avoid extra walking (and, per his own crude aside, avoid an embarrassing accident from nerves). He picks up a Smeargle here and immediately recognizes how broken Sketch is — a move that permanently copies any move it's seen once, teachable to his other Pokémon — treating it as the biggest strategic find of his early game.

**Ch. 12 — Battling Surge.** An Electivire-vs-Pidgeot fight shows real stakes: Pidgeot's wings take visible damage before Electivire's speed spikes mid-battle from an ability trigger, forcing Jord to adapt on the fly rather than coast on being overpowered.

**Ch. 15 — Hilda's introduction, and an author's slip.** Hilda — reimagined here as a real ongoing travel companion rather than a cameo — is established as present by this point. The chapter's own author's note admits, mid-draft, to having accidentally written an entire section from Hilda's point of view before catching the mistake and snapping back to Jord's first-person narration, which is the clearest window into how loosely and conversationally this particular book gets written.

**Ch. 22–23 — Rematch with Surge, the trainer card.** A second Electivire fight escalates further (a "massively powerful" lightning-covered haymaker lands square on Pidgeot). Ch. 23 pauses to spell out Jord's full stat block directly: thirteen years old, Viridian City listed as home, over 2.8 million in prize money, twenty-seven Pokémon owned, two badges, zero ribbons, a twelve-Pokémon carry limit — the book periodically stops to let the reader see the numbers rather than just narrate around them.

**Ch. 39 — Name-dropping the ceiling.** Cynthia, Sinnoh, Silph Co., and a full roster of regional champions (Lance, Wallace, Steven, Alder) all get referenced in the same stretch, explicitly establishing the power ceiling Jord is being measured against well before he's anywhere near it.

**Ch. 40–41 — Aerodactyl, and Giovanni notices.** Jord catches Aerodactyl among a run of other captures, and the chapter's own internal monologue has him realize Team Rocket's boss almost certainly already knows who he is — a photographer named Todd Snapp had filmed him fighting with both Gyarados and Dragonite, and that footage is framed as having probably reached Giovanni directly. This is the exact point the story marks Jord as a person of interest to Team Rocket's actual leadership, not just its grunts.

**Ch. 45–52 — The dragon core solidifies, milestone chapter.** Professor Oak and Nurse Joy recur as steady background presences while Dragonite becomes the unambiguous centerpiece of Jord's team across this stretch. Ch. 49's author's note is explicitly a milestone celebration — "we're up to chapter 49... one more and we break the fifty milestone" — thanking readers directly, which places Ch. 52 as a small structural landmark in the book's own pacing rather than a plot beat.

**Ch. 57–62 — The Regi trio and Season 1's close.** Jord assembles Regirock, Regice, and Registeel over this stretch, with narration explicitly tying them to Brandon's canon ownership of the same three and counting Registeel out loud as "my third" legendary capture — the book wants the reader tracking the number. Ch. 62 (the FF.net finale for this run, matching Webnovel's Ch. 194 "Season 1: End") delivers a full closing trainer-card recap: 142 Pokémon owned in total, with an active roster including Pidgeot, Charizard, Fearow, Blissey, Milotic, Blastoise, Gyarados, Raticate, Golem, Persian, Raboot, Servine, Krabby, Kabutops, Aerodactyl, Altaria, Dragonite, Togepi, Exeggutor, Rotom, several fossils, Tyranitar, the completed Regi trio, a shiny Rayquaza, Venusaur, Mewtwo, Steelix, Rhyperior, Regieleki, Regidrago, and Kubfu. Two further Webnovel-only chapters immediately follow with the same treatment for the canon Ash/Misty/Brock and Lance/Leon squads, giving the whole cast a scoreboard before the story moves on.

**Webnovel Ch. 185 (catalog #186) — the one mature-content chapter.** Flagged explicitly in the Webnovel catalog as "R-18"; not individually reread, but its existence and position are confirmed directly from the chapter listing.

**Webnovel Ch. 195 ("Season 2 Start") through Ch. 205 — Kalos, freshly reset.** The story restarts its geography rather than its tone: Vaniville Town, Serena's actual hometown in canon. Jord immediately starts flirting with Serena and Shauna, then almost as an afterthought beats Tierno and Trevor in a Professor Sycamore-hosted local tournament. The prize he actually cares about is a Key Stone — his reaction is quoted directly: "A key stone! I could fucking mega evolve some of my pokemon now!" — and the furthest-readable point in this pass has him plotting how to get Mega Stones through Sycamore and Lysandre, mid-conversation about Sinnoh/Hoenn's own Champion-tier trainer Tucker, unresolved.

**What's real but unverified here:** author's notes across the run reference a Frontier Brain arc under Brandon somewhere in the chapters between 52 and 194, Pokémon Journeys characters Delia, Chloe, and Goh entering around chapters 108–126, and later material touching Naranja Academy, Orre, and eventually Galar and Paldea. These are genuine plot points, just not individually reread chapter-by-chapter in this pass — with no titles to skim, closing that gap means reading the raw 62-chapter FF.net text (or the ~209-chapter Webnovel split of the same material) start to finish rather than sampling a table of contents.
