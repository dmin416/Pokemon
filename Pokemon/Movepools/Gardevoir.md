# Gardevoir (#282)

Psychic / Fairy
Evolves from Kirlia. Same learnset for base, Mega, and Dynamax; Mega/Dynamax do not add learnable moves.

Companion: `Pokemon/Ralts.md`.

## Full Movepool

Sorted by type. Scarlet & Violet (level-up, evolution, egg, TM) plus Legends: Z-A.
