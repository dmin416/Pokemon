# Honedge → Doublade → Aegislash

Steel / Ghost
Honedge (#679) → Doublade (#680) at Level 35 → Aegislash (#681) with Dusk Stone

On D's Fighting Team. See `Ideas/Fighting Team.md`.
Bonded with D and Deoxys: `Pokemon/Deoxys.md`.

## Abilities

### No Guard
Attacks by and against this Pokémon never miss.

### Stance Change (Aegislash)
Shield Forme is default. Using an attacking move shifts to Blade Forme. King's Shield or switching out returns Shield Forme.

### Cursed Body (story)
When damaged by a foe's ability, seals that ability so the foe cannot use it.

### Spectral Evasion (story)
Full immunity to incoming attacks. While immune this Pokémon cannot attack.

## Base Stats (Aegislash)

Stance Change swaps focus.
Shield Forme: highest Defense and Special Defense. Fortress.
Blade Forme: highest Attack and Special Attack. Glass cannon offense.
King's Shield or a switch returns Shield Forme.

## Evolution

Honedge → Doublade (Level 35) → Aegislash (Dusk Stone)
Aegislash learns King's Shield and Head Smash on evolution.

## Special Notes

- Unique moves: 62. All three share 57. Aegislash only: 3 (King's Shield, Head Smash, Close Combat). Egg moves: 3.
- Learnset spans Generation 6 through 9.
