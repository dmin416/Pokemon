# Potential 2

Species cut from `Potential.md`, organized by generation.

**Found** = primary home region and iconic wild / story sites (not every remake spawn).

## Gen 1

### Beedrill
Weedle → Kakuna → Beedrill
Bug / Poison
**Found:** Kanto: Viridian Forest. Also Johto trees/routes; forest Bug habitat.

### Machop
Machop → Machoke → Machamp
Fighting
**Found:** Kanto: Rock Tunnel, Mt. Moon, Victory Road caves. Mountain and quarry Fighting habitat across regions; Paldea East Province rock.

### Poliwrath
Poliwag → Poliwhirl → Poliwrath / Politoed
Water (Poliwrath: Water / Fighting)
**Found:** Kanto: Routes near Cerulean, waterways and ponds. Common freshwater Poliwag lines; evolve with Water Stone (Poliwrath) or King's Rock trade (Politoed).

### Snorlax
Munchlax → Snorlax
Normal
**Found:** Kanto: Routes 12 and 16 (roadblock Snorlax). Munchlax: Sinnoh honey trees / rare spawns. Sleeping Normal habitat on roads and clearings.

## Gen 2

### Larvitar
Larvitar → Pupitar → Tyranitar
Rock / Ground → Rock / Ground → Rock / Dark
BST 300 / 410 / 600. Born underground feeding on soil; Tyranitar can level mountains.
**Found:** Johto: Mt. Silver area / mountain caves. Also Sinnoh Victory Road and similar highland rock.

## Gen 3

### Blaziken
Torchic → Combusken → Blaziken
Fire → Fire / Fighting → Fire / Fighting
**Found:** Hoenn starter (Littleroot / Professor Birch). Not wild in main Hoenn; later transfers and remakes.

### Latias
Latias
Dragon / Psychic
BST 600. Can refract light to become invisible; shares a telepathic bond with its trainer.
**Found:** Hoenn: Southern Island / Eon Ticket (game-version exclusive with Latios); roaming in remakes. Alto Mare (film *Heroes*).

### Latios
Latios
Dragon / Psychic
BST 600. Latias's male counterpart; able to fly at jet-like speeds.
**Found:** Hoenn: Southern Island / Eon Ticket (game-version exclusive with Latias); roaming in remakes. Alto Mare (film *Heroes*).

## Gen 4

### Darkrai
Darkrai
Dark
BST 600. Mythical. Causes nightmares in those who sleep near it, though it does not intend harm.
**Found:** Sinnoh: Newmoon Island (Member Card event). Canalave nightmare plot. Film *The Rise of Darkrai* (Alamos Town).

### Cresselia
Cresselia
Psychic
BST 600. Said to appear in people's dreams, bringing positive emotions.
**Found:** Sinnoh: Fullmoon Island (roaming after). Counterpart to Darkrai's Newmoon Island.

## Gen 8

### Rolycoly
Rolycoly → Carkol → Coalossal
Rock → Rock / Fire → Rock / Fire
**Found:** Galar: Galar Mine and mining tunnels (Route 3 area). Coal-rock habitat.

### Urshifu
Kubfu → Urshifu (Single Strike / Rapid Strike)
Fighting → Fighting / Dark or Fighting / Water
**Found:** Galar: Isle of Armor. Kubfu from Mustard; Tower of Darkness (Single Strike) or Tower of Waters (Rapid Strike).

## Gen 9

### Slither Wing
Slither Wing
Bug / Fighting
BST 570. Paradox. Believed to be an ancient relative of Volcarona.
**Found:** Paldea: Area Zero (Scarlet / past Paradox).

### Iron Thorns
Iron Thorns
Rock / Electric
BST 570. Paradox. Resembles a mechanical, future version of Tyranitar.
**Found:** Paldea: Area Zero (Violet / future Paradox).
