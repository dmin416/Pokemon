# Deoxys (#386)

Psychic
Mythical. Does not evolve. Forms: Normal, Attack, Defense, Speed.
Extraterrestrial DNA-based life. Arrives by meteor. Signature move: Psycho Boost.

On D's Fighting Team. See `Ideas/Fighting Team.md`.
Farm meteor and power spot: `Ideas/Dynamaxing.md`.

## Abilities

### Pressure
Foes use more PP when attacking. Alien presence drains their fight.

### Adaptive DNA (story)
When hit by a super-effective move, resistances shift to handle similar threats better.

### Cosmic Regeneration (story)
Restores HP gradually under certain battlefield conditions.

## Forms and Stats

DNA reshapes the body for the role it wants.

- Normal: balanced Attack, Special Attack and Speed. Defenses 50 / 50.
- Attack: highest Attack and Special Attack. Glass cannon. Defenses 20 / 20.
- Defense: highest Defense and Special Defense at 160 / 160. Highest Special Defense Psychic-type. Counter and Mirror Coat punish attackers.
- Speed: highest Speed at 180. Fastest Psychic-type. Extreme Speed specialty. Defenses 90 / 90.

Base Speed: Normal and Attack 150. Defense 90. Speed 180.

## Form Changing

- Gen 3 Emerald: interact with meteorites in different locations.
- Gen 3 FR/LG: cannot change forms.
- Gen 4-5: meteorites in various locations.
- Gen 6+: meteorites as Key Items in the bag.

Meteorites: none for Normal (default). Attack Meteorite. Defense Meteorite. Speed Meteorite.

Form shift takes less than one second by willpower. Mid-battle shifts are canon in anime and manga. Aurora can appear during transformation. Optional not required.

Partial transforms: Normal Forme arms can shift between coiled double-helix tentacles and five-fingered hands without a full form change.

## This Specimen (story)

Shiny. Silver and gunmetal body. Crimson crystal core instead of typical orange, red, purple or green.
Bonded with D through a silver scar after he healed it with Viridian Energy instead of himself. Lives in him as bio-nano tissue as well as a partner presence.
Triple bond base: D ↔ Honedge ↔ Deoxys. Rotom can join later and bond into the Deoxys colony.
Author kit: `Characters/D.md`.

## Core, Regen and Durability

The chest crystal is brain and power source. Destroy it and Deoxys dies. Keep it and the body can regrow from nothing.

Destiny Deoxys: purple-crystal individual took four years to rebuild after Rayquaza's Hyper Beam obliterated it. Survived as only the core on the Arctic Ocean floor.

Recover restores half max HP. Cosmic Power absorbs mystical power from space. Sustains on cosmic and psychic energy. Survives space vacuum. Functions in Earth's atmosphere.

Defense Forme can raise external barriers for allies. In Destiny Deoxys both Deoxys shielded Rayquaza from Block Bots. Green-gem Deoxys used Defense Forme to redirect an attack meant for Rayquaza.

Story extrapolation: regenerative cells may form temporary armor plating around a bonded trainer. Viridian Energy may stabilize that.

## Speed and Flight

- Speed Forme: superior agility. Extreme Speed is +2 priority blinding charge.
- Teleport: instant reposition. Anime and manga give no hard range limit.
- Battles Rayquaza in the ozone layer. Massively hypersonic combat speeds.
- Survived atmospheric entry riding a meteor.

Flight is psychokinetic levitation. Atmosphere and outer space. Deep ocean to orbit. No apparent altitude ceiling.

Passenger transport is canon. Destiny Deoxys: blue-gem Deoxys levitates Ash, Pikachu, Tory, Plusle and Minun. Grip with tentacles or lift with psychic force.

## Sensing

Perceives through electromagnetic fields not normal vision.

Destiny Deoxys: purple Deoxys sensed the green crystal from the Arctic Ocean floor while the crystal was in LaRousse City. Thousands of miles for familiar signatures.

Aurora is primary language. Wavelengths carry meaning. Green aurora: friend. Purple aurora: where are you. Psychic Sight may read aurora as structured speech.

Electromagnetic interference obscures perception. LaRousse City electronics overwhelmed it. It cut city power while hunting its companion. Dense urban tech may degrade sensing.

Yellow heard Deoxys's thoughts. Max felt its pain and loneliness. Gardevoir or Alakazam could relay a psychic bond network.

## Stealth

Pokémon Adventures: Organism No. 1 went invisible. Still readable on Green's upgraded Silph Scope.

Organism No. 2 faked a Normal Forme aurora while in Attack Forme to deceive Mewtwo.

Double Team for illusory copies. FireRed Pokédex: can fool enemies by altering appearance. May minimize its own energy signature.

Sub-second form shifts plus invisibility plus illusion make a stealth operative that can look like one thing while being another or vanish.

## Combat Feats

Tentacles in Pokémon Adventures: grapple, fist strikes, pierce wings, ambush from odd angles.
Protected former enemies when facing shared threats.
Used invisibility in ferry combat. Used aurora deception against Mewtwo.
Long-range crystal detection. Fluid form tactics mid-fight.

## Bonding Feats

Blood bond with Red in Pokémon Adventures. Red's blood called when Deoxys was near. Deoxys treated Red as ancestor. Yellow heard its thoughts.

Tory Lund befriended green Deoxys's energy form over four years. Deoxys repaid that by catching Tory when Block Bots failed.
