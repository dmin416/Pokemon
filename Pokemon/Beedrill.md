# Weedle → Kakuna → Beedrill

Bug / Poison
Weedle (#13) → Kakuna (#14) at Level 7 → Beedrill (#15) at Level 10

On D's Fighting Team. See `Ideas/Fighting Team.md`.

## Abilities

### Royal Guard (story)
Attack and Speed rise when D or teammates are threatened. Strengthened by a sacred spring blessing.

### Sniper (Hidden)
Critical hits deal more damage. Finds and exploits weak points.

### Adaptability (Mega)
Same-type moves hit at full STAB power.

## Base Stats (Beedrill)

Physical attacker. Highest focus on Attack and Speed. Strong Special Defense. Moderate HP. Hit-and-run stinger strikes before the foe can answer.

## Evolution and Mega

Weedle → Kakuna (Level 7) → Beedrill (Level 10)
Mega Beedrill: Beedrillite (Gen 6-7).
Gen 8+: Beedrill learns Pin Missile on evolution.

## Special Notes

- Unique moves: 85. Shared by all three: Electroweb, Facade, Poison Sting, String Shot. Kakuna + Beedrill: Harden, Iron Defense. Egg moves: 6.
- Cross Poison would suit a stinging wasp. Beedrill cannot learn it in standard games.
- Learnset spans Generation 1 through 9.
