# Ralts → Kirlia → Gardevoir

Psychic / Fairy (Psychic only in Gen 3-5)
Ralts (#280) → Kirlia (#281) at Level 20 → Gardevoir (#282) at Level 30
Does not cover Gallade.

On D's Fighting Team. See `Ideas/Fighting Team.md`.

## Abilities

### Synchronize
If this Pokémon is statused, the foe gets the same status.

### Trace
Copies the foe's ability.

### Telepathy (Hidden)
Avoids damaging allies. Mental communication.

## Base Stats (Gardevoir)

Special attacker. Highest focus on Special Attack and Special Defense. High Speed. Solid HP, Attack and Defense. Hits hard with Psychic and Fairy energy and holds up against special hits.

## Evolution and Mega

Ralts → Kirlia (Level 20) → Gardevoir (Level 30)
Gardevoir learns Dazzling Gleam on evolution.
Mega Gardevoir: Gardevoirite (Gen 6-7). Type stays Psychic / Fairy from Gen 6 on.

## Special Notes

- Type gained Fairy in Gen 6.
- Unique moves: 126. All three share 108. Gardevoir only: 18. Kirlia + Gardevoir only: Hyper Beam, Triple Axel. Egg moves: 8.
- Learnset spans Generation 3 through 9.
