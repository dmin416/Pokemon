# Rayquaza (#384)

Dragon / Flying
Legendary. Does not evolve. Sky High Pokémon. Lives in the ozone layer and rarely descends. Part of the Weather Trio with Groudon and Kyogre.

On D's Fighting Team. See `Ideas/Fighting Team.md`.

## Abilities

### Air Lock
Negates all weather effects on the field.

### Pressure
Foes use more PP when attacking.

### Delta Stream (Mega)
Creates strong winds that weaken super-effective Flying-type moves.

## Base Stats

Mixed attacker. Highest focus on Attack, Special Attack and Speed. High HP. Solid Defense and Special Defense. Strikes first and hits hard from both sides.

## Mega Evolution

Rayquaza → Mega Rayquaza
Must know Dragon Ascent. No Mega Stone.
Type stays Dragon / Flying.
Ability becomes Delta Stream.
Attack and Special Attack rise to 180. Defense and Special Defense rise to 100. Speed rises to 115.

## Special Notes

- Dragon Ascent is the signature move and the Mega requirement.
- Air Lock shuts down weather strategies.
- Mega Rayquaza was banned to Anything Goes in competitive play.
- Learnset spans Generation 3 through 9.
