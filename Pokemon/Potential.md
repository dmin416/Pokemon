# Potential

Candidate species for D, organized by generation. Battle cut: `Ideas/Fighting Team.md`.

**Found** = primary home region and iconic wild / story sites (not every remake spawn).

## Gen 1

### Caterpie
Caterpie → Metapod → Butterfree
Bug → Bug → Bug / Flying
**Found:** Kanto: Viridian Forest and early routes. Also Johto, Kalos, Alola forests; common Bug habitat.

### Gastly
Gastly → Haunter → Gengar
Ghost / Poison
Gengar: BST 500. Lurks in shadows and is said to try to steal the life of anyone it finds.
**Found:** Kanto: Pokémon Tower (Lavender). Haunted towers, graves, and dark caves overnight in many regions.

### Chansey
Happiny → Chansey → Blissey
Normal
**Found:** Kanto: Safari Zone / rare routes. Happiny: Sinnoh (Hearthome Day Care eggs, wild). Nurse-line rarity; Pokémon Centers lore-adjacent.

### Dragonite
Dratini → Dragonair → Dragonite
Dragon → Dragon → Dragon / Flying
BST 300 / 420 / 600. Rare deep-water line; Dragonair stores weather energy in its tail orb; Dragonite is immensely strong but gentle-natured.
**Found:** Kanto: Safari Zone / rare water. Johto: Dragon's Den (Blackthorn). Deep lakes, dragon shrines; wild Dragonite very rare at sea.

### Zapdos
Zapdos
Electric / Flying
BST 580. Legendary bird; said to appear from dark thunderclouds and cause massive lightning storms.
**Found:** Kanto: Power Plant. Also roaming / remake encounters; thunderstorm Legendary bird.

### Mew
Mew
Psychic
BST 600. Mythical. Said to hold the genetic code of every Pokémon species.
**Found:** Mythical / event. Lore: Faraway Island (Emerald); Guyana-analogue jungle origin. Not a normal wild spawn.

### Mewtwo
Mewtwo
Psychic
BST 680. Legendary created through genetic engineering using Mew's DNA.
**Found:** Kanto: Cerulean Cave (postgame). Created at Pokémon Mansion, Cinnabar Island. New Island (anime/film).

## Gen 2

### Slugma
Slugma → Magcargo
Fire → Fire / Rock
**Found:** Johto: Routes 16-18 (volcanic rock). Also Hoenn Mt. Chimney / Route 113 ash; Magcargo on Stark Mountain and similar lava habitats.

### Celebi
Celebi
Psychic / Grass
BST 600. Mythical. Travels through time; said to bring happiness wherever it appears.
**Found:** Johto: Ilex Forest shrine (event / GS Ball lore). Time-travel Mythical; not a normal wild spawn.

### Lugia
Lugia
Psychic / Flying
BST 680. Guardian of the seas; said to calm raging storms with a single wingbeat.
**Found:** Johto: Whirl Islands (depths). Silver Wing plot. Sea-guardian Legendary.

### Ho-Oh
Ho-Oh
Fire / Flying
BST 680. Phoenix Legendary; said to grant eternal happiness to anyone who sees it.
**Found:** Johto: Bell Tower / Tin Tower, Ecruteak City. Rainbow Wing plot. Rainbow-guardian Legendary.

## Gen 3

### Ralts
Ralts → Kirlia → Gardevoir / Gallade
Psychic / Fairy (Gallade: Psychic / Fighting)
Ralts: BST 198. Senses nearby emotions and hides when it feels danger.
**Found:** Hoenn: Route 102 and early tall grass. Also Kalos and other early Psychic/Fairy routes; Gallade needs Dawn Stone (male Kirlia).

### Rayquaza
Rayquaza
Dragon / Flying
BST 680. Lives in the ozone layer; said to have ended a war between Kyogre and Groudon.
**Found:** Hoenn: Sky Pillar. Ozone-layer / sky Legendary; Delta Episode (ORAS) expands its role.

### Deoxys
Deoxys (Normal / Attack / Defense / Speed)
Psychic
BST 600. Mythical. Born from a virus mutated by laser exposure.
**Found:** Mythical / event. Birth Island (FR/LG); Mossdeep Space Center meteor plot (R/S/E); Sky Pillar Delta Episode (ORAS). Form changes via meteorites.

## Gen 4

### Rotom
Rotom (Normal / Heat / Wash / Frost / Fan / Mow)
Electric / Ghost (appliance forms: Electric plus Fire, Water, Ice, Flying or Grass)
**Found:** Sinnoh: Old Chateau (Eterna Forest). Possesses appliances in later games (Rotom Catalog / boxes).

### Heatran
Heatran
Fire / Steel
BST 600. Lives in volcanic caves, moving along walls and ceilings with clawed limbs.
**Found:** Sinnoh Battle Zone: Stark Mountain. Volcanic cave Legendary.

### Giratina
Giratina
Ghost / Dragon
BST 680. Banished to the Distortion World for its violent nature.
**Found:** Sinnoh: Distortion World / Turnback Cave (Platinum). Origin Forme in Distortion World; Altered in Turnback Cave. Film *Giratina and the Sky Warrior*.

## Gen 6

### Honedge
Honedge → Doublade → Aegislash
Steel / Ghost
Honedge: BST 325. Formed from the spirit of someone who died; wielded like a sword.
**Found:** Kalos: Route 6 and haunted sites; Tower of Mastery lore-adjacent. Ghost-sword caves and ruins.

### Carbink
Carbink
Rock / Fairy
BST 500. Born from underground high heat and pressure; stone body with jewels that never dull.
**Found:** Kalos: Reflection Cave. Also cave jewel habitats in later games (Galar, Paldea caves). Linked lore to Diancie.

### Hoopa
Hoopa
Psychic / Ghost
BST 600. Mythical. Can summon anything through rings it conjures (base Confined forme).
**Found:** Mythical / event. Film *Hoopa and the Clash of Ages* (Desert Resort / Dahara City). Rings summon across space.

## Gen 7

### Cosmog
Cosmog
Psychic
BST 200. So light a breeze can blow it away; first stage toward Solgaleo or Lunala.
**Found:** Alola: Lake of the Sunne / Lake of the Moone (and related UB / Aether plot). Evolves toward Nebby line.

### Marshadow
Marshadow
Fighting / Ghost
BST 600. Mythical. Hides in shadows and mimics others' movements to learn their fighting style.
**Found:** Mythical / event. Shadow-lurking; no standard wild habitat. Film *I Choose You!* era distribution lore.

### Celesteela
Celesteela (Tekkaguya)
Steel / Flying
BST 570. Ultra Beast (UB-04 Blaster). Looks like a giant bamboo rocket; absorbs particles from the air to fuel launches from its arms and head.
**Found:** Alola: Ultra Wormholes / Ultra Space encounters (Sun/Moon, Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon). Not a normal regional wild spawn.

## Gen 8

### Alcremie
Milcery → Alcremie
Fairy
**Found:** Galar: Bridge Field and similar; Milcery with Sweets while spinning. Cream Fairy habitat; often bakery/café adjacent in writing.

### Calyrex
Calyrex
Psychic / Grass
BST 500. Once worshipped as a king; able to fuse with Glastrier or Spectrier.
**Found:** Galar: Crown Tundra (Freezington / Crown Shrine). King of Bountiful Harvests plot.

### Glastrier
Glastrier
Ice
BST 580. Legendary horse; freezing breath can instantly turn anything to ice.
**Found:** Galar Crown Tundra: Ice steed bound to Calyrex (Crown Shrine / Freezington arc).

### Spectrier
Spectrier
Ghost
BST 580. Legendary horse; steals life energy from its surroundings, leaving withered husks.
**Found:** Galar Crown Tundra: Ghost steed bound to Calyrex (Crown Shrine / Freezington arc).

## Gen 9

### Nacli
Nacli → Naclstack → Garganacl
Rock
**Found:** Paldea: Asado Desert and West Province salt-rock areas. Story 1: Casseroya / ranch salt line.

### Smoliv
Smoliv → Dolliv → Arboliva
Grass / Normal
**Found:** Paldea: South Province olive country and grassy fields. Story 1: Happy Ranch purchase / ranch oil line.

### Cyclizar
Cyclizar
Dragon / Normal
**Found:** Paldea: roads and open routes region-wide (common ride/transport species). Story 1: Mayor Thomas / Porto Marinada gift.

### Tinkatink
Tinkatink → Tinkatuff → Tinkaton
Fairy / Steel
Tinkatink: BST 297. Crafts a small hammer from scavenged metal to defend itself.
**Found:** Paldea: East Province (Area Three and scrap/metal habitats). Hammer grows through the line.

### Koraidon
Koraidon
Fighting / Dragon
BST 670. Known as the "Winged King" in old texts from Paldea's ancient past.
**Found:** Paldea: Area Zero / Great Crater (Scarlet). Story 1: crashes onto D Ranch from the crater.

### Wo-Chien
Wo-Chien
Dark / Grass
BST 570. Formed from the vengeful spirit of someone punished for exposing a king's wrongdoing.
**Found:** Paldea: Grasswither Shrine (South Province). Treasure of Ruin; eight stakes seal.

### Chien-Pao
Chien-Pao
Dark / Ice
BST 570. Born from centuries-old hatred; said to control massive avalanches of snow.
**Found:** Paldea: Icerend Shrine (West Province). Treasure of Ruin; eight stakes seal.

### Flutter Mane
Flutter Mane
Ghost / Fairy
BST 570. Paradox. Resembles an ancient relative of Misdreavus.
**Found:** Paldea: Area Zero (Scarlet / past Paradox).
