# Pokemon Sounds (Rancher, Ch. 1-100)

Short cry / longer sentence-length sound, each entry.

## Cry convention

Name-echo cries (Bulbasaur says "Bulbasaur," Squirtle says "Squirtle") match the standard convention for ordinary Pokémon and cover most of the list correctly.

Legendaries, Mythicals, and Ultra Beasts break that convention in the actual show and games. Those get roars, screeches, or mechanical drones instead of name-speech:

- **Cobalion, Virizion** — sword-beast roars
- **Darkrai** — eerie growl/screech, no name enunciation
- **Eternatus** — monstrous roar
- **Giratina** — deep resonant roar
- **Ho-Oh** — majestic bird screech
- **Lunala, Solgaleo** — moth-wail / lion roar
- **Manaphy** — high-pitched coo, not a name-echo
- **Raikou** — thunder-beast roar
- **Regice, Regigigas, Regirock, Registeel** — low mechanical groans/creaks, near-silent ancient golems
- **Xerneas, Yveltal, Zekrom** — majestic hum / screech / electric roar
- **Zygarde** — buzzing hive-mind drone
- **Tapu Koko** — sharp electric chirp/screech
- **Buzzwole** — alien insectoid growl

These 19 entries need a different sound register than the syllabic name-echo pattern used for the rest of the list. Everything else holds.
