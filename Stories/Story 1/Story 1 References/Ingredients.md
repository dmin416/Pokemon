#### Black Truffle

- Top gourmet ingredient found in the back forest
- Pairs with eggs (steamed eggs) or butter (creamy soup)
- No inherent enhancement; significantly improves a dish's effects
- First found: Chapter 5 (under dead grass by a tree)
- Market: normal ~5000/lb; top quality ~15000/lb (Zack, Chapter 6)
- Status: main ~2.2 lb top-grade sold for **33000** (15000/lb); small scrap kept for cooking (Chapter 6). About one quarter of the scrap used in Black Truffle Milk Porridge (S), Chapter 39. More powder on Berry Platter (B+) Chapter 51. Remainder stored. D wants Oinkologne later for back-mountain truffle hunting
- Sell note: gourmet override vs Stardew Truffle table — see `Stardew/Forage.md` / `Stardew/Currency.md`

#### Purifying Salt (Garganacl)

- Pink rock salt from Garganacl fingertips; minerals and trace elements
- Instantly heals injured Pokémon when sprinkled (does not harm the producer; they regenerate salt)
- Many Pokémon gather to lick Garganacl
- Adds **Stamina Recovery** to dishes
- Sells high; food with this salt is more delicious than ordinary
- Gift jar from Happy Ranch shopkeeper (Chapter 24); shop Nacli still 10000
- Five wild Nacli joined Chapter 40. Salt House complete. First film harvest **3250 g** (~**19500** at 3000/500 g, Chapter 50). They share salt with those they like. Moderation needed when small
- **298-01-31** haul: five pounds rock salt sold for **16800** with milk and mushrooms (Chapter 52)
- Caramel Rock Salt Cream with Alcremie (Chapter 43)
