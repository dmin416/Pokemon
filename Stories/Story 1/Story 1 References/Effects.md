# Effects / Recipe Grade Guide

Rough Culinary Master appraisal rules for Story 1. Restaurant food gives no buffs — only D’s cooked (or juiced) dishes do. Early “January” meal stamps = **League 298** ([`Dates.md`](Dates.md) / [`Timeline.md`](Timeline.md)).

## Grade → effect tier

| Grade | Effect tier | Rough meaning |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| **D and below** (D, D-, E, F…) | Little / none, or wild niche | Fills belly at best, or a bizarre exclusive (Steamed Chicken Feed F: Egg explosion). Bad seasoning can wipe bonuses. |
| **C** (incl. C- / C+) | **Level 1** | One weak buff (or a slight exclusive niche effect). Short duration. C- is thinner / shorter; C+ a bit stronger or longer. |
| **B** (incl. B- / B+) | **Level 2** | Mid buffs: “large” recovery/stat spikes, or several small effects together. Hour-scale duration is typical so far. |
| **A** (incl. A- / A+) | **Level 3** | Strong combat/ranch buffs. Longer duration and/or multiple strong effects. No A-letter dish yet. Exclusive lv3 seen on Steamed Egg B+ (Slippery). |
| **S** (incl. S- / S+) | Permanent or equal power | Lasting change, or a temporary effect as strong as a permanent would be. Rare. Not yet seen. |

### Working notes

- Grade is the dish rating; effect *level* is what the buff line shows (e.g. Cold Resistance **lv1** on a **C** dish). Exclusive niche lines can punch above the letter: Slippery **lv3** on Steamed Egg **B+** (Chapter 26).
- Same recipe can climb a letter with better ingredients (Hot Soup Noodles C- → C with wild leeks). A proper cake base would have raised Cream Fruit Cake above D-.
- Special ingredients (Black Truffle) don't add their own buff; they **amplify** the dish (stronger/longer effects) and can help the grade. Confirmed: Milk Porridge (B) → Black Truffle Milk Porridge (S), Better Milk lv1 → lv3 (Chapter 39).
- Purifying Salt (Garganacl) adds **Stamina Recovery** to dishes (confirmed on fried pork cutlets, clear soup noodles, and Steamed Egg, Chapter 26) and can heal Pokémon wounds on its own.
- Happier Alcremie produces richer cream (Chapter 26).
- Exclusive effects (e.g. bovine-only) still follow the grade tier for power unless the appraisal prints a higher lv (Berry Milk C ≈ slight / lv1 niche; Slippery lv3 on B+).
- Multiple effects on one dish still sit under that dish's grade cap (B steak = several mid-tier lines, not an A package).
- Non-food ingredients can cook: grass, metal, chicken feed, and (untested) stone. F-band can still carry strong ranch exclusives (Egg explosion, Chapter 27).
- Some dishes carry extra favorability lines for a named species (Spicy Iron Block → Heatran).

## Effect catalog (confirmed)

### Cold Resistance lv1

- Tier: Level 1 (C-band)
- Hot Soup Noodles C- (30 min) / C (35 min)
- Helps endure severe cold; body feels warm
- Confirmed: Cyclizar

### Large HP recovery

- Tier: Level 2 (B-band)
- Pan-Seared Pork Steak (B), 60 min with other steak buffs
- Confirmed: Cyclizar, Koraidon

### Large strength increase

- Tier: Level 2 (B-band)
- Pan-Seared Pork Steak (B), 60 min
- Confirmed: Cyclizar, Koraidon

### Small EXP and insights

- Tier: Level 2 package (secondary on B steak)
- Pan-Seared Pork Steak (B), 60 min
- Confirmed: Cyclizar, Koraidon

### Bovine milk quality up (slight)

- Tier: Level 1 niche (C-band)
- Berry Milk (C)
- Exclusive to bovine Pokémon; no bovine eater on hand yet (Ch. 8)

### Energy boost (small)

- Tier: D-band (weak)
- Sandwich (D)
- More energetic; less prone to drowsiness
- Confirmed: Cyclizar, Koraidon (Ch. 9)

### Heat relief / body cool

- Tier: unrated dish (Ch. 10)
- Cold Cucumber Slices
- Beats the heat; body feels cool after eating

### Body warming

- Tier: unrated dish (Ch. 14)
- Warming Hot Juice
- Caterpie felt warmer after drinking

### Bug Silk Enhancement (lv1–lv3)

- Tier: Level 2 package (B-band); level on the silk buff varies per ball
- Glutinous Rice Ball (B)
- Bug-type exclusive; tougher / stronger Bug Silk
- Confirmed: Caterpie after lv3 ball — more silk volume; silk stretches instead of snapping (Ch. 12)

### Life Recovery (Extra Large)

- Tier: Level 2 (B-band)
- Grilled Rainbow Trout (B)
- Instantly restores vitality and energy to peak (Max Potion + comprehensive PP vibe)
- Confirmed: Cyclizar, Koraidon (Ch. 16)

### Skill Refinement

- Tier: Level 1 band (C- dish) but outcome is RNG, not a fixed buff
- Berry Platter (C-)
- After eating, a move may: level up, level down, be forgotten, specialize, or a new move may be learned
- Confirmed: Caterpie learned Electroweb (Ch. 16); Cyclizar learned Dragon Tail and kept other moves (Ch. 49)
- D treats as high-risk. Held back from Dragonite until trust and Dragon Tail practice were solid (Ch. 49)

### Grass-type move comprehension (slight)

- Tier: D-band (slight)
- Steamed Green Grass (D-)
- After eating, Grass-types have a slightly higher chance to comprehend Grass-type moves
- Not fed to anyone yet (Ch. 22); leftover dumped

### Fire/Steel energy control (slight)

- Tier: D-band (slight)
- Spicy Iron Block (D)
- Slightly improves control of Fire-type and Steel-type energy
- Extra: feeding Heatran slightly increases favorability
- Refused by Cyclizar and Koraidon (Ch. 22); unused short-term

### Stamina Recovery (Purifying Salt)

- Ingredient line, not a finished dish (Ch. 24)
- Garganacl Purifying Salt adds Stamina Recovery to dishes
- Sprinkled on an injured Pokémon, wounds heal at once
- Jar on hand; confirmed on fried pork cutlets, clear soup noodles, and Steamed Egg (Ch. 26)

### Sweet Dream lv1

- Tier: D-band (lv1 on a D- dessert)
- Cream Fruit Cake (Caramel Flavor) (D-)
- After eating, Pokémon temporarily forget worries and sadness and have a sweet dream of who and what they wish to see
- D compares it to Cresselia (Ch. 26)
- Cook can see dream bubbles over sleeping Pokémon (Ch. 27). Confirmed: Koraidon, Cyclizar, Smoliv, Alcremie. Metapod did not eat the cake.

### Slippery lv3

- Tier: exclusive lv3 on a B+ dish
- Steamed Egg (B+)
- Hairless Pokémon only
- Body becomes smooth and shiny; easier to escape contact moves
- Duration: 3 hours
- Plus Stamina Recovery from Purifying Salt (Ch. 26)
- Cyclizar slurped a bowl without tasting it (Ch. 27)

### Egg explosion

- Tier: F-band exclusive (Steamed Chicken Feed)
- Roosters: accelerates fertilization of eggs
- Hens: many eggs in a short time, then 15-day cooldown (no laying, cannot eat this dish, need lots of food)
- Confirmed on one hen: ~20 eggs in ~1 minute (Ch. 27–28). Borrows the next 15 days of laying. Does not break the hen. Hen ate the whole trough.

### Sweet memory / energy restore (caramel Berry Milk)

- Tier: unrated (Ch. 30)
- Caramel-flavored Berry Milk (MooMoo Milk + Alcremie cream + berries)
- Reminds Pokémon of sweet, joyful events and restores energy
- Separate from Berry Milk (C) bovine line
- Confirmed: Arven's Mabosstiff (and Arven liked the taste)

### Better Milk lv1

- Tier: exclusive on Milk Porridge (B)
- Milk-producing Pokémon only
- Slightly enhances milk quality (tastier, more nutritious) plus energy restore
- Superseded in practice by Black Truffle Milk Porridge (S) Better Milk lv3 on Skiddo (Ch. 46)

### Better Milk lv3

- Tier: S-band exclusive on Black Truffle Milk Porridge (S)
- Milk-producing Pokémon only
- Significantly enhances milk quality (tastier, more nutritious) plus quick energy restore
- Black Truffle raised Milk Porridge (B) to S
- Fed to Skiddo (Ch. 46). Later milk appraises as **Goat Milk (Top Quality)**; Zack pays **600**/bottle (Ch. 50)

### Grass-move comprehension + Grass affection (slight)

- Tier: C-band (Grass Ball / Green Grass Dumplings)
- Slightly higher chance Grass-types comprehend Grass moves; slight Grass affection up
- Confirmed path: Skiddo binge → Grassy Terrain and Solar Beam (Ch. 48). Separate from Steamed Green Grass (D-)

### Health restore (Fried Pork Loin)

- Tier: C-band
- Fried Pork Loin (C). Fed to Cyclizar (Ch. 49)

### Dessert fondness up (Xue Mei Niang)

- Tier: B-band
- Greatly increases fondness of dessert-loving Pokémon; strong attraction to Alcremie (Ch. 49)

### Water-type attraction (carried)

- Tier: C+ band
- Lemon Sole Sandwich (C+): attracts Water-types when carried (Ch. 49). Not field-tested yet
