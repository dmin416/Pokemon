## Happy Ranch (Porto Marinada; Chapters 23–24)

Sells ordinary animals and farm Pokémon. League-legal (not black market). Sign: grinning Miltank. Glass-dome yard like Oak's lab. Shopkeeper: kindly white-haired old man with reading glasses; friends with D's grandfather; held D as a baby. Granddaughter delivers orders by tricycle. Store phone: 9573868 (Chapter 25). D has a lab video phone as of Chapter 31.
