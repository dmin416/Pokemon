### Steamed Chicken Feed (F)

- Source: chicken feed (grain mix like cat food) steamed 3 minutes so it stays in shape
- Rating: F
- Tier: unappraisable / not for humans
- Effect: Egg explosion. Roosters: accelerates egg fertilization. Hens: many eggs in a short time, then a 15-day cooldown (cannot lay, cannot eat this dish, need lots of food to recover).
- Notes: Looks like a dark dish; grain smell. Confirmed on one hen: about 20 eggs in a minute (Chapter 27–28). Borrows the next 15 days of laying. Hen ate the whole trough, then slept. Does not break the hen. Half the eggs kept, half hatching.
- First made: Chapter 27
