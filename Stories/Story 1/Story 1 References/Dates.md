# Specific Dates in Rancher Source

Every concrete calendar date found in the Chinese chapter dumps (`Stories/Story 1/Chinese/`), mapped to **第N章** (story chapter number in the dump headers). Vague seasons alone are omitted.

Chapter-List / English dump indexes can drift by a couple of numbers later in the book; prefer **第N章** here.

Story calendar uses **League years** (聯盟歷). Main arrival year = **League 298** (source 198 + 100).

**Project adjustment:** League years are **source + 100** so the League calendar is ~300 years old (aligned with the anime Orange League trophy age). Chinese dumps and Story 1 refs use these adjusted numbers.

**Story 1 usage:** [`Timeline.md`](Timeline.md) (and other Story 1 refs) take **this file** as the date basis. Early Ch1–10 have no day stamp; Story 1 assigns arrival to **298-01-05** to match the first ledger stamp (Ch11).

**Master spine:** `Reference/Timeline/pokemon-master-timeline.md` bridges with **League 298 = Year 2** (Master Year = League − 296).
