# Crop Projection

Maturity from **298-01-06** (Chapter 13 planting). Lists only what is in the ground that day. See [`Farm.md`](Farm.md).

Split: the plant keeps living after a harvest, or the harvest takes the plant and it must be replanted.

Times come from [`Growth-and-Season.md`](../../../Reference/Places/Growth-and-Season.md) and [`Berry-Plant-Replacements.md`](../../../Reference/Places/Berry-Plant-Replacements.md). **Pokémon berries:** 7 days to mature. Each fruit then takes 7 days to peak sweetness. Rarer berries add 7 days per extra in-game growth day (2-day berries add 7, 3-day add 14, 4-day add 21) to both clocks. Common fruit, vegetables and saplings use analogue times.

**Paldea seasons:** Spring Jan-Mar. Summer 298-04-01 to 298-07-24. Autumn 298-07-25 to 298-09-30. Winter from 298-10-01.

**Add years:** planted 298-01-06. A 2-year first crop is the harvest season in **300**. A 3-year first crop is **301**. Ranges use that count.

Turnip is not in the growth tables. Garden turnips are listed as 40 to 70 days.

Source Ch50 (late January) has turnips, potatoes, garlic and radishes near mature after about one month. That is faster than the times below. This file keeps the reference times.

## Keeps living after harvest

Pick fruit, leaves, or pods. The plant stays. The seven Pokémon berries planted this day are common (1-day in-game), so extra is 0: mature **298-01-13**, peak sweetness **298-01-20**. Each later fruit takes 7 days to peak. Stand-in names are flavor only.

Spinach, kale, cucumber, green bean, pea and tomato still die at season end (frost or bolt). Replant them next season, not after each pick.
