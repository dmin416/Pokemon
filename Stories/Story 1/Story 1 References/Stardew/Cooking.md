# Stardew Cooking

Prices in **LL** (Stardew g × **4**). See `Currency.md`. Story dishes / grades live in `../Recipes.md` — this file is Stardew reference only.

Unlocked after first farmhouse upgrade (kitchen + fridge). Fridge (36 slots) and Mini-Fridges count as inventory for recipes. Cookout Kit (Foraging 3) cooks anywhere. Fried Egg is known at start; all other recipes must be learned.

**Buffs:** One food buff set at a time (new food with buffs replaces old). Food without buffs does not clear existing buffs. Drink buffs (Coffee, Triple Shot Espresso, Green Tea, Ginger Ale) stack with one food.

**Qi Seasoning** (Qi's Walnut Room: 10 Qi Gems → stack of 10): cooked food Gold quality (+80% energy/health, +50% sell); non-Speed buffs +1. Used automatically from inventory/fridge/Mini-Fridge.

**Achievements:** Cook (10) · Sous Chef (25) · Gourmet Chef (all).

Queen of Sauce Cookbook unlocks all QoS recipes immediately (including not-yet-aired).

## Profit notes

Usually Preserves Jar / Keg / Mayo / Cheese / Fish Smoker beat cooking. Exceptions with lowest-quality ingredients often include: Algae Soup, Pale Broth, Bean Hotpot, Eggplant Parmesan, Fruit Salad, Lucky Lunch, Red Plate, Roots Platter, Spicy Eel, Tom Kha Soup, Tropical Curry, and several baked goods **if** flour/sugar/oil/vinegar are milled/homemade. Sashimi beats raw fish only if fish sell for &lt;300 LL (stricter with Fisher/Angler; Fish Smoker usually better).

Qi's Cuisine quest: 400,000 LL of **Fresh** cooked items (do not stack with pre-quest food).

## Ingredient search order

Bottom-right → top-left: player inventory → main fridge → Mini-Fridges (last placed first). Organize prefers lower quality first.
