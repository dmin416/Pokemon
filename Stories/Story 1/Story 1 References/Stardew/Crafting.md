## Fences

Decay over time unless Gold Clock is on the farm. Fresh fences drop the item when demolished; damaged ones do not. Gates pass through; torches can sit on fences.

**Story 1 (Chapter 23):** Robin sold a Chicken House run of wooden fences for 1500 league coins. Bought. Machamp installed them.

## Sprinklers

Water crops at 6am. Can water Slime Hutch troughs; not pet bowls or Garden Pots. Cannot place on sand. Ranges: basic 4 tiles · quality 8 · iridium 24.

**Story 1 (Chapter 21):** stick-in-ground sprinklers do not exist. Robin analogue is buried pipes, faucets, a pump, and a timer at about 30000 to 40000 league coins for the whole farmland. Not bought. Cheaper story method is Water-type Pokémon using Water moves.

| Name | Ingredients | Recipe Source |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| Sprinkler | Copper Bar (1), Iron Bar (1) | Farming 2 |
| Quality Sprinkler | Iron Bar (1), Gold Bar (1), Refined Quartz (1) | Farming 6 |
| Iridium Sprinkler | Gold Bar (1), Iridium Bar (1), Battery Pack (1) | Farming 9 |

Also: Traveling Cart (Sprinkler / Quality); Krobus Friday 40,000 LL (Iridium); Mystery Boxes (Quality). Upgrades: Pressure Nozzle, Enricher.

## Notes

- Achievements: D.I.Y. (15), Artisan (30), Craft Master (all). Wedding Ring does not count.
- Crafted items outside the farm can be destroyed if a villager walks through (chests are pushed aside). Safer zones: Backwoods, Tunnel, southern Cindersap, Quarry, Railroad above tracks.
