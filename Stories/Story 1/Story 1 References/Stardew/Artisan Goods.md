# Stardew Artisan Goods

Prices in **LL** (Stardew g × **4**). See `Currency.md`.

Items from Artisan Equipment. Process time: a few hours to a few days.

**Quality:** Ingredient star quality is ignored for most goods. Exceptions: Ostrich Egg mayonnaise and Smoked Fish (match ingredient quality). Cloth has no stars, but higher Wool quality raises chance of 2 Cloth.

**Artisan profession** (Farming 10): +40% sell (not Oil or Coffee). Tree syrups (Maple Syrup, Oak Resin, Pine Tar, Mystic Syrup) are labeled Artisan in-game but use **Tapper** profession instead — not covered here. In multiplayer, any connected Artisan player applies the boost to all sales.

**Piña Colada:** Island Resort only (Gus at the bar); not machine-made.

**Time note:** 1 hour = 60 minutes from 6am–2am; 1 hour = 100 minutes from 2am–6am.

Machine craft recipes also listed in `Crafting.md`.

**Story 1 (Chapters 24–50):** Alcremie caramel cream. Caramel Rock Salt Cream with Nacli (Chapter 43). Smoliv oil bitter until Dolliv. Combee honey needs flower field + hives (not bought). Nacli salt: Salt House complete; first bag 3250 g ≈ 19500. Goat milk Top Quality after Better Milk lv3 porridge; Zack 600/bottle. Black Truffle grated into Skiddo porridge (Chapter 46).
