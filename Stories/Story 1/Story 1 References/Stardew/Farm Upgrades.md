# Farm Upgrades

Prices in **LL** (Stardew g × **4**). See `Currency.md`. Story build costs in the mapping table below override these when they differ.

Stardew Valley farm buildings and house upgrades (Carpenter’s Shop / Robin). Magical buildings → Wizard’s Tower (not listed here).

## Rules

- Build as many buildings as space allows, including duplicates.
- Placement needs all tiles clear (green). Rocks, twigs, weeds, placed items block. Tree seeds, flooring/paths, and artifact spots do not block but are destroyed. Animals and pets are pushed aside if you place on them.
- Construction menu can paint, move, or demolish. Moving is free and instant (contents move with the building). Starter buildings (Greenhouse, default Shipping Bin) can be moved the same way.
- Tip: build a **Silo** before Coop/Barn to stockpile Hay early.

## House upgrades

Footprint does not change. Each upgrade takes **3 days**. Adds interior space; first upgrade unlocks the kitchen for cooking.

| Upgrade | Description | Cost |
|---------|-------------|------|
| House Upgrade 1 | Larger house + Kitchen | 40,000 LL + Wood (450) |
| House Upgrade 2 | Two new rooms (one empty; one crib + two child beds); larger kitchen and bedroom; unlocks renovations and building/trim painting | 260,000 LL + Hardwood (100) |
| House Upgrade 3 | Cellar under the house (via kitchen); Casks to age Cheese, Goat Cheese, Beer, Mead, Pale Ale, Wine. Exterior unchanged | 400,000 LL |
