# Stardew — Pierre's General Store

Prices in **LL** (Stardew g × **4**). See `Currency.md`.

Shop hours: **9am–5pm**. Building open until 9pm (home). Closed Wednesdays until Community Center complete or Key To The Town. Closed most festival days (exceptions: Desert Festival, Trout Derby, SquidFest, Night Market, Dance of the Moonlight Jellies).

Buys forage, crops, seeds, artisan goods, cooked dishes, animal products (instant cash; does **not** count toward shipping achievements/collections). Competitor: JojaMart.

**Missing Stocklist:** out-of-season seeds sold year-round at **+50%**. Year-2-only seeds still wait for year 2. Bouquet unlocks at 8♥ with a marriage candidate.

**Story 1 (Chapter 32):** Zack says buy fertilizer in town before the next crop. Higher quality crops sell for more. Not bought yet. Stardew Basic Fertilizer is the analogue (400 LL in the table below).
