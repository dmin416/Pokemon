## Through-line

D inherits a failing Paldea ranch, cooks Culinary Master dishes, and builds a place where Pokémon thrive. The ranch grows into a second home for legends. A World Tree rises into a cloaked **Paradise**. D’s quiet life keeps colliding with region crises (Hoenn gods, Ruin beasts, League politics) while he falls for **Lono**, starts a family, and eventually stands with **Calyrex / Giratina** against the cost of **Arceus’s** order. The ending turns the ranch over to the next generation.

Spine of desire across the book:

1. **Make the ranch work** (food, money, friends)
2. **Make a home for Pokémon** (Tree → Paradise → auspicious beasts)
3. **Keep identity split** (rancher vs Motorcycle Mask / legend-keeper)
4. **Choose people** (Lono, Lewis, friends, child)
5. **Settle the sky gods’ quarrel** (faith vs Creator; then life after)

## Major arcs

### Arc A — Dead Ranch, Living Start (Ch1–50 · League **298** winter–spring)

D arrives in Naoki’s body, meets **Cyclizar**, finds **Koraidon**, earns the first money, and learns Culinary Master cooking. The ranch stops dying. Early cast (Indeedee path, Skiddo, Butterfree, first sales) locks the “farmer not Trainer” vow.

**Beat idea:** Survive winter and prove the ranch can feed Pokémon without chasing Gym glory.

---

### Arc B — Hands and Helpers (Ch51–100 · **298** spring)

Dragonite gambling for moves, letters and town ties, Combee / Pawmi lines, Pokémon House, typhoon, Miltank / Gogoat growth. Travel prep toward larger world.

**Beat idea:** The ranch needs more than one strong dragon; community and species variety become the engine.

---

### Arc C — Trials, Faith, and the King (Ch101–150 · **298** spring–summer)

Grass / Thunder / Storm Trials draw challengers. **Calyrex** faith and Abundant Shrine tourism begin. Land and buildings expand. Summer fireworks and town festivals appear as calendar anchors.

**Beat idea:** D’s power is public enough to attract Trainers and gods of harvest alike.

---

### Arc D — Reversed World Door (Ch151–200 · **298** summer–autumn)

Deeper Culinary Master work (Reversing cuisine). **Giratina** contact path opens. Ranch social life (tea parties, neighbors) sits beside dangerous ingredients from the Reversed World.

**Beat idea:** Cooking can rewrite nature — and that invites the Distortion World’s master.

---

### Arc E — Crowns and Cruises (Ch201–250 · **298** autumn)

Giratina light/shadow beats. Galar trip: **Glastrier / Spectrier**, Freezington, Calyrex’s past. Geeta and League pressure. Horse King politics begin.

**Beat idea:** Off-region legends treat D as peer; the ranch’s reach is no longer local only.

---

### Arc F — Film Snow, Tree Sapling (Ch251–300 · **298** late autumn–**299** early spring)

Alice’s *Man and Dragon*, first snow, Arven, berry wines. Second nurture / **Leaf Cake**. **Life Sheep Milk** miracle and market. Abundant Shrine boom. Area Zero reunion cliff (**Flutter Mane** / Miraidon).

**Beat idea:** Faith and milk make Calyrex a public god; the Tree becomes a multi-year project.

---

### Arc G — Catch, Kitakami, Unova Stage (Ch301–350 · **299** spring)

Flutter Mane caught. Kitakami **Ogerpon** / Pecharunt. Land buy (**−7.7M**). Alder / Volcarona heal. Spectrier race crown. Unova banquet, breeding notes, Lance win, phone Rotom → Nikar.

**Beat idea:** D can leave Paldea as a Breeder-celebrity and still come home to expand the farm.

### Arc H — Parks, Hammers, Coins (Ch351–400 · **299** spring–summer)

Home gifts, **Dragonite Egg**, Snorlax Park, Apple Garden. World Tree moved to Reversed World. Pawmot **King of Hammers**. Rayquaza **Sky Scale**. Wish coins → **Gholdengo**. Eevee army hatch. Shop soft-open path.

**Beat idea:** Paradise infrastructure and money scale; Delivery and shop are inevitable.

### Arc I — Open for Business, Name Paradise (Ch401–450 · **299** autumn–**300** spring)

Land of Dragons. **Spectrier** Horse King. Mewtwo colony. Explorer Guild complete. World Tree named **Paradise**. Leon Ancient Trial / Darkest Day counsel. Steven tip-line.
