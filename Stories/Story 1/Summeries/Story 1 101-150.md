# 101: Chapter 101: Slick as Oil! The Invincible Skill!

D makes honey gummies with gelatin, honey and Nomel Berry juice.

Honey Grilled Fish and Honey Lemon Water only restore stamina or energy. The gummies appraise as **Honey Gummies (A)** with **Run Away**: guaranteed escape ignoring the foe's strength.

He feeds Teddiursa a piece and tells it to flee danger outside. Teddiursa agrees.

Three more days of cooking yield no other special dishes. Teddiursa's travel day arrives. D sees it off with Koraidon and the others and promises a honey banquet if it ever comes home.

For a last Lucky Pudding he cooks with sincere wishing. Appraisal: **Lucky Pudding (S)** — **Fortune Accompanies**: permanent luck for humans and Pokémon and permanently higher rare Pickup odds for traveling Pokémon.

He could keep the permanent pudding for himself but gives it to Teddiursa. Teddiursa hugs him then leaves on Mew in Fearow form.

Mew sets Teddiursa on the cruise ship Saint Ann bound for Vermilion City in Kanto.

---

# 102: Chapter 102: Flower Field

D watches Teddiursa go and feels odd that a Pokémon is traveling while he stays on the ranch.

Late February. Crops are near harvest again. Next morning the flower strip is in bloom. Alcremie, Smoliv, Nacli and Pawmi play among the flowers. Combee and Butterfree gather nectar in peace.

He harvests tomatoes, cucumbers and turnips with Koraidon and Cyclizar. Farming pays less than livestock but he keeps planting for food and to avoid waste. He wants an ice cellar and dreams of Ice-types like Cryogonal, Avalugg or especially Glaceon so he can collect all Eevee evolutions.

He also thinks of Calyrex as a harvest king for the farm then prays for bounty. In southern Galar a weakened Calyrex feels that faint faith and wonders that someone still trusts it.

---

# 103: Chapter 103: Does Dragonite Want to Return to Dragonite Island?

League **298**, March 14. D spends **120000** on four air conditioners for the Salt House, Pokémon House, Chicken House and cabin. Ranch revival continues. Goals: expand the residence with a cellar for hops and brewing and cook summer dishes to strengthen the Pokémon.

Combee tulip honey appraises as Tulip Honey. Lucky Pudding batches no longer hit permanent fortune. Lunch is the usual steak and sandwiches.

Dragonite shows Rain Dance plus Hurricane plus Thunder. Lightning blasts a pit in the grass. Moves now include Extreme Speed, Dragon Tail, Thunder Punch, Hurricane, Agility, Dragon Dance, Rain Dance, Thunderbolt and Thunder.

D asks what it plans next once there is nothing left to teach. Dragonite fears being sent away. D clarifies he only asks whether it will return to Dragonite Island. Dragonite realizes it stays because it likes him not only for strength.

---

# 104: Chapter 104: The Day of the Dragon King's Return!

Dragonite shakes its head: it will stay with D but wants him to visit Dragonite Island first to show the others its moves. D agrees for a same-day trip. Koraidon tags along.

Over the ocean Dragonite points out where it saw the black flying dragon. D names it Rayquaza and regrets missing the shiny.

They land on a lush island of berries and wildflowers. A Dratini startles D from a leaf. Inland lake: many Dratini, Dragonair and Dragonite including a pink Shiny Dratini.

Dragonite performs Rain Dance, Hurricane and Thunder for the tribe. A defiant wild Dragonite challenges it. Extreme Speed breaks Dragon Rage. Dragonite tanks Hurricane then lands Thunder Punch and wins Wing Attack vs Dragon Tail. D heals the loser with Berry Milk.

Dragonite says a Trainer helped it grow. Every ambitious Dragonite stares at D. Dragonite stands in front of him: this is my Trainer.

---

# 105: Chapter 105: Dragonite's Love

Afternoon by the central lake. D and Koraidon rest while Dragonite recounts ranch life to the tribe. Dratini hatchlings hover curiously. A Dragonair fetches the shy pink Shiny Dratini. D has no snacks to coax it and Koraidon scares it.

A female Dragonite lands. D's Dragonite goes tense and shy. Nearby Dragonair explain she only accepts a mate that can defeat her. She takes a fighting stance. Dragonite dodges Dragon Rush then opens Rain Dance and Hurricane. The storm wraps her. She is shocked but refuses to lose easily.

---

# 106: Chapter 106: Returning to the Nest

The duel moves over the sea. The female answers with a stronger Hurricane then Extreme Speed interrupts Thunder and Dragon Tail knocks D's Dragonite onto the island. She says it is still not enough. Dragonite is not crushed and plans harder training back home. She glances at the changed clumsy dragon with new interest.

Dragonite wants to leave. Three young Dragonite try to follow D for the same growth. Dragonite and Koraidon block them. D refuses for now: appetite cost and effort matter. He promises that later he may bring food that helps them grow and imagines a Dragonite delivery company. They agree to wait.

On the flight home Dragonite admits mixed feelings about the female then Rain Dances over the sea. Koraidon answers with Sunny Day and Orichalcum Pulse. D stops the weather war. Sunset over the sea. Time to go home.

---

# 107: Chapter 107: Days Without a Ponyta

Morning ranch life. Combee gather nectar. Dragonite practices Rain Dance. Pawmi herds hens. Chicks free-feed. Pasture sprouts by the Miltank house. Nacli play outside so salt output drops. Smoliv lounges in dense Grassy Terrain with the three Gogoat.

D builds a wooden fence around the flower garden and plans sunflowers for summer. Dinner prep is interrupted by Mayor Lewis on video phone: Spring Horse Race on the **25th**. Betting coupons can be traded for Evolution Stones and Trainer gear. D has only one Water Stone from Teddiursa and wants five stones for Eevee evolutions. Ten days may be too short to catch and train a Horse-type for the win.

---

# 108: Chapter 108: Preparations

D favors Rapidash but skips racing this year: ranch work and no speed food yet. He still wants Glaseado Mountain Ice-types for an ice cellar.

After dinner Smoliv has moved into the Gogoat pen for good. Next morning he buys thermal gear and a kerosene stove in town then asks Robin about an ice cellar. She warns against under-house cold seepage and suggests remodeling the mushroom cave instead.

Robin pitches cooling gear then a Pokémon power generator at **30000**. Pawmi and Dragonite can supply power. D plans to buy and install it.

---

# 109: Chapter 109: Glaseado Mountain

D pays. Robin and Machamp install the generator in the storeroom by the power box. Robin mentions her carpenter parents died twenty years ago and she learned alone. She reclaims the mushroom trays for scrap pay.

D packs Berry Milk and cold gear. Cyclizar will hand out lunch. He cooks improved **Hot Noodle Soup (B+)** — Cold Resistance **lv3** for **6 hours** — and Lucky Pudding for himself and Koraidon then flies toward Glaseado Mountain east of Casseroya Lake. Koraidon charges into the white peak.

---

# 110: Chapter 110: The Strange Trainer on the Snowy Mountain

Snow falls. Cold Resistance holds so D wears a thin jacket. Ground search: Snom eating snow (unsuitable — no ranch snow), Snorunt that flee from Koraidon then an Avalugg that boards Bergmite and migrates downriver into the Paldea Sea.

**Everett** from Alola with Incineroar explains Avalugg migration. He obsesses over Koraidon's muscles. He has not seen Alolan Vulpix but has mapped a wary Glaceon. A wrong cave yields angry Beartic. Camp Pokémon are all muscular: Machamp, Grimmsnarl, Obstagoon. D wants all Eevee forms. Everett wants a muscle battle with Koraidon before giving the Glaceon mark. D asks if he is sure.
