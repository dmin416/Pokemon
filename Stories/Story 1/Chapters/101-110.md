# 101: Chapter 101: Slick as Oil! The Invincible Skill!

Those were honey gummies!

Gelatin powder gave a stable structure. Kitchens used it for jelly, mousse and cheese.

Gummies were one of those foods.

D recalled the method of making honey gummies and started a new batch.

He mixed gelatin powder with water then combined the honey and Nomel Berry juice.

He heated the pot on medium-low until the mix was slightly warm.

He poured the gelatin into the pot and stirred until it melted.

The mix turned clear and golden with no impurity.

When it looked ready he turned off the heat.

He let it cool a little then filled the molds that had come with the refrigerator and set them to chill.

While he waited he made other honey treats.

The results were not ideal.

[Honey Grilled Fish: Cooking Effect Stamina Recovery (Large)]

[Honey Lemon Water: Cooking Effect Revitalization. Consuming it will relieve fatigue and restore energy.]

*"None of these are quite right!"*

He looked at the food and felt a bit frustrated.

Then the alarm rang.

He opened the refrigerator and took the solidified honey gummies out of the molds.

As he looked down the appraisal panel appeared.

[Honey Gummies (A): Made with honey and Nomel Berry these gummies are tangy, sweet and chewy. They are quite favored by some Pokémon.

Cooking Effect: Run Away. Allows Pokémon to gain the skill: guaranteed escape ignoring the opponent's strength.

Evaluation: Don't underestimate this small piece of gummy!]

*"..."*

*Run Away? That effect felt bizarre.*

He looked a bit odd as he glanced at Teddiursa eating pudding nearby.

The skill would not help him much but it might help Teddiursa.

If it met a dangerous Pokémon outside and could not win it could turn and run and be sure of getting away.

Seen that way the honey gummies looked even more exaggerated than the Honey Bun.

If he thought further, Pickup plus Lucky Pudding plus Honey Gummies together might even let Teddiursa steal from Legendary Pokémon.

It could sneak a feather from Zapdos then run and leave Zapdos discharging behind it.

That sounded quite thrilling.

He fed Teddiursa a big piece at once.

Then he advised it earnestly. *"I have one more thing to tell you. If you encounter danger outside like a powerful Pokémon don't hesitate at that time. Just turn around and run. Remember?"*

*"Gurr!"*

Teddiursa agreed with the advice.

Looking at its face D felt the reminder was unnecessary.

Teddiursa was not the type to lock horns with an opponent.

When it first came to steal honey it fled as soon as it saw how scary the ranch was.

Its nature was still that of a cunning little bear.

*"..."*

He decided to pretend he had said nothing.

He turned back to new recipes.

After three days he still had no other dish with a special effect.

The food he made restored stamina or energy. Some of it pulled attention. Pokémon sat in happiness and ignored the world outside.

Time passed and the day came for Teddiursa to leave.

D saw it off at the ranch entrance with Koraidon and the others.

Teddiursa was full of reluctance.

*"Gurr..."*

D smiled. *"Go ahead! If one day you don't want to travel anymore just come back to the ranch. I'll make a sumptuous honey banquet waiting for you."*

*"Gurr..."* Teddiursa wiped a tear then nodded heavily.

Before leaving it wished for one more piece of pudding.

D went back to the kitchen to make it.

The last batch had long been eaten by Teddiursa and Mew.

For a short while he would not see Teddiursa.

He wished it a safe journey and that it would fulfill its dreams.

Life would go on and Teddiursa and Mew would start a new life.

With that sincere wish he made the last pudding for Teddiursa.

About forty minutes later the pudding was done.

He took it from the refrigerator to hand over then noticed a different effect.

The appraisal panel appeared.

[Lucky Pudding (S): Made with emotions of sincere wishing using light cream, MooMoo Fresh Milk and Honey.

Cooking Effect: Fortune Accompanies. When consumed by humans and Pokémon luck permanently increases and when given to Pokémon the probability of picking rare items during their travels permanently increases.

Evaluation: A delicious dessert. Consuming it can make one feel happy and tends to bring good luck.]

He could not believe it as he looked at the pudding.

The effect had changed from temporary to permanent.

The process and ingredients were the same. Why was the effect so different?

Could the mood while cooking have made the difference?

He looked up. Teddiursa waited in front of him with a bright expectant face for the last piece before the journey.

He hesitated a little.

Humans could eat this pudding too and the effect would be permanent.

Then he looked at Teddiursa, took a deep breath and still chose to give it the pudding.

*"Eat it!"*

Teddiursa took it happily, ate it in one bite then rushed into D's arms for a hug.

D caught it and patted its back.

After a while Teddiursa jumped down.

It went outside, waved to D then boarded Mew in Fearow form.

The Fearow flapped and flew high.

They flew on with the wish to see this vast world.

After some time a wide blue sea appeared ahead.

Beside the sea was a coastal city with many cruise ships at the port.

Mew set Teddiursa down on a luxury cruise ship that was about to sail.

There was a flash of light. The Fearow was gone and Mew was there instead.

Mew and Teddiursa floated above a secluded deck and looked out at the view.

**Honk!**

The whistle sounded and the ship started toward the blue sea.

A voice came from the loudspeakers.

*"Attention all passengers. The Saint Ann is officially setting sail. The next stop will be Vermilion City in the Kanto Region!"*

# 102: Chapter 102: Flower Field

The Fearow carrying Teddiursa disappeared into the blue sky.

D stood at the ranch and looked after them with a sense of melancholy.

He had crossed into the Pokémon world yet he was not the one on a journey. He had sent a Pokémon off to travel instead.

That feeling was strange.

Teddiursa had left but life still had to go on.

He took a deep breath and went home to clean the cooking utensils he had just used.

Time had moved into late February.

The crops in the fields were almost ready for harvest again.

It was time to get busy.

Early the next morning he planned to harvest this batch of ripe crops.

When he arrived at the farmland he was surprised to find the flowers he had planted were in bloom.

Many kinds of flowers were mixed together in color. The ranch had a floral fragrance.

The Pokémon living on the farm were all very happy.

Alcremie, Smoliv, Nacli and Pawmi had all been drawn over.

Little Alcremie played hide-and-seek with little Nacli among the tall flowers.

Smoliv found a comfortable shady spot and lingered there.

Pawmi ran to a flower and sniffed it.

It drew in some pollen and sneezed several times in a row.

The happiest of them had to be Combee and Butterfree.

Early in the morning the Combee gathered here and flew back and forth over the blooming flower fields collecting nectar.

Butterfree flapped its wings and paused among the flowers.

D wore a straw hat and watched the scene with a smile.

He remembered how these two kinds of Pokémon lived in the wild.

In the wild a Butterfree and a Combee would often fight over nectar.

On this farm they did not. They stayed in harmony.

*"You guys have fun here. I need to go to work now."*

He said that to the Pokémon then went to the farmland to start the harvest.

Koraidon and Cyclizar came to help and put the tomatoes, cucumbers and turnips into the large baskets he had prepared.

He had never expanded the planting much. Plowing, planting, fertilizing, pest work and weeding all needed his own attention.

He also did not raise too many Pokémon. He was only one person.

If he raised too many he would be overwhelmed. He would neglect some of them and make them unhappy. The quality of their products would fall.

Even so the field work kept them busy for several hours.

Near noon the large baskets were full and D was sweating from exhaustion.

He wiped his forehead and looked at the fruit and vegetables.

The profits from agriculture were far less than those from animal husbandry.

Leaving such a large piece of land unplanted would be wasteful and the crops could be kept for himself and his Pokémon to eat besides selling.

The only drawback was that these seasonal vegetables could not be stored for long.

*It would be great to have an ice cellar.*

Crops like potatoes and turnips could be stored for a long time in a low-temperature place.

Building an ice cellar required money and specialized cooling facilities.

Those large cooling facilities used a lot of electricity and he would probably pay quite a bit in bills every month.

Was there another way?

The first thing that came to mind was Ice-type Pokémon.

Ordinary Ice-types would not do. It had to be the kind that liked to live in ice caves.

Like Cryogonal, Avalugg or Glaceon.

An ice cellar could serve as a home for them or be used to store food. That would save effort and money.

Among these Glaceon was the one D most wanted to capture.

He had a dream that one day he would collect all of Eevee's evolutions.

He pictured Glaceon living in the ice cellar. Leafeon would take care of crops. Flareon would provide warmth in winter. Jolteon would supply electricity. Vaporeon would water with Dragonite from time to time.

The Eevee family was simply versatile.

Besides those he would also need a Pokémon capable of cultivating the land.

Looking at the farmland and crops in front of him he was lost in thought.

He suddenly thought of a Pokémon.

The Pokémon was Calyrex.

Calyrex was the legendary king who once ruled ancient Galar. It was said to possess great wisdom and to view all things past, present and future.

This Legendary Pokémon was also known as the King of Bountiful Harvests.

It had the ability to heal spirits and make plants sprout. When it waved its right hand flowers bloomed and green grass thrived. When it waved its left hand fields became fertile and crops grew well.

That was practically the god of agriculture.

If he could entice it to the farm it would be a perfect match for the job.

Thinking of this he felt he was going too far.

Not long ago he had been contemplating stealing its horse. Now he wanted to bring the king itself here.

He silently prayed. *"Sinful sinful. Calyrex-sama please bless my farm with bountiful harvests every year!"*

He even pondered if he should build a shrine for Calyrex.

According to what he knew Calyrex indeed got things done.

The only pity was that it was unclear whether Calyrex in the Galar Region could sense faith coming from the Paldea Region.

Meanwhile at the southernmost tip of Galar a snowfield was covered in snow.

A small and slender Pokémon, always elegant and dignified, weakly opened its eyes.

It looked across the distance and saw a human praying to it.

Feeling that slight trace of faith it regained some of its spirit.

If D were here he would have recognized the King of Bountiful Harvests, Calyrex.

It had the special ability to gain strength by absorbing faith.

In recent years the faith from the people of Galar had grown thin.

The people of the new era no longer believed in the King of Bountiful Harvests. Its power had waned and its body had weakened.

Feeling the faint trace of faith Calyrex murmured softly.

*"Who would have thought that in this era there would still be humans who trust in me..."*

# 103: Chapter 103: Does Dragonite Want to Return to Dragonite Island?

League Calendar Year 298, spring, March 14.

This was the last month of spring. Scorching summer would follow close behind.

D really disliked being drenched in sweat. That morning he took the money he had saved and headed to Porto Marinada Town to buy a few air conditioners.

He rode Cyclizar to the department store in town and under the guidance of a sales assistant he returned to the floor selling appliances.

There he selected four air conditioners. He planned to install one in the Salt House where Nacli lived, one in the Pokémon House where Miltank lived, one in the Chicken House and one in his own cabin.

A hundred and twenty thousand League coins were spent in an instant. That was all the money saved over the past half month from selling crops, rock salt, milk and goat milk.

He had been mentally prepared but looking at his nearly empty wallet he still felt a pang of pain.

There was no choice. These were necessities of life that had to be installed before summer arrived.

Seeing this customer buy so many air conditioners at once the sales assistant was surprised but still arranged for staff to come and install them.

Once the four air conditioners were all installed D felt relieved.

This way by the time summer came he would not be too hot to sleep.

Thanks to Miltank and the rest joining the ranch the funding problem was solved and the ranch officially entered a revival phase.

In this phase D mainly prepared to do two things.

First he would expand his residence into a real home. A fully functional home with a bedroom, living room, cellar, study and bathroom.

The bedroom was for resting. The living room could entertain guests who visited the ranch. The study could hold account books, reading and recipes.

As for the cellar, summer would arrive in about two weeks and hop planting could officially start. Without a cellar he could not start a brewing business.

Besides that he also needed to prepare some money to purchase barrels for brewing.

The second thing was to plant more summer crops and use them to develop even more new dishes.

Then he would see if any of the dishes met the criteria and use them to strengthen the Pokémon on the ranch, achieving a cycle.

At noon D began preparing lunch for all the Pokémon.

He opened the refrigerator, looked at the abundant ingredients inside and paused on a jar of golden sticky liquid.

Soon after the information about this jar of liquid appeared.

[Tulip Honey: Honey made from the pollen and nectar of tulips. Carefully tasting can bring out a faint floral aroma from it making it an excellent ingredient for desserts.]

This was the honey the Combee had gathered from the flower field recently.

The first time he saw them gathering he got a fright.

He remembered tulips contained mannose and galactose which were toxic to bees in that other world. Bees gathering tulip nectar would die on the flowers.

The constitution of Pokémon was beyond what ordinary bees could compare to.

They were both bees but tulip nectar had no effect on the Combee.

Seeing this D felt relieved and let them gather.

It was a pity Teddiursa had left too early. If it had stayed a few more days it could have tasted this new flavor of honey.

All this honey had been used to make Lucky Pudding but despite making so much of it he had not managed a batch with a permanent luck effect again.

He was not discouraged. To him whether it was permanent or not did not matter.

While he was on the ranch he could have a pudding for the luck effect at any time. If he kept eating them the effect they brought was virtually permanent when averaged out.

*I wonder where Teddiursa and Mew are right now.*

He pressed down the concern and started preparing lunch for Koraidon and the others.

Today's lunch was the same as usual. There was grilled steak and sandwiches plus a serving of smashed cucumbers and a cup of optional Berry Juice and MooMoo Milk.

During the meal D looked at Dragonite and realized he had not asked about its move training in a long time. He asked Dragonite a question.

*"Did you master the Electric Type move Thunder I taught you earlier?"*

Talking about moves Dragonite immediately became excited.

*"Kroooh!"* It nodded its big head.

It had also managed the combo of Rain Dance plus Hurricane plus Thunder that D had taught it last time.

*"Oh?"* D was a bit surprised. *"You've progressed quite quickly!"*

*"Kroooh~"*

So after the meal Dragonite dragged D outside with its big claws to show the progress it had made.

D stood under the eaves with Koraidon and Cyclizar on either side of him.

They saw Dragonite flap its wings and fly into the air.

It concentrated and the once clear sky was covered with a dense layer of dark clouds. Tendrils of Electric-type energy swirled within.

It started to rain.

At first it was a light drizzle. Gradually the rain grew heavier and poured down with a patter.

Dragonite was bathed in this heavy rain. Its body was drenched glossy by the raindrops and it looked fierce.

The next second Dragonite flapped its wings and a Hurricane formed. It merged with the pouring rain to create a large storm.

D watched the rather spectacular scene.

If an ordinary Pokémon faced this move without special environmental training it would probably end up in a flustered mess.

**Boom!**

A giant bolt of lightning flickered from the thundercloud down and struck the grass below into a deep pit.

**Sss!**

D took a sharp breath.

The power was terrifying.

If a Pokémon got hit by that move he simply could not imagine it.

The storm began to subside. The dark clouds above gradually dispersed and revealed the clear blue sky.

**Clap clap clap!**

D started applauding. *"That's amazing!"*

*"Kroooh..."*

Hearing this Dragonite sheepishly landed beside D and used its big claw to scratch its head.

It still had a long way to go.

In Dragonite's mind flashed the image of the mysterious Pokémon it had seen above the typhoon's eye, entirely pitch black, resembling a roaming dragon.

Looking at the Dragonite before him D could not help but feel moved.

Extreme Speed, Dragon Tail, Thunder Punch, Hurricane, Agility, Dragon Dance, Rain Dance, Thunderbolt and Thunder.

Before he knew it Dragonite had learned so many moves.

D felt like there was nothing left for him to teach it.

Thinking of this he asked, *"You've already learned so many moves. What are your plans next?"*

Hearing this Dragonite immediately panicked.

Hearing D's words it seemed like he was saying he had nothing left to teach and it could leave now.

Was he trying to send it away?

Dragonite did not want to leave. It looked forlorn as it furrowed its brow. *"Kroooh..."*

Humans and Pokémon could not speak the same language but looking at Dragonite's expression D understood what it meant.

He felt a mixture of amusement and helplessness. *"I'm not trying to send you away. I just want to ask about your next plans. I mean I'll soon have nothing left to teach you. By then will you want to leave the ranch and return to Dragonite Island?"*

D had not forgotten. Dragonite had initially followed him wanting to learn moves.

Hearing this Dragonite fell into thought.

At first it had followed D wanting to learn moves.

D had given it a Berry Platter to help it learn Extreme Speed.

D had gotten Koraidon to teach it to use Dragon Tail.

D had accompanied it under the rain to practice Thunder Punch and Rain Dance and had even gotten sick from it.

Worried about D it had then realized Agility while delivering milk.

Dragonite was not staying with D just to get stronger anymore.

It liked this human and wanted to stay with him forever.

# 104: Chapter 104: The Day of the Dragon King's Return!

Thinking of this Dragonite resolutely shook its head.

It wanted to stay and live together with D.

But before that Dragonite hoped D could accompany it to Dragonite Island.

It wanted to show off the moves it had learned in front of the other Dragonite and tell them it had become the most powerful Dragonite in the group.

Dragonite looked serious and tugged at D's arm with its chubby paw.

*"Kroooh!"*

*"What's wrong?"*

D did not react at once. It took him a while to realize that Dragonite seemed to want to take him somewhere.

Dragonite could not speak and the two of them could not communicate normally.

After a long time of calling, seeing that D still did not understand, Dragonite drooped its head looking disheartened and aggrieved.

It looked very pitiful in this state.

D's heart was moved. He reached out to pat Dragonite's head, recalled its recent actions and guessed.

*"You want me to go somewhere with you?"*

Hearing this Dragonite looked happy at once.

*"Kroooh!"*

The place Dragonite wanted to take him...

Intuition told D it could not be Casseroya Lake and it could not be the cliff where they first met.

From what he had seen in Yousuke's picture book about Dragonite's habits they flew from the sea to Casseroya Lake every evening, stayed the night and returned to the sea at dawn.

That meant the place Dragonite wanted to take him was likely Dragonite Island in the sea, home to many Dragonite.

While thinking about it D asked, *"Is it far from the ranch?"*

There were things to do at the ranch every day and he could not leave for too long.

*"Kroooh!"*

Dragonite shook its head. At its speed they would get there quickly.

Seeing this D agreed. *"Alright."*

Upon seeing D agree Dragonite immediately cheered and indicated they could set off now.

D looked up at the sky.

It was still early. He reckoned he could be back before dark.

To avoid any accidents on the way he prepared food for the Pokémon at the ranch in advance.

He told them he would be going out with Dragonite and if he returned too late they should not wait up for him.

None of the Pokémon had any objections except for Koraidon, who wanted to come along.

With no choice D took it with him.

After handling affairs at the ranch D rode on Koraidon, left the ranch under Dragonite's lead and set off toward Dragonite Island.

The two Pokémon gradually ascended. The ranch below became a small black dot and then disappeared into the Paldea meadow.

They felt the wind on their ears. The sky above was a clear blue and in the distance Paldea Sea was endless.

The speed of the two Pokémon was not slow. After about a dozen minutes they left Paldea's borders and reached the ocean.

Looking at this familiar place Dragonite happily said to D, *"Kroooh~"*

It mimicked the powerful mysterious Pokémon with intense dance movements.

Seeing its actions D suddenly realized. *"Did you meet that black Pokémon here?"*

Dragonite nodded repeatedly. *"Kroooh!"*

It looked up toward the higher sky and indicated that the Pokémon had flown higher and disappeared above the clouds.

He added with regret, *"I wish I had come with you back then..."*

After all it was a shiny Rayquaza.

Seeing it even once would make his journey to this world worthwhile.

Most people rarely if ever saw Legendary Pokémon in their lifetime.

Seeing D so obsessed Dragonite could not help but look upwards. *"Kroooh?"*

Should they try to find it there?

D understood what Dragonite meant and shook his head. *"Better not."*

For Rayquaza the whole planet's atmosphere was its domain. It had been in Paldea before and there was no telling where it had flown to now. It might even be on the other side of the planet.

Occasionally a small island of white sand appeared with a few coconut trees. Some Wiglett poked their heads from the beach and enjoyed the afternoon sun.

Now and then Finizen would leap high out of the water chasing and playing with their companions.

In mid-air D looked down at the vibrant scene.

*"These are Pokémon rarely seen on land!"*

This also proved they were already far from the Paldea region.

As time ticked away, just when D no longer remembered how long he had left the ranch, a small island suddenly appeared on the horizon.

It was a vibrant island covered in lush vegetation as seen from afar.

The vegetation formed a circular belt around the center of the island. Under the sunlight the entire island looked lively and green.

Seeing the island Dragonite looked excited. It immediately turned to D and exclaimed, *"Kroooh!"*

D raised an eyebrow and asked, *"Is that where you used to live?"*

*"Kroooh!"*

Dragonite nodded its head.

D did not ask further. Seeing they were about to reach their destination Dragonite and Koraidon lowered their flying altitude, flew close to the sea surface then landed on a steep cliff.

Waves hit the cliff below. D jumped off Koraidon.

*"Kroooh~"*

Having returned to its former home Dragonite was very happy and eagerly led the way.

D and Koraidon followed behind and curiously observed the unique surroundings.

Unlike other places the plants on this island were exceptionally lush. There was deep grass, robust trees and yellow and white wildflowers growing in the forest with branches laden with plump berries.

It was a very special place.

The land there was covered with beautiful flowers and trees and the flowers bloomed all year round.

It was said that all of this was influenced by the power of Dragon-type Pokémon.

If this was Dragonite's hometown then there must be more than just one Dragonite here.

Were these lush plants also influenced by the Dragonite on the island?

He wanted to pick a large leaf to examine but in the next second a small figure suddenly peeked out from behind the leaf.

*"Ah!"*

Koraidon and Dragonite turned back at the sound and saw a petite pale blue-scaled Pokémon with a bright white belly peeking curiously at this stranger from behind the leaf.

The Pokémon had blue eyes and a white bump on its forehead which was the developing horn.

It had white fins on the sides of its head and a large round white nose. Although it was snake-like it lacked the sinister coldness of snakes and instead looked very cute.

The Dratini immediately looked like it was about to cry.

It was not scolding Dratini. It was just worried about Dratini's safety.

Dratini was at its weakest stage. During that time it would be protected by the Dragonite and Dragonair in the tribe until it evolved.

On this island all Dratini lived in the central lake and could not sneak out without an adult.

That was a Shiny Dratini.

D was very charmed.

The wild Dragonite by the lakeside watched this scene dumbfounded.

It was showing off to its companions the powerful moves it had mastered.

How had it become so powerful?

It had found its Trainer.

The wild Dragonite looked puzzled. *"Kroooh?"*

It did not know what a Trainer was.

Thinking of Koraidon's description of a Trainer Dragonite narrated. *"Kroooh!"*

A Trainer was a human who could help Pokémon become stronger and learn moves.

At that very moment hearing these words all the Dragonite seeking to grow stronger turned their gaze to the only human present.

D immediately felt a chill down his spine.

But before he could speak Dragonite put on a wary expression standing in front of him. *"Kroooh!"*

This is my Trainer.

# 105: Chapter 105: Dragonite's Love

Dragonite Island, the central lake, three in the afternoon.

D and Koraidon sat by the lake gazing at the sky dotted with white clouds.

Not far away a Dragonite was surrounded by everyone recounting events that had occurred recently.

D and Koraidon chose not to disturb them. They appreciated the island's unique landscape from their spot by the lake.

Although the island sat in the depths of the sea the atmosphere here felt no different from that on land.

The circular lake among the trees looked like a full moon.

If night came D could imagine the scene here.

The lake surface would reflect the night sky. A full moon in the sky and also a full moon in the lake water. When Dratini swam through it the moon would break along the ripples.

There was no need to worry. After a moment the ripples would settle and the moon in the lake would look whole again.

The Dratini would rest on that full moon bathing in the moonlight and entering their dreams.

The grass by the lake was soft. Sunlight painted patches through the tree shadows and the wind was gentle.

His heart was at peace. He could not help but lie down beside Koraidon his gaze passing through the circular opening to the blue sky above.

Suddenly a shadow passed over his head. When D looked closely he saw several petite Dratini hovering over him their big eyes curiously observing him lying on the ground.

Their shadows blocked the light above. Their expressions of wonder looked puzzled as to why this human was suddenly lying on the ground.

D was slightly startled then reached out to poke the cheek of one of the hatchlings.

Sensing the touch a smile appeared on D's face.

The little Dratini felt smooth and soft to the touch.

Surrounded by so many adorable Dragonite hatchlings D felt he had reached a high point.

Dratini were so cute.

Compared with Salamence, Hydreigon and those Dragon-type Pokémon Dragonite truly was very tame and friendly both to humans and hatchlings.

*"Dra-tini~"*

The touched Dratini let out a melodious and innocent cry.

D then sat up and turned his head to start searching the lake's surface for that Shiny Dratini.

However after searching for a long time he could not find any trace of it.

*"Strange. Could I have seen it wrong?"*

He asked a Dragonair nearby. *"Have you seen a pink Dratini around here?"*

*"Draaaco-nair?"*

The Dragonair blinked seemingly not quite understanding his question.

Seeing this D explained. *"It's a different color not the same as you Dratini."*

This time the Dragonair understood. It knew which Dratini this human was talking about then happily dived into the lake and soon returned with a pink Dratini.

Gazing at this uniquely colored Dratini D was delighted at once.

Indeed it was the Shiny Dratini.

Ordinary Dratini are light blue but this Dratini was light pink looking very girlish and extremely cute.

This Dratini brought by its companion looked at him shyly.

Seeing this scene D felt that this Dratini might have a timid personality.

When he attempted to reach out and touch it the Dratini immediately showed a frightened expression.

Noticing this D instinctively wanted to pull out candy from his pocket to coax it like a child but soon realized he had brought nothing except two bottles of emergency Berry Milk on this trip.

*"What a pity."*

D looked a bit regretful.

If only he had known Dragonite was bringing him to Dragonite Island he would have brought all the snacks from his refrigerator.

So D could only try to convey friendliness to it. *"Don't be afraid. We won't harm you. Um... we just want to be friends with you."*

Yet this Dratini still felt very scared.

Following its gaze D then understood what the Dratini was afraid of.

*"hrrm..."*

Upon realizing D's gaze Koraidon next to him immediately showed an innocent expression.

*"..."*

He wanted to say more when suddenly all conversations among the gathered Dragonite ceased.

A wild Dragonite flew over from the sky and slowly landed on the island.

Then D noticed his own Dragonite looking at the other appearing quite tense.

Something was not right.

D narrowed his eyes picking up on an unusual vibe.

Pokémon could also experience love like humans then reproduce and flourish.

Just like Meowth in the anime which loved the female Meowth Madonna but Madonna eventually chose to be with Persian and rejected it.

Some Pokémon even fall in love with other species. The anime even featured phrases like Tropius falling in love with Miss Joy's Meganium.

And now seeing Dragonite muster courage to step forward this scene reminded D of a shy young boy confessing to his crush.

Dragonite looked very nervous and shy stepping forward without knowing what to say to the other.

Immediately after D saw that wild Dragonite assume a fighting stance.

D was taken aback and involuntarily asked, *"What are they doing?"*

*"hrrm..."* Koraidon did not understand such matters but recognized that wild Dragonite.

Nearby Dragonair also watched that scene softly speaking as if answering D's question.

*"Draaaco-nair..."*

That Dragonite was confessing to the other.

But all the Dragonite on the island knew that the female Dragonite was quite special.

It was extremely strong and every Dragonite that confessed to it got beaten badly.

According to it only a Dragonite that could defeat it head-on could become its future mate and together raise even stronger Dratini.

However D could not understand Pokémon language and naturally could not grasp Dragonair's words.

He only saw his Dragonite and that female Dragonite start battling.

The battle began. The female Dragonite unleashed her full aura exuding terrifying killing intent then dove towards Dragonite.

Faced with this seemingly dreadful attack Dragonite was initially quite panicked but quickly pulled itself together.

It increased its speed to dodge the Dragon Rush then used Rain Dance.

Vast clouds rolled in and the sky began to pour down rain.

Dragonite flapped its wings creating a Hurricane. The storm mixed with rain roaring as it swept forth.

In an instant the female Dragonite was enveloped by this storm and rain.

It was very shocked.

Though puzzled why this Dragonite which once could not learn any powerful moves had become so strong it still clenched its teeth focusing completely on the battle.

It would not be defeated so easily.

Just as Teddiursa had said this world was vast with many things it could do.

For example covering the entire world with its rain.

A torrential downpour fell from them. Raindrops hit the sea surface and merged into the ocean.

Dragonite flapped its wings stirring currents and soared freely amidst the rain.

The Kilowattrel and Pelipper that had been flying outside all day began to return home.

Gazing at that scene D could not help but exclaim, *"How beautiful!"*

Evening was approaching. It was time to go home.

*"Paw-pawmi!"*

Ahead was the Pokémon House where three Miltank lived.

The pasture seeds he sowed on the land in front of the house over a fortnight ago had sprouted. He believed it would not be long before they grew into lush delicious pasture.

White snowflakes swirled down from the peak.

This snowfall covered the entire Glaseado Mountain. As far as the eye could see the mountain was silver-white.

The temperature here was extremely low and the breath turned into white mist the moment it hit the air dissipating quickly.

Thanks to the effect of the hot soup the cold-resistant suit had not yet been needed.

Since the effect of Cold Resistance lasts for 6 hours D only wore a thin jacket into the mountain.

Koraidon slowed its pace gliding in mid-air while D looked down to observe the situation below.

The entire mountain was covered with a thick layer of white snow and occasionally pine trees could be seen standing amidst the white.

These are very hardy trees that can grow normally even in environments as cold as minus fifty degrees.

The trunk was upright and the pine needles were dark green adding a splash of green to this place.

Perhaps due to the weather very few Pokémon were wandering in the wild.

At this time the wild Pokémon inhabiting this mountain range were likely all hiding in their cozy nests.

Either sleeping, gazing at the snowy scenery outside or snuggling with companions for warmth licking each other's fur.

If it were him in such extreme weather D thought he would choose to stay home and not go out.

Looking at the vast expanse of white below D pondered as he patted Koraidon's back and said to it, *"Let's land first!"*

The body colors of Ice-type Pokémon are mostly white or light blue making them easily blend into the snowy landscape when viewed from above and carelessness might let them slip by unnoticed.

Therefore D planned to explore from the ground.

*"hrrm!"*

Koraidon received the command and immediately descended onto the snow below.

D jumped down from Koraidon's back.

The pair walked on the snow. D's black leather boots made a crunching sound and with every step Koraidon took it left several footprints.

**Crunch.**

Snom an Ice and Bug-type Pokémon.

In reality Snom looked very cute its white round body like a dumpling.
