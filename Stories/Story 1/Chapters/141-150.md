# 141: Chapter 141: Braised Chicken with Wild Mushrooms

In the end D came back to the ranch with a basket of wild ginger, apricots and mountain mushrooms on his back.

Butterfree had found the apricots. That tree had a lot of them. D only picked a few to taste and left the rest for the wild Pokémon in the forest.

Mountain mushrooms were almost everywhere on the back hill and easy to find.

The load was too much and D found it a bit strenuous.

Seeing that Koraidon and the other Pokémon playing on the ranch hurried over wanting to take the basket for him.

D smiled. *"I will make something tasty for you all for lunch today!"*

Koraidon helped him take the basket down and could not help being curious. *"Kor?"*

*"Have you heard of chicken stewed with mushrooms?"* D asked.

*"Kor!"* Koraidon looked eager and nodded again and again.

*"Oh? Where did you hear about that?"* D was a bit surprised. Paldea had plenty of Chinese restaurants but Koraidon had been in the Paldea Giant Pit the whole time before.

Could Professor Sada and Professor Turo have made it?

He could not figure it out so he treated it as Koraidon talking nonsense and did not think more of it.

He asked Koraidon to take the basket to the kitchen so he could wash the vegetables later.

Koraidon went to do it.

Meanwhile Pawmo heard that D meant to use the chickens under its care for cooking and looked sad.

*"Paw-mo..."*

Seeing Pawmo's face D paused then understood.

*"Pawmo, are you worried I will cook the hens from the Chicken House?"* D asked.

*"Paw-mo..."* Pawmo nodded.

Cyclizar beside them was a bit puzzled. *"Cy?"*

In its understanding hens were meant to be eaten.

D smiled and explained to the Pokémon. *"Pawmo spends time with the hens every day so it does not want to see them eaten. That is a very normal thing."*

Cyclizar nodded with a blank cute look. *"Cy..."*

To keep from leaving the kids with a bad memory D thought for a while then went on.

*"Do not worry. These hens are kept for eggs. You should know Dragonite and the others can eat up to a hundred eggs every day."*

*"Kroooh!"* Dragonite hurried to defend itself. It did not eat that many eggs! It still liked fish most!

D explained carefully. *"Egg pudding is also made with eggs. You can eat two baskets every day. Sandwiches have fried eggs too. I usually put egg sauce in cooking. All of that uses eggs."*

Dragonite thought it over carefully and realized it might really eat a hundred eggs.

It scratched its head in embarrassment.

Seeing Pawmo ease up D asked again. *"What if I buy other chickens?"*

Pawmo thought seriously then nodded a little. *"Paw-mo!"*

*"Good kid."* D rubbed its head then went inside for his wallet. He mounted Cyclizar and said to the others. *"I am going to town to buy an old hen. I will be back soon."*

Hearing that the Pokémon waited at home.

It was almost noon. Most of the vendors at Porto Marinada Market had already packed up to go home for lunch.

D walked around several times before he found a meat shop that had not closed yet and bought a freshly killed three-yellow chicken.

This kind of chicken had firm rich meat. After a long simmer the broth was fresh and the meat tender. It went perfectly with the smell of mountain mushrooms.

After paying D thought of how many Pokémon were on the ranch. One chicken might not be enough so he bought a few more and picked up two clay pots while he was at it.

After he got back to the ranch he sent Cyclizar out to play and started preparing lunch.

He planned to make chicken stewed with mountain mushrooms but that dish took a long time. It needed four hours on low heat.

So for today's lunch D made the usual. Rice, tomatoes and eggs, fried meat patties, sandwiches, Berry Juice and MooMoo Milk.

After lunch D prepped the ingredients and put them in the clay pots to simmer.

Several Pokémon watched the stove eagerly.

Seeing them like that D found it a bit funny and waved them off. *"Go play outside. It will take a long time. You will get to eat it at dinner."*

The Pokémon had no choice but to give up.

They only craved it because Koraidon had said how good it was. Otherwise they would not have been so eager.

Since D said it would take a long while they all left the house and went to play on the grass outside.

D was reading in his room. He glanced up and saw Koraidon leading Cyclizar and Dragonite to the riverside.

The soil there had been softened by the river. The jumping Psyduck had left several mud pits there too.

D was afraid they would get dirty and shouted from afar. *"Do not roll in the mud pits! Koraidon, bring Cyclizar and the others back to play on the grass!"*

Koraidon heard D from far off and looked at the mud pits nearby with disdain.

It answered loudly. *"Kor!"*

It would not roll in the mud pits! It had not even touched the mud!

Right after saying that Koraidon saw Cyclizar step into a mud pit, run to the river's edge and lower its head to drink.

Koraidon: *"..."*

It felt exasperated and pulled Cyclizar onto clean grass.

It fumbled a Berry from somewhere and traded with a Psyduck across the way.

Soon a Psyduck came over and used Water Gun on Cyclizar to wash off the mud.

Koraidon was satisfied. As long as D did not see it had not rolled in a mud pit.

*"Kroooh..."*

Dragonite watched and thought Koraidon was quite nice to Cyclizar. It had even used a Berry to get Psyduck to wash it.

Koraidon gave the Berry to the Psyduck.

The Psyduck made a happy sound and ran off holding the Berry.

Koraidon watched the Psyduck across the way and suddenly recalled something from the not-so-distant future.

The crops in the field matured and attracted the Psyduck to come steal produce. D caught them and brought them into the ranch.

Since then those Psyduck had taken on the role of harvesters.

They would pick all the fruit when the crops matured and put it into large baskets D had prepared. Some would be sold and some would be kept to make tasty treats for them.

Since they were companions it did not care anymore!

Koraidon brought Cyclizar and Dragonite back to the grass in front and like an adult started guiding them.

D glanced over from his book, saw they were getting along well and kept reading.

This book had been given to him by Mayor Lewis. It held a lot of knowledge about ranch life and D would take it out to read now and then.

# 142: Chapter 142: Another Customer Orders Milk

At four-thirty in the afternoon the chicken soup was ready.

D lifted the lid of the clay pot. A rich smell of chicken mixed with mountain mushrooms rose to his nose.

Inside the pot the broth was bubbling with a layer of shiny oil on top. The chicken and mushrooms were plump and soaked with flavor.

As he looked down the panel appeared.

[Braised Chicken with Mushrooms (A): A dish made by simmering freshly picked mountain mushrooms with three-yellow chicken. The soup is fresh and delicious, favored by certain Pokémon.

Cooking Effect: Oily and Slippery lv3. After consumption the Pokémon's fur becomes slick and shiny, not prone to shedding and its health improves to the maximum.

Evaluation: The perfect dish for autumn!]

*Oily and Slippery? It seemed to work only on Pokémon with fur.* D thought. *The effect is not bad. It is just right for Pawmo and Glaceon on the ranch.*

Other breeders went through a lot of trouble feeding many Pokéblocks to get this effect. Now one dish of braised chicken with mushrooms would suffice.

D picked up a piece of chicken for a closer taste.

The chicken was tender and flavorful. The meat was firm yet juicy with the rich smell of mountain mushrooms. It was a perfect combination.

Lying on the grass outside basking in the sun Koraidon and the other Pokémon twitched their noses and suddenly caught that supremely tempting smell.

*"Kor!"*

Koraidon sat up at once. Dragonite and Cyclizar beside it caught the scent too and could not resist drifting toward the house.

Hearing the commotion behind him D turned off the stove and used tools to set the hot clay pot on the small dining table.

*"Be careful. It is hot!"* he reminded them.

The clay pot was indeed hot and several Pokémon were not very good with utensils so they could only sit at the table staring blankly.

D thought for a moment then took a few small bowls from the kitchen and began serving the meat into them.

Alcremie, Dolliv and Nacli were not interested in this smell so they glanced over then kept playing outside.

Glaceon and Pawmo seemed to like it. D noticed them salivating.

Glaceon looked at the steaming piece of chicken in front of it and tried to paw at it then got scalded.

After a brief thought Glaceon blew a cool breath at the meat then swallowed it easily.

Seeing that Cyclizar and the other Pokémon all looked at it at once.

*"Bui?!"* Realizing so many were watching Glaceon was startled.

Cyclizar looked down at the meat in front of it then at Glaceon's empty plate.

*"Cy..."*

*"Bui."*

With that Glaceon turned its head and blew a cold breath on the piece of meat.

Only then did Cyclizar start eating happily.

The remaining Pokémon followed suit so Glaceon could only help blow on the meat for the others while sneaking bites for itself.

Watching this group of Pokémon getting along so well D could not help but smile.

He looked toward Pawmo eating joyfully and asked. *"How does it taste?"*

*"Paw-mo~"* Pawmo nodded again and again.

D teased on purpose. *"What if I want to use those chickens for cooking in the future?"*

*"Paw-mo?!"*

Pawmo froze. It blinked and thought over his words seriously looking quite conflicted.

All these days it had been spending time with that group of hens. Although they could not talk and could not understand its intentions watching the fluffy little chicks grow into big chickens gave it a real sense of accomplishment.

But the chicken tasted so good.

Delicious chicken on one side. The hens it had raised on the other. Pawmo did not know how to choose.

It even felt that if it saw those chickens again it would think of the taste of today's dish.

*"Paw-mo..."*

Pawmo looked troubled.

Just then Cyclizar said. *"Cy."*

Hens were food by nature.

It had known that even before it came to the ranch.

At those words the scales in Pawmo's heart tipped.

*"Paw-mo..."*

Seeing it so conflicted D could not help but laugh. *"Alright. Do not worry. I will not use them for cooking."*

They were just a few ordinary chickens. If Pawmo liked raising them he would let it. He would not mind the expense.

D sat down eating while placing meat into the bowls in front of the Pokémon.

He had cooked several chickens and before long they were all gone.

After the meal Koraidon took Cyclizar and the other Pokémon outside to lie contentedly on the grass.

Looking at that scene D felt he would not need to make dinner tonight.

That was just as well so he could take a break.

After washing the dishes it was already evening.

Zack arrived in his large truck to pick up the goods. D chatted with him while helping to load the buckets of cow and goat milk along with a large bag of rock salt onto the truck.

*"Hey, did you hear? There was a mine collapse over near Zapapico Town!"*

D was a bit surprised. *"A mine collapse? What happened?"*

Zack said. *"Apparently a construction crew accidentally disturbed a wild Tyranitar living in the mountains when they were digging a tunnel. That Tyranitar got really angry and collapsed many of the mine shafts! Fortunately it happened at night so besides a dozen miners getting injured there were no deaths."*

Tyranitar also known as the Sandstorm Monarch was powerful. It was a pseudo-legendary in the Johto Region.

D's collector instincts stirred a little. *"Is it still there?"*

*"I do not know."* Zack shook his head. *"Maybe it left! But after the cave-in many ancient ruins appeared underground. Quite a few adventurers headed there upon hearing the news hoping to find the legendary ancient treasures."*

Ancient ruins and ancient treasures ran through his mind.

D thought it over and realized that there were not any famous ruins in the Paldea Region.

As for ancient treasures they likely referred to the Auspicious Armor and Malicious Armor. Those were special items that allowed Charcadet to evolve into Armarouge and Ceruledge.

*"Alright, I have to go pick up goods at other farms. See you later!"*

With a wave Zack went back to his truck and left with a load of goods.

With everything for the day done D took a deep breath feeling completely relaxed. He planned to take a bath later then watch some TV before going to sleep.

At this moment another guest arrived at the ranch.

Upon hearing a sound D turned to see the Seed Shop Owner.

He could not help feeling a bit surprised. *"Elo? What brings you here?"*

Elo explained. *"Well, I heard that the goat milk from your place in town is very popular and hard to get so I was wondering if I could order directly from you..."*

*"Of course!"* D called over Dragonite and told it about the matter.

After listening Dragonite thumped its chest making a promise. *"Kroooh! Kroooh!"*

Leave it to me!

*"I will leave it to you then."* D patted its head.

Seeing this Elo was very grateful. *"Thanks a lot."*

D waved his hand casually. *"It is nothing. We are all neighbors! Plus you gave me a bag of fertilizer."*

Seeing D so generous Elo broke into a smile. He thought of that incident and once again sincerely thanked D.

*"Thanks to you. If it were not for you Abby and I would still be pushing Bell to do something she does not like."*

# 143: Chapter 143: Dragonite Delivery Service

Speaking of Bell D thought of the girl suffering from paranoia.

He asked casually. *"How is Bell doing now?"*

Elo sighed. *"She is still the same. She stays in her room all day drawing and barely comes out except at meal times."*

D nodded and suddenly had a good idea.

*"I have a method we could try but I am not sure if it will work."*

*"Oh?"* Elo asked quickly. *"What method? Tell me about it!"*

D said. *"Let your daughter start raising a Pokémon from an egg."*

*"I think she is not just afraid of Pokémon. She is afraid of the outside world. Like you said her childhood experiences left her with a psychological shadow making her mistakenly believe that everything outside is full of dangers."*

*"In this case doing it all at once is impossible. We have to take it slow first by giving her a Pokémon egg to take care of."*

*"To her a Pokémon egg would not seem so frightening. It is just a harmless egg."*

*"Once she is gradually familiar with the egg if the Pokémon hatches then she might not feel so scared and might even accept the Pokémon she hatched with her own hands."*

Elo nodded again and again looking excited. *"Why did I not think of this?!"*

D laughed. *"It is just a suggestion. Maybe it can help you."*

Elo thought carefully then asked again. *"What kind of Pokémon egg do you think I should give Bell?"*

Hearing this D pondered while saying. *"Firstly it cannot be a Scyther egg nor any Pokémon that looks large and frightening."*

Elo said. *"Mm-hmm. Then what?"*

D continued. *"Secondly it should be a Pokémon that is easy to get close to people and does not randomly emit fire or electricity like Pikachu because when stimulated it discharges."*

Elo agreed with a nod.

He thought of a few suitable Pokémon.

A few types of Pokémon popped into D's mind and then he suggested them.

*"Ralts. A small humanoid Pokémon that can use psychic powers to sense the Trainer's feelings and thoughts."*

*"And also Vulpix, Togepi, Sunkern, Slowpoke, Eevee..."*

Saying this D suddenly realized that Pokémon like Ralts and Vulpix were rare and it was hard for ordinary people to obtain their eggs.

After some thinking he suggested. *"Then how about Eevee!"*

Eevee looked harmless and many people raised this Pokémon as a pet in ordinary families. Their eggs were relatively easy to obtain.

Elo responded with excitement. *"I got it. Thank you so much for your advice D!"*

D smiled. Seeing that Elo was about to leave he asked Dragonite to follow and familiarize itself with the route so they could start delivering milk to them early in the morning.

Dragonite had no objections and nodded before following Elo to Porto Marinada Town.

Watching their backs D could not help thinking about what to do when the number of people ordering milk from the ranch increased and Dragonite could not keep up.

He thought of the group of Dragonite on Dragonite Island.

Could he hire them to come over and help with work?

D remembered last time he went to Dragonite Island several Dragonite had wanted to follow him and he thought this method was feasible.

But this matter still needed to be discussed with Dragonite.

Taking advantage of this time D went and washed up. When he returned to the house drying his hair Dragonite landed in the ranch.

D was a bit surprised to see what Dragonite was carrying. *"What is that?"*

*"Kroooh~"*

Dragonite walked forward happily and placed the bundle on the table. D opened it to find a pile of Berries inside.

He raised an eyebrow. *"From Mr. Elo?"*

*"Kroooh!"* Dragonite nodded.

*"Alright."* D understood it should be thanks for the suggestion he had given.

Refusing would be inappropriate so he accepted it and placed it aside as snacks for the Pokémon.

As it was about to get dark after delivering the items to D Dragonite planned to go outside to watch fireflies and stars but D stopped it.

Dragonite looked puzzled. *"Kroooh?"*

D asked. *"Dragonite, what do you think about the group of Dragonite on Dragonite Island?"*

Upon hearing this Dragonite became alert.

It wondered why. Did D take a liking to the other Dragonite?

That would not do! D was its Trainer! One Dragonite was enough!

With a serious expression Dragonite shook its big head and started badmouthing the group of Dragonite. *"Kroooh kroooh!"*

D: *"..."*

Though he could not understand D roughly guessed Dragonite's meaning.

His Pokémon were indeed quite jealous.

After some thought D said. *"It is not to catch them. It is to hire them to help work on the ranch."*

Dragonite tilted its head in confusion. *"Kroooh?"*

*"I am thinking that when more people start ordering milk here and you are overwhelmed it will exhaust you! That is when we can call them over to help with deliveries."*

*"Moreover they are surely unfamiliar with the outside world of the island. At that time you can be their leader introducing them to the outside world and leading them to town for deliveries."*

Dragonite thought it over carefully envisioning itself as a Dragonite King commanding a bunch of Dragonite to deliver milk across the sky.

The once weakest Dragonite becoming the leader of Dragonite. The more it thought about it the more comfortable it felt.

Seeing Dragonite's expression soften D quickly asked. *"How is it? Want to give it a try? If they do not do well we will fire them."*

*"Kroooh?"* What did fire mean?

*"It means not letting them deliver anymore. Sending them back to Dragonite Island."*

*"Kroooh!"* That was acceptable!

*"So it is settled then?"* D asked.

Dragonite nodded then joyfully hugged D.

D was so kind! Concerned about its fatigue he called other Dragonite to help with the work! How was there such a kind Trainer in the world?!

Dragonite was very touched.

D patted its back smiling. *"Okay. There is no rush for this. Go play outside!"*

This was just his plan for the future. Currently only two people ordered from the ranch so Dragonite could still manage.

Moreover the ranch was in the development stage. At this time if suddenly dozens of Dragonite showed up it would completely bankrupt him.

At times like this it would be great to develop a hunger-satisfying dish.

Speaking of which D remembered in the anime May made "May's Food No. 2" based on a formula given by Professor Oak.

It was a very special Pokéblock. One piece could fill even a Munchlax known for its large appetite.

# 144: Chapter 144: Housekeeper Pokémon

Early the next morning D was standing by the farmland watching Dragonite water the crops when Allen suddenly came rushing in from outside on a Cyclizar.

From a distance he shouted excitedly to D. *"D, I have news about the thing you asked me to do last time!"*

Hearing this D was slightly startled wondering what task he had assigned to Allen.

Then he remembered. During the spring competition he had asked Allen to help him find a Housekeeper Pokémon.

He thought of the Housekeeper Pokémon.

D was intrigued and quickly said. *"Tell me more!"*

Allen jumped off the Cyclizar and explained in detail.

*"Here is the thing. A few days ago I received a letter and the person asked me to deliver it to someone in the neighboring Cascarrafa City."*

*"That person lives in a very large mansion. When I delivered the letter I heard that the owner of the mansion was looking for someone to take care of two Indeedee!"*

*"Did I not remember that you needed a Housekeeper Pokémon? So I specifically asked around. An elderly man who seemed quite frail received me. He asked me some questions about you and then suggested we come together to take a look when we have time."*

Indeedee? And two of them?

D looked surprised. *"Two?"*

Allen nodded. *"Yes. Two Indeedee with a lot of housekeeping experience!"*

D was a bit puzzled. *"Why would that person give away such Pokémon?"*

Allen shook his head. *"I do not know. His only requirement is that someone can care for the two Indeedee well. Think about when you have time and we can go together."*

*"I am available right now."* Saying that D headed back to the house to change his clothes. *"Which city is it again?"*

Allen said. *"Cascarrafa City. It is very close to us. You can get there in less than two hours on a Cyclizar."*

*"Okay."* D nodded.

He turned back into the house to change his clothes, grabbed some change and a backpack and told Koraidon to watch over the ranch. He would be back in the afternoon. There was food in the fridge and it should be given to everyone for lunch.

Koraidon nodded back obediently and was not jealous at all.

*"Good. I will make you something tasty later."*

D patted its head then got on his Cyclizar just like Allen.

The two left the ranch and headed toward the target city.

On the way D asked curiously. *"Are you not working today?"*

Allen looked puzzled. *"Today is the weekend! The post office is off for two days. No work!"*

D: *"..."*

He had completely forgotten about that.

Allen laughed. *"Have you been living alone so long that you have forgotten the days?"*

*"No."* D said truthfully. *"I just did not expect you guys to have two days off without working overtime. I am really envious."*

Allen said speechlessly. *"Is the work at the ranch that tiring?"*

*"Not really."* D said.

With so many Pokémon to help his daily work was very light except when plowing and planting crops which was a bit busier and preparing large amounts of food daily.

Although tiring seeing so many Pokémon living on the ranch was a joy for him.

He just felt this world was really easygoing without overtime and with two days off a week which was unthinkable for a working-class person from his previous life unless in specific jobs.

But he could not explain the specific reasons to Allen so he switched the topic. *"I have not been to Cascarrafa City yet!"*

Not thinking much of it Allen heard this and laughed. *"Cascarrafa City is much bigger than our Porto Marinada Town! It is a famous Water City also known as the aquatic paradise."*

D recalled the map of the Paldea Region in his mind.

Porto Marinada Town was located in the northwest part of the map while Cascarrafa City was in the central area of the Paldea Region situated next to the Paldea Giant Pit with a vast desert area between it and Porto Marinada Town.

Thinking about this D asked. *"Do we need to go through the Asado Desert first?"*

Allen shook his head. *"No need. The desert is too dangerous. The Pokémon there are very aggressive and the weather is extremely hot. Today we are taking the main road which was officially built by the League. There is no danger from Pokémon on the road so we can walk safely."*

D nodded and did not say much more.

The two continued along the main road.

Because of Allen's words D made a point of observing the Pokémon by the roadside and indeed found just as Allen had said that they were all small in size, mild-mannered and non-aggressive Pokémon.

Along the way besides themselves they occasionally saw a few passing trucks, backpackers camping in the fields or Trainers engaged in Pokémon battles.

When running at full speed Cyclizar's speed could rival that of a real motorcycle and it was said that it could reach speeds of up to 120km/h.

As time ticked by the sun rose high and the air became hot. At last a lively bustling city appeared ahead.

*"We are here!"* Allen said happily.

D looked up to see the entire Cascarrafa City situated on a cliff. Due to the terrain the whole city was divided into three tiers.

Through Cascarrafa City he could see further into the distance a stretch of endless rugged cliffs unreachable by sight obscured by white misty clouds.

*Could that be the Paldea Giant Pit?* D thought.

Just by looking from the outside one could feel the grandeur and majesty of the Paldea Giant Pit. Who knew what it was like inside?

D wanted to go in for a visit but feared it would not be permitted.

The two entered Cascarrafa City. D dismounted from the Cyclizar which transformed into an upright form and walked alongside him through the streets.

The streets were paved with a layer of blue and white tiles. At intervals beautiful fountain parks could be seen with huge magnificent water curtains flowing from the top layer connecting the entire city.

People in hot bikinis and shorts strolled leisurely nearby with a lively atmosphere everywhere.

Allen watched the scene and laughed. *"Look. Is it not lively?"*

*"It sure is!"*

D nodded. Cascarrafa City gave him the feeling of being in the Water City Venice where water-based elements could be seen everywhere.

However they did not have much time to enjoy the sights. Taking an elevator to the third floor Allen turned a corner passed through a bustling district and reached a luxurious manor.

Two guards stood in front of the manor. As the two approached Allen stated their purpose.

Upon hearing this the guard on the left glanced at D for a moment then stepped aside opened the door and said respectfully.

*"The master has been expecting you for a while. Please come in!"*

The term master... How rare that sounded. D could not help musing internally.

Following Allen into the manor D looked at the vast expanse of land before him and his first thought was. *Is this place not too big?*

Allen whispered. *"I was startled the first time I came too! But do not worry. The owner here is very amiable. Nothing like the pompous rich people you see on TV."*

D nodded and continued forward. Yet soon they found themselves lost in the vast manor.

Allen said. *"...Strange. Did I remember wrong? How is this not the right path?"*

Looking at the huge garden maze around them D finally noticed something was amiss.

He asked. *"Did you not have someone leading the way last time you came?"*

All along they had not seen a single person.

Allen said. *"I did! There were quite a few people in the manor last time! Gardeners trimming the gardens, housekeepers in black tailcoats, maids watering plants..."*

As he spoke he suddenly realized something was wrong. *"Huh? Why is there not a single person today?"*

D thought of scenes from movies and asked. *"Could this be a ghost manor? Like in the movies where everyone is a ghost trying to lure people in to kill them."*

Just then a chilly breeze blew past as if something was watching them from the dark.

Allen was very scared. *"Do not scare me!"*

The two Cyclizar following them trembled in fear. *"Cy..."*

D said. *"...I was just joking."*

Allen then heaved a sigh of relief.

D added. *"But there are indeed ghosts in this world."*

Allen and the two Cyclizar widened their eyes. They traversed the garden maze frightened yet curious.

*"Have you seen one?"* Allen asked.

D nodded. *"I encountered one when I traveled through the Galar Region before."*

He then vividly described how he had met a ghostly little girl and helped deliver her letter to an old friend only to find out that her old friend was already an elderly man in his sixties or seventies.

The old man said the little girl was his childhood sweetheart whom he had not seen since they grew up.

D said. *"This means the girl had already died when she was a child wandering the streets as a ghost."*

After hearing this story Allen held his Cyclizar tightly in fear.

D chuckled. *"Is it really that scary?"*

Allen and his Cyclizar nodded in unison.

D wanted to say more but when he turned around he saw his Cyclizar was also very scared.

*"Cy..."*

*"Do not be afraid!"*

D patted Cyclizar's shoulder then took its paw comforting it in this way.

Seeing that Cyclizar was not so afraid he continued to move forward while saying.

*"Actually that ghost girl meant no harm. Someone once said the ghosts you fear are the individuals whom others long to see but never can again."*

*"And after I delivered her letter she gave me a gift. A Reaper Cloth imbued with amazing power."*

*"Look. Thinking of it that way it is not so scary, right?"* D spread his hands. Although these were just encounters he had in a game.

Allen felt a little better contemplating D's words carefully and nodded. *"Indeed."*

He wanted to say more but at that moment they finally passed through the garden maze and saw a beautiful fountain on the lawn ahead.

A circle of Pokémon was gathered around the fountain.

There was a Sprigatito, Fuecoco, Quaxly, Pawmi, Tandemaus, Fidough…

Suddenly seeing these rare Pokémon D could not help showing surprise.

But soon he noticed the small creature surrounded by the Pokémon.

Allen saw it too. It was a Meowth.

It was an injured Meowth to be precise.

*"It is injured!"* Allen exclaimed.

The group of Pokémon also noticed them and turned their heads one after another.

They did not seem to be afraid of strangers.

Seeing this D stepped forward to check on the Meowth's condition. Seeing that it was very weak he took out a bottle of Berry Milk from his backpack to restore its strength.

This was originally prepared for Cyclizar fearing that the journey was too long and Cyclizar would get thirsty so D had brought several bottles.

Unexpectedly they came in handy here.

The Meowth drank the entire bottle of Berry Milk in one go and its wounds began to heal at a speed visible to the naked eye.

The surrounding Pokémon looked on in wonder casting curious glances at this human.

Meowth stood up. *"Meow~"*

Seeing its energetic appearance D smiled and said. *"Alright. Be more careful next time."*

*"Meow~"*

A group of Pokémon then hopped and skipped away running into the garden maze to start a game of hide and seek.

Watching this scene Allen once again felt the grandeur of the mansion's owner.

*"Could it be that the purpose of building this garden maze was to let the Pokémon play hide and seek?"*

D said. *"It seems that way at the moment."*

He pondered whether to build a small maze for hide and seek at the ranch later.

Allen muttered. *"Truly extravagant!"*

D chuckled and looked up at the building ahead. *"Let's go! That should be the destination."*

Passing the garden fountain they finally saw the third person besides themselves.

It was a maid wearing an apron holding a broom and cleaning.

D walked up and politely introduced himself and his purpose for coming.

After listening the maid smiled and nodded then turned around with her broom. *"Please follow me! The master has been waiting for you for a long time."*

Hearing this D felt something was not quite right.

He looked at Allen who seemed to have noticed nothing.

The two followed the maid into the large villa.

Pushing open the door D saw an elderly man dressed in a blue kimono with white hair, a face full of wrinkles and a slightly tired expression sitting on the sofa in the villa's hall.
