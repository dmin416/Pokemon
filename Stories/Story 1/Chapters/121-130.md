That was the cream milkshake!

Just in time they had the full set of machines and ingredients at home.

MooMoo Fresh Milk. Gogoat's milk. Alcremie's cream. Berries.

The machine was a brand-new multifunctional juicer which he bought from the town just yesterday. He had initially intended to make Berry Juice for the Pokémon to drink. Unexpectedly it found its use here.

Thinking of this D turned around and returned to his room.

He opened the refrigerator and took out a can of milk and a few Berries.

*"Vee?"*

Glaceon looked a bit puzzled but still used its Ice-type Energy to condense an ice chunk and handed it to him.

*"You'll see later."* D winked mysteriously at Glaceon.

Next D began preparing the cream.

He called over Alcremie.

Upon hearing that D needed help making a dessert Alcremie rushed over to help make a big bowl of light cream.

*"It's almost ready."* D said.

Looking at Alcremie's cream as white as a cloud then at the sky-blue milkshake an image suddenly appeared in his mind.

Clear blue skies with fluffy white clouds floating. A leisurely and tranquil feeling.

From the outside it looked as if fluffy white clouds were floating in the blue sky.

D mentally named it Cloud Milkshake and finished it by swirling the cream into a spiral shape as a topping then decorating it with a fresh red Cheri Berry.
