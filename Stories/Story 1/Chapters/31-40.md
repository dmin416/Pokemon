# 31: Chapter 31: Video Phone

D felt a bit awkward. "I don't have a phone yet."

Professor Sada was slightly stunned.

She thought of what she had seen outside.

This ranch looked newly started. From the young man's clothes and living setup, money looked tight.

She did not say much.

She said she would come back in the afternoon and left with little Arven.

D watched her back, bewildered, and only turned when they were out of sight. Koraidon hid behind the door, secretly watching.

He exhaled and smiled. "They've left."

"hrrm..."

He stepped forward and petted it like a dog. Koraidon went so comfortable it almost purred.

"Do you like me that much?"

All in all they had lived together about half a month.

Koraidon opened its eyes happy and licked his face wild with its big tongue.

Nowadays he was not afraid. He knew it meant him no harm.

Casseroya Lake would slip one day. Letting Casseroya wait one day was fine.

He checked the cave behind the cabin after one night of trays.

Two Small Mushrooms and a scatter of ordinary ones had come up.

Sell prices followed the forage table he remembered. Ordinary Common Mushrooms ran about 160 to 320 League coins. Small ones mapped to Morels at about 600 to 1200. Two mundane Smalls alone were roughly 1200.

Not huge money. It was still better than nothing.

He took Koraidon up the back mountain next.

Bug-types and Grass-types fled the moment they felt that aura.

There was no easy catch there.

At the ranch river he found only Magikarp and tiny Wishiwashi.

Water-types for watering would have to wait for Casseroya after all.

At noon Koraidon stretched its whiskers into wing shapes and lifted.

It had never fought that other Koraidon, so it had never been grounded.

It was the first flight for D.

Forest. Back mountain. Ranch roofs. Porto Marinada streets. The Paldea Sea shining west.

Not up into the clouds. Still enough to steal his breath.

Around three in the afternoon Sada and Arven returned with a workman in a blue cap.

Lab funds covered the latest video phone. A gift for keeping Koraidon.

She wanted his notes on its habits when he could spare them.

Thanks spoken. Then they left again toward Area Zero.

D watched them go and thought of a future he had only seen in games.

Sada and Turo dying later in an experiment explosion.

He held that quiet and went back to work.


# 32: Chapter 32: Familiarity (Two Chapters in One)

Sada's visit placed him about ten years before Scarlet and Violet.

If Ash existed he might not have started or had just set out.

Then Mewtwo might not be created yet. Lugia might not have exploded. Groudon and Kyogre might still sleep. Team Galactic might not have seized the space-time gods.

He was a rancher.

No income yet today.

He took an iron bucket upstream and filled it with fish.

Zack arrived at five in a t-shirt and grinned at how the ranch changed every day.

Crops would be ready in about half a month. Buy fertilizer in town before the next batch.

Fish haul: 2620 League coins.

Alcremie and Smoliv stayed indoors.

At Happy Ranch they had lived in the fields. Here only the cabin and D felt safe.

After dinner he would walk them around the ranch.

Dinner that night ran rich.

Creamy mushroom soup. Chinese-style meat buns. Peppered grilled meat patties. Plain boiled eggs. Five cups of milk with different fruit juices.

Grades did not print. Everyone ate.

Koraidon and Cyclizar made a mess again.

Outdoor faucet after last time they oiled the sheets.

Koraidon stood in battle form, nearly two meters, and washed Alcremie and Smoliv at the faucet.

They froze first, then relaxed.

Cyclizar stayed back. Fairy against Dragon sat in its bones.

Koraidon ignored the type chart and kept gentle.

D towel-dried them. They liked Koraidon after that.

Twilight walk along the fence line.

Safe enough. Wildflowers and leftover daytime butterflies.

He did not scythe the grass.

He planned a stone path from the Chicken House to the cabin using storeroom stone.

Alcremie back to the cupboard. Smoliv back to the pot.

Night thoughts: Casseroya in the morning.

Nutritional sandwiches and Berry Juice for whoever stayed.

Hire Water-types with Water Gun and Rain Dance if he could.

Pack portable food for berry, meat, sweet, spicy, and sour tastes.

Capture by food or bond when possible, not only by battle.

Sada never left a ball behind.

Koraidon had smashed the special one.

A regular ball still did nothing. The mark remained.

He offered a red-and-white ball to Cyclizar.

Cyclizar leaned into the open ball.

Release by pressing the button, not by a throw.

Luxury Ball later when he could afford one.

# 33: Chapter 33: The Pokemon of Casseroya Lake

Morning of January 16.

Chickens fed. Crops watered.

Cabinet packed. Berry Juice and MooMoo Milk on top. Sandwiches and smoked sausages below.

Cyclizar, Alcremie, and Smoliv would stay.

Combat-form Cyclizar could work like a person when it had to.

Cyclizar still drooped anyway at the news.

Koraidon could fly too.

"Casseroya is dangerous," D said. "Flying keeps me off the wild ground. Your job is to care for Alcremie and Smoliv, guard the home, and keep wild Pokémon off the crops and chicks."

Cyclizar took the job without complaint.

Alcremie and Smoliv waved.

Metapod still hung under the eaves.

Cyclizar watched them on the lawn instead of chasing butterflies.

Koraidon reached Casseroya in under an hour.

They were in northwest Paldea now.

Cauldron lake. Mist. Islands like dumplings.

East: Glaseado Mountain waterfall into the lake, then west toward the Paldea Sea.

Three lookout towers stood around the lake.

Deerling. Tropius. Slowpoke. Gyarados.

Tatsugiri baited with Dondozo in the water.

Goal for the day: Dragonite, Dratini, or Dragonair. Cute plus Rain Dance.

Half a day in the air.

He found none of the Dragon line.

Game spots might not match this world.

Tropius carried fruit on its neck twice a year. Kids snacked on it in the south. Farms kept them in warm country.

Skiddo and Gogoat meant goat milk, less famous than MooMoo.

On a hillside by the lake he found Nacli and Naclstack.

Town Nacli cost 10000 League coins.

Catching here would save tens of thousands.

Six Poké Balls bought in town.

One used on Cyclizar.

Plan if they agreed: Tropius, Skiddo, Gogoat, Nacli.

No force. Unwilling workers would not water.

Forty-eight species logged.
