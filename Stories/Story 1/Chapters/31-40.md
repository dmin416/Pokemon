# 39: Chapter 39: S-Tier Milk Porridge

Now with eggs and milk available it was time to start experimenting with new recipes.

By the time he returned to the ranch it was almost noon.

D did not have time to rest and started processing the ingredients.

Since there were many eaters and with Koraidon's huge appetite, if there was not enough food it might end up with an empty stomach.

Initially he lacked experience and worked late but later he simply started cooking in advance.

For example right now it was only 10:46 but D started bustling around.

He scraped the fish scales, removed the guts, added ginger, Galar leek and a little garlic then used a prepared sauce for extra freshness and flavor. Finally he placed it in the pot for steaming.

Cooking every day improved D's culinary skills. Even his knife skills became much more proficient.

**blub blub**

*"Cy..."*

*"Kor..."*

Cyclizar and Koraidon did not want to play outside so they sat on the wooden floor and watched from there.

Seeing them like this D shook his head helplessly and started simmering the rice porridge.

The ordinary rice porridge D had made once before was E-level cuisine, with the effect of slightly restoring Pokémon's energy.

But this time, looking at the rice porridge in the pot, D suddenly fell into deep thought.

What should be added to it?

Century egg and lean meat porridge. Carrot and millet porridge. Mushroom chicken porridge. Oatmeal porridge. Eight-treasure porridge. Corn porridge. Purple sweet potato porridge.

The window sill was empty. Smoliv and Alcremie were probably playing in the sunshine now.

Compared to the meat-loving Koraidon and Cyclizar, Alcremie and Smoliv had a preference for sweet tastes.

And with the steamed sea bass and white radish ribs soup which were meat dishes, one more dish that Alcremie and Smoliv liked was still missing.

Thinking of this D turned to open the cupboard, looking at the ingredients inside. He finally took out a bottle of MooMoo Milk and white sugar.

He poured the MooMoo Milk into the pot, added an appropriate amount of white sugar and soon looked down. The panel appeared.

[Milk Porridge (B): Milk porridge with added white sugar and MooMoo Milk.

Cooking Effect: Better Milk lv1. For milk-producing Pokémon only. After eating it will slightly enhance the quality of the milk produced by the eater, making it taste better and more nutritious, also having the effect of restoring energy.

Rating: A nutritious milk porridge, suitable for all ages.]

It was a new product.

*Better milk.*

As the name implied, eating it could improve the quality of the milk produced by Pokémon.

The suitable Pokémon for it...

The image of Miltank and the Skiddo family appeared in D's mind.

*"Interesting. Just when visiting Nacli this afternoon I should see if there's a chance to capture a Skiddo."*

But sadly the effect level of this dish was only lv1.

D guessed it might be because the rice he used was of average quality.

If replaced with higher quality rice and white sugar the enhancing effect of the dish should increase further.

Seeing the porridge was almost ready D wanted to serve it and call Smoliv and the others back to eat.

There was still a small piece of Black Truffle he had left in the cupboard.

Thinking of this D immediately turned and took out that piece of Black Truffle.

Information about Black Truffle popped into his mind.

[Black Truffle: A top-quality premium ingredient, a rare and delicious delicacy. Can be made into Black Truffle steamed egg with eggs or creamy thick soup with butter. Though it does not possess its own benefits it can significantly enhance the effects of a dish.]

Sure enough he had not remembered it wrong. It was an enhancing cuisine effect.

D's heart skipped a beat. If he put it into the milk porridge...

Thinking of this he immediately cut off a quarter of that small piece of Black Truffle and added it to the pot.

Soon he looked down. The panel appeared again.

From ordinary Milk Porridge it became Black Truffle Milk Porridge.

[Black Truffle Milk Porridge (S): Milk porridge with added Black Truffle, white sugar and MooMoo Milk. The additional Black Truffle greatly enhanced the quality of the dish.

Cooking Effect: Better Milk lv3. For milk-producing Pokémon only. After eating it will significantly enhance the quality of the milk produced by the eater, making it taste better and more nutritious, also having the effect of quickly restoring energy.

Rating: An exquisite milk porridge, rich in nutrients suitable for the growth of all Pokémon and a rare delicacy.]

An S popped out.

At this moment D even thought about adding Black Truffle to the pork rib soup and steamed sea bass to see the effect.

But rationality stopped him because the effects of pork rib soup and steamed sea bass were very ordinary dishes.

One enhances stamina and strength temporarily while the other restores energy.

He carefully stored the remaining Black Truffle, simultaneously planning for the future in his heart.

He had to raise pigs. He must raise pigs.

Raise a large herd of Oinkologne and send them to the back mountain to help find Black Truffles.
