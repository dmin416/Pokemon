The sky changed over Dragonite.

Thunderbolt was a renowned Electric-type move, so it was harder to learn.

It had not grasped the essence of Thunderbolt, which was why the move failed.

So what about Rain Dance?

Dragonite had just summoned the clouds. One last step would have made the rain and it failed at that last step.

Still, Dragonite seemed to have sensed something from this rain.

That was already enough. At least it could summon clouds. With more practice it would succeed at Rain Dance.

This was its first try and it already looked solid.

For an ordinary Pokémon, that was extraordinary.

Dragon Fang or Dragon Scale.
