Especially since the Dragonite it liked was stronger than it.

So every night it quietly left Dragonite Island and went somewhere secluded to daydream.

Now it had learned Dragon Tail. The moves it mastered had risen from four to five.

That truly made the dragon happy.

*"Cy?"*

They had comprehended a lot of new moves too!

Grassy Terrain, Synthesis, Solar Beam, Vine Whip and Razor Leaf.

Green light wrapped the pen. A strong Solar Beam swept the sky. Green vines whipped through the air. Green leaves fluttered like snow.

An ordinary pen looked like an international fashion stage.

*"Kroooh!"*

*"What happened this time?"*

*"hrmm..."*

*"I see!"*

Life on the island flashed through its mind plus the Skiddo showcase.

*"Kroooh!"*

*"Kroooh!"*

*"Feel any changes within?"*
