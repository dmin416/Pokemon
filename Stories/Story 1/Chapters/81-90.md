Behind him the cabin was cheerful and lively.

Smoliv nestled in its flowerpot and swayed its body while it watched.

Alcremie ran around the wooden floor with the five Nacli and cried out in joy.

Butterfree landed on top of the cabinet and enjoyed the lively room.

Three Skiddo squeezed by the bed and chewed grass dumplings slowly. Pawmi circled them unsure of what it was doing.

Koraidon and Cyclizar lay on the ground waiting for the meal to start.

Dragonite stood by the door and looked at the rain outside while it thought.

Dinner was nearly ready. One special dish still needed cooking before they could start the meal.

He focused on the soil in front of him. It looked like flour.

Though it was soil its appearance was more like flour polished by human hands.

That flour made him think of frying.

After a moment he poured it into the pan and added an appropriate amount of Rock Salt to stir-fry.

The soil could be fed to the Nacli raw. Cooking was what unlocked those special abilities.

Every time he thought about this he felt like an alchemist. He was not just cooking. He was enchanting food.

He shook his head to push that thought away. The Rock Salt and soil fused together. A faint salty aroma came from the pan.

For humans this scent was not appealing.

For the Nacli it had an astonishing effect.

The Nacli that had been chasing each other around the room stopped one by one. They cried *"Nacl-krkt!"* and came over with expectant eyes.

*What smell is this? So fragrant!*

When it looked done he turned off the heat and scooped the soil into feeding bowls.

He looked down. The information about this dish appeared.

[Rock Salt Fried Soil (B-): A special dish made by frying mineral-rich soil with nutritious Rock Salt possibly favored by certain Pokémon.

Cooking Effect: Salty lv1. Slightly enhances the quality of Rock Salt produced by Nacli.

Special Effect: Strengthening lv1. Slightly enhances the physical strength of Nacli.

Evaluation: A mind-blowing dish! Using human cooking methods to prepare Pokémon cuisine is also a great choice!]

*"Huh?"*

He was surprised looking at the fried soil.

A dish with two effects...

He was not too surprised by the first effect. The second Strengthening truly amazed him.

He thought of the Grassy Seed the Skiddo ate. Besides helping them learn Grass-type moves it seemed to have no other effects.

And now there was this.

The Nacli were already impatient.

He set the bowls on the floor.

The five Nacli jumped around and buried their heads in the bowls. They ate with delight.

D watched and thought.

Soon he hit on a possibility.

Nacli were born in underground Rock Salt layers. They left salt traces wherever they went. Their bodies were largely composed of Rock Salt.

So this Rock Salt Fried Soil enhanced not only the quality of Rock Salt they produced. It also strengthened their bodies.

The doubts in his mind disappeared.

*This was great stuff!*

He wondered what kind of Pokémon he would raise if he fed the Nacli this soil every day.

After all there was the precedent set by the Skiddo.

He felt a stir. He decided to visit Porto Marinada Market again after the typhoon passed and see if he could order a large quantity of this soil from that person.

*"Zapapico Town..."* He mused over the town that produced this soil. *"If there's a chance I should visit there."*

It was said that the Auspicious Armor and Malicious Armor needed for Charcadet's evolution were found deep within the mines there.

Because of this Zapapico Town sparked a gold rush. Many Trainers went looking for hidden treasures underground to help their Charcadet evolve.

The rarity of the Auspicious Armor and Malicious Armor was beyond their imagination. No one managed to find any.

D had heard about this from a show called Paldea's Curious Cases and Oddities.

Dinner was prepared. He served it at the table and summoned the host of Pokémon to begin their meal.

Dragonite looked at the weather with longing.

In such weather if it went outside could it learn a new move like last time?

D sat cross-legged on the bed grooming Skiddo's fur.

Although Skiddo lived outside all the time it was never dirty. It did not have the strong smell of ordinary goats. Its brown fur gave off a faint scent of fresh grass.

Hearing Dragonite's growl D turned his head.

Soon he understood what Dragonite meant.

After a little thought he said, *"It's not impossible."*

Dragonite knew it. *"Kroooh!"*

In a hurricane the move to practice was obvious.

Hurricane used powerful winds to sweep the opponent for an attack. It could sometimes confuse the opponent.

In the game besides having a relatively low hit rate this move had almost no drawbacks. It had massive power and a chance to confuse the opponent.

That was not necessarily true in reality.

This move sounded like a large-scale AOE skill.

D explained the general information of this move to Dragonite.

Dragonite shook its head and refused.

It looked serious. *"Kroooh!"*

Pokémon like Dragonite were naturally unafraid of wind and rain. Because of that they could rescue ships in distress during sea storms.

Every Dragonite had to experience such a storm.

This was passed down by word of mouth on Dragonite Island. Only a Dragonite that truly mastered hurricanes and storms could become the king of Dragonites.

Dragonite stopped him there.

With its chubby paw it pressed D back onto the bed. *"Kroooh!"*

After the last fever incident Dragonite worried that D would fall ill again after getting caught in the rain. This time it refused to let him come along no matter what.

Understanding Dragonite's meaning D reached out and patted Dragonite's head.

This Dragonite had initially followed him back to the ranch solely to become stronger.

Now it was slowly adapting to life on the ranch and becoming a part of it. It was learning to care for him.

*"Kroooh?"* Dragonite looked puzzled.

Dragonite let out a howl and flapped its wings to regain stability. Memories of the past and D's face flickered through its mind.

Its eyes brimmed with determination.

Ahead the violent air currents swept in once again.

The experience gleaned from countless failures came to bear at this moment.

If it did not want to be repelled now it should...

That dodge was a success.

No time to celebrate. Another swirling gust followed right behind.

Dragonite's focus never wavered. It quickly shifted its form to confront it.

The wind around was incredibly ferocious.

Dragonite gritted its teeth charging forward with all its might.

It was almost there. Just one more step!

Gazing at the storm's eye within reach an unprecedented force emerged within its body.

A powerful Flying-type energy surged from within. The streaming air currents dispersed the last enveloping gust.

Dragonite accelerated suddenly and dove headfirst into the storm's eye.

As it entered all the surrounding sounds vanished.

It was utterly tranquil here with no wind or rain as if in another world. The atmosphere was serene and peaceful.

This was Dragonite's first time here.

Streams of air flowed around its body the valiant testament to mastering the storm.

Dragonite curiously surveyed the surroundings then looked upward.

Suddenly it wanted to know what lay atop these high heavens.

So it flapped its wings ascending carrying its battled body upwards.

Just as it was about to reach the clouds Dragonite suddenly sensed a mysterious gaze observing it.
