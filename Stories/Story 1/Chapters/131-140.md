# 131: Chapter 131: Summer and the Night of Fireflies

D turned back toward the cabin to freeze ice cubes in the refrigerator with molds so he would not always have to trouble Glaceon for ice.

On the way back he saw Cyclizar who had just finished a cream smoothie practicing moves with Koraidon.

He stopped and stood beside Koraidon watching Cyclizar work at it.

He had to admit Koraidon was a good teacher and Cyclizar was a dedicated student.

D had been watching how the two Pokémon got along.

At first Koraidon had not looked very interested in teaching Cyclizar.

Cyclizar had also been afraid of fierce Koraidon.

Even so Cyclizar kept at it.

Seeing that effort Koraidon's attitude toward Cyclizar slowly improved and it began to teach in earnest.

The move they were practicing this time seemed to be Dragon Pulse.

It was a special attack.

The method was roughly to move the Dragon-type Energy in the body to the mouth then spit it at the enemy in one go.

Koraidon's teaching was direct and simple.

It demonstrated first then had Cyclizar follow the motions.

From Cyclizar's view it could only see Koraidon spit out Dragon Pulse.

It did not know how to spit it itself.

So Cyclizar never found the knack of the move.

D watched from the side for a while then spoke up. *"Try concentrating the Dragon-type Energy in your body into your mouth?"*

Koraidon heard that and let out a helpless cry. *"Kor..."*

It had already told Cyclizar this trick. Cyclizar was its past self and really was not very smart.

*"Cy?"*

Cyclizar blinked then tried again.

It gathered all the Dragon-type Energy in its body into its mouth until the energy puffed its cheeks wide.

*"Yes. Just like that! Spit it out!"* D said.

Cyclizar tried hard to spit the energy out.

It failed and swallowed all of it down in one gulp.

Cyclizar opened its mouth wide as if it had burped and let out a puff of air.

D: *"..."*

Koraidon: *"..."*

It could not even look.

Cyclizar's face had gone bright red and its eyes were wet as if it might cry.

*"Cy..."*

*"Alright. Alright. Take your time. There is no rush."* D stepped forward to comfort it.

Cyclizar nodded and started practicing earnestly again.

D watched a while longer then left the time to Koraidon and Cyclizar and went back to the house to keep working.

On the grass Koraidon kept guiding Cyclizar through the move.

Looking at that diligent Cyclizar Koraidon gradually recalled its past self.

It thought of many things.

Maybe when it was still a Cyclizar that other Koraidon had looked at it this way.

When it had been about to fall into the time machine the other had rushed over with all its strength to stop it and had failed.

At that time maybe that Koraidon's heart had accepted it.

Koraidon was a bit confused.

Sometimes it could not even tell if it was Koraidon or Cyclizar.

It did not know how all of this had come to be.

For it as long as it could return to D's side that was enough.

Looking at its past self learning in earnest Koraidon thought maybe its past self had not been that foolish either.

Koraidon stopped at that thought.

If it and Cyclizar were the same being then it had been looking down on itself all along.

Koraidon suddenly realized that.

It could not help thinking that maybe it should treat itself better then together with D make itself as smart and sensible as it was now.

The summer night sky was always so beautiful.

Dragonite lay on the grass as usual watching the stars.

The evening breeze blew gently across its body as if D were giving it a massage.

Dragonite felt very comfortable.

A rustling of footsteps came from the side.

Dragonite turned its head and saw Cyclizar coming over.

It thought of Cyclizar practicing with Koraidon these days and of Cyclizar saying last time that it was afraid of Koraidon.

Dragonite could not help asking. *"Kroooh?"* *Are you not afraid of Koraidon anymore?*

Cyclizar shook its head and answered in some confusion. *"Cy..."*

*I do not know why.*

*It seems less scary now.*

Dragonite looked very happy.

*"Kroooh!"* *That is right! Koraidon is actually a very good Pokémon! It even taught me Dragon Tail!*

Hearing that Cyclizar showed a silly grin.

Soon it hung its head in disappointment.

It still could not grasp Dragon Pulse.

*"Kroooh..."* Dragonite shook its head to show that it could not either.

The two Pokémon sat on the grass in silence looking worried.

D had finished washing up and came out to check the Chicken House.

He saw Dragonite and Cyclizar as he passed by.

*"I was wondering why I did not see you in the house. So you came here to look at the stars?"* D walked over with a smile and sat down beside the two Pokémon. *"What is it? You both look a bit unhappy."*

Looking at Cyclizar D guessed. *"Is it because you cannot use Dragon Pulse?"*

Cyclizar nodded with a disappointed face.

D comforted it. *"Do not worry too much. Maybe you just have not found the knack yet. If you keep trying you will grasp it one day then become as strong as Koraidon."*

*"Cy?"* Cyclizar tilted its head.

Speaking of Koraidon D remembered a folk rumor and smiled at Cyclizar.

*"You and Koraidon have a deep connection too! Some urban legends say Koraidon is how you used to look."*

Cyclizar looked completely blank.

It had not understood.

D thought a moment then explained. *"It is how you looked a long long time ago."*

*"Besides Koraidon there is Miraidon. People say Miraidon is how you will look a long long time from now. One belongs to the past. One belongs to the future. You belong to the present."*

*"Cy?"* Cyclizar looked even more confused.

That made it think of the evolution of the Pokémon on the ranch.

Then the question came.

*Could it also evolve into Koraidon and Miraidon?*

*If it evolved would it become as strong as Koraidon?*

Cyclizar looked excited at that thought.

D sensed Cyclizar might have misunderstood something.

He did not dwell on it.

He only patted Cyclizar's head.

*"It is fine if you do not understand. The era they live in is too far from us."*

Hearing that the Dragonite beside them could not help asking. *"Kroooh?"*

*What about it?*

*Did Dragonite also have past and future forms?*

D could not understand Dragonite's words.

He still guessed the meaning from its face and eyes.

He was about to shake his head then suddenly remembered the giant Dragonite that had appeared near Bill's Lighthouse in the anime hovering over the sea in mist.

Rumor said that giant Dragonite was an ancient Dragonite like the ancient Jigglypuff, ancient Gengar and ancient Alakazam sealed in the mountains behind Pallet Town.

Thinking of that D answered. *"Maybe."*

Other Pokémon started gathering around.

Butterfree, Pawmo, Alcremie and Koraidon came over.

Butterfree saw D sitting on the grass looking at the stars and flew over to stay with him.

Pawmo, Koraidon and Alcremie had come out because D had not gone back inside to sleep for a long time.

D looked at them and kept answering Dragonite. *"Maybe a long long time ago Dragonite all had bodies as huge as a mountain."*

*A body as huge as a mountain.*

*How terrible would that power be?*

Dragonite widened its eyes and imagined the scene.

D sat on the grass smiling as he enjoyed the quiet night with the group of Pokémon.

He looked at the empty grass and suddenly felt something was missing.

He went back inside for a picnic cloth, spread it on the grass then set out snacks.

Lucky Pudding, Berries, strawberry milk, Berry juice, Honey Buns, sandwiches, cream smoothie and Grassy Seed.

D asked Alcremie to call everyone over.

Alcremie was happy to do it.

Before long Glaceon, Gogoat, Nacli, Naclstack, Miltank, Smoliv, Combee and the rest of the Pokémon had all gathered on the soft grass.

Glaceon stared at the cream smoothie on the picnic cloth with a face that wanted to eat it badly.

D handed the cream smoothie to it then took the chance to rub Glaceon's head.

Glaceon had never been touched by a human and froze at once.

*"Eat up!"* D grinned.

*"Vee..."*

Glaceon lowered its head and silently licked the smoothie with its small tongue.

D took a Grassy Seed from the picnic basket.

The three Gogoat smelled it and nudged over.

D petted their fur while he fed them the Grassy Seed.

*Hmm. Maybe I can make some other dishes with grass.*

Things like grass ice cream, grass pie and grass cake.

The kitchen tools for making cake were already at home.

D planned to try Green Grass Cake tomorrow to give the Gogoat a change of taste.

*"Go-goaat!"*

The Gogoat seemed to sense his thought.

They suddenly looked happy and licked him with tongues that smelled faintly of grass.

Their motion startled him.

He quickly pulled his hand back from their horns.

Soon after he realized the Gogoat had sensed the thought in his mind through his hand.

That was Gogoat's special ability.

A touch of their horns let them feel the other party's mood.

They could also read a Trainer's thoughts from the small differences in how the horns were held.

D asked in surprise. *"You sensed my mood?"*

The Gogoat squinted happily. *"Go-goaat~"*

*"Good job!"* D praised it and petted the Gogoat in the middle.

That Gogoat was the one that had battled Koraidon earlier.

It was also the leader of the other two.

After evolving from Skiddo to Gogoat it had won at horn games and become the boss.

When they finished the Grassy Seed the three Gogoat lay down nearby.

D then looked at the Miltank rolling around on the grass not far away.

That Miltank had a lively nature.

The move it was best at was Rollout.

Watching it D suddenly thought of a traditional snack called donkey rolls.

Then the question came.

*Could that snack enhance Miltank's Rollout?*

*I can try making it later.*

He ate a piece of Lucky Pudding then started playing with the Nacli and Naclstack that had come over.

He stacked the Nacli together first then put them all onto Naclstack.

He tilted his head and looked at Naclstack for a while.

This Pokémon's look was very different from the others.

It was square and neat.

It did not look like a creature from the Pokémon world.

It looked more like it had come from the game Minecraft.

Butterfree beside him suddenly noticed something. *"Fweeee~"*

D turned and followed its gaze.

Scattered yellow-green lights had appeared over the small river that ran through the night.

They flew lightly above the water in the quiet dark.

D wondered if those were fireflies.

D was a bit surprised.

Fireflies should by rights show up around July and August.

Then he thought of the climate here.

It was very different from his last life.

The seasons were distinct.

Porto Marinada sat by the sea so the nearby weather stayed warm and damp year-round.

Fireflies appearing was not strange.

More and more fireflies gathered by the river.

They looked like a bright band in the night.

*"Creaam..."*

Alcremie was stunned by the sight and murmured.

The other Pokémon all turned their heads to look.

D could not help saying it. *"Yes. It is truly beautiful!"*

In his last life he had always been busy in the city.

He had never had the chance to see a sight like this.

*Maybe this is the charm of nature.*

It was beautiful and it also made one feel close.

The group of dazed Psyduck on the other side of the river noticed it too.

One Psyduck tilted its head and tried to reach a firefly with a duck foot.

It failed to catch the firefly and tumbled into the river instead.

**Plop.**

Then it scrambled back up looking a mess.

It ignored the river water all over it and only stared up at the lights above the water.

Clearly it was stunned by the sight too.

Watching them D could not help thinking that maybe they were the same in this.

Whether humans or Pokémon they were all part of nature.

# 132: Chapter 132: Is Gogoat Lazy or Not?

Early the next morning after breakfast D noticed the grass in the pasture had grown a good deal taller.

He had nothing else to do at the moment so he took a sickle and started cutting.

He gathered all the clippings to make Grassy Seed for the Gogoat later.

The ranch was simply too big.

He spent several hours at it and his back and waist ached before he had finished.

*"Phew!"* D stood up, took a deep breath and thought. *Looks like it is time to buy a lawnmower.*

He walked back, pulled off his leather gloves and put the sickle in the storage room.

When he came around front he saw Koraidon teaching Cyclizar moves.

Dragonite was at the side watching and trying to steal the lesson.

*Hmm. Looks like I will have to ride another Pokémon into town.*

D did not bother them.

After a brief thought he went to the goat pen, picked a Gogoat at random and climbed on.

*"Let's go. Come with me to town!"*

*"Go-goaat~"*

Gogoat agreed happily.

The evolved Gogoat stood a full 1.8 meters tall.

Carrying an adult of ordinary weight was easy for it.

D rode the Gogoat past Koraidon and Cyclizar and told them.

*"I am going to town. You keep practicing."*

*"Kor!"*

*"Cy!"*

*"Kroooh~"*

The three Pokémon blinked.

Koraidon wanted to take D to town.

It still had work so it could only watch them leave.

This was the first time D had ridden a Gogoat.

Compared with Koraidon and Cyclizar the feeling of riding Gogoat did not seem much different.

To be precise Gogoat was even furrier and sitting on its back felt softer.

More interesting still Gogoat was very intelligent.

If he put his hands on the pair of long horns on its head Gogoat could sense the thought in his mind.

It would turn, speed up or slow down according to the rider's wish.

From a certain angle Gogoat was even more like a motorcycle than Cyclizar.

*No wonder it has been helping humans work since five thousand years ago.*

It really was a reliable Pokémon.

The only bad part was the glowing.

He watched the Gogoat under him light up with a thick green glow while it ran and for a moment he did not know what to say.

Why did this feel like those flashy mounts in trashy pay-to-win online games?

*Recharge 998 and get a deluxe glowing mount on the spot!*

He shook his head to throw the odd thought out then stopped Gogoat at the roadside.

*"Release the energy in your body first!"*

*"Go-goaat~"*

Gogoat started dancing out in the wild.

A powerful wave of Grass-type Energy burst from its body.

It wrapped everything around them at once and formed a lush green field.

That was only the start.

On that Grassy Terrain Gogoat used other moves as well.

Leaf Blade, Vine Whip, Razor Leaf, Solar Beam and other Grass-type moves came out without holding back.

Watching this D had been worried that doing it every day would burden Gogoat.

Maybe its body would feel unwell.

Then he noticed Gogoat's face.

It looked like it was enjoying itself.

D: *"..."*

The green glow kept spreading.

The scene drew quite a few wild Pokémon over to watch.

As time passed more and more wild Pokémon gathered around.

Then D noticed something.

Compared with the start Gogoat's dancing time seemed to get longer every day.

When the energy in its body was spent Gogoat still looked unsatisfied.

Using moves alone no longer seemed enough for their abundant energy.

Watching this D fell into thought.

There was no question.

This was the result of eating large amounts of Grassy Seed every day.

To stop it he would need to stop feeding the three Gogoat Grassy Seed.

Grassy Seed had already become their favorite food.

If he stopped the three Gogoat would likely be unhappy.

Besides dancing what other method could let out this extra energy?

The first thing that came to D's mind was battling.

Venting energy through battle was clearly more effective than only using moves.

There was something else.

D remembered how this Gogoat had even wanted to battle Koraidon.

Clearly it was not a Pokémon that disliked fighting.

He looked at the Gogoat that had walked up in front of him and asked.

*"Gogoat, if I let you battle other Pokémon would you be willing?"*

*"Go-goaat?!"*

Hearing that Gogoat looked excited.

Its face was full of fighting spirit.

D: *"..."*

*At heart it was still a lively combative Gogoat!*

Life on the ranch was nothing but eating and sleeping.

There was nothing else to do so Gogoat could only lie lazily on the grass.

That had given him the false sense of a laid-back personality.

After a bit of thought D asked again. *"What if the opponent is a very strong Pokémon?"*

*"Go-goaat!"*

**Shing.** A huge exaggerated grass blade formed in Gogoat's mouth.

It was not going to back down!

*Whoever invades the pasture the sheep will strike and break!*

*"Alright. Alright. We will talk about this later. For now put that power away!"*

Gogoat obediently withdrew its power.

D climbed on again and kept heading toward Porto Marinada.

At the same time he thought about how to help Gogoat let out the extra energy and meet its strong need to battle.

*Take them to battle other Trainers?*

That would not work.

There was still a lot of work waiting at the ranch.

He probably would not have that much time.

*What about letting Gogoat spar with Dragonite and Cyclizar so they could learn from each other?*

That was possible.

One Dragonite and one Cyclizar might not be enough for three Gogoat at once.

As Porto Marinada drew nearer D's mind ran through past scenes looking for a way to help Gogoat let the energy out.

At last the picture stopped on a few youths in Naranja Academy uniforms.

*He had it then.*

If he said the ranch could help Trainers train could he draw some young Trainers to practice at the ranch?

Then he would send Gogoat to battle them.

The Trainers would gain experience.

Gogoat would let out the extra energy.

They would even get exercise and shed some weight.

On top of that the ranch had cooking that could restore stamina.

There was no need to worry about Pokémon getting hurt.

It was truly a win-win.

# 133: Chapter 133: Grass Trial

It was like playing a game.

The player challenged a dungeon then gained a lot of experience from it.

If a Pokémon had a good grasp of things it might even learn some move techniques from Gogoat.

D had already thought of a name for this dungeon.

It was the Grass Trial!

If other Pokémon were interested they might take part too.

Like Dragonite which was training hard because it wanted to get stronger.

It could test the effect of Rain Dance plus Hurricane plus Thunder on the challengers.

D felt a slight stir.

The details would have to wait for later.

For now he needed to buy a lawnmower first.

After they entered Porto Marinada Gogoat slowed down.

Its hooves hit the red brick path. **Da-da-da.**

The sound pulled D's attention.

He looked at the handsome red brick path below and thought of the simple wooden path at the ranch.

He had laid that path with leftover wood from the ranch.

It had a rustic look and a plain tidy shape.

Its lifespan was simply too short.

After a few heavy rains the wood on the ground would go soft and rot.

D even thought it would not last through this summer.

*Hmm. It is time to plan the roads on the ranch properly.*

Otherwise on rainy days the road would be full of wet mud.

One trip out would cake his shoes.

A red brick path would avoid that.

He was going to Robin's shop anyway so he could ask along the way.

The town was still busy even though he had not come for a few days.

Porto Marinada sat by the sea so many inland visitors made a special trip here to see the ocean.

Speaking of the seaside D remembered Allen had said the town held a grand fireworks festival there every summer.

People would gather by the sea and set up a lively market selling sushi, takoyaki, oden and snacks like that.

It was only the beginning of the month so that day was still a long way off.

He gathered his thoughts and rode Gogoat toward Robin's shop.

When he arrived Robin was sitting on a wooden stump beside the house.

A group of Grass-type Pokémon stood around her.

There was a summer-form Deerling, a fragrant Bounsweet, a cute Sprigatito and a Mankey.

Robin was feeding them Pokémon snacks.

She turned at the sound and smiled when she saw it was D.

*"Changed mounts this time?"*

D jumped down from Gogoat. *"Cyclizar and the others are practicing moves at the ranch so I had Gogoat bring me."*

He looked at the group of Pokémon and asked. *"Are they Pokémon from the forest?"*

D remembered Robin had said some injured wild Pokémon would come to her for help.

Robin nodded gently. *"Yes! These little ones rarely gather together like this. I did not have much to do today so I came to play with them for a while. Being with them always gives my heart a peace I have not felt in a long time."*

*"Maybe that is the charm of Pokémon?"* D said with a smile.

His gaze went to the Sprigatito snuggling at Robin's feet. *"Sprigatito is quite a rare Pokémon!"*

Sprigatito was the Grass-type starter of the Paldea region.

It was a very popular Pokémon.

Robin looked down at Sprigatito and said gently. *"Common or rare Pokémon are no different to me. Maybe that is because I am not a Trainer!"*

D paused then agreed. *"True."*

Whether it was a Mankey commonly seen by the roadside or a rare Sprigatito deep in the woods they all had a simple heart besides their looks.

Robin stood up with a smile and asked. *"What are you here to buy today?"*

*"A lawnmower. The wild grass at the ranch is growing too fast. Gogoat and Miltank do not eat much of it so I plan to cut it all down."*

Hearing that Robin could not help saying. *"The Pokémon living at your ranch are really fortunate."*

D raised an eyebrow. *"Hmm?"*

Robin explained. *"Few ranchers go to the trouble of preparing individual meals for them every day like you do."*

After all whether Gogoat or Miltank their food in the wild was usually wild grass and wildflowers.

If they were lucky they might come across a Berry tree and pick a few Berries.

Gogoat and Miltank raised on ranches mostly ate fodder and feed bought from the store.

By comparison D treated his Pokémon very well.

D understood what Robin meant and laughed. *"That is why my gas bill each month is several times higher than other ranches!"*

Robin chuckled then led the way. *"Come with me!"*

The two went inside.

Robin pushed several lawnmowers out of the warehouse and introduced them one by one.

*"Look. These are all of them. Each has a different price and performance. The cheapest here is 5000 League coins. It is an older model that makes a lot of noise in use. The others are similar. Different brands have different prices."*

D nodded thoughtfully, looked through them all and finally picked a push lawnmower.

He spent 7000 League coins.

Then D asked. *"By the way do you have red bricks here? I want to lay a red brick path at the ranch."*

*"Yes. We do."*

Robin nodded.

She thought of the size of D's ranch then said.

*"I suggest you plan the areas of the ranch first though. If you lay the red brick path now it might get in the way of other facilities later and you would have to redo it. That would be quite a bother."*

*"True."* D nodded. He thought about it and decided to go back to the ranch and plan first. *"I will go back and look then come when I am ready."*

*"Of course. No problem. You are welcome anytime."* Robin nodded with a smile.

After leaving town D went straight back to the ranch.

Gogoat went back to the pen.

D took paper and pen, called Dragonite and flew into the air.

From above he looked down on the whole ranch and sketched the rough terrain and the locations of the buildings.

He did not start planning the ranch at once.

He still had other work.

He handed the paper and pen to Cyclizar to put on the desk then pushed the lawnmower and started trimming the grass.

The ranch was huge.

Even with the new facilities a large piece of land still lay idle.

According to the original owner's memory that stretch used to be pasture.

The original owner's grandfather had once raised dozens of cattle and sheep Pokémon there.

D pushed the lawnmower and figured the size of the ranch in his mind.

About a quarter of the whole ranch was in use now.

He usually did not feel it.

Now that he was mowing he saw it.

This place was enormous!

He thought of the vast cherry garden of the Elif family.

He did not know how a family of four had planted so many cherry trees.

They would never keep up!

# 134: Chapter 134: Green Grass Cake

The grass-cutting only came to an end in the afternoon.

Looking at the pile of grass in the pasture stacked like a small hill D said to the Gogoat.

*"Now you will have a feast!"*

The three Gogoat walked around the pile and sniffed at it from time to time.

They did not take a bite.

D did not mind.

He took the towel Koraidon handed him and wiped the sweat off his face then carried some of the grass back to his room and soaked it in a basin of water.

He planned to use part of the grass to make Grassy Seed and new Grass-type dishes.

The rest he would dry and store as hay.

That way when snow covered the ground later and no grass grew there would still be snacks for Gogoat and Miltank.

It was like humans eating dried salted fish and cured meat.

D opened the fridge, took out a bottle of juice and drank it down to quench his thirst.

**Gulp gulp.**

He checked the time.

It was still early so he started trying new grass dishes.

He boiled water in a pot.

Once it was boiling he added grass and waited a few minutes.

A simple boiled grass dish was done.

He looked down and the information on the boiled grass appeared.

[Boiled Grass (F-): A very poor dish. Boiling the grass in a large amount of water lost its original flavor. Cooking it too long ruined the texture. The terrible taste makes it hard to swallow.

Cooking Effect: Easily lowers the favorability of Grass-type Pokémon.

Evaluation: The taste is like chewing wax. Maybe only a Grass-type Pokémon starving for a week would try it.]

D: *"..."*

*So there really is a dish with a debuff. I thought there was not.*

This was only a small test.

Such a result had been within his expectations.

He dumped the boiled grass in the trash then looked at the remaining grass.

*If boiling does not work what about juicing?*

He opened the juicer, put the washed grass in and added a suitable amount of water.

As the juicer turned the clear water slowly went green.

Before long several cups of grass juice were ready.

He poured them into bowls.

As he looked down the information appeared.

[Grass Juice (C): A drink made from ripe summer grass. Many Grass-type Pokémon favor it for its rich flavor and texture.

Cooking Effect: Restorative potion lv1. Slightly recovers the Grass-type Energy in a Pokémon's body and restores PP of Grass-type moves.

Evaluation: Juicing tender spring grass might give a better result.]

*He thought it over.*

D stared at the grass juice in front of him.

It was a restorative potion.

It was likely like a small PP restore item in a game.

The effect was decent.

The downside was that it only restored PP of Grass-type moves and was not as useful for Pokémon of other types.

Then D started trying fried grass, spicy grass, steamed green grass and every cooking method he could think of.

The results were never that satisfying.

[Fried Grass (F): Effect is to reduce Grass-type Pokémon favorability.]

[Steamed Green Grass (E): Effect is to slightly restore Grass-type Pokémon stamina.]

[Spicy Grass (D-): Effect is to occasionally make a Grass-type Pokémon breathe fire and raise the favorability of certain Pokémon.]

D could not help feeling a bit speechless.

No doubt the certain Pokémon here meant the Fire and Grass dual-type Capsakid and its evolution Scovillain.

In his mind he pictured that odd-looking Pokémon with a humanoid body and two large chili-like heads.

One head was green and one was red.

When the green head was stimulated by spicy elements it turned fierce, converted the spiciness into Fire Energy and spat explosive flames from the red head.

Honestly it was a bit hard to appreciate.

D thought Hydreigon with its three heads looked better than Scovillain.

He called Cyclizar over to help deliver the edible grass dishes to the Gogoat.

The rest went in the trash.

Next was the highlight of this try which was Green Grass Cake.

This was a very special dish.

In that farm game from Chu Feng's memories players fed Green Grass Cake to the little characters they were raising.

Then they had them tend the crops on the farm.

The crops those characters cared for would mutate a little.

D recalled the cake-making steps in his mind then started trying.

He used the juicer to crush the grass then took a suitable amount of cake flour.

He added vegetable oil, the crushed grass paste and three fresh egg yolks.

After mixing them evenly he added half a bottle of MooMoo Milk and kept stirring.

It soon became a deep green liquid batter.

He whipped the leftover egg whites with sugar into something like cream then added that to the green batter.

When the two mixes were fully combined he poured them into a cake mold and put it in the oven to bake for 55 minutes.

He sat in his chair hoping for the cooking effect.

After a long wait there was a sound.

**Ding.**

The cake was ready.

He took it out at once and looked down.

The information on the cake appeared.

[Incomplete Green Grass Cake. Effect unknown.]

*The cake was incomplete?*

D was slightly stunned then remembered that in the game the cake still needed a layer of cream on top.

He went outside, called Alcremie and borrowed a big bucket of light cream from it.

The light cream was white and did not have a grass flavor.

After thinking a moment D took the freshly crushed grass, added it to the light cream and stirred until the white cream turned the color of grass.

Next was the last step.

He set the baked cake base and spread the grass-scented green cream evenly on it until everything was smooth.

Finally he placed a tender green piece of grass on top and the cake was officially complete.

From the outside it looked almost the same as Lazy Sheep's Green Grass Cake.

That could well mean he had succeeded on the first try.

He looked at it eagerly and found the cake had changed from [Incomplete Green Grass Cake] to [Green Grass Cake].

[Green Grass Cake (A): A cake made with mature summer grass, flour, MooMoo Fresh Milk and Alcremie's cream.

Cooking Effect: Flower Trick. When fed to Grass-type Pokémon there is a slight chance the crops they tend with Grass-type Energy will show minor mutations.

Evaluation: Perfectly integrates the flavors of different ingredients. Popular among Grass-type Pokémon. Grass-type Pokémon who eat it might bring you a surprise out of gratitude.]

*Just right. I can go back and buy a sand pot to make soup.*

He pulled up the wild ginger by the roots and put it into his backpack.

Butterfree curiously watched this scene. *"Fweeee?"*

D explained the use of these mountain goods then asked with a laugh. *"Butterfree, do you know where else there are edible things growing nearby?"*

Butterfree blinked, thought carefully then nodded repeatedly. *"Fweeee!"*

*Oh. It really does know?*

Butterfree led the way and D quickly followed.

After passing through a dense thicket they arrived in front of a large tree.

Sure enough the tree was laden with many green-skinned very unripe fruits.

Butterfree gently plucked one and placed it in D's palm.

At first D did not recognize it.

Soon he realized what it was.

They were unripened mountain almonds!

*"Fweeee~"* Butterfree said some Pokémon come here to eat these when they cannot find food.

D did not quite understand.

He nodded then shook his head. *"No. You cannot eat this now. It is not ripe yet."*

*"Fweeee?"*

D thought for a moment. *"It probably needs another month or two to mature! This is also delicious when ready. It has a crunchy and refreshing taste. It is a very nutritious fruit."*

*"Fweeee!"*

Butterfree had already started drooling.

D laughed. *"I will bring you back here when they are ripe."*

Butterfree nodded very eagerly.

D asked again. *"Besides these, what did you eat when you were living in the wild?"*

*"Fweeee..."*

Butterfree thought about it carefully.

When it was still a Caterpie its food was mostly leaves from trees.

Occasionally it was lucky enough to find a Berry.

Most of the time they stayed hidden in dense foliage and grass to avoid being eaten by predatory Bird-type Pokémon.

Though the chances were slim they had observed the eating habits of many other Pokémon.

Butterfree sniffed the air then led D further into the forest.

Soon they arrived under an ancient towering tree.

D peered past the trunk and spotted a hive of wild Combee ahead.

Combee were constantly flying in and out of the hive to collect nectar while their leader Vespiquen patrolled the area guarding the honey painstakingly made by the Combee.

*"Wild honey, huh..."* D was a bit surprised and softly asked. *"Have you ever come here to steal honey before?"*

Butterfree shook its head and whispered back. *"Fweeee!"*

*It never dared to come!*

Those Combee were fierce and Vespiquen was terrifying.

They were not friendly like the Combee on the ranch.

But other Pokémon would come like Ursaring and Teddiursa.

Mothim would sneak over at night while the Combee were asleep to steal honey.

*"Forget it. There is honey on the ranch. Let us not disturb these Combee."* D said.

Butterfree had no objections.

Quietly they went on continuing their search for usable wild goods in this area.

Meanwhile in Mesagoza City.

The renowned Paldea Academy is located in this biggest city of the Paldea region.

Contrary to what D thought Naranja Academy is just a branch of Paldea Academy.

Together with Uva Academy they form Paldea Academy.

The two academies have different names, different uniforms and different teaching focuses.

Students coming here can choose their preferred academy based on their interests.

Today is the midterm exam day at Paldea Academy.

Apart from the basic written exam the academy specifically organized a Pokémon battle competition between Naranja Academy and Uva Academy.

The ultimate winner besides getting an A rating on the performance board can also receive the academy's Treasure Hunt Scholarship.

It is a significant amount of money attracting many students to enroll in the competition.

In the open-air battle venue at the center of Mesagoza a tense Pokémon battle is underway.

Students in Naranja Academy and Uva Academy uniforms have gathered around the battle venue.

They shouted excitedly while discussing the two Trainers in the match with their companions.

*"Hey, Delibird versus Crocalor. Quickly guess who will win this battle!"*

*"Of course it is Crocalor! Fire-type is strong against Ice-type Delibird!"*

*"Plus the Trainer of that Delibird is quite famous in our Naranja Academy!"*

*"Oh? Tell me more. What is the story?!"*

*"His name is Carter and he is said to be a young master from a big family in Cascarrafa City. He has been pampered since childhood and is terrible at training Pokémon. His Delibird is notoriously weak. Even a regular Sprigatito can defeat him with the wrong type matchup."*

*"I heard not long ago the young master resolved to work hard. Every day he took his Delibird to the playground to do push-ups and run. But he ended up exhausted in less than a week causing quite a fuss."*

On the battle stage Carter's face flushed as he listened to others' comments about himself.

But soon images of the past few days emerged in his mind.

At that ranch with the sparring of those Gogoat comparable to Elite Four level in strength his Delibird gained considerable experience and has now undergone a complete transformation.

It even learned more powerful moves.

*So they certainly will not lose!*

As if feeling the Trainer's emotions the Delibird on the battlefield also responded loudly to him.

*"Deli-bir-bird!"*

At this moment the referee waved the flag and announced. *"Let the battle begin!"*

*"Delibird, use Rain Dance!"*

Carter immediately issued the command.

Rain Dance can reduce the power of Fire-type moves which is very effective when facing Crocalor.

The opposing Trainer did not issue a command right away but instead looked up at the scene with Crocalor.

*"Rain Dance, huh? I will let you have one move then lest others say I relied on type matchups to beat you."*

*"No need!"* Feeling belittled Carter was annoyed.

At this moment it began to rain in the sky.

Crocalor's Trainer finally got serious. *"Use Ember!"*

Command given Crocalor took two steps forward and a string of hot embers spewed from its mouth aiming straight at the Delibird in mid-air.

*"Dodge and use Double Team!"* Carter reacted quickly.

Delibird flapped its wings, ducked down in the rain and the embers brushed past it.

Then Delibird moved swiftly and numerous clones appeared in the rain curtain.

*"Double Team? When did it learn that?!"* The crowd was astonished knowing the move was quite hard to master.

Carter's ears were sharp and he caught people's voices.

A smile of excitement showed on his face. *It is all thanks to Mr. D!*

In the Grass Trial the relentless attacks from Gogoat forced out the potential within Delibird prompting it to learn Double Team to dodge attacks.

And that is not all!

Thinking of this Carter shouted. *"Use that move!"*

At the same time Crocalor's Trainer issued a command. *"Do not think I cannot counter Double Team. Crocalor, use Heat Wave!"*

Both Pokémon moved simultaneously on the field.

Crocalor took a heavy step forward.

Fire light surged from its throat transforming into a gust of hot wind sweeping toward Delibird.

Delibird did not hurry.

It first flew forward charging straight into the hot wind.

*"What is it doing?! Is it not afraid to die?!"*

The onlookers were shocked.

But just as Delibird was about to be engulfed by the Heat Wave its body suddenly changed direction exaggeratedly then shot straight into the sky.

Delibird played with the Heat Wave as white light emerged on its wings forming into a blade then swiftly cutting forward slicing at Crocalor.

Crocalor's body staggered backward from the hit.

The crowd held their breath unable to believe it.

Soon someone recognized the move's name.

*"It is Aerial Ace!"*

Carter felt a surge of pleasure in his heart pointing toward Crocalor and declared loudly.

*"Compared to Mr. D you are still far behind! Delibird, use Fling!"*

The opposing young Trainer panicked and hurriedly said. *"Crocalor, dodge!"*

But it was too late.

Delibird had already positioned itself behind Crocalor, tightly holding Crocalor with its wings then using all its strength to fling it into the sky.

*"Whoa!"*

The crowd gasped in shock their gaze following Crocalor up into the air.

Delibird followed closely moving even faster to place itself above Crocalor.

*"Now, use Brick Break!"*

*"Deli-bir-bird!"*

Delibird raised its wings high, chopped down hard and smashed Crocalor directly to the ground.

Everything on the field settled.

People gathered around Carter shocked by his growth.

*"It all started with getting lost."*

*Mr. D, Gogoat, make sure to teach those annoying people a good lesson. Do not let me suffer alone!*
