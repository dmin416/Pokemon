Mew and Teddiursa agreed.

After D finished his daily ranch work he went to the town.

He found a shop that makes accessories and spent a hefty sum to rush a custom-made wristband for Teddiursa that is waterproof, electric-proof and heat-resistant.

After it was made D went to the Porto Marinada Market to buy an abundance of ingredients.

When he rode Cyclizar back home Teddiursa was sitting on the roof with Mew playing together.

D called Teddiursa down and put the wristband on its arm.

This wristband was specially designed with a hidden space inside.

D placed a note and several large denomination League coins there.

The note indicates Teddiursa's family address, which is the ranch's location.

D told Teddiursa that if it couldn't find its way back it should go to a human city and find Miss Jenny or Miss Joy, show them the note and let them help buy a ticket or boat ticket to send Teddiursa back.

Anyway Miss Joy and Miss Jenny look identical worldwide.

After thinking a bit D also took Teddiursa to the town to recognize Miss Joy and Miss Jenny.

Looking at the wristband on its arm Teddiursa was very touched.

D smiled, patting its head and once everything was prepared he took it back to the ranch.

Sending a group of Pokémon outside to play D stood in the kitchen, quite touched as he gazed at the large amount of ingredients laid out before him.

*"Hope today I can make super powerful food!"* D silently wished in his heart.

He recalled the Honey Pudding and Honey Bun he had made before. The ingredients enhancing Teddiursa seemed inseparable from honey.

Thus in the upcoming days of cooking experiments D planned to start with honey.

With a glance at Teddiursa waiting obediently beside him D turned around, planning to take out a few Lucky Puddings from the fridge for it to eat.

But when he opened the fridge he found the Lucky Pudding was already eaten up.

D: *"..."*

Oh well, since he has no idea now he might as well make some Lucky Pudding for Teddiursa to eat.

After all it won't be able to eat any for a long time afterward.

And just then D suddenly thought there were still some Black Truffles at home.

Now here's the question: if he adds high-grade enhanced ingredient Black Truffle into Lucky Pudding, could he make a permanently luck-enhancing dish?

Without hesitation D began attempting immediately.

He took out the last small piece of Black Truffle, ground it into powder and mixed it into the prepared pudding liquid, then poured it into a container and put it in the fridge to chill.

After patiently waiting for a while D couldn't wait to take out the batch of pudding that had Black Truffle added.

Sure enough the information about the dish changed.

The dish appraisal appeared.

[Black Truffle Caramel Honey Pudding (A+): A uniquely flavored dessert made with Black Truffle, Light Cream, MooMoo Fresh Milk and Honey.

Cooking Effect: Extreme Luck. After consumption humans experience a significant temporary boost to luck; for Pokémon the chance of picking up rare items during the journey increases.

Evaluation: A delicious dessert that makes people feel happy after eating it, more likely to bring good fortune.]

*"Extreme Luck?"* Looking at the expected cooking effect D slightly frowned. *"It just further enhances luck but the duration doesn't seem to change. What a pity."*

He divided the batch of pudding between Teddiursa and Mew, then looked at the remaining Gelatin Powder and sticky honey, suddenly having a flash of inspiration in his mind, thinking of a new dessert!
