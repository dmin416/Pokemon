# Events

## Chapter 1

- D transmigrates into the Pokémon world after being struck by lightning; wakes by Rota Gym's lake among Oddish, Magikarp, Goldeen and Slowpoke.
- He confirms same name and face in an eighteen-year-old body and accepts he is now Gym Leader of Rota Gym.
- He reviews his situation: parents away on an Alola honeymoon, gym founded by his father, several million left for living expenses.
- He recalls his weak challenger lineup (Sprigatito, Oddish, Goldeen, Slowpoke and Spearow) and Rota's trash reputation next to Cerulean.
- He checks League site comments: Rota Gym mocked as a waste of time; Cerulean Gym praised for looks and dance more than battle strength.
- He notes Kanto gym pressure: more gyms than the famous eight, League review can close failing gyms and trainers want real fights before tournaments.

## Chapter 2

- D resolves to try saving Rota Gym with a journey as backup if it fails.
- Receptionist announces Queen Ilene; Sprigatito joins D from the backyard after watching a Pidgey.
- Ilene visits Cameran Palace business in Rota; comforts him about remote location north of Pewter and Mt. Moon.
- Talk covers his short Pokémon School stint, father's "enjoy life" exit and Ilene's limited non-Trainer queen role under the League.
- Ilene delivers father's Alola package: letter plus two occupied Poké Balls (rare League purchase and one caught locally).
- Letter pushes gym progress or closure, jokes about a stepmother and grandparents via Ilene; D stores it blank-faced.

## Chapter 3

- D and Ilene release the gifts in the backyard: Mareanie and Goomy; Pokédex confirms types, abilities and moves.
- D feeds typed Pokéblocks and uses Power of Viridian; both warm to him while Sprigatito inspects them.
- He notes the world mixes Adventures-style Viridian power with anime-like Lance as Champion.
- He plans a unique gym feature (Cerulean's dance as model) because raw strength is not ready yet.
- He teaches Mareanie more than ten TMs at once and aims for a few days of training before challengers.

## Chapter 4

- Same outing yields Oddish, Nidoran, Venonat, Zubat and a Nidorino; D commits to a temporary Poison specialty.
- He frames Poison as trainer education and lasting impressions, shrugs off Fuchsia Gym as competition and plans to request Corrosion Salandit/Salazzle and Paldea Glimmora from his father.
