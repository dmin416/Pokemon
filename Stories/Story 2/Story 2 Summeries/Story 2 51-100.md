When Arcanine Extreme Speeds in again, Sableye Disables Extreme Speed.

Arcanine finishes Sableye with a second Play Rough. The referee awards Arcanine the KO.

Gary revives in the stands and boasts that Arcanine will sweep the rest of the match. Selene and the receptionist ask whether Blue knows how hard Gary talks him up.

D returns Incineroar. Both Intimidate abilities lower each other's Attack.

Incineroar Fake Outs Arcanine, then Parting Shots again, lowering Attack and Special Attack before switching out.

Blue finally understands the full support rotation: Incineroar Intimidate and Fake Out, Parting Shot out, then Sableye trapping with Mean Look.

D sends Drapion. Gary checks its moves and feels uneasy after remembering D's battle video against Red.

Arcanine Intimidates Drapion. Blue orders Flamethrower carefully because Extreme Speed is still sealed.

D has Drapion set Toxic Spikes while tanking the Flamethrower as a Poison-type.

Blue realizes Arcanine cannot leave the field to clear the spikes because of Mean Look.

He angrily asks why a so-called Dark team is using Toxic Spikes and calls the setup fraud.

D answers with a mild smile and asks whether Drapion is Dark-type or not.

# 94: Chapter 94: Who In The World Allowed You To Become A Gym Leader

Blue is stunned by the question. He knows Drapion is Dark and Poison, but he still feels Toxic Spikes betray the Dark-team presentation.

D explains that using every useful type and move a Pokémon knows is normal battle craft, and that Blue's mindset is too narrow.

Blue laughs in frustration and orders Flamethrower again, determined to crush D anyway.

Drapion's Cross Poison overpowers the weakened Flamethrower. When Arcanine tries to dodge, Toxic Spikes pulse through its body and stall it, letting Cross Poison land hard.

Drapion follows with Pursuit. Arcanine escapes with Extreme Speed once Disable ends, but Drapion hits it again with Cross Poison in midair.

Blue orders Roar as Drapion prepares Earthquake. Roar forces Drapion off the field and randomly brings out another of D's Pokémon.

The Pokémon that appears is Darkrai.

D looks sheepish and says Blue was extremely unlucky. He had counted Darkrai as part of the Dark team but had not planned to use a Mythical Pokémon in this gym match.

Blue is furious that a Mythical Pokémon has entered an ordinary gym challenge and silently asks who allowed D to become a Gym Leader.

Spectators fall silent. Selene stares. The receptionist nearly laughs. Gary goes quiet over his Pokédex entry.

When Blue hesitates, D asks whether he is going to attack. Blue then declares that even Darkrai will not stop Blue of the Oak family.

Arcanine Extreme Speeds in but is slowed by Toxic Spikes. Darkrai hits it with two Dark Pulses before it can recover.

Arcanine faints. Darkrai wins the exchange.

Selene adores Darkrai's appearance. The receptionist teases Gary again. Blue admits Darkrai is as strong as the legends claim and braces for the next round.

# 95: Chapter 95: This Is a Gym Battle Where I Bet My Dignity!

Blue decides he must clear Toxic Spikes first even though he faces Darkrai. He refuses to give up against a Legendary Pokémon.

He sends Blastoise, which Rapid Spins the Toxic Spikes away. The Blastoise is larger, more muscular, and more aggressive than usual, with Torrent and moves including Aura Sphere, Hydro Cannon, Iron Defense, Shell Smash, Aqua Tail, Tackle, Rapid Spin, and inherited Water Spout.

D judges it stronger than Red's Blastoise and notes that prodigies like Red and Blue grow quickly in short periods.

Darkrai closes in with Dark Pulse and tears through Blastoise's Hydro Pump.

Blue thinks close range favors Blastoise and orders Body Press. D answers with Dark Void at short range.

Dark Void swallows Blastoise mid-charge and puts it into a deep sleep. D explains that Dark Void's accuracy is poor at distance but very high up close.

Darkrai's Bad Dreams ability then begins damaging the sleeping Blastoise. Blue calls the effect nasty. D takes it as a compliment.

Before Thunder can finish Blastoise, Blue recalls it in time. He is annoyed by D's calm teaching tone but cannot deny D's strength.

Blue sends Machamp next for type advantage. D warns that it may not be ideal; Blue insists on fighting anyway.

Machamp closes with inherited Bullet Punch. Darkrai freezes it with Psychic and lifts it into the air.

Machamp breaks free enough to use Rock Slide, forcing Darkrai to drop the Psychic hold. Machamp then charges with Cross Chop.

D has Darkrai advance instead of retreat, hold Dark Void in its claws, dodge Cross Chop at close range, and slam Dark Void into Machamp's face.

Machamp falls asleep on the spot. Blue realizes he never taught Sleep Talk or Snore and briefly considers surrendering for dignity's sake, then refuses to quit.

Darkrai finishes Machamp with Psychic attacks, then knocks out the still-weakened Blastoise with Thunderbolts and Bad Dreams, completing a three-Pokémon streak.

D praises Darkrai. Blue stares at Blastoise's Poké Ball in shock that he lost a complete gym battle.

D tells Blue the loss is unfortunate but that Blue's strength and training quality are excellent. Blue finds praise hard to accept as the loser.

D then mentions that Red spoke highly of Blue and that Red also challenged him in a 4v4. Blue immediately asks about the result.

When D says Red defeated two of his main Pokémon, Blue refuses to believe it because Blue himself only defeated one.

D says the battle video is online. Blue's pride collapses into desperation.

He asks to use the gym's Pokémon transfer machine so he can bring more main Pokémon from Professor Oak's laboratory and continue the challenge at once.

D notes that it is getting late. Blue begs sincerely for the first time, saying this is a gym battle where he is betting his dignity.

# 96: Chapter 96: D, You've Got the Wrong Person!

D only meant to tease Blue by mentioning Red, but Blue's pride makes him demand an immediate rematch.

D refuses to continue that day and instead offers Blue the Rota Badge, saying Blue's strength already qualifies even though he lost.

This is the third time D has tried to give the badge away: once to Red, once to a persistent glasses-wearing challenger, and now to Blue.

D's standards are high. He wants strength or potential, willpower, mindset, and command skill, and he wants the Rota Badge to be the most prestigious in Kanto.

Blue immediately rejects the offered badge as an insult. He says he will only accept it if he beats D fair and square.

D shows him a Dark Rota Badge made with unusual materials and a small black diamond. He explains that Rota currently has separate Dark and Poison badges for the matching team types.

Blue refuses again, saying taking a free badge would make him no better than a beggar.

Selene and the receptionist gain respect for Blue's principles despite his arrogance.

Blue claims he has beaten Red three times out of five and that he will stand at the top of the world. D does not deny Blue's future strength, only that being the absolute best is uncertain.

Blue asks again to keep battling with more Pokémon. D tells him to come back fully prepared instead of underestimating the rematch.

Blue agrees to rebuild his strategy and team, especially against Darkrai, then vows to return and take the badge by winning.

Before leaving, Blue says D may call him Blue and asks about the gold section of the wheel.

D explains that gold is the Legendary Gold lineup: a 2v2 against Legendary Pokémon. Beating it earns a Legendary Rota Badge plus a mysterious gift that D does not describe.

Blue is shocked that D treats a Legendary battle as a challenger reward, then leaves awkwardly with Gary.

Outside, Blue confirms he will return with two prepared lineups after asking his grandfather to lift restrictions. When Gary worries about spinning gold, Blue insists it will not happen and scolds Gary into silence.

Three days after Blue's challenge, it is Friday at Rota Gym.

Selene's Litten has evolved into Torracat. She plans to catch Poison-line Pokémon at Mount Moon and says D thinks she may handle some challengers herself in about a month.

Dad texts that President Vernon praised D, that he and Amelia will leave for Hoenn in a couple of days, and that Amelia wants D to visit Alola.

Mimikyu is Ghost and Fairy with Disguise and knows Double Team, Shadow Sneak, Wood Hammer, Copycat, Astonish, and inherited Destiny Bond.

Zorua is Dark-type with Illusion and knows Knock Off, Taunt, Torment, Scary Face, Fury Swipes, and inherited Sucker Punch.
