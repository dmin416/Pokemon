# D

Reusable character kit for any Pokémon fiction. Slot in whole or in part. May stay off-page as author headcanon.

Related: `Pokemon/Deoxys.md`, `Pokemon/Honedge.md`, `Ideas/Dynamaxing.md`, `Ideas/Fighting Team.md`.

---

## Summary

D is a transmigrator with the Power of Viridian. A meteor nearly kills him. The Deoxys inside is weaker and dying. In its last moments it feels him heal it instead of himself. It returns the favor and enters his body as a bio-nano symbiote. Later a Honedge lets him absorb life energy and soul energy. Later a Rotom bonds into the Deoxys colony and the triple symbiosis lets him reshape body and toolkit almost freely. His transmigration package also includes Growth Mode (game sim to strengthen Pokémon) and a Cooking System (ingredient appraisal and special-effect dishes).

---

## Core identity

- Name: D.
- Role flexes by story: rancher, gym leader, traveler, or whatever the fiction needs.
- Starting virtue: heals the dying alien before himself.
- Partner tone: Deoxys is a mutual survival contract, not a parasite and not a slave. Honedge adds hunger. Rotom adds mischief and tech reach.
- Late-game fantasy: with all three bonds and both systems mature he can basically do whatever the plot needs. Early use stays limited and expensive.

---

## Backstory (portable)

1. Arrives in the Pokémon world with Power of Viridian.
2. A meteor strikes. His body is maimed. Survival is not certain.
3. The Deoxys in the stone is worse off and near death.
4. He spends Viridian energy on it instead of closing his own wounds.
5. It understands that choice and enters him as repayment.
6. From then on Deoxys is both Pokémon and internal system: repair, armor, form assist, body upgrade.

Where the meteor lands (farm, road, gym grounds) is story-dependent. If it hits a farm it can leave a local Dynamax power spot; see `Ideas/Dynamaxing.md`.

## Power ladder

### Power of Viridian
- Heal Pokémon.
- Ease bonding and trust.
- Soft edge in ranch, gym, or journey frames.

### Deoxys (bio-nano symbiote)
- Adaptive nano-tissue in flesh and nerves. Specimen notes: `Pokemon/Deoxys.md`.
- Reinforces bones, nerves, muscles and senses.
- Can surface as armor, weapons, tendrils or combat forms (Normal / Attack / Defense / Speed flavors).
- Shares awareness with D.
- Early cost: stamina drain or recovery time. Cost falls as the bond deepens.

### Honedge (life / soul siphon)
- Found later. Blade that can cut more than flesh. Line notes: `Pokemon/Honedge.md`.
- With Deoxys mediating, D can absorb life energy and soul energy from willing targets, defeated foes or special sources.
- Absorbed energy feeds nano repair, Viridian reserves or temporary buffs.
- Risks if the fiction needs them: overdraw, corruption, ghost backlash, League scrutiny.

### Rotom (triple symbiosis)
- Bonds with the Deoxys colony rather than only with appliances.
- Electric / ghost data-spirit plus alien nano plus host.
- Effects as needed: possess and rewrite machines, ride signals, hybrid combat modes, faster Deoxys adaptation, system UIs in the head.
- Peak package with Deoxys and Honedge already in place.

## Transmigration systems

### Growth Mode
- Enter a game-like regional run.
- Levels, catches, badges and Hall of Fame progress can map back to real Pokémon by export rules.
- Hardens the team without waiting on wild grinding alone.
- Region order and export limits are story-dependent.

### Cooking System
- Appraises ingredients: grade, traits, hidden properties.
- Cooking imparts special effects into dishes (recovery, buffs, fondness, move chance, type bait and similar).
- Quality grades track results (C- through S style works fine).
- Optional later synergy: dishes store siphoned energy or stabilize symbiote load.
