# 121: Chapter 119: Koraidon Gets Jealous (Double-Length Chapter)



Early the next morning, as the sky just started to lighten, D got out of bed.



Outside, a light rain was falling. The sky was gray, and the entire farm was shrouded in a misty drizzle, with the grass glistening with a soft sheen of moisture.



"Still raining, huh?"



D muttered, opening the door to let the cool breeze in, then grabbed some sandwiches and eggs from the fridge, making a few simple sandwiches which Cyclizar delivered to Glaceon.



After finishing breakfast quickly, Dragonite was already standing under the eaves outside the door, with its small red satchel on its back, waiting to start work.



D handed it two bottled goat milk: "Thanks for your hard work!"



"Roar~"



Dragonite put away the milk and disappeared into the rain.



D then put on rain boots and carried an umbrella as he stepped out of the house, turning back to stop Cyclizar and Koraidon who wanted to follow:



"I'm going to feed the Gogoat; don't come along. Stay inside, I'll be back soon!"



Rainy days would bring mud pits to the ground, and though Koraidon and Cyclizar wouldn't roll around in the mud, their paws would still get somewhat muddy, tracking it all over the house.



Thus, the two Pokemon stood under the eaves, watching eagerly as D walked into the misty rain.



The rain did nothing to dampen the Gogoat's exuberance.



When D arrived, the whole goat pen had transformed into a stage with neon lights flashing everywhere.



The three Gogoat were dancing wildly inside.



Nearby, Smoliv seemed unfazed by this scene.



It basked in the abundance of Grass  Type energy, swaying its body comfortably.



D, unfazed, poured a bag of Grassy Seed into the feed trough and filled the water trough nearby with water.



The three Gogoat immediately gathered around, shoving their heads into the food trough to start their breakfast.



D stood by, watching this scene with a sense of awe.



The Gogoat were becoming more robust; after evolving, their height had increased significantly.



If they stood up fully, they would give off an imposing presence, as if confronting not Gogoat but the legendary Sacred Swordsmen, Cobalion, and Virizion.



D shook his head, dismissing those odd thoughts.



Then he went to the Chicken House, taking feed from the silo and placing it in the feed trough, shutting the doors and windows to prevent the hens from getting soaked and sick.



In the Pokemon House, three Miltank had already milked themselves.



D came over and filled barrels with milk, placed them in a corner, then took out a brush to start grooming them.



The Miltank looked utterly content.



For them, this life was the epitome of happiness.



Every day, someone helped with milking, feeding, grooming, and they had fresh, fertile grass to eat.



They loved it here.



As D brushed the Miltank, he observed their mood. Seeing their enjoyment, his mood became uplifted.



Speaking of these three Miltank, though they looked alike, their personalities were completely different.



One was gentle and caring, often sharing its fresh milk with the weaker Pokemon in the farm.



Another was calm and liked tranquility, enjoying the lush grass outside and sunbathing on sunny days.



The third was lively and active, always full of energy, enjoying communicating with other Pokemon on the farm.



"All done, you're brushed!"



D patted the Miltank's head, hung the brush on the wall, and said, "Go play! I still have to go next door to feed Nacli."



The Miltank agreed gladly.



Seeing this, D smiled, then turned and headed next door.



As Rock Type Pokemon, Nacli did not like rainy days, mostly choosing to stay indoors and not venture out in such weather.



At this moment, the door of the Salt House was closed. The image of those five small figures flashed through D's mind, bringing a smile to his lips as he pushed open the door of the Salt House.



The first sight that greeted him was a huge figure made of rock.



It was a Pokemon made entirely of rock, its body square, like something assembled from building blocks.



D's eyes widened as he realized what had happened.



Nacli had evolved! Overnight, from Nacli to Naclstack!



Before he came in, the other four Nacli had gathered before the Naclstack, looking at their evolved companion with envy.



Seeing D approach, they ran over, encircling him.



"Nacli!"



"Nacli..."



The evolved Naclstack also moved its limbs and stepped forward.



"Naclstack~"



Its growl had clearly changed since its time as Nacli, losing its previous naivety, and now sounded like a mix between a deep voice and a rumble.



D looked with curiosity at this Naclstack, asking, "When did you evolve?"



"Naclstack... stack!" Naclstack responded.



It was just last night; while it was sleeping, it suddenly felt a mysterious power surge from within.



When it awoke, it found itself in its current state.



"Congratulations!" D smiled, reaching out to pat its head.



But in the next moment, the Naclstack in front of him spat out a large amount of crystal-clear, spotless Rock Salt.



In the blink of an eye, the Rock Salt piled into a "salt mountain" on the floor beside them.



---



"Wow!"



D widened his eyes, quite shocked as he watched the scene unfold.



He only knew that Naclstack could produce more Rock Salt, but he hadn't expected it to be this exaggerated.



The amount produced in one go exceeded what used to be gathered in two weeks before!



Nearby, the ecstatic Naclstack looked at the pile of Rock Salt, then at D: "Stack! Stack~"



Life on the farm, carefree as it was, hadn't erased Naclstack's past memories.



It still remembered how D protected them, cared for them, rescued them from the dangers of the wild when they were powerless, and brought them back to the farm, even building a beautiful house for them.



Naclstack never forgot these things for even a moment.



At this moment, the simple and pure-hearted Naclstack was expressing its gratitude to the human in front of it in the only way it knew.



This was all it could do.



Affectionately, Naclstack nudged D with its blocky head.



Though the language barrier existed, D miraculously felt the emotions of this pure-hearted Pokémon.



It was expressing gratitude towards him!



The emotions of the Rock Pokemon were as simple and profound as a mountain.



D was touched and patted Naclstack, saying, "Thank you, Naclstack, I can feel your sincerity."



"Stack~"



The four unevolved Nacli nearby were incredibly envious.



They jumped around beside him, uttering cries of "Nacli, Nacli."



Seeing this, D laughed and picked them up one by one, saying, "You guys also have to work hard! But remember, Naclstack isn't the final stop, there's the mightier Garganacl ahead."



"Nacli!" They would try their best!



Suddenly, D remembered that Garganacl was over two meters tall.



If all five Nacli evolved into Garganacl, their current Salt House would become very low and cramped for them.



"Looks like I need to ask Keri if she can build a bigger Salt House," D murmured to himself.



Then he went back to the cabin and fetched a bag, preparing to collect that pile of Rock Salt while mentioning Nacli's evolution to Alcremie.



Sure enough, upon hearing the news, Alcremie immediately expressed a desire to see for itself.



So D took Alcremie to the Salt House.



Upon seeing Naclstack for the first time, Alcremie's eyes showed a shocked expression: "Alcrem..."



Was this really the tiny Naclstack it used to know?



In front of Nacli, Alcremie had always been the big sister leading them around to play.



Now upon seeing Naclstack, larger than itself, Alcremie suddenly felt a reversal in roles.



Its little followers became bigger than itself overnight...



Naclstack, on the other hand, didn't notice Alcremie's thoughts. It greeted Alcremie happily: "Stack~"



As D was busy collecting the Rock Salt, he said to Alcremie: "Once Naclstack evolves again, they'll grow even larger! Then they'll be bigger and taller than me!"



"Alcrem?!" Alcremie was even more shocked.



D continued, "Then you can stand on Garganacl and let them carry you around to play."



In his mind, he imagined five Garganacl lifting Alcremie high.



At that time, Alcremie would become a Garganacl knight.



Hearing this, both Nacli and Naclstack felt very happy, looking forward to that day.



"Alright then, I'll just put these salts back in the house. Do you want to come with me, or stay here and play with Nacli?" D stood up and asked.



Nacli and Naclstack really wanted Alcremie to stay and play.



Seeing the familiar look in Naclstack's eyes, Alcremie suddenly realized it didn't matter how they changed; they would still be the friends playing by its side.



So Alcremie no longer hesitated and nodded joyfully, indicating it wanted to stay and play with Nacli.



"Okay then." D smiled, "You guys have fun; I won't disturb you!"



He carried that bag of Rock Salt away from the Salt House.



The rain outside hadn't stopped, and once D had put the salts away, Dragonite, who had gone to deliver milk, had also returned.



It landed under the eaves, and D stepped forward to take off the bag hanging from its waist and then took out a towel to dry its rain-soaked body.



A lot of work cannot be done on a rainy day.



The fields don't need watering, and the hens can't graze, so D gave Pawmi and Dragonite a day off to rest at home.



Dragonite sat on the floor, with a basket of small breads that D had made by its side. These breads were small enough for Dragonite to devour in a single bite.



Pawmi watched this scene with admiration.



Ever since witnessing Dragonite release so much electricity yesterday, Pawmi had been in awe of Dragonite.



D noticed this and suddenly realized how interesting it was to observe the interactions between different Pokémon.



Just like Alcremie and Nacli, Butterfree and Combee, Gogoat and Smoliv, Dragonite and Pawmi, and all the Pokémon giving flowers to Glaceon.



Of course, the most amusing of all was Koraidon and Cyclizar.



It turns out, even Pokémon get jealous because of each other's relationship with their Trainers.



For example, now D sat on the carpet watching TV, leaning against Cyclizar, and suddenly noticed Koraidon's expression full of jealousy.



So D stood up, made a cup of the red tea Mrs. Dantes had once gifted him, then casually sat down next to Koraidon.



Koraidon's face immediately lit up with a happy expression.



Seeing this, D moved back beside Cyclizar.



Koraidon began to feel jealous again.



"Pfft!" Watching this scene, D couldn't help but laugh.



Koraidon, focusing intently on the distance between D and Cyclizar, finally noticed D's behavior. Its eyes widened in shock, seemingly unable to believe it.



"Ah gah hiss!"



Then, it turned away, sulking.



D hurriedly approached to console it, "Alright, I didn't mean to. Come watch TV together!"



Koraidon lay on the carpet with its eyes closed, pretending to be asleep, but its twitching eyelids betrayed it.



Beside them, Cyclizar was looking over with a clueless expression, gazing at Koraidon: "Gao ow?"



Koraidon pretended not to hear anything.



D said, "Cyclizar, Dragonite, and Pawmi are all watching you!"



Upon hearing this, Koraidon couldn't sit still anymore.



It always thought its past self was silly, but now its behavior seemed unchanged from before.



How could it let Cyclizar see it like this?!



Koraidon was mortified and desperately wanted to leave.



But at that moment, D patted the spot next to him, "Come watch TV with us!"



Koraidon: "..."



In its helplessness, Koraidon ran over and lay behind D, acting as his cushion.



Cyclizar didn't understand Koraidon's actions and tilted its head looking at Koraidon: "Gao ow?"



Koraidon closed its eyes.



Not listening, not hearing, turtle chanting scripture.



Seeing this, D wanted to laugh but managed to hold it in.



This Koraidon's personality was really too cute!



Externally flashy and dominant, yet reliably steady, occasionally revealing a hint of silliness just like the goofy Cyclizar.



D took its large claw in his hand.



Hmm, what should he say at this moment?



"You are all my wings?"



He fell into thought, but just then, a piece of news on the TV caught his attention.



"...Starting today, the newly discovered Terastalization by Professors Sada and Turo will officially be included in battles. Can this innovative battling method revolutionize the Trainer industry? Stay tuned!"



Then the screen showed a scene of two Trainers in battle, both using Terastalization.



Seeing this scene, D was somewhat surprised.



Terastalization is only now being used in Trainer battles?



He thought it had started long ago!



"No wonder the Trainers I met before didn't use Terastalization," D murmured.



But this also proved that now was still a very early time, Arven was just a few years old, and his parents were still around, meaning there's at least ten years before the Scarlet and Violet storyline begins.



Ten years later...



By then, he'd already be thirty!
