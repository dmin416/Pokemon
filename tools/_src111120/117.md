# 117: Chapter 115: Good Friends (Double-Length Chapter)



The small pearl in his hand emitted a beautiful silvery sheen.



Looking at it, D could almost imagine how Teddiursa and Mew managed to find it.



It might have been born or fallen from a pearl-producing Pokemon, finally drifting ashore after many years of being battered by the sea.



At that moment, Teddiursa and Mew, who were on an adventure, happened to pass by and discovered it.



With its Pickup ability, Teddiursa thought this shiny thing was quite pretty, so it picked it up.



To let him know it was a gift, Teddiursa and Mew, who couldn't speak human language, found some paper and left their paw prints on it with muddy hands.



Perhaps it was Teddiursa who came up with the idea, as it was the first to press its little paw print onto the paper.



Mew was attracted by this scene and thought it was fun, so it too left its paw print on the paper.



Afterwards, they entrusted the paper and the pearl to their good friend, Pelipper, whom they had met during their travels, asking it to deliver the items to the ranch in the Paldea region.



However, D was a bit puzzled.



How did Pelipper know the exact location of the ranch?



After pondering briefly, he arrived at a possible answer.



That is, just as the human world has its operating logic, the Pokemon World also has its own operating logic.



It's like the Meowth that wander through city alleys; they form a vast network sharing information that is unseen by humans.



Or like the Clefairy that gather and dance under the full moon, worshiping the moon as a deity, much like ancient human clans.



"What a pity..."



D couldn't help thinking, if only he had bought Teddiursa a camera when it left.



That way, Teddiursa could capture photos of what it encountered on its travels and send them back to the ranch, allowing him to know about their adventurous experiences.



However, since he has now received the pearl and postcard sent back by Teddiursa, it means their journey is going well.



D's worries were mostly eased, and he looked up at the sky, but Pelipper was already nowhere to be seen.



D looked again at the pearl in his hand, images of Mew and Teddiursa appeared in his mind.



Mew, which Team Rocket had spent decades searching for, had come into his life just like that.



They never even found it, only discovering a fossilized eyelash of Mew.



That eyelash fossil was later used by Team Rocket to create Mewtwo through genetic technology.



"I wonder what Boss Giovanni would feel if he knew about this incident."



Sometimes, humans can't help but have whimsical thoughts about things far removed from their lives.



Just like now, D, with a hint of mischievousness in his mind, imagined himself traveling to the Viridian Gym with Teddiursa, Mew, Koraidon, and Gogoat.



At that time, Boss Giovanni's expression would surely be priceless.



Unlike other regions that have some security problems, the Paldea region doesn't have a truly evil organization.



Team Star is just a group of former bullying victims forming a defense against bullying.



However, living a peaceful and stable life like this is also pretty nice.



Returning to his room, D carefully put the small pearl and postcard with paw prints away, storing them with the other possessions Teddiursa had previously accumulated in the drawer.



Cyclizar, with a growling stomach, was pitifully calling out from behind, just like a hungry child seeking their mother.



However, Cyclizar is indeed just a kid, as it's only a few months old, not yet a year.



He needed to start preparing dinner.



This evening, D planned to make pan-fried pork chops, paired with nutritious fruit juice and fresh milk.



He took the ingredients out of the refrigerator and began to busy himself.



When the sun had completely set, and darkness enveloped the ranch, dinner was finally ready.



D brought the steaming dishes to the table, letting Koraidon and the others eat first, while he took a Pokemon treat and a sandwich to the ice house at the back.



Pushing the door open, a burst of cold air hit him.



Glaceon was resting on the Ice Stone and immediately opened its eyes when it heard the sounds from outside.



D placed the food in front of it, smiling, "I wasn't sure what you like to eat, so I prepared these for you to try and see if they suit your taste."



"Bui...?"



Glaceon was quite surprised by this.



It looked down at the food it had never eaten before and couldn't help but wonder: Does this place actively provide food for Pokemon?



D crouched beside it, smiling, "Let me formally introduce myself, Glaceon. I'm the rancher of this ranch, and all the Pokemon you saw today came from different places to live here with me."



"Providing food for the Pokemon in the ranch is the responsibility of a good rancher, so please, eat without worry!"



"Bui..." Glaceon blinked, hesitating to touch the food.



In its mind, it was already grateful to have been given such a nice place to live.



And coupled with the fact that D had protected it from that Froslass previously.



Glaceon felt it owed him a lot of favors, and having done nothing in return, it couldn't bring itself to comfortably accept his food.



---



As a healthy Glaceon, it can solve its own food problems.



So, Glaceon gently shook its head: "Eevee."



Seeing this, D was a bit surprised.



"Not hungry?" he asked.



Glaceon curled back up on the Ice Stone.



D roughly guessed its thoughts and couldn't help but laugh: "All the berries growing on this ranch are mine, you know. If you want to pick some yourself at midnight, it's not much different from now, is it?"



"Eevee?!"



Glaceon had a surprised expression, what was going on?



D recalled the way Glaceon had refused his soda before, thinking that this Glaceon probably didn't like to owe anyone.



That's why it refused the food he offered.



How to put it? This kind of personality is quite cute.



Only this Glaceon was completely unaware of its current situation.



Glaceon felt full of doubt.



Suddenly, it got the feeling that it might have fallen into a trap.



But seeing the sincere look of the human in front, it thought maybe it was wrong.



D didn't explain to Glaceon because he had his own plans.



Glaceon must have a lot of questions now. If he didn't explain, Glaceon would be compelled to ask the Pokemon on the ranch.



At that time, it could enhance their relationship and bring them closer together.



Thinking of this, D stood up and bid farewell to Glaceon: "Well, good night, Glaceon!"



He walked out of the Ice Cave and back to the cabin, where he asked Alcremie, who was making cream in the kitchen:



"Alcremie, do you still want to be friends with Glaceon?"



Alcremie loved making friends, all the Pokemon on the ranch were its good friends.



Nacli, little Pawmi, the lazy Gogoat…



Hearing this, it nodded: "Gream..."



But then it dropped its head sadly because the Glaceon didn't respond to it, as if it didn't really want to be friends.



Seeing this, D called it over and whispered a few words into its ear.



After listening, Alcremie's eyes lit up: "Gream?" Could that really make it friends with Glaceon?



D nodded: "Of course, it's just that it appears very cold on the outside but is very kind inside. In human terms, it's called cold exterior, warm interior."



Alcremie looked at Koraidon, asking, "Gream?" And what about Koraidon?



D glanced at Koraidon and replied, "It's all warm."



"Gream!" Got it!



Alcremie nodded happily.



After giving instructions, D picked up little Pawmi to get ready for bed.



After more than a month of care, the once weak Pawmi had returned to a healthy shape, its fur becoming oily and slippery, fluffy.



Pawmi lay beside D, looking at Alcremie, it couldn't help but ask: "Paw?"



"Don't worry! They'll become good friends!" D patted its head: "Sleep now! I have a little task for you tomorrow."



He wanted to try the power facility Keri installed.



"Paw!" Hearing this, Pawmi began to look forward to it.



Compared to Dragonite, Koraidon, Gogoat, Pawmi felt like a small grass growing in the shadow of a big mountain.



It was just so tiny.



The weak Pawmi also hoped to do more for D like Dragonite, Gogoat, and Miltank.



Because D was very kind to it, not only bathing it but also preparing nutritious food, just like a mother.



But D was not its mother; they met by chance, yet he accepted it and forgave its stealing food.



Pawmi wanted to do more things, but it couldn't water nor produce milk.



So, when it heard D say he had something for it to do tomorrow, it was very happy.



"Paw?" What sort of task?



D teased: "You'll find out tomorrow, time to sleep now."



So Pawmi closed its eyes, started snoring, and looked forward to tomorrow's arrival.



Looking at this Pawmi, D couldn't help but admit he was charmed by its cuteness.



Such a well-behaved little Pawmi!



Like real family, wanting to do more for each other to share the load.



D smiled and patted Pawmi's cheek.



"Sleep now!"



————



At eight in the evening, most Pokemon on the ranch were already dreaming.



Alcremie arrived at the Ice Cave, holding a freshly picked Berry in its hand, as D suggested bringing a little gift.



Hearing footsteps outside, Glaceon in the Ice Cave immediately opened its eyes and cautiously looked at the door.



At this moment, a "Gream" call came from outside the door.



It was that Alcremie from the day. Glaceon thought.



The other was greeting it.



By nature, Glaceon wasn't planning to respond, but remembering what D had said earlier, it silently jumped off the Ice Stone.



Outside, Alcremie felt a bit nervous, wondering if the other would speak as D said?



And just then, the door was nudged open, and a Glaceon emerged from inside.



It came out!



Seeing Glaceon, Alcremie's eyes lit up: "Gream!"



Good evening! It greeted warmly.



---



"Bui..."



Glaceon looked at the Alcremie and finally responded.



Actually, it hadn't meant to ignore Alcremie before; it simply didn't know how to express itself or what to say.



Alcremie didn't seem to mind; it happily handed Glaceon a Berry and proactively said:



"Mama?" (Let me show you around this ranch! There are many Pokemon living here, and everyone is good friends.)



Good friends... Glaceon pondered on this word.



Combined with its inner confusion, it didn't refuse Alcremie. Instead, it lightly raised its forelimbs, followed behind Alcremie, and began familiarizing itself with this place.



Alcremie eagerly introduced the situation around them.



Glaceon moved forward, observing the surroundings; this was a habit it developed in the snowy mountains.



"Mama!" (Over there are Gogoats—they may look lazy, but they're actually quite impressive! With them is Smoliv, who came here with me.)



Upon hearing this, Glaceon glanced in that direction.



With just one look, it sensed a terrifying natural energy emanating from there.



The natural energy there was incredibly rich, almost as potent as its Ice Stone!



Intuition told it that if it hadn't gone to the snowy mountain initially but came here instead, it might have evolved into another form!



"Bui..."



Glaceon felt somewhat shocked inside.



Meanwhile, Alcremie seemed to be accustomed to it.



It continued to lead the way and said to Glaceon while passing by some houses:



"Mama... Mama!" (This is where Nacli, Miltank, and the hens live, but it's late; they're already resting... Tomorrow I'll introduce them to you!)



"Bui."



Then, they arrived at an orchard.



Alcremie said that D planted the fruit trees and crops here, and by autumn, these trees would bear delicious Berries.



Seeing this, Glaceon finally understood the words D spoke earlier.



It softly asked: "Bui?" (Have you always lived in this place?)



Alcremie nodded: "Mama~" (Yes!)



Glaceon: "Bui?" (Don't you need to find your own food every day?)



"Mama." Alcremie shook its head gently. Of course not! D cooks many delicious meals for them!



Glaceon was filled with confusion: "Bui?" (Why does D provide food for you?)



Upon hearing the question, Alcremie paused, seemingly not knowing the answer either.



It asked Glaceon to wait there for a moment, then ran back to the house and woke up D, who had just fallen asleep.



D yawned and rubbed his sleepy eyes, asking: "What's wrong?"



Alcremie: "Mama?" (Why do you cook so many delicious meals for us?)



Unfortunately, D didn't understand.



However, Koraidon, who was lying on the ground asleep, gave an answer.



Alcremie turned its head and saw Koraidon's mouth moving, although its eyes remained closed: "Ah-gah-s." (That's because D likes Pokemon.)



Having received the answer, Alcremie quickly ran outside, leaving D feeling confused.



He yawned, felt a wave of drowsiness, and fell back onto his pillow to continue sleeping.



D: zZZ



Watching Alcremie rush back, Glaceon found itself unsure of what to say.



Alcremie happily conveyed the answer to it.



Glaceon was slightly startled.



So, is it because he likes them that he prepares food for them?



Seeing it was still puzzled, Alcremie carefully recalled everyone in the ranch and then said to Glaceon:



"Mama!" (D prepares food for us, and we all help him manage the ranch!)



D works with Koraidon and Cyclizar to plant crops, Dragonite helps with watering, Combee collects honey, Gogoat produces goat milk...



It can also make delicious cream!



Upon hearing this, Glaceon felt a bit lost: "Bui?" (Why?)



Alcremie answered: "Mama!" (That's because we all like D!)



Mutual affection...



Glaceon didn't speak further.



A night breeze blew by.



It smelled a scent it had never experienced before.



Glaceon couldn't help but look up, and saw a beautiful flower field not far ahead—a flower field that couldn't exist in the snowy mountains.



Its gaze was irresistibly drawn to those flowers.



It walked forward, gently lowered its head, and inhaled the fragrance of the flowers.



At that moment, Glaceon felt like it understood something.



After everything settled, Alcremie escorted Glaceon to the entrance of the Ice Cave, nervously asking: "Mama?" (Can we be friends?)



Upon hearing this, Glaceon was slightly taken aback.



It thought about how Alcremie had given it the Berry, enthusiastically introduced the surroundings, and that moment in the afternoon when Alcremie received no response from it and left feeling disappointed.



After a moment of silence, it finally responded.



"Bui."



Alcremie was overjoyed, happily bidding farewell to Glaceon: "Mama!"



See you tomorrow!
